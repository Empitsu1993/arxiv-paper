#1 Scalable Learning of Determinantal Point Processes with Kernel-Structured Interactions
著者: Takahiro Kawashima, Hideitsu Hino
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12829
要約:
Determinantal point processes (DPPs) on a finite set are probability models that exhibit repulsion where inter-item similarities are encoded by a positive definite kernel matrix. Scaling DPP learning to large datasets typically requires imposing structural constraints on the kernel matrix, such as a low-rank-plus-diagonal form. However, structural assumptions introduce a severe trade-off between expressive power and computational scalability. Our study addresses this challenge by (a) modeling the full-rank interactions via a nonlinear kernel function and latent features, (b) designing a surrogate function for the maximum likelihood estimator, and (c) adopting stochastic optimization using random features of the nonlinear kernel. Numerical experiments on real-world datasets demonstrate that our method significantly improves this trade-off, achieving better generalization performance with remarkable parameter efficiency.
#2 Two-way Guidance: Simultaneous Learning of Summary Statistics for Generative Bayes
著者: Percy S. Zhai, Max Pachman, Peverel D. Shipley, Jungeum Kim
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12850
要約:
Bayesian simulation-based inference (SBI) can struggle to recover increasingly concentrated posteriors as observation size grows. We consider learning sufficient summary statistics jointly with the posterior as a general principle for scalable inference in various SBI methods. A common measure taken in SBI is dimension reduction through predetermined summaries, which may partially discard posterior information. Our approach instead imposes structure on how the posterior learner depends on a learned summary (e.g., affinity), and learns the summary simultaneously with the posterior. We identify an important mechanism named two-way guidance: under a structural restriction on the summary, the posterior sufficiency of the learned summary simplifies posterior learning, whereas a suitable posterior objective guides the learned summary towards posterior sufficiency. This mechanism explains a counterintuitive finding that the structural restriction on the summary statistics improves posterior learning, rather than limit its performance. We establish theoretical guarantees on both posterior estimation error and the learned summary's departure from sufficiency, thus identifying conditions on the simulation budget for recovering increasingly concentrated posteriors as observation size grows.
#3 Universal Bounds for Self-Normalized Kullback-Leibler Estimation
著者: Buu Phan, Lester Mackey, David Alvarez-Melis
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12851
要約:
This work studies an entropy-based estimator of Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence constructed from normalized importance weights. We prove a universal finite-sample bound showing that its expectation can exceed the target-proposal KL divergence by at most $\log 2$ for any sample size. We then characterize the KL divergence between the distribution induced by importance-weighted resampling and the proposal, showing that it is upper bounded by both the expectation of the entropy-based estimator and the target-proposal KL divergence. Importantly, these bounds require neither bounded importance weights nor finite higher moments. We apply these results to test-time alignment of generative models via Soft Best-of-$N$ sampling, an importance-sampling-based scaling technique proposed by Verdun et al. (2025), and to relative entropy coding, obtaining finite-sample policy-divergence certificates and moment-free bounds on coding cost.
#4 Generalized Determinantal Point Processes for Attraction and Repulsion with Distinct Interaction Kernels
著者: Hideaki Ishibashi, Takahiro Kawashima, Hideitsu Hino
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12894
要約:
Determinantal point processes (DPPs) model subset selection probability with repulsion, but many applications also exhibit attraction arising from pairwise relations or shared latent structures. Nonsymmetric DPPs (NDPPs) capture both effects, but their kernels are difficult to interpret and specify. We propose the determinant-ratio point process (DRPP), which models repulsion and attraction via a ratio of the determinants of two symmetric positive-definite kernels. This formulation preserves the feature-based interpretation of standard DPPs while allowing the two effects to be specified separately. Furthermore, we introduce an auxiliary-variable representation that enables efficient MCMC sampling and yields tractable expectation formulas for key statistics including the normalizing constant, which together facilitate maximum-likelihood learning. We theoretically characterize the interaction structures well suited to DRPPs: for clustered attraction with $C$ latent groups, DRPPs exactly realize the target pairwise interactions with an attraction rank $C$, whereas NDPPs can require a rank that grows with the number of items given the same repulsion component. Conversely, nontransitive pair-specific attraction may require compensation through the DRPP repulsion kernel. Experiments support these predictions, showing that DRPPs fit transitive attraction with substantially fewer parameters than NDPPs.
#5 Beyond Second-Order Information: Paid Logistic Regression with Noisy Features
著者: Sanghwa Kim, Kyoungseok Jang, Se-Young Yun
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12919
要約:
We consider online binary prediction with paid stochastic features, where the learner can spend resources to obtain better observations of the underlying features. This setting is motivated by applications such as medical testing, where additional cost can provide more precise measurements of patient's features for predicting a binary outcome. At each round, a latent feature is drawn i.i.d., the response is generated through a logistic model, and the learner chooses how much to pay before observing a noisy version of the feature. The goal of learner is to jointly control prediction loss and acquisition cost. In contrast to the former result on the online linear regression with paid features, covariance information alone is no longer sufficient: two noise distributions with the same covariance may induce different optimal logistic losses, and lower variance can even lead to higher loss. Under a structural ordering in which larger payments yield better observations by removing independent noise, we obtain $\widetilde O(\sqrt{T})$ regret when the noise structure is known and $\widetilde O(T^{2/3})$ regret when it is unknown, with matching lower bounds up to logarithmic factors.
#6 Sparse Dictionary Learning for Measure-Valued Data via Linearized Optimal Transport: Efficient Algorithms and Sample Complexity
著者: Brendan Mallery, Shuchin Aeron, James Murphy
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12924
要約:
We study dictionary learning for probability measure-valued data by embedding measures into Hilbert spaces using features derived from optimal transport geometry. We learn data-adapted dictionaries through sparse autoencoder parameterizations, producing fast, scalable representations of probability measures as sparse finite-dimensional codes. The resulting framework combines the rich, geometric structure of optimal transport with the interpretability and efficiency of sparse representation learning. We demonstrate its utility across diverse data modalities, including hyperspectral images, geometric point clouds, and document embeddings in large language model latent spaces. We also prove generalization bounds for sparse dictionary learning objectives on broad classes of $L^2$-valued measure embeddings, expressed in terms of the metric complexity of the atom class. For optimal transport and entropic transport map dictionaries, we derive new covering number bounds that yield explicit rates. Together, our computational and theoretical results support a new, scalable model for representation learning in the space of probability measures via embedding into Hilbert spaces.
#7 From Imbalance to Evidence: Deep Nonparametric Testing for Complex Data under Severe Sample Imbalance
著者: Guanhao Zhou, Xiufan Yu
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12946
要約:
Comparing distributions under severe sample imbalance requires effectively using abundant majority observations while retaining sensitivity to differences involving the smaller sample. This challenge is particularly relevant for complex, non-Euclidean data, such as graphs, whose structural characteristics call for flexible testing procedures. Classifier two-sample tests (C2STs) provide such flexibility, but existing procedures are primarily designed for balanced or comparably sized samples. In this paper, we study C2STs in a highly imbalanced regime, and show that severe sample imbalance may substantially degrade testing performance. Different from conventional imbalanced learning approaches that treat sample imbalance merely as a nuisance for classifier training, we turn it into a source of repeated testing evidence. Building on this insight, we propose an Enhanced Imbalance-Aware Deep Classifier Two-Sample Test (EIAD-C2ST), a classifier-agnostic framework designed for sample imbalance that accommodates diverse data modalities by tailoring classifiers to data structure. For computationally expensive deep classifiers, we further develop a scalable representation-amortized implementation strategy. Theoretically, we establish rigorous guarantees on asymptotic size and characterize conditions for power consistency, allowing the imbalance ratio to diverge. We demonstrate the empirical gains using simulations and an application to blockchain forensics. The results reveal that the majority-sample surplus, when properly leveraged, can be organized into useful statistical evidence, rather than an obstacle, for distributional comparisons with complex data.
#8 Scan-Parallel Variational Inference for event-driven Latent Jump Models
著者: Vansh Bansal, Min Chen, James G. Scott
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12986
要約:
Continuous-time latent state-space models are widely used for irregularly observed time series, typically representing uncertainty through a random initial state followed by smooth ordinary differential equation (ODE) dynamics, or Brownian perturbations accumulated by a stochastic differential equation (SDE). While these constructions are natural for smooth or diffusive systems, they do not directly encode a finite set of unobserved shocks whose timing, magnitude, and effect on memory are themselves uncertain. We instead introduce latent jump models (LJMs): the latent state follows closed-form deterministic dynamics between Poisson-driven event times and undergoes a learned instantaneous jump at each event, with state-dependent intensity and mark distributions. This defines a time-homogeneous Markov process whose jumps are intrinsic state transitions rather than numerical integration steps. For training, we derive a tractable event-augmented path-space evidence lower bound (ELBO) and introduce Scan-Parallel Variational Inference (SPVI): an observation-conditioned variational posterior samples the complete latent event configuration before the state recurrence is evaluated using triangular affine scans. This enables fast and scalable training for LJMs in logarithmic temporal depth. Across synthetic stochastic systems and real irregular time series, LJMs remain competitive with continuous-time latent baselines in forecast accuracy, often with fewer latent updates than numerical solver evaluations, while training one to two orders of magnitude faster than the sequential baselines.
#9 Why Heavy-Tailed Weight Spectra Predict Model Quality
著者: Joseph Wilson, Chris van der Heide, Liam Hodgkinson, Zhichao Wang, Fred Roosta, Michael W. Mahoney
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13123
要約:
A solid understanding of the predictive success of deep neural networks (DNNs) remains elusive. For example, while many data-dependent metrics exist that seek to predict model quality of DNNs, these metrics may be expensive to compute for large datasets and/or may be impossible to compute when training datasets are not released. A practical and effective predictor of model quality involves analyzing DNN weight matrices, as prior work has established that heavy-tailed spectral distributions in trained weight matrices can be strongly predictive of model quality. However, such metrics lack a rigorous statistical justification. In this work, we aim to understand why these metrics perform well, from a statistical perspective. To do so, we employ a probabilistic framework to derive the marginal likelihood for a DNN, evaluated using only the final layer weights. Across large-scale convolutional DNNs and LLMs, we find that this marginal likelihood predicts model quality. To understand the role heavy-tailed spectra play in predictive performance, we derive the limiting log-marginal likelihood for spectra that follow the (heavy-tailed) High-Temperature Marchenko-Pastur (HTMP) distribution. We show that this limiting quantity is convex in a heaviness parameter, and we derive an optimal heaviness that empirically predicts over-fitting for large-scale DNNs.
#10 Fast Gradient-Based Approximation of Continuous Dynamic Time Warping
著者: Pierre Houedry, Romain Tavenard, Nicolas Courty, Romaric Gaudel
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13129
要約:
Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) is a common choice for measuring the discrepancy between time series. It proceeds by finding an optimal warping between timestamps through dynamic programming, which can be slow and is not differentiable. Consequently, several variants have been proposed. Among them, Soft-DTW proposes a smooth formulation of DTW, amenable to gradient computation, while Continuous-DTW (CDTW) introduces a continuous formulation through parametrizable warping functions. Yet, computational costs remain high for both methods and impractical for large datasets in modern machine learning pipelines. This paper revisits CDTW in several ways. After establishing its differentiability under mild conditions, we introduce FastCDTW, which is an efficient approximate solver with two different computational strategies: an exact closed-form scheme for piecewise-linear warping functions, and an unbiased Monte Carlo estimator for smooth ones, such as implicit neural networks. Both strategies provide gradients with respect to the warping function and the input time series, and the resulting algorithm is GPU-friendly. We demonstrate its benefits through its integration into more complex learning problems, including time series averaging, classification and deep domain adaptation.

We develop a model-free, noise-aware Bayesian clustering framework that constructs a martingale posterior over clusterings from a predictive Bayesian perspective. This avoids explicit likelihood-prior model specification, expensive MCMC sampling, and mode-seeking variational approximations, while accommodating irregular cluster shapes and enabling parallel generation of posterior draws. To interpret the posterior over high-dimensional, noise-aware clusterings, we introduce a Wasserstein-type summarization method. #12 Learning by Compression: Risk Estimates Attain the Law-of-Large-Numbers Rate
著者: Simone Garatti, Marco C. Campi
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13442
要約:
Compression schemes provide a general framework for deriving data-dependent risk bounds for learning algorithms. For learning rules based on preferent compression, existing results upper-bound the risk in terms of a sample-dependent complexity $\boldsymbol{k}$, defined as the cardinality of the compression augmented by the training examples on which the reconstructed hypothesis is inappropriate. These results, however, yield a margin of order $O(\sqrt{\ln N/N})$ above $\boldsymbol{k}/N$. In this paper, we establish that the logarithmic factor is not intrinsic. We prove that, for every fixed confidence level $1-\delta$, the risk is bounded with probability at least $1-\delta$ by $$ \frac{\boldsymbol{k}}{N} + O\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\right), $$ uniformly over all possible values of $\boldsymbol{k}$. Thus, risk estimates for learning algorithms based on preferent compression attain the same law-of-large-numbers rate as the empirical estimate of the probability of a fixed event. We also show that the leading term $\boldsymbol{k}/N$ is necessary and investigate its relation to the empirical risk. In particular, we identify regimes in which the empirical risk retains the $O(1/\sqrt{N})$ convergence rate, as well as regimes in which it converges more slowly or ceases to be consistent, while the complexity-based estimator preserves the $O(1/\sqrt{N})$ rate.

When a language model is distilled from a teacher that shares its base parameters, the student can inherit behavioural traits of the teacher---including misalignment---even from data that is semantically unrelated to the trait (Cloud et al., 2026). #14 CorrFlow: Exact and Permutation-Equivariant Flow Matching on the Correlation Elliptope
著者: Gautier Marti
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13606
要約:
Correlation matrices combine nonlinear positive-definiteness constraints with a simultaneous row-column permutation symmetry. Their generation therefore involves two distinct problems. First, every output must remain on the correlation elliptope, irrespective of how its variables are ordered. Second, the generator must learn the domain-specific distribution occupied by empirical data inside that valid space. Existing financial image generators mix these problems: they learn ordered matrix entries and repair invalid draws afterward. We introduce CorrFlow, which separates them. A generalized Fisher transform maps full-rank correlation matrices bijectively to unconstrained coordinates, guaranteeing valid outputs without projection. A graph flow-matching network learns the empirical distribution in those coordinates and is permutation equivariant: reordering the variables only reorders the output. We compare a conditional CorrGAN reimplementation, image diffusion, and CorrFlow on $80\times80$ Taiwan equity matrices using five seeds per neural method. CorrFlow produces 100% valid draws without repair and obtains feature sliced-Wasserstein distance $.712\pm.141$, versus $4.339\pm.252$ for CorrGAN and $4.610\pm.191$ for image diffusion after their invalid outputs are projected back to the correlation space. It also improves this distance over historical lookup, while lookup retains higher local coverage. These results support the combined construction under the evaluated training budget; they do not establish superiority over fully tuned image models or isolate the contribution of each component.
#15 Some remarks on the connection between generative diffusion and optimal transport
diffusion
著者: Giulio Biroli, Marc M\'ezard
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13614
要約:
We discuss three simple connections between generative diffusion and optimal transport. First, the joint law of the data and of their Ornstein-Uhlenbeck noising is, at every time, the solution of an entropic optimal-transport problem, at a temperature set by the noise level. Backward diffusion can consequently be read as an annealing which gradually decreases the entropic regularization. Second, we study the speciation transition--how the modes of the data distribution emerge during generation--and compare how it happens in diffusion and flow matching with respect to optimal transport. Using Gaussian mixtures as high-dimensional example, we show that the dynamical instability through which diffusion and flow matching separate the modes becomes, for optimal transport, an exponentially thin boundary layer at the beginning of generation, which is rounded by entropic regularization. Third, considering a generic forward dynamics with a general confining potential, we show that the backward process driven by the score also satisfies a drift-based variational principle whose minimal cost is the entropy difference between initial and final distributions.
#16 A Critical Look at Power Sampling when Test Time Sharpening LLMs
著者: Abiel Malkani Talwar, Christopher Nemeth, Henry Moss
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13653
要約:
Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) has substantially improved LLM reasoning, yet growing evidence suggests it mainly performs distribution sharpening: concentrating probability mass on reasoning paths the base model already produces. Recent work therefore seeks RLVR's gains without needing reward signals, by sampling from the power distribution $\pi_\alpha \propto \pi_{\text{base}}^\alpha$ via Markov chain Monte Carlo or sequential Monte Carlo. We show that these samplers fail to converge to $\pi_\alpha$ under realistic compute budgets, and that their gains are largely recreated via simple Best-of-$N$ with appropriate grading . This suggests they succeed by selecting high-probability sequences, not by approximating $\pi_\alpha$, raising a natural question: is the power distribution itself a worthwhile target? We test this hypothesis directly by fine-tuning the base model to match $\pi_\alpha$, sidestepping sampler convergence issues entirely. Despite approximating the target far more faithfully than existing samplers, this strategy yields gains that are noisy and specific to certain models and sampling tasks. Together, our results suggest that the value of power sampling lies not in faithfully targeting $\pi_\alpha$, but in the simpler goal of extracting sequences the base model already deems likely.

#20 From Pixels to Privacy-Sensitive Features: An Information-Theoretic Framework for Reconstruction Bounds

要約:
What can an adversary recover from a model trained with DP-SGD? Its $(\varepsilon,\delta)$ guarantee does not answer this question directly. We derive a distortion bound that can be computed before training and applies even to an adversary who observes the parameter trajectory during training, knows every other training record and the population distribution from which the training records are drawn. The analyst may choose the representation in which error is measured, allowing the bound to target the aspects of reconstruction that are privacy-sensitive in a given application. Our information-theoretic framework separates two contributions: the information about a record revealed by training, measured through conditional mutual information, and the uncertainty already present in the data distribution, measured through source entropy. Rate-distortion theory combines them into a lower bound on the expected error of every reconstruction attack. For DP-SGD, the leakage bound depends only on the sampling rates and noise multipliers, while the source entropy is lower-bounded from a sample with finite-sample confidence. An orthogonal block construction keeps the resulting bound informative for high-dimensional images. We instantiate the framework in pixel space and in LPIPS feature space, obtaining, to our knowledge, the first lower bound on reconstruction distortion measured directly by LPIPS.
