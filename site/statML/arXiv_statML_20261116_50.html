#1 Enumerating Posterior-Supported Priors: First-Order Error of Finite-Integral Cross-Categorical Measurement Morphisms
著者: Ning Ni, Yongxin Zhu
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17390
要約:
In generative modeling, standard measure theory adopts the a.e. convention, which legitimately ignores any error component carried by a null-support set: a single null element can hide out-of-domain error inside null support and never be explicitly accounted for. The contribution of this paper is not a new distributional distance, but rather a verifiable structure that writes down which underlying predicate supports a high-level object, when it is invertible, and at which layer the error resides, from which we propose the enumerating posterior-supported prior framework. The framework operates on a three-layer categorical chain Quot->MorTop->Set: the first two layers posit no point elements and take binary-predicate quotient partitions as objects; the replica-index counting measure successively discriminates one-to-many collections into injections, lifting object collection from surjection to a quotient-layer bijection; the mutual exclusion of preimages in the shared measure space is resolved by an invertible MPT. Four results consolidate into Thm. 7: binary quotient-partition meta-categorical objects support the set categorical functional along categorical support via measure descent--recovery, enumerating the morphism itself rather than its preimage and object; their multiplicity is normalized through replica-index injection and accounted as an explicitly first-order error. The contribution of this paper is a transformation from nontrivial-input construction -> engineering-computable first-order output: the binary quotient-partition meta-categorical objects, supported via categorical support measure descent--recovery, constitute a substantive nontrivial input---precisely because they lift the out-of-domain error that hid inside null support into a first-order falsifiable coefficient mu_err, a finitely obtainable finite integral first-order error.
#2 Multifidelity Operator Inference for Dynamical Systems
著者: Vignesh Sella, Anirban Chaudhuri, Karen Willcox
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17503
要約:
This paper presents a multifidelity (MF) formulation of Operator Inference (OpInf) for learning non-intrusive, physics-based reduced-order models (ROMs) of nonlinear dynamical systems. OpInf learns reduced operators from projections of snapshot data by solving a least squares regression problem. However, generating high-fidelity snapshots can be computationally expensive, limiting parameter coverage and the temporal extent of training trajectories for many aerospace applications, which can lead to inaccurate or unstable ROM predictions. This work proposes MF-OpInf, which combines limited high-fidelity snapshots with more plentiful low-fidelity snapshots through a control-variates-inspired MF least squares formulation to learn a single set of reduced operators across multiple trajectories. Low-fidelity trajectories provide additional parameter coverage and extend beyond the high-fidelity training time horizon. The low-fidelity snapshots are also used to construct an improved reduced basis through weighted proper orthogonal decomposition. We demonstrate MF-OpInf on a nonlinear reaction-diffusion problem with parametrized sinusoidal forcing and a two-dimensional incompressible flow past a cylinder with a parametrized inlet pulse. In these experiments, under an equal training simulation budget, MF-OpInf reduces the mean relative prediction error at unseen parameters by up to $87\%$ for the reaction-diffusion problem and $28\%$ for the cylinder flow problem over the test time regime, which extends beyond the high-fidelity training time horizon. These results demonstrate that MF-OpInf can improve temporal extrapolation and prediction at unseen parameters without increasing the training simulation budget.
#3 Uncertainty-Aware Continual Learning for 2D XRD Interpretation Using Simulated and Experimental Data
著者: Zihan Wang, Hyun Sang Park, Ali Rachidi, David Elbert, Todd C. Hufnagel, Wei Chen
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17547
要約:
Autonomous experimentation systems require characterization models that can interpret heterogeneous measurements and maintain predictive performance as data accumulate. Developing such models involves addressing domain mismatch between data sources, overconfident predictions on unfamiliar inputs, and catastrophic forgetting during sequential updates. In this work, we present a proof-of-concept framework for uncertainty-aware continual interpretation of two-dimensional X-ray diffraction (XRD) data. The framework integrates established components: maximum mean discrepancy-based domain adaptation to reduce simulation-to-experiment discrepancies, a convolutional neural network with a spectral-normalized neural Gaussian process (CNN--SNGP) for predictive uncertainty estimation, and replay with knowledge distillation for sequential model updating. We evaluate the framework using simulated and previously-collected experimental data for crystal structure classification of single-phase body-centered cubic, face-centered cubic, and hexagonal close-packed patterns. Data are organized into predefined sequential blocks to assess adaptation and retention of previously learned knowledge. Within this setting, the results show accurate classification across simulated and experimental data, improved alignment between the two domains, and reduced forgetting compared with na\"ive fine-tuning. Predictive uncertainty is also evaluated on unfamiliar and noisy inputs. The contribution is the integration and evaluation of these capabilities for sequential XRD interpretation. The framework provides a basis for future integration into autonomous experimentation systems, with experimental planning, instrument control, and closed-loop deployment remaining areas for future development.
#4 Efficient and Practical Clustering of Variables in Causal Graphs
著者: Otto Tabell, Jani Nyk\"anen, Santtu Tikka, Juha Karvanen
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17971
要約:
Clustering variables in a graph may improve visual clarity and simplify causal inference. Harnessing the full potential of clustering requires an automated method to detect useful clusters in a graph. We introduce a novel class of clusters called conduits, and present a sound, complete and efficient algorithm for detecting all conduits in the graph. In addition to algorithmic detectability, conduits appear naturally in causal graphs stemming from real-world applications, enabling substantial size reduction. Furthermore, the structure of conduits enables the application of known identifiability results regarding clustering. We demonstrate these properties in a simulation study with real-world graphs.
#5 Training-point consistency of kernel ridge regression with varying correlation scales
著者: Weiyan Mu, Shifeng Xiong, Yuming Shen
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18095
要約:
The scale parameter in kernel ridge regression plays a pivotal role in modeling the correlation structure of data. In modern applications, sampling density and the underlying correlation range often change simultaneously, making a fixed-scale assumption highly restrictive. However, establishing asymptotic theories under varying scale parameters is fundamentally challenging. To address this gap, this paper develops a unified theoretical framework for kernel ridge regression under varying scale parameters. We establish that, on uniform tensor grids with product exponential kernels, the consistency of the training-point risk holds if and only if the effective local sample size diverges to infinity. This sharp criterion universally covers all regimes along arbitrary deterministic scale sequences, seamlessly unifying the long-range, fixed-scale, and short-range. Furthermore, we provide an explicit, signal-independent construction of a deterministic regularization penalty that guarantees this consistency. Numerical experiments illustrate the criterion and the corresponding penalty across all the three regimes.
#6 Detection and Classification in Latent Spaces: High-Dimensional Analysis and Validation
著者: Enis Chenchene, Anil S. Baslamisli, Paolo Braca
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18164
要約:
Foundation models produce high-dimensional latent representations in which suitably designed statistical decision rules can be effective yet analytically tractable. We study such detectors in the high-dimensional regime, where latent dimension and training sample size grow proportionally. For linear and quadratic statistics, we derive explicit large-deviation characterizations of false-alarm and miss-detection performance under the Neyman-Pearson paradigm, and show how the binary analysis extends to multi-class classification. The resulting error probabilities and corresponding decay rates exhibit non-monotonic dependence on the shape parameter, including double-descent and, in some regimes, multiple-descent. Experiments on vision, text, and audio datasets using modern foundation models show close agreement between theoretical predictions and empirical performance. In particular, the proposed framework allows us to set a prescribed false-alarm level and accurately predict the corresponding miss-detection probability. Overall, our results provide a theoretically grounded and interpretable framework with competitive empirical performance across different modalities.
#7 LaMe: a Joint Latent Mediation Framework for High-Dimensional Systems
著者: Ga\"el Poux-M\'edard, Kaniav Kamary, Yohann De Castro, Vivian Viallon
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18180
要約:
Mediation analysis seeks to understand how an exposure influences an outcome through intermediate variables. However, as increasingly rich datasets become available, hundreds to thousands of potential mediators make these systems difficult to model and hard to interpret. Latent-variable approaches offer a natural way to accommodate these high-dimensional settings. However, existing latent mediation methods face limitations, including overparameterization, biased inference, or limited scalability. We develop LaMe, a joint linear-Gaussian framework that summarizes a high-dimensional exposure-mediator-outcome system through a small number of latent variables. We identify natural direct and indirect effects under an explicit assumption that the manifest measurements fully capture the latent mediators, and characterize conditions under which the latent representation effectively restricts the observed cross-covariance structure. Simulations assess recovery of observed association parameters, natural effects, and manifest features associated with latent mediation pathways across a range of challenging settings. Four real-world applications demonstrate competitive predictive performance with compact latent representations, including single-dimensional representations of biomarker and proteomic measurements. Together, these results establish LaMe as a practical framework for estimating and summarizing multivariate mediation systems under explicit structural and measurement assumptions.
#8 Lipschitz-Constrained Discriminators in Generative Modeling: Asymptotic Behavior and Support Mismatch
著者: Eduardo Pinto, Panagiota Birmpa
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18394
要約:
We study the asymptotic behavior of optimal discriminators associated to $L-$Lipschitz regularized KL-divergences of two probability distributions $Q$ and $P$ that interpolate between the rescaled Wasserstein$-1$ metric by some parameter $L>0$ and the KL divergence. The parameter $L$ plays the role of a Lipschitz constraint and is viewed as a regularization parameter: small values of $L$ enforce a more geometry-aware discriminator, while as $L$ increases the geometric regularization is progressively relaxed. It is a known result [3] that such divergences converge to the KL divergence as $L\to\infty$. In this paper, we characterize the asymptotic behavior of the optimal discriminators as $L\to\infty$ in different regimes: Firstly, under $Q\ll P$ and the Radon-Nikodym derivative $\frac{dQ}{dP}$ is bounded from above and bounded away from zero. Secondly, when $Q$ and $P$ are discrete distributions satisfying $Q\ll P$, and finally when $Q$ and $P$ are non absolutely continuous discrete distributions. This result motivates training strategies which involve discriminators, such as adversarial generative models. In practice, the data distribution is often represented by a discrete empirical measure, while, especially at the early stages of the training, the model distribution may have little or no overlap with the empirical data distribution.
#9 Mesh-Free Weak-Form Regression for Learning Interaction Kernels from Particle Trajectories
著者: Daisuke Inoue, Dante Kalise
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18455
要約:
We propose mesh-free weak-form regression for learning interaction kernels from noisy particle trajectories. Our method uses smooth test functions to avoid differentiating noisy data and empirical particle averages to avoid density reconstruction and spatial grids. We then fit sparse combinations of candidate functions, evaluating interaction terms on sampled particle pairs to avoid all-pair quadratic cost. By transferring identifiability from the mean-field limit, we prove high-probability coefficient error bounds that separate finite-particle, time-discretization, observation-noise, and pair-sampling errors, without grid-resolution or density-estimation terms. We evaluate one-dimensional (1D) Newtonian-repulsion, 2D predator-prey, and 10D distance-localized experiments. The numerical results show trends consistent with the theoretical coefficient-error bounds. Comparisons of weak and forward-difference formulations with LASSO, Ridge, and OLS show that weak LASSO gives the smallest coefficient and drift errors in the three nominal experiments.

Node localization aims to estimate the positions of nodes based on collected data from other nodes. Depending on the data characteristics, different procedures are utilized to estimate the locations. In this paper, we introduce a comprehensive multidimensional scaling algorithm based on iterative majorization that comprises many of the different data characteristics to estimate node locations. The implicitly normalized loss function provides absolute distances and, given anchors, absolute node locations. #11 Contrastive latent representation learning with denoising score matching
著者: Christopher Marouani, Grigory Bartosh, Christian A. Naesseth
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18469
要約:
Contrastive representation learning methods operate by pulling different views of the same data sample together while pushing them away from views of different samples. A fundamental challenge is the estimation of the repulsive force, which typically requires large batch sizes, can exhibit high variance, and is susceptible to the curse of dimensionality. We introduce ScoreCLR, a contrastive representation learning method that offers a novel way to estimate this repulsion term. We consider the framework of representation learning by maximizing the mutual information between two latent views of a sample. By deriving the gradient of the Shannon entropy with respect to the encoder parameters, we obtain an equivalent objective that involves the score of the latent representation. We then use denoising score matching techniques to estimate this score with a lightweight network that is trained jointly with the encoder. We further propose regularization techniques using different noise levels and a Gaussian prior on the latent representation. ScoreCLR replaces the batch of explicit negative samples in contrastive methods with a learned repulsion whose cost is linear in the batch size rather than quadratic. On CIFAR-10 and ImageNet-100 it does so with minimal loss of linear-probe accuracy compared to SimCLR under a shared recipe, while producing projector outputs of higher effective rank, closer to the Gaussian prior.
#12 Bregman geometry in regular exponential families: dual representations and relative smoothness
著者: Mame Diarra Fall, \'Eric Barat, Nicolas Dobigeon
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18480
要約:
Regular exponential-family distributions are ubiquitous in statistics due to their appealing and convenient properties. Notably, they can be expressed in terms of Bregman divergences, revealing fundamental connections between statistics, convex analysis, and information geometry. However, this geometric structure has not been systematically exploited to characterize these distributions with respect to their expectation parametrization. Building on a projective characterization, we establish a general construction of equivalent dual Bregman representations, together with normalization and uniqueness results. These representations express log-densities as ordinary Bregman divergences in dual coordinate systems, providing geometric characterizations of the parameter space. So-called $\beta$- and $\alpha$-scaled dual representations emerge as particular instances of this framework. We further derive dual Bregman representations of conjugate priors and establish that they preserve conjugacy. Applications to classical distributions illustrate the geometric structures underlying seemingly different statistical models. Moreover, we show that these representations provide natural reference geometries for establishing relative smoothness of the primal Bregman representation of the distribution, with explicit bounds that are sharp in the scalar and separable settings. An illustrative application to the Poisson distribution sheds new light on several works in the literature, providing complementary geometric justifications for reference geometries while deriving existing algorithms.
#13 Elliptic Sampling: Optimal Interpolant Schedules for Gaussians and Beyond
著者: Cole Becker, Taylan Kargin, Kimia Hassibi, Asuman Ozdaglar, Pablo A. Parrilo
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18542
要約:
Generative modeling based on stochastic interpolants requires specifying two key choices: an interpolant schedule bridging the initial and target distributions, and an efficient discretization of the resulting probability-flow ODE. In this work, we introduce a new framework for joint schedule/discretization design based on the analyticity properties of the (complexified) ODE trajectories, which in turn ensure good numerical behavior. Our formulation can be solved exactly via conformal mapping, yielding a natural family of variance-preserving elliptic schedules, parametrized by Jacobi elliptic functions. Pairing an elliptic schedule with a tailored discretization, we obtain Zoloflow, a new sampling algorithm closely connected to Zolotarev's optimal rational function approximants. Crucially, both the elliptic schedule and Zoloflow explicitly incorporate the condition number $\kappa$ of the target distribution and reduce to the classical trig schedule in the limit $\kappa \rightarrow 1$. For Gaussian targets, Zoloflow achieves minimax optimality among all oblivious score-span algorithms, attaining total-variation error $\varepsilon$ using $\Theta(\log\kappa\,\log(\sqrt d/\varepsilon))$ (exact) score queries. For the log-concave case, the elliptic schedule allows sampling with at most $\varepsilon$ 2-Wasserstein-error by requiring merely $\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{d} \, \mathrm{polylog}(\kappa, 1/\varepsilon))$ score evaluations. Complementing these theoretical results, we empirically evaluate the elliptic schedule on general (non-log-concave) target distributions, including detailed comparisons with alternative schedule proposals on both synthetic benchmarks and existing image generation datasets.
#14 Distributionally Robust Risk Evaluation with Low-Rank Perturbations
著者: Linglingzhi Zhu, Dongze Wu, Yao Xie
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17447
要約:
We study distributionally robust risk under perturbations restricted to low-rank matrices whose row and column spaces are shared across observations, focusing on how much worst-case risk this restriction sacrifices and how to compute the restricted worst case. Under moment or Wasserstein budgets, we derive quantitative bounds on the gap between the unrestricted and low-rank worst-case risks, characterize when the gap vanishes, and identify the smallest lossless row and column dimensions. To generate worst-case perturbations, we apply projected gradient ascent to a curvature-corrected concave problem and derive computable risk certificates. We establish global sublinear convergence for the corrected objective and linear convergence to a worst-case distribution when the budget constraint is sufficiently active.
#15 SURFACE: Preference Distribution Matching on Pareto Fronts via Scalarization Steering
著者: Liuyuan Jiang, Prasanna Sattigeri, Lisha Chen
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17484
要約:
Constructing a finite Pareto representation requires allocating a limited number of solutions across the Pareto front (PF). A desired preference distribution specifies this allocation by assigning more solutions to some PF regions and fewer to others. Scalarization generates Pareto solutions by varying objective weights and solving single-objective problems. However, a distribution of scalarization weights generally does not translate into the same distribution of Pareto solutions along the PF, because the weight-to-solution map is nonlinear and unknown in advance.To account for this distortion, we introduce SURFACE (Scalarization Updates for Redistributing Finite Approximations with Chart Estimation}), which adaptively updates scalarization weights to match a desired PF distribution. SURFACE estimates how each region of the weight grid corresponds to an area of the PF from the current scalarized solutions, then redistributes the weights accordingly. The method supports parallel, warm-started, inexact optimization with standard single-objective solvers. Theoretically, we establish that, under the stated regularity conditions, SURFACE achieves linear convergence of the preference distribution mismatch to a finite-resolution error floor of $\mathcal{O}(N^{-2/(M-1)})$, where $N$ is the number of adaptive weights and $M$ is the number of objectives. Empirically, experiments across multiple benchmarks demonstrate improved target-mass balance and PF coverage compared with established baselines.

Data in forms of networks has been encountered in various prevalent fields at the arrival of big data era. #18 Complex Schr\"odinger Bridges
著者: Dong-Sig Han, Tolga Birdal
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17746
要約:
Diffusion bridges have emerged as versatile frameworks for facilitating transformations between arbitrary data distributions. However, these approaches are hindered by an immense optimization space and its underlying high-order structures. This paper explores a variant of diffusion bridges for solving real-world problems in complex vector spaces governed by scaling and rotation laws, such as Hamiltonian structures. To address this, we introduce conformal optimal transport (COT), a complex-valued transportation framework designed for physical systems with geometric and dissipative dynamics under a geometric lifting. Building on this, we propose a new class of dynamical diffusion bridges, called complex Schr\"odinger bridges (CSBs), whose derived algorithms can handle diffusion-based tasks using a conformal geometric prior. Finally, we implement a physics-informed CSB algorithm within simulations of quantum electrodynamics, mimicking the logical behavior of quantum logic gates. Our extensive results validate the underlying theory while highlighting advantages of complex-valued diffusion.
#19 Hierarchical Bayesian physics-augmented neural networks for populations of hyperelastic laws: specimen variability and uncertainty from scarce, noisy data
著者: Vincent C. Scholz, Phaedon-Stelios Koutsourelakis
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17892
要約:
The hyperelastic law of a material such as soft tissue differs between specimens, and often only a few specimens can be tested. Physics-augmented neural networks (PANNs) learn such laws from data without a prescribed functional form while keeping them physically admissible, and Bayesian versions of related models quantify the uncertainty of the law of one specimen or of a pooled group of specimens. A pooled fit, however, attributes the differences between specimens to measurement noise or to uncertainty about a mean law, and neither describes a new specimen. We first develop a Bayesian PANN for one specimen, whose priors keep the learned law plausible for few, noisy data. We then extend it to a hierarchical Bayesian PANN, a nonlinear mixed-effects model in which all specimens share learned polyconvex features and each specimen is a draw from a population of laws. Features, specimens, and population are inferred jointly by variational inference, with a separate model of the measurement noise, so that the variability between specimens is not mistaken for noise, the population carries its own uncertainty, and every specimen drawn from it is polyconvex. The population predicts the response of new specimens with credible intervals, without data or from a partial test, and assigns them to cohorts with quantified uncertainty, all without a prescribed form of the law. On synthetic populations, it recovers the population from few specimens and predicts new specimens more reliably than three published methods. On Treloar's rubber and on healthy and aneurysmal aortas, it reaches the accuracy of a deterministic PANN and assigns no aorta specimen to the wrong cohort with confidence, although it underestimates the uncertainty of untested curves.
#20 Small Is Not Dead: Distance-Aware Cluster Revival for e-means
著者: Eyal Weiss
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18238
要約:
e-means (Bessa et al., 2026) is a recent mini-batch k-means algorithm, now part of the FAISS library, that reaches the error of Lloyd's algorithm about an order of magnitude faster on large embedding datasets. Every five mini-batches, it removes every cluster whose running point count falls below a tenth of the average and reuses its centre to split a large cluster. In the authors' ablation, removing this mechanism, called dead-cluster revival, raises the error more than removing any other component added to the moving-average update. The rule, however, considers only cluster size: it cannot distinguish a cluster that is small because it is redundant from one that covers a rare, isolated group of points, and on data with such groups it repeatedly removes centres whose removal is costly. We add a second condition, based on the distance between centres: a small cluster is removed only if its centre is close to another centre, relative to a running estimate of the mean squared error. When a centre equals the mean of its points, this squared distance is an upper bound on the average increase in squared error, per point of the cluster, caused by removing the centre. The condition costs one nearest-centre search for the small clusters every five mini-batches and adds no measurable running time. On the e-means paper's embedding benchmarks, it never prevents a removal, so the results are identical to those of FAISS. On datasets with small isolated groups not used during development, it reduces the test error after two epochs by roughly 10% to 80% relative to count-based revival, and it preserves the advantage of k-means++ initialization; on the other evaluation datasets, it stays within about 1% of the better of revival enabled and disabled.
