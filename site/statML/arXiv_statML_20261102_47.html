#1 CausalGrade: Evidence-Guided Causal Graph Scoring with Jev
著者: Jiangming Zhong, Jiawei Zhu, Guanting Wu, Qiaoxi Cai, Wanyi Chen, Jiale Li
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40692
要約:
Evaluating the structural quality of candidate causal graphs when the true causal structure is unknown is an important problem in applying causal discovery results. This paper proposes CausalGrade, a causal graph scoring method based on the probabilistic judgment model Jev. The method jointly provides Jev with evidence of association, conditional dependence, and directionality from observational data and edge probabilities produced by the causal discovery foundation model CDFM. Jev produces probabilistic judgments about direct causal edges, and a graph-level score is obtained by measuring the agreement between the adjacency structure of the graph under evaluation and these edge-level judgments. Across 300 synthetic datasets, the score exhibits a relatively strong negative correlation with structural error in all 20 combinations of four causal discovery algorithms and five variable counts, indicating that higher scores generally correspond to smaller structural errors. In 500 synthetic graph selection tasks, CausalGrade achieves a best-candidate hit rate of 54.4\%, exceeding the 48.4\% achieved by the Self-Compatibility metric \(\kappa_G\), further supporting its ability to discriminate the structural quality of graphs under evaluation.
#2 The Geometry of Multitask Learning: Sharp Rates for Shared Subspace Estimation
著者: Tony Cai, Abhinav Chakraborty, Samory Kpotufe
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40713
要約:
Learning a shared subspace in multitask learning is an important statistical problem, yet exact rates remain poorly understood in many of the formal settings considered in the literature. To address this gap, which arises from limitations of existing upper-bound techniques, we propose a general multitask lemma. The lemma provides sufficient conditions for fast subspace estimation in generic multitask settings, in the form of geometric conditions on the task regression functions and their estimators. We subsequently show that the multitask lemma yields sharp minimax rates---previously unknown---for linear regression and classification in the regimes considered, and rates matching up to logarithmic factors for additive-noise regression.
#3 INLA for Adaptive UAV Sampling of Radio Environment Maps in IoT Networks: Prior Width Governs Accuracy and Calibration
著者: Changhao He, Kaichuang Yang, Xiaojuan Zhang
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40723
要約:
We study adaptive UAV sampling for radio environment map (REM) construction from non-Gaussian IoT observations. Integrated nested Laplace approximation(INLA) updates a Mat\'ern GMRF posterior, with waypoints selected by entropy pre-screening and Sherman--Morrison lookahead over the reachable set. We show that the likelihood-induced Skew-Normal correction has a sextic entropy effect, explaining its limited influence on waypoint selection. Across 19 retained ray-traced windows,Random and lawnmower sampling cost a median 18.0\% and 14.0\% more residual NMSE than posterior-informed sampling, equivalently reductions of 15.3\% and 12.3\% when referred to those baselines, while differences among informative rules remain unresolved. Widening the prior shadowing standard deviation from 6.0 to 10.025\,dB improves NMSE and credible-interval coverage, with monotone improvement over a 4--16\,dB sweep on this corpus and no observed turning point, although overconfidence persists. Online width estimation from the UAV's own counts yields about 6.5\,dB without recovering the gain. Across 30 measured LoRaWAN propagation windows paired with simulated counts and scored only on received cells, the margin over non-adaptive sampling reproduces, the training-derived width minimizes the tested interval score, and GP-UCB has nominally $3$--$5\%$ lower NMSE. The results show substantial, corpus-dependent sensitivity to prior specification, while fine differences among acquisition rules have limited or unresolved effects, the preferred width depends on the corpus, scored cells and model mismatch.
#4 Robust-by-Design Distributional Learning from Contaminated Samples
著者: Nevena Gligi\'c, Arya Farahi
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40884
要約:
Modern data analysis pipelines increasingly rely on large datasets assembled through retrieval, scraping, automated filtering, and post-hoc curation, where the observed sample is often a contaminated mixture rather than the target population. Standard distributional objectives either ignore contamination and converge to the wrong target, or retain only a small trusted subset and discard most of the data. We study supervised and unsupervised learning when a clean reference sample is compared with a pooled sample from $r=(1-\epsilon)q+\epsilon z$, where $q$ is the latent target distribution and $z$ is an unknown contaminant. Motivated by settings with noisy membership scores for all observations and exact labels for a small audited subset, we propose contamination-corrected maximum mean discrepancy (CC-MMD), a design-based estimator that combines proxy scores with audited residual corrections to recover the oracle MMD that would have been computed from the latent clean sample. CC-MMD requires no calibration assumption on the proxy scores and no structural assumptions on the contaminant beyond standard kernel regularity. We prove finite-population design-unbiasedness of the corrected kernel sums, establish asymptotic normality of the audit correction, and show consistency for the target discrepancy $\mathrm{MMD}^2(p,q;k)$. Empirically, CC-MMD tracks the oracle under increasing contamination, improves parameter recovery and prediction in contaminated regression, and enables generative models trained on heavily corrupted mixtures to recover the clean target distribution. These results show that contaminated data need not be discarded or treated as the estimand. With sparse trusted labels and abundant noisy supervision, distributional objectives can be made robust by design.
#5 Adaptive Uncertainty Quantification on Simplicial Complexes with Conformalized Simplicial Neural Networks
著者: Lekun Wang, Jaidev Goel, Yulia R. Gel, Yao Xie
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41166
要約:
Simplicial neural networks (SNNs) are powerful tools for prediction tasks on higher-order relational datasets stored on simplicial complexes (SCs), a higher-order generalization of graphs. However, SNNs usually lack rigorous uncertainty quantification (UQ), limiting their usefulness in high-stakes scenarios. We propose conformalized SNN (CF-SNN), a framework that, given a simplicial-complex entity with m prediction targets, generates prediction sets for all m targets simultaneously. Under mild assumptions, CF-SNN guarantees that, for each target, the prediction set contains the true label with a user-specified nominal coverage probability, and the realized miscoverage proportion across all m targets is guaranteed to remain close to the nominal level with high probability. Extensive experiments across a wide range of SC prediction tasks demonstrate that CF-SNN is a flexible UQ framework that achieves user-specified coverage while producing informative prediction sets of small size.
#6 Coarse-Graining Optimal Transport for Rollout-Free Post-Training of Flow Matching
著者: Soon Hoe Lim, Aditi Gupta, Michael W. Mahoney, N. Benjamin Erichson
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41269
要約:
Flow matching depends critically on the coupling used to pair source and target samples. Optimal transport (OT) couplings can reduce velocity conflict and straighten trajectories; but, in practice, challenges remain: the source comes from a continuous distribution, only a finite number of target samples data are accessible, and the neural network has finite capacity. For these reasons, making the coupling more OT-like may not consistently improve sampling quality, in particular in the low number-of-function-evaluation (NFE) regime. Motivated by this, we introduce ReCouple, a controllable framework that constructs coarse-grained OT couplings, to better understand rollout-free OT-based post-training. We also consider extensions of this basic framework: ReCouple+ extends this toward the continuous-source setting by increasing source resolution, converging to semi-discrete OT in the appropriate limit; and ReCouple++ further adds spatial regularization, motivated by the observation that straight trajectories need not correspond to spatially simple velocity fields. Empirical validation demonstrates the effectiveness of these methods, and it reveals an NFE-dependent interplay between coupling optimality, neural realizability, and generation quality.
#7 Conditional Neural SDEs for Financial Time-Series Generation
著者: Sina Molavipour, Alireza M. Javid, Bj\"orn L\"ofdahl, Mikhail Nechaev
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41405
要約:
Conditional generation of time-series paths is a central problem in probabilistic modeling, where the goal is to simulate future trajectories that are both realistic and responsive to external context. It has direct applications in finance and economics. Neural stochastic differential equations provide a natural framework for this task by combining continuous-time stochastic dynamics with learnable drift and diffusion functions. However, directly modeling the target state can be unstable in conditional settings, especially when the target and conditioning variables have strong level dependence, limited training data, or different dimensions.We propose a residualanchor Neural SDE architecture that decomposes the target into a learned conditional anchor and a stochastic residual. The anchor captures the context-dependent baseline, while the Neural SDE (Stochastic Differential Equation) models the residual dynamics around this baseline. This separation reduces the burden on the stochastic dynamics and generalizes spread-based representations to more general conditioning settings.We evaluate the approach on real-world financial time series, including conditional interest-rate scenarios and a comparison with a time-series foundation model. The results show that the proposed model can generate plausible conditional paths, maintain competitive point accuracy, and provide calibrated uncertainty estimates.
#8 Reliability-Weighted Direct Preference Optimization: Penalized Weights for Robust Preference Learning
著者: Bin Luo, Param Venkat Vivek Kesireddy, Anil Vallepu
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41588
要約:
Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) treats every reported comparison as correct, yet preference data contain reversed, tied or otherwise noisy comparisons. RW-DPO gives each comparison an unknown reliability weight $w_i\in[0,1]$ that enters the likelihood linearly -- DPO is $w\equiv1$, a reversed comparison $w_i=0$ -- and estimates the weights jointly with the policy under a minimax-concave penalty on $-\log w_i$. The penalty keeps every comparison the model agrees with at exactly $w_i=1$ and acts only on those it contradicts, through closed-form weight updates at negligible cost over DPO. We prove that recovering all flipped comparisons from a single annotation per comparison requires margins of order $\log n$. Furthermore, in a linear-index model, starting from an initial fit pointing in the correct direction, our estimator jointly consistently recovers the preference direction and weights, as well as identifies the flipped comparisons above this margin scale, even when a fixed fraction of comparisons is flipped. In simulations RW-DPO has the lowest average estimation error among DPO, noise-rate corrections and recent robust methods, with far more precise flags than recent label-correction methods. In DPO fine-tuning of a language model with 10-30% flipped labels, its win rate does not fall as the flip rate grows and exceeds that of DPO trained on clean labels, matching the strongest recent method with corrections concentrated on the flipped labels.
#9 PDE-Residual-Informed Kriging
著者: Yu Tan, Shichang Du, Yongxiang Li
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41590
要約:
Physics-informed Gaussian process (GP) methods provide a probabilistic framework for PDE solving. Existing approaches incorporate PDE information in different ways: pointwise conditioning uses the governing equation only at finitely many selected locations, globally constrained GPs restrict the admissible functions to satisfy the PDE exactly, and residual-penalized methods control the PDE mismatch over the whole domain through an additional penalty weight. What is still missing is a GP formulation that directly incorporates global PDE-residual information of the entire domain into its covariance structure, while retaining model flexibility and standard GP conditioning without a separate residual-weight parameter. To solve this issue, this study proposes PDE-residual-informed Kriging (PRIK) based on a PDE-residual kernel. The PDE-residual kernel is constructed by equipping a kernel-section space with an inner product defined by the $L^{2}$ inner product of PDE residuals. This construction makes PRIK yield the posterior mean with minimum integrated squared PDE residual. It also provides direct statistical interpretations: the variance-scale estimate equals the normalized PDE-residual energy (i.e., the integrated squared PDE residual of the posterior mean divided by the number of labeled observations), and the profile likelihood balances this residual energy against model complexity. Under the stated regularity and stability conditions, we establish $H^{2}$-quasi-optimality and convergence rates for the posterior mean, together with a nonasymptotic bound for its PDE-residual norm. We further show that the normalized posterior kernel converges to a variational Green-type kernel and derive a uniform nonasymptotic contraction bound for the posterior variance. Numerical studies provide empirical evidence consistent with the theory and demonstrate the PDE-solving capability of PRIK.
#10 Statistical Foundations of Reinforcement Learning: A Non-Asymptotic Perspective
著者: Yuxin Chen, Yuejie Chi, Jianqing Fan, Gen Li, Yuting Wei, Yuling Yan
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41903
要約:
Reinforcement learning (RL) embodies a foundational paradigm for sequential decision-making in the face of uncertainty. The rapid growth of model complexity in emerging applications has significantly amplified the challenges of achieving data-efficient RL, particularly in sample-starved scenarios where data collection may be costly, time-consuming, or even high-stakes. Consequently, understanding and improving the data efficiency of RL algorithms has become a central problem of both fundamental theoretical interest and practical importance. Despite decades of research, the statistical underpinnings of RL remained remarkably underdeveloped until relatively recently, particularly in the finite-sample regimes most relevant to modern practice. Over the past few years, there has been a surge of activity advancing the frontiers of non-asymptotic statistical RL theory, drawing on ideas from high-dimensional statistics and probability, information theory, and statistical learning theory. In this monograph, we present a non-asymptotic treatment for RL based on tabular Markov decision processes, focusing on three canonical sampling mechanisms --- RL with a generative model, online RL, and offline RL --- together with two algorithmic paradigms: model-based and model-free approaches. Centered around non-asymptotic sample complexity analysis, our treatment emphasizes minimax-optimal algorithm design, particularly methods that attain statistical optimality without significant burn-in costs, as well as sharp statistical characterizations of several widely used RL algorithms. Whenever possible, we provide largely self-contained analyses together with intuitive explanations. We also briefly discuss several extensions, such as robust RL, competitive multi-agent RL, federated RL, uncertainty quantification, and RL from human feedback.
#11 WASABI: Wasserstein Sampling via Bridge Interpolants
著者: Sebastian Salazar, Dilum Fernando, Michal Kucer, Emily Casleton, Luhuan Wu, David M. Blei
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41958
要約:
Sampling from Boltzmann distributions is a fundamental problem in physics, chemistry, and biology. We introduce WAsserstein SAmpling via Bridge Interpolants (WASABI), a method that trains a diffusion model to generate samples from a target Boltzmann distribution given access only to the energy function. WASABI minimizes the KL divergence between the distribution generated by its diffusion model and the Boltzmann target using Wasserstein gradient flows. This approach builds on a principle underlying classical sampling methods such as Langevin dynamics. However, applying Wasserstein gradient flows to diffusion models is challenging because the updates require the gradient of the model's own log density, which is generally intractable. We derive a practical training algorithm that avoids computing this gradient. Once trained, WASABI can generate new samples without further evaluations of the energy or its gradient. On alanine dipeptide, WASABI outperforms the best reported baselines using an estimated 3.3 times fewer energy evaluations. It also achieves competitive performance on interacting particle system benchmarks.
#12 Regret-Optimal Wasserstein-Robust Linear Regression
著者: Sloan Nietert, Daniel Kuhn
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42109
要約:
Distributionally robust optimization (DRO) minimizes worst-case risk under distributional uncertainty, but its decisions can be suboptimal in terms of their excess risk, or regret. We study this gap for multivariate least-squares regression, assuming that the observed source law and unknown target law differ by an adversarial 2-Wasserstein perturbation of size $\varepsilon$. We first identify the asymptotic complexity of robust decision-making in the $\varepsilon \to 0$ limit, characterized by a problem-specific condition number $\kappa$. Here, a wide spectrum of simple algorithms, including DRO, achieve the instance-optimal rate of $O(\kappa \varepsilon^2)$. For fixed $\varepsilon > 0$, we prove that DRO still achieves the minimax rate if the problem has bounded residual effective rank. On the other hand, we identify significant failure modes where DRO is provably suboptimal by dimension-dependent factors. Fortunately, we prove that two alternative procedures, minimax regret optimization (MRO) and spectral-norm regularization (SNR), attain the minimax rate. We derive an exact convex reformulation of MRO and an accelerated proximal algorithm for SNR, along with high-probability finite-sample guarantees. We support these theoretical results with numerical experiments separating the performance of MRO and SNR from DRO.
#13 Dual-Corrected Stochastic Block Model ($\text{DC}^2\text{SBM}$): A Generalized Structure Detection Method for Attributed Networks
著者: Zhenhai Chang, Shuo Wang, Yumeng Liu, Wei Liu
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40676
要約:
Community detection in attributed networks is one of the core tasks of complex network analysis. The stochastic block model (SBM) and its variants are able to detect generalized network structures such as assortative, disassortative and mixed structures. However, existing generative methods either ignore node heterogeneity, or characterize node activity in the topological and attribute dimensions by one and the same factor, making it difficult to correctly model node activity when the two kinds of activity are decoupled. In this paper, we propose the Dual-Corrected Stochastic Block Model ($\text{DC}^2\text{SBM}$), which introduces independent correction factors---a topological degree factor and an attribute abundance factor---into the link-generation and attribute-generation channels, respectively, thereby effectively decoupling node activity from membership inference, and which further introduces a global scaling parameter to resolve the mismatch between the normalization constraints and the network scale. The model is inferred by the expectation--maximization (EM) algorithm, and the newly introduced factors cancel out completely in the posterior computation, so that no additional computational cost is incurred. Experiments on synthetic and real attributed networks show that DC^2SBM outperforms state-of-the-art methods in community detection accuracy, with an especially pronounced advantage when the topological signal is weak. The two correction factors can be recovered independently from their respective channels without cross-talk. Analytical as well as controlled experiments further demonstrate that modeling with a single shared factor fails when the two kinds of activity are decoupled---a regime that is prevalent in real networks---thereby validating the necessity of dual independent correction.
#14 Certified Behavioral Retention for Continual Adaptation with Held-Out Anchors
著者: Ahanaf Hasan Ariq, Abrar Shahid
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40740
要約:
Continual adaptation requires deciding not only how to update a model, but whether to commit an update without exceeding a declared budget for changes to protected behavior. We introduce Certified Anchor-Based Adaptation (CABA), a candidate-agnostic post-update gate that reserves historical observations from candidate optimization, compares old and candidate policies on frozen representations, and selects the largest tested interpolation coefficient whose worst-anchor forward KL divergence remains within budget. The finite check requires neither action labels nor complete trajectories. We develop a theory-led certification framework that separates measured anchor retention from conditional off-anchor guarantees. Under explicit representation-coverage and Lipschitz assumptions, an accepted update satisfies a uniform behavioral-divergence bound on protected support. The analysis gives regularity bounds for affine softmax heads, probabilistic coverage and compression guarantees, interpolation monotonicity conditions, grid-resolution bounds, and trajectory-level and sequential-composition guarantees. In a controlled UCI Human Activity Recognition study used as a non-stationary sensor-policy proxy, CABA reduces mean forgetting from 1.01 percent for unconstrained adaptation to 0.00 percent at budget epsilon = 0.05, while reducing final average accuracy from 93.04 percent to 88.48 percent. The experiment illustrates a retention-plasticity trade-off and does not establish foundation-model or robotic performance. CABA provides an auditable retention check for candidate generators exposing comparable policy distributions and a declared update path, while deployment also requires separate evidence of current-task utility.
#15 Near-Optimal First-Order Regret for Bandits with Feedback Graphs
著者: Mengxiao Zhang, Yuheng Zhang, Haipeng Luo, Paul Mineiro
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40861
要約:
We study first-order regret guarantees for adversarial bandits with a fixed, known directed feedback graph. For strongly observable graphs, [ACBDK15] establishes a minimax regret of $\widetilde{\Theta}(\sqrt{\alpha T})$, where $\alpha$ denotes the independence number of the graph and $T$ denotes the horizon. On the other hand, the best existing first-order bound scales as $\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{(\kappa+1) L^\star})$ [LLZ20], where $\kappa$ denotes the clique partition number, satisfying $\kappa+1\geq\alpha$, and $L^\star$ denotes the cumulative loss of the best arm, leaving a gap between the graph dependence in worst-case and first-order guarantees. In this paper, we bridge this gap by obtaining an expected regret bound of $\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{\alpha L^\star}+\alpha)$. Our first result is an algorithm for the special case of self-aware graphs, in which every vertex has a self-loop. The algorithm combines the update of EXP3 [ACBFS02] with a sampling rule inspired by exploration by optimization [LS20]. More specifically, we construct a map whose fixed points yield sampling distributions that control the one-round regret remainder in terms of the learner's loss and the graph's independence number, which is crucial in deriving optimal first-order guarantees. We further show that a sufficiently accurate fixed point can be computed in polynomial time by a geometrically convergent iteration. Finally, we extend the algorithm to general strongly observable graphs and achieve the same $\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{\alpha L^\star}+\alpha)$ guarantee by adapting the two-level aggregation approach of [LLZ20].
#16 Density-Guided Bootstrap Boundaries for Latent World-Model Reinforcement Learning
著者: Shashank Gupta, Pilhwa Lee
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41022
要約:
Latent world models allow reinforcement learning agents to learn policies from imagined trajectories, yet determining the optimal depth of model reliance remains a fundamental challenge. Fixed imagination horizons apply uniform prediction depths regardless of trajectory reliability, exposing target values to compounding model error or over-relying on critic approximations. We introduce density-guided bootstrap boundaries, a trajectory-dependent mechanism that adapts the depth of imagined returns using a score-informed relative density proxy. The proxy accumulates the estimated divergence of a score-corrected auxiliary field formed by a feature displacement network and a noise-conditioned score estimator along imagined paths. We propose two gating mechanisms: a hard boundary that halts return expansion at a discrete state, and a soft boundary that continuously shifts the return recursion toward critic bootstrapping. Both variants preserve boundary value estimates and apply corresponding survival weights to actor and critic losses. Formulated independently of specific dynamics models and instantiated within Dreamer V3, we prove that the resulting gated Bellman operator is a strict contraction mapping and that early bootstrapping monotonically reduces conditional target variance. We clarify the theoretical assumptions distinguishing our expansion proxy from exact path likelihoods. Across continuous-control tasks and Atari Pong, empirical evaluations show that dynamic boundary selection enhances sample efficiency and late-training performance over fixed-horizon and auxiliary-enabled baselines.
#17 Omniprediction for All Mixable Proper Losses and Beyond
著者: Junyan Liu, Haipeng Luo, Lillian J. Ratliff
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41023
要約:
Omniprediction concerns the task of making predictions that are simultaneously accurate with respect to different losses. Earlier results by [CV09] show that in an online learning setting, $O(\log(|\mathcal{F}||\mathcal{L}|))$ regret is achievable simultaneously against every predictor in a finite predictor class $\mathcal{F}$ and for every loss in a finite class $\mathcal{L}$ of mixable proper losses, while recent advances [OKK25, FP26] show that $\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{T\log(|\mathcal{F}||\mathcal{L}|)})$ regret is achievable simultaneously for every loss in a finite class $\mathcal{L}$ of bounded losses. In this work, we ask whether sublinear regret remains possible when $\mathcal{L}$ is an infinite class of proper losses. Our first result gives a negative answer in general: even when $\mathcal{F}$ contains only two linear predictors, it is impossible to achieve sublinear regret simultaneously for all bounded proper losses. We then show that $\widetilde{O}(T^{1/3}(\log|\mathcal{F}|)^{2/3})$ regret is achievable simultaneously for \textit{all} mixable proper losses and that this bound is optimal up to logarithmic factors. This optimal rate stands in stark contrast to the constant (in $T$) regret achievable for a finite class of mixable proper losses. Finally, we extend our algorithm to obtain a best-of-all-worlds result: in addition to the new regret guarantee for all mixable proper losses, the resulting algorithm simultaneously achieves the existing guarantees for finite loss classes. With appropriate choices of these finite classes, our algorithm simultaneously achieves constant regret for squared loss and log loss, $\widetilde{O}(T^{1/3}(\log|\mathcal{F}|)^{2/3})$ regret for all mixable proper losses, and $\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{T\log|\mathcal{F}|})$ regret for $0$-$1$ loss, all Lipschitz losses, all GLM losses, and other structured families.
#18 Where, When, and How Many Bits? Scaling Laws for SGD with Stochastic Rounding
著者: Zhichao Wang, Michael W. Mahoney
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41074
要約:
Low-precision training reduces computation but introduces additional rounding error. We theoretically study how stochastic rounding (SR) affects prediction risk as model size, training time, and precision change. We consider a two-layer linear network with a fixed random first layer, trained by mini-batch SGD on fresh Gaussian data with power-law spectra. At each SGD step, the features used in forward and backward computations are rounded independently. We prove matched upper and lower bounds on prediction error for the final model and for uniform parameter averaging, with general learning-rate and precision schedules chosen before training. For fixed precisions and constant learning rates with parameter averaging, we derive how the smallest prediction error decreases with compute, and we identify model sizes and training times that achieve this optimal-compute scaling. Rounding only the forward features preserves the compute scaling of exact arithmetic, while rounding backward features changes it. Averaging parameters can change which computation needs higher precision and when higher precision is useful. Our results also give bit requirements for a given accuracy tolerance and show how backward rounding can change learning-rate schedule design. At a fixed compute budget, lower precision can improve prediction when the benefit of a larger model or longer training outweighs the additional SR-induced error. Model simulations illustrate these tradeoffs, and experiments with an MLP and a small transformer examine rounding choices beyond the theoretical model.
