#1 ToroFit: Fitting Wedge Sums of Tori to High-Dimensional Data
著者: Tanishq Bhatia, Jose A. Perea, Tarek M. Zikry
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06990
要約:
Data arising in scientific applications often exhibits complex nonlinear structure. Identifying and parameterizing said nonlinearities can enable better modeling, enhance predictive analysis, data interpolation, denoising, and anomaly detection. In this paper, we develop theoretical and computational methods to detect and parametrize Euclidean point cloud data lying near a wedge sum of tori. Specifically, we use persistent cohomology and cup products to identify the distinct toroidal components, and on each component, we optimize over low-energy multivariate trigonometric polynomials for best data fit. The ensuing optimization problem reduces to generalized ridge regression, which can be solved efficiently via standard techniques. We demonstrate the utility of the proposed framework in both synthetic and real datasets, including interpolation of low frame-rate recurrent videos, attractor recovery in quasiperiodic dynamical systems, and the parametrization of gene-space cycle data for parasites and humans.
#2 Bayesian Tensor Train Kernel Machines with Automatic Model Complexity Inference
著者: Afra Kilic, Kim Batselier
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07129
要約:
Tensor Network (TN) kernel machines accelerate supervised learning by using low-rank TNs to reduce computation and memory. However, most TN-based methods are deterministic with manually tuned ranks and feature dimensions, while the existing Bayesian TN kernel machine is restricted to the CANDECOMP/PARAFAC decomposition (CPD), whose single rank is shared by all modes. We extend this framework to the tensor train (TT) and propose Bayesian Tensor Train Kernel Machines (BTT-KM), whose hierarchical ARD-type priors on the TT cores infer the TT ranks and feature dimensions from data. Since each TT core is coupled to the others only through its neighbors, its closed-form posterior update requires propagating the contributions of all other cores along the chain. Mean-field variational inference yields a Bayesian alternating least squares algorithm with the same computational complexity as its deterministic counterpart and a closed-form predictive distribution. A synthetic experiment illustrates that BTT-KM recovers the true TT ranks and feature dimensions from an over-parameterized initialization.
#3 ShapleyOG: Fast Adaptive Shapley Value Estimation with Odd Gaussian Processes
著者: David Rundel, R. Teal Witter, Santo M. A. R. Thies, Matthias Feurer, Fabian Fumagalli, Bernd Bischl
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07173
要約:
Shapley values are a central tool for attribution in machine learning. Since their exact computation requires evaluating the cooperative game to be explained on exponentially many player coalitions, they are typically estimated from a limited number of evaluations. Recently, ShaplEIG has shown that adaptively selecting the coalitions to evaluate substantially improves sample efficiency over classical estimators. This, however, comes at the cost of a sequential procedure that, in every iteration, refits a Gaussian process surrogate of the game and selects the coalition with the largest expected information gain about the Shapley values. The resulting computational overhead limits its applicability to costly games with low evaluation budgets. We present fast adaptive Shapley value estimation with Odd Gaussian Processes (ShapleyOG) to overcome this limitation. ShapleyOG leverages the fact that Shapley values depend only on the odd component of the game, and we show that under paired sampling a Gaussian process surrogate on this component alone yields the same Shapley value posterior and coalition selection criterion from half as many observations. Moreover, ShapleyOG refits the surrogate only on a sparse schedule and efficiently updates the selection criterion in between, and it extends quadrature-based computation schemes to the prior covariance of the Shapley values, which allows for exact or cheaper approximate computation. In extensive experiments on diverse games, ShapleyOG matches the estimation accuracy of ShaplEIG while reducing its overhead by up to three orders of magnitude. As a result, it outperforms state-of-the-art non-adaptive estimators at equal total computation time already when a single evaluation takes a tenth of a second and a budget of a few thousand evaluations suffices, thereby widening the regime in which adaptive coalition selection is applicable and pays off.
#4 Task-Calibrated Random Features via Influence-Based Error Cancellation
著者: Shinto Eguchi
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07209
要約:
Random features approximate kernel integrals by Monte Carlo, yet only selected frequency-sampling errors affect a downstream task. We propose Task-Calibrated Random Features (TCRF), which balance frequency moments constructed from task-specific influence using weights shared across kernel entries. Closed-form weights coincide with classical minimum-distance calibration; the contribution is a reusable, task-derived calibration space. We show that first-order variance equals the residual variance after projection onto this space. For kernel ridge regression, complete cancellation yields a non-Gaussian second-order limit. For kernel Stein discrepancy, a subset identity and disjoint-block variance decomposition eliminate the linear component of pilot noise. Positive exponential-tilting weights preserve the positive-semidefinite feature representation and support higher feasible calibration ranks. Paired experiments show improved approximation to exact kernel ridge regression at larger frequency budgets and to the exact empirical Stein discrepancy under a fixed alternative, even after accounting for a frequency-equivalent pilot budget. Gains are smaller with cross-validated ridge, landmark pilots require conservative rank selection, and near the Stein null allocating the pilot budget to additional ordinary frequencies is preferable.
#5 Network Effect Decomposition for Causal Effect Estimation under Network Interference
著者: Zhirong Huang, Wenzhen Zhang, Guangquan Lu, Boyan Chen, Debo Cheng, Shichao Zhang
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07879
要約:
Estimating causal effects from observational network data requires accounting for both individual treatment and neighborhood exposure. Existing outcome models commonly incorporate these intervention variables into a joint predictor and estimate causal effects through prediction contrasts, leaving the corresponding response components implicit. We propose the $\underline{N}$etwork $\underline{E}$ffect $\underline{D}$ecomposition $\underline{Net}$work (NEDNet), which parameterizes the conditional outcome response as a query-invariant pre-treatment baseline plus separate treatment- and exposure-response pathways. Path-specific neighborhood contextualization supplies local pre-treatment information to the two pathways. #6 Joint Modeling of Conditional Mean and Variability in Multilevel Functional Data: Application to Postprandial Glucose Trajectories in the AEGIS Study
著者: Yang Xiao, Francisco Gude, Marcos Matabuena
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07937
要約:
Functional data analysis provides a framework for studying time-varying associations between covariates and high-frequency biological signals. In digital health, participants often contribute repeated functional trajectories, yet existing multilevel regression methods largely focus on the conditional mean, overlooking variability and its role in biological heterogeneity. We propose a heteroskedastic functional mixed-effects regression framework that jointly models the conditional mean and variance of repeated trajectories, with participant-specific random effects in both components. We also develop a participant-level bootstrap to quantify uncertainty in estimated functional parameters. We apply the method to postprandial glucose trajectories from the AEGIS study. Premeal glucose, carbohydrate intake, and meal type show distinct time-dependent associations with glucose variability, whereas HbA1c is more strongly associated with the conditional mean than with variability. Substantial between-participant differences in residual variability remain even after accounting for observed covariates. These findings show that modeling conditional variability alongside the mean provides insights into glucose regulation and individual heterogeneity that mean-focused analyses may miss. More broadly, the proposed framework enables joint characterization of average responses, variability, and participant-specific heterogeneity in repeated biological signals.
#7 Identifying Neural Dynamics Using Nonlinear Interventional State Space Models
著者: Hanna M. Tolle, Carles Balsells-Rodas, Yixin Wang, Pedro A. M. Mediano, Amin Nejatbakhsh
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08113
要約:
State-space models (SSMs) recover low-dimensional latent dynamics from high-dimensional observations, but latents are generally not identifiable from observational data alone. Interventional state-space models (iSSMs) use perturbations to break observational symmetries and identify parameters up to permutation, scaling, and shift. However, prior work restricts identifiability to linear latent dynamics and piecewise-linear emissions, excluding many behaviors characteristic of neural circuits. We extend iSSMs to a substantially broader model class: we prove that (i) affine identifiability holds under general injective emission functions and Gaussian latent transitions, and (ii) with interventions, identifiability up to permutation, scaling, and shift is preserved under general nonlinear latent dynamics. We validate our theory on simulated FitzHugh-Nagumo dynamics, and then demonstrate two applications: adjudicating between competing models of decision-making; and predicting responses to unseen micro-stimulation in macaque and human neural recordings, with proof-of-concept generalization to a held-out stimulation site in the latter. Finally, simulations yield practical guidance for designing stimulation protocols that improve identifiability.
#8 Patch Attention Wisely: Understanding Patch Length in Attention-Based Forecasting
著者: Junyu Chen, Likai Chen, Weining Wang
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08160
要約:
Attention-based time series forecasters commonly group consecutive observations into patches. Patch length is known to have a substantial impact on forecasting performance, yet its selection remains largely empirical. We develop a theoretical framework that explains why patch length matters for forecasting accuracy and provides a principled basis for its selection. For linear state-space models, we derive an exact factorization of the forecasting coefficient matrix that separates forecast dynamics from filtering dynamics. We show that dominance of a single forecast component at long horizons does not generally imply a rank-one representation. This explains why longer horizons alone need not reduce representational complexity. We combine the factorization with estimation bounds to develop a patch-selection criterion that balances representation and estimation errors under temporal dependence. Our analysis establishes conditions under which balanced patching is justified and clarifies how predictive structure, head count, training sample size, and long-run dependence enter the selection criterion.
#9 Series-wise Cross-validation for Global Time Series Forecasting
著者: Vitor Cerqueira, Mariana Oliveira, Luis Roque
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08252
要約:
We consider the problem of cross-validation (CV) in time series forecasting, with a focus on global forecasting models based on deep learning. The prevailing CV strategy in forecasting is to partition data across time with the last part of each series used for testing. With global models trained on collections of time series, however, a fundamentally different approach becomes possible: partitioning across series and time. We formalize series-wise CV for global forecasting models and benchmark several instantiations, including K-fold CV, bootstrapping, and Monte Carlo CV, on 11 datasets with 10 neural network architectures. We evaluate CV procedures along five dimensions: performance estimation, overoptimism rate, model selection accuracy, regret, and downstream forecasting performance. We withhold a random fraction of series from training so that they appear only in the test set. This protocol simulates the emergence of new series not seen during training, a common scenario in global forecasting. Under this protocol, our results reveal a nuanced trade-off: series-wise CV yields better downstream forecasting accuracy by guiding model selection toward configurations that generalize across both time and series, while time-wise CV retains a slight advantage for estimating out-of-sample error.
#10 Unifying Central Flow, Rod Flow, and the Edge Coupling
著者: Eric Regis, Sinho Chewi
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08358
要約:
At the edge of stability, the iterates of gradient descent oscillate along the sharp directions of the Hessian. This poses a significant challenge for continuous-time models as the usual continuous-time approximation -- the gradient flow -- admits no oscillations. Several continuous-time models have been proposed to capture the behavior at the edge of stability, among them central flow and rod flow. Although both models can be interpreted as performing a coarse-graining in time, the connection between them remains poorly understood. Here we show that both flows can be derived from a third object, the edge coupling: a functional on consecutive iterates whose critical points are the fixed points and period-two orbits of gradient descent. It is revealed that the two flows correspond to coarse-grainings at different time scales. Rod flow coarse-grains over only the fastest time scale, while central flow coarse-grains over both the fast and intermediate time scales. Furthermore, using the edge coupling, we derive rod flows and central flows for momentum methods such as heavy ball and Adam. We verify the derived flows experimentally on representative neural network architectures, where they track the discrete iterates throughout all phases of training -- including the transition from the stable regime to the edge of stability.

#12 Satisficing as competitive Bayesian weighting: a minimal model of goal-directed choice under an unattained target
著者: Moto Shinriki, Yu Kono, Tatsuji Takahashi
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07270
要約:
Animals and humans often do not maximize; they act relative to goals (aspiration levels) that are frequently not yet met. How can a goal that is not (yet) realized organize choice? Deacon (2012) calls such absence-referenced organization \emph{ententional}, and Garc\'{i}a-Valdecasas and Deacon (2024) argue that ends are best understood as constraints rather than as particular states. We examine this idea in a minimal formal setting: Risk-sensitive Satisficing (RS), a bandit learning rule that values each action by its share of past trials multiplied by its surplus or shortfall relative to an aspiration level. We show that RS can be rewritten as a Bayesian-like estimator, Attention Competitive Bayes (ACB), in which the aspiration level is the prior mean of every action and the finite budget of trials sets how strongly evidence can override it. Two consequences follow. First, actions that are no longer sampled are partially overridden by the goal rather than by past observations: their estimates relax toward the aspiration level without any data being discarded. This ``pseudo-forgetting'' distinguishes ACB from models with an explicit forgetting rate. Second, when the aspiration is unattainable, exploration does not settle; in Bernoulli bandit simulations, adjusting the aspiration level from evidence reduced regret (expected loss). ACB offers an analyzable model in which an unattained goal and a limited attentional resource jointly constrain behavior, and it yields testable predictions about how organisms revalue options they have abandoned.
#13 Neural Realized Measures: Forecast-Supervised Intraday Aggregation for Volatility Forecasting
著者: Brian Sing Fan Chan, Jennifer So Kuen Chan
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07287
要約:
High-frequency volatility forecasting typically separates measurement from prediction: intraday observations are first compressed into a daily realized measure using a fixed aggregation rule, and the resulting statistic is then passed to a forecasting model. This conventional pipeline therefore treats measure construction as forecast-agnostic preprocessing. We ask whether the aggregation stage itself can instead be learned from the downstream forecasting objective while preserving the statistical structure of established realized measures. We propose Neural Realized Measures (NRM), a primitive-preserving, forecast-supervised aggregation framework that retains the original measure-specific intraday contributions but replaces uniform aggregation with positive, mean-normalized context-dependent weights. Uniform weights exactly recover the corresponding raw realized measure (RRM), so the classical construction is nested as a special case. Because NRM remains an explicit weighted sum of established primitive contributions, it also retains direct bar-level attribution. Across SF, HAR, and MIDAS forecasting specifications, NRM is evaluated against corresponding RRM specifications under controlled comparisons. In Heston simulations, NRM reduces RMSE for both contemporaneous and next-day latent integrated variance across all evaluated primitive--regime combinations. On six cryptocurrencies using five-minute data, NRM improves QLIKE in all 54 paired out-of-sample comparisons, with mean gains of 7.23\%, 8.15\%, and 9.22\% under SF, HAR, and MIDAS, respectively. Validation-selected NRM specifications also achieve the lowest mean QLIKE across the six assets among the evaluated approaches. Ablation and SHAP analyses reveal systematic time-of-day and nonlinear local-context effects in the learned allocation.
#14 Last-Iterate Convergence of Stochastic Gradient Methods for Unbounded Problems
著者: Kevin Kurian Thomas Vaidyan, Ahmet Alacaoglu, Michael P. Friedlander
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07334
要約:
Last-iterate convergence guarantees for stochastic gradient methods have attracted significant attention as practical implementations typically return the last iterate instead of an average of the iterates. In this paper, we study two approaches for analyzing the last-iterate convergence of proximal and projected stochastic gradient methods without bounded domain or bounded variance assumptions, where black-box variational inference serves as a running example. Under two related assumptions relaxing the bounded variance, we show near-optimal anytime rate $\widetilde{\mathcal{O}}(1/\sqrt{T})$ for the last iterate. Moreover, when the horizon $T$ is known, we remove the logarithmic factor and obtain the optimal order $\mathcal{O}(1/\sqrt{T})$, matching the order known under bounded variance or bounded domain assumptions.
#15 Changepoint localization via nested exchangeability tests: from point estimates to confidence intervals
著者: Rohan Hore, Aaditya Ramdas
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07399
要約:
We study distribution-free uncertainty quantification for a changepoint estimate in an offline setting. We observe a sequence with a single changepoint and are given an estimate of its location. The pre- and post-change segments are independent and each consists of i.i.d. (or, more generally, exchangeable) observations from arbitrary and unknown distributions. We introduce Changepoint LOcalization via Nested Exchangeability testing (CLONE), a novel method for constructing distribution-free confidence intervals around that estimate by testing exchangeability in nested windows centered at the reference estimate. Under these assumptions, CLONE provides finite-sample coverage for any black-box reference estimator. Using a geometrically shrinking radius grid, CLONE requires only $O(log n)$ permutation tests. We characterize optimal scores for the framework and provide practical recommendations that result in sharp confidence intervals. Under mild assumptions, CLONE confidence intervals have asymptotically vanishing normalized length. Simulations and experiments on image and text data support our theoretical findings.

要約:
We study learning in zero-sum games with delayed feedback. We introduce Multi-Anchor Optimistic Online Mirror Descent (MAO-OMD), a single algorithm which obtains optimal fast rates in self-play on smooth games, while simultaneously maintaining minimax optimal guarantees against arbitrary opponents. In particular, in smooth zero-sum games with arbitrary and unknown feedback delays, MAO-OMD obtains $O(\sigma_{\max}+1)$ social and individual regrets when used by both players, where $\sigma_{\max}$ denotes the maximum number of outstanding gradients, while also guaranteeing minimax optimal $O(\sqrt{T+d_{\mathrm{tot}}})$ individual regret against arbitrary opponents, where $d_{\mathrm{tot}}$ is the total delay. 要約:
We propose a doubly-robust method to estimate the causal effects of multi-valued treatment regimes on patient outcomes. A multi-valued treatment regime assigns personalized treatments from a set of competing options based on a patient's history. Existing methods for optimal treatment regimes are largely limited to binary or continuous treatments. We extend the longitudinal targeted minimum loss-based estimator (LTMLE) to accommodate multi-valued treatments by modeling treatment assignments with a multinomial model. We further integrate recurrent neural networks (RNNs) into the LTMLE framework as learners for the outcome, treatment, and censoring models, and compare this approach to ensemble machine learning. We identify an intriguing phenomenon when linearly interpolating between permutation-aligned neural networks: test accuracy briefly improves near the interpolation endpoints before dropping toward the midpoint, which we term BUMP (Brief Unexpected Mid-interpolation Peak). #20 Conformal Prediction for Time-Dependent PDEs
著者: Joshua Stiller, Annika Schneider, Eyke H\"ullermeier
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08409
要約:
Uncertainty quantification is crucial in scientific machine learning, where models inform safety-critical tasks such as flood forecasting, financial risk management, and thermal control in machines. Conformal prediction provides distribution-free coverage guarantees, but in time-dependent settings common to physics and engineering, these guarantees can break down, leading to systematic undercoverage. We study this problem in the context of surrogate models for time-dependent physical systems described by partial differential equations. We prove that in a function space setting, distributions at arbitrarily close times can be mutually singular, making exact coverage guarantees impossible. #21 Spectral and Subspace Dynamics of LoRA in Supervised Fine-Tuning and Reinforcement Learning
著者: Chenruo Liu, Zihan Wang, Yijun Dong, Qi Lei
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08414
要約:
Low-rank adaptation (LoRA) is commonly used in both supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL), often under similar optimizer settings. Yet, how the different dynamics of the two stages manifest in the low-rank increment that LoRA optimizes remains unclear. Tracking the singular spectrum and subspaces of LoRA updates on mathematical reasoning tasks, we find that RL concentrates the spectrum at every learning rate, while SFT expands it at moderate learning rates; moreover, RL rotates the singular subspaces much faster than SFT at small learning rates. Interpolating between SFT and RL one ingredient at a time, we pinpoint group-mean baseline subtraction as the source of both contrasts. A quadratic multi-index model updated via LoRA unveils the underlying mechanism: group-mean baseline subtraction tilts the balance between two competing forces, coherent fitting and gradient noise, toward spectral concentration and faster subspace rotation, while forcing the risk-favorable learning rate to be low. Guided by these dynamics, we tailor LoRA updates to each stage: in SFT, we flatten the spectrum of the full update to spread adaptation across singular directions, whereas in RL, we flatten the part of the update outside the current LoRA increment span to promote subspace rotation. Experiments on mathematical reasoning tasks across multiple models show that the respective configurations for SFT and RL yield the best performance among the compared methods in most evaluated settings.
