#1 Source-Aware Dynamic Pricing with Strategic Buyers
著者: Jinjiang Zou, Yang Li, Zemin Zheng
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22516
要約:
In modern commerce, firms and platforms typically use customer features from different sources to personalize prices: some features are verified using seller records or third-party data, while others are self-reported and may be strategically manipulated by buyers seeking lower prices. We therefore introduce a novel source-aware framework for contextual dynamic pricing with strategic buyers in a high-dimensional semiparametric setting, where buyer valuations follow a sparse linear model with an unknown noise distribution. To account for differences in manipulability across feature sources while enabling effective valuation learning, we develop the Source-Aware Localized exploration and calibrated Exploitation (SALE) pricing policy, which draws on verified features to localize price exploration and combines verified and reported features to correct for strategic manipulation during exploitation. Our localized exploration design allows converting binary purchase feedback into unbiased signals of conditional mean valuations, yielding a new population equation that supports consistent sparse estimation despite non-i.i.d. buyer arrivals. Building on this estimate, we develop a localized empirical-process analysis that exploits intrinsic sparse geometry to establish uniform guarantees for nonparametric estimation of the noise distribution in the high-dimensional setting. With these estimation guarantees, we establish SALE's self-recovery property, which keeps localized exploration valid with high probability and leads to a sublinear expected regret bound. Both simulation studies and real-data experiments demonstrate the appealing performance of SALE. To the best of our knowledge, our study is the first to provide a theoretical and practical framework for incorporating information provenance into dynamic pricing with strategic buyers.
#2 Is a Linear Probe Evidence of a Linear Representation?
著者: Nadir Kutluozen, Christian Intern\`o, David Klindt
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22655
要約:
The linear representation hypothesis holds that pretrained vision models encode generative factors as linearly decodable directions in feature space, and comes with a standard methodology: train a linear probe, declare a factor represented if accuracy is high. We show this test is too permissive. Asymptotically, any sufficiently rich feature map, including a random one, admits a linear readout, so high probe accuracy is consistent with two very different geometries: a true linear world model, where the latent-aligned subspace dominates the representation, or a kernel machine, where the latents lie linearly inside a much larger curved manifold. This distinction matters because the curved structure orthogonal to the probe is relevant for out-of-distribution generalization, robustness, and compositional transfer. We propose three asymmetric diagnostics that distinguish the two cases: reverse predictivity, spectral concentration, and out-of-distribution generalization. We validate them in a fully observed simulation where ground truth is known. Applied to six pretrained vision transformers on SVG-World, our deterministic generative benchmark with paired visual styles, and to two of them on CelebA, they show pretraining produces structure beyond a random kernel but well short of a linear world model. On dSprites, 3D Shapes and MPI3D, forward decodability and latent-aligned variance dissociate. Thus, forward decoding alone does not show that the probed factors align with the dominant geometry of the representation. In summary, our work cautions about the success of linear probes as opposed to truly linear and low dimensional representations.
#3 Optimal approximation of smooth functions by ReLU neural networks
model extraction
著者: Thomas Nagler
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22693
要約:
We study uniform approximation of the H\"older ball $\mathcal H_1^s([0,1]^D)$ by fully connected ReLU networks. Except in the one-dimensional Lipschitz case, existing constructions remain separated from the information-theoretic efficiency limit. This article closes the gap for every $s>0$ and $D\ge1$: networks with width $O(N)$, depth $O(L)$, and parameter magnitude $O(1)$ attain uniform approximation error of order $(N^2L^2\log N)^{-s/D}$. No relation between width and depth is required. The construction uses new encoding and decoding mechanisms that exploit higher-order finite-difference bounds for local Taylor coefficients of the target function. As a statistical consequence, empirical risk minimizers over the corresponding clipped network classes attain the minimax regression rate $n^{-2s/(2s+D)}$ without additional logarithmic factors. The paper also gives a detailed exposition of the key approximation techniques, covering both established and new mechanisms.
#4 Unit-level aggregation breaks coverage in causal foundation models
著者: Gabe Schulman, Lucius E. J. Bynum, Yoav Wald
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22770
要約:
Causal foundation models (CFMs) show promising results for treatment-effect estimation, with unit-level estimates that are competitive on standard benchmarks. Many causal analyses, however, inform decisions through effects averaged over groups of units, such as the full sample, the treated units, or a subgroup, and for such decisions a CFM is useful only if its credible intervals are calibrated. We examine these intervals and find that their coverage degrades, both in simple synthetic settings and on standard benchmarks. On ACIC 2016, for example, temperature-calibrated 95% intervals from CausalPFN fall below nominal coverage beyond groups of 30 out of 4802 units, and intervals for the full-sample average contain the true value in only 20% of datasets. We trace this problem to how these models are trained and how their intervals are formed. They output a predictive distribution for one unit at a time and, at inference time, combine the unit-level variances as if the errors on different units were independent. We show formally that posterior uncertainty over the data-generating process makes these errors correlated. As a result, the combined variance is too small by a factor that grows with the size of the group. Empirically, this factor predicts where coverage fails and by how much. We then show that two neural process models, which make joint predictions for a group of units, account for this correlation and substantially improve coverage on synthetic tasks and, as heads on a frozen CausalPFN, on all six benchmarks.
#5 Distinguishability and Recovery for a Doubly Sparse Spiked Wigner Model
著者: Ioana Dumitriu, JD Flynn, Zhichao Wang
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23033
要約:
We study distinguishability and recovery of a sparse rank-one signal in a spiked Wigner model with independently sparse noise through a variety of algorithms and parameter regimes. The signal and noise have separate sparsity parameters, while the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) remains of constant order as the matrix size grows. For sub-Gaussian noise and spikes whose nonzero coordinates are bounded away from zero, a leave-one-out quadratic thresholding procedure (a generalization of Ding et al., 2024) identifies the support. Sign synchronization yields exact recovery for a Rademacher spike, and spectral refinement yields strong recovery. The quadratic thresholding search parameter gives an explicit tradeoff between signal sparsity, noise sparsity, and computational cost. We also study extensions to sub-Gaussian spike priors through diagonal and quadratic thresholding. For Rademacher spikes with continuous nonzero noise, we develop an identical search algorithm that exploits unblurred signal entries, the existence of which permits distinguishability; connectivity of the graph they reveal provides a sufficient condition for exact recovery. These guarantees do not require moment assumptions on the continuous noise distribution. Together with our previous PCA results (Dumitriu et al., 2026), our analysis covers almost the entire space of parameters and regimes for distinguishing and recovering in our doubly sparse spiked Wigner model.
#6 Information-Geometric Blind Spots of Conformal Test Martingales
著者: Johan Hallberg Szabadv\'ary
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23131
要約:
Conformal test martingales (CTMs) are e-processes for sequentially testing exchangeability via p-values produced by a conformity function $A$. However, any distribution shift that preserves the push-forward $P\circ A^{-1}$ leaves the p-value distribution invariant, rendering it invisible to every CTM based on $A$. We formalise this blind spot through the \emph{$A$-equivalence class} $[P]_A$ and show that, for any target distribution $T$, there is a unique closest element $Q^\ast\in[P]_A$ in KL divergence---the \emph{$A$-cryptic I-projection}---with explicit density $q^\ast(z) = t(z)\,p_S(A(z))/t_S(A(z))$ and a Pythagorean decomposition into detectable and cryptic components. When $A$ is the Neyman-Pearson likelihood ratio, the cryptic component vanishes, connecting our result to optimal e-value design. We demonstrate the theory on high-dimensional image data by empirically constructing this I-projection. By conditionally resampling EMNIST letters to identically match the conformity scores of MNIST digits, we demonstrate that a distribution can be shifted to a disjoint support while leaving the test martingale mathematically blinded.
#7 The Loss or the Architecture? What Matters in Survival Analysis with Competing Risks

Often, \emph{eg} in healthcare and predictive maintenance, what matters is which of several competing causes will strike first, and with what probability: survival analysis with competing risks. Corresponding deep learning methods are developed as bundles: a new architecture together with a new training loss, tested on one to five datasets against a handful of baselines. Thus, a state-of-the-art result cannot be attributed to the architecture, the loss, or the evaluation protocol. The coupling is not accidental: with one incidence curve per cause, a network's head is tied to the losses expressible on it. Proper scoring rules reveal these dynamics; the concordance index (C-index), which is not proper, does not separate models: neither axis moves it, not even tuning on the loss built to improve it. #8 Towards optimal selection schedules for online active learning
著者: Hugo Cui, Francesca Mignacco, Francesco Mori
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23477
要約:
We consider the problem of online linear classification, where a stream of unlabeled samples is available, and annotation is costly. We investigate the optimal query policy, that prescribes whether and when a sample should be labeled and used for a training step, or instead discarded, so as to minimize a given objective. We focus on the realizable case, under Gaussian design, in the high-dimensional regime where data dimension and number of unlabeled samples grow proportionally. We consider two objectives: 1) the generalization error under a labeling budget, and 2) a cumulative cost function where mistakes have unit cost and queries cost $\Delta$. We first show that the myopic policies that greedily minimize at all times the increment in the objective correspond to either small or intermediate-margin policies, that prescribe the selection of samples whose margins fall within certain intervals that shrink with time. Within this class, we numerically ascertain the optimal schedules for the selection thresholds that minimize the final values of the objectives. Taking inspiration from the myopic policy for the cumulative cost, we design a very simple query policy schedule that we compare to other proposed heuristics, on synthetic data and real medical image datasets. Finally, we discuss myopic policies for two-layer neural networks, and exemplify how naive margin-based policies can cease to be optimal.

We introduce a conformal prediction framework for regression with Grassmann-valued responses. The proposed method constructs prediction regions in the form of metric balls directly on the Grassmann manifold, using basis-invariant geometric distances as non-conformity measures and scaling the corresponding non-conformity scores through a learned prediction difficulty estimator. This yields locally adaptive, input-dependent prediction regions that respect the underlying manifold geometry, while retaining the finite-sample marginal coverage guarantee of split conformal prediction under exchangeability. #10 A general recipe for data-adaptive anytime-valid envelopes for iterative algorithms
著者: Soham Bonnerjee, Aaditya Ramdas
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23570
要約:
Most convergence guarantees for iterative stochastic algorithms are stated at a fixed iteration count and do not justify stopping at a data-dependent time. Recent work has constructed anytime-valid envelopes that do, but deterministic envelopes are typically too conservative for practical stopping, and existing data-adaptive ones are derived case-by-case, each coupling a specific algorithm to a specific noise model and boundary construction. This paper provides a general recipe for constructing such anytime-valid data-adaptive boundaries for a large class of iterative algorithms. Performance measures of many of these algorithms satisfy a one-step recursion that reduces the entire error analysis to a single general requirement: time-uniform concentration for predictably weighted sums of noise. We formalize this through a strongly adapted sub-$\psi$ condition, and derive off-the-shelf boundary constructions via continuous and discrete mixtures, stitching, and product betting martingales. We show how these boundaries can be directly coupled with a separate algorithmic analysis that derives the one-step recursion to provide a class of readymade anytime-valid envelopes across diverse settings including stochastic gradient descent (SGD), temporal difference learning, and Bures-Wasserstein SGD. In particular, for SGD, our framework either recovers or significantly sharpens every existing envelope we are aware of. Our constructions come with explicit implementation details and are substantiated by numerical experiments.
#11 Mesoscopic learning dynamics of extensive-width neural networks
著者: Shu Zhou, Jean Barbier, Sebastian Goldt
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23779
要約:
Understanding how stochastic gradient descent learns internal representations in wide neural networks trained on rich target functions is a central problem in machine learning. We study this question in two-layer neural networks learning target functions represented by networks of the same architecture, with arbitrary nonlinearity, and with network width and input dimension growing proportionally. In this regime, standard approaches for describing the learning dynamics require tracking a diverging number of overlaps, which measure correlations between learned and target features, leaving no obvious description in terms of a few order parameters. Here, we develop a mesoscopic description of learning by characterizing the distribution of overlaps. In the early stages of learning, we find that a Gaussian description of overlap statistics reduces the learning dynamics to a few collective variables that track learning of the target function's linear and quadratic components. We then estimate the timescale of specialization, when Gaussian closure breaks down and individual learned features begin to strongly correlate with specific target features. We find that specialization in extensive-rank networks is accelerated via a quadratic shielding mechanism, whereby learning the linear and quadratic components of the target function filters gradient fluctuations. We finally describe how the activation function determines the pattern of specialization and characterize the subsequent refinement of learned features. Together, these results show how collective learning gives way to individual feature discovery and clarify when learning in large neural networks admits a simple statistical description.
#12 Parametric Resonance in Primal-Dual Learning for Slowly Non-Stationary Constrained MDPs
著者: Maxime Baelde
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23788
要約:
Primal-dual methods for constrained Markov decision processes are analyzed under a two-timescale assumption, with non-stationarity entering through two channels. In the additive channel, where the constraint level oscillates, the dual oscillation saturates at a floor set by the moving optimum and is amplified linearly by the timescale ratio. In the parametric channel, where the constraint sensitivity oscillates, a coherent modulation carries no resonant forcing at any depth: the dual update's stiffness modulation cancels the primal update's damping modulation. Instability instead comes from a mismatch between the constraint estimate used by the primal update and the measurement used by the dual update, producing a parametric resonance at twice the pair's natural frequency. The tolerated mismatch grows with the square root of the forcing bandwidth and collapses with the square root of the dual-to-primal step-size ratio. These results hold on the tangent linear-quadratic reduction around an active saddle, for one constraint and for $K$ constraints under a common modulation and an isotropy condition. Driving both updates from the same estimate removes the resonant forcing but introduces a stationary offset equal to the estimator's bias. In a Lagrangian soft actor-critic agent, the multiplier locks onto the sub-harmonic at the timescale ratios its identified linear loop predicts and a coherent cost critic suppresses the lock-in, while a proportional (PID) term, predicted to leave the lock-in in place, suppresses most of it through the projection of the multiplier.
