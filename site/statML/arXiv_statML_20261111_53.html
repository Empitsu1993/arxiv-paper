#1 Near-Optimal Sample Complexity of Deterministic Agnostic PAC Learning
著者: Shai Ben-David, Steve Hanneke, Farnam Mansouri, Amirreza Shaeiri
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13210
要約:
We study the problem of agnostic PAC learning under deterministic labels, where the examples are labeled by an arbitrary deterministic function, which need not belong to the concept class $C$. This model captures misspecification without stochastic label noise and lies between the realizable and fully agnostic settings. Prior work showed that, when $C$ has finite diameter $K$ and VC dimension $d$, deterministic labels can improve the sample complexity upper bound from the classical agnostic guarantee $O(d/\varepsilon^2)$ to $\widetilde{O}(K/\varepsilon)$, but left a large gap between upper and lower bounds. Our main result sharpens this picture. We prove that every class of VC dimension $d$ and finite diameter $K$ is learnable with sample complexity $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{K^{1/3} \, d^{2/3}}{\varepsilon} \right)$, and we construct classes for which this bound is tight up to logarithmic factors. We also study the online analogue of this problem and show that the minimax regret is again partly controlled by the diameter of the class. Technically, our upper bounds are obtained via memorization-based learning algorithms that exploit the deterministic label structure.
#2 MediPFN: Amortized In-Context Nuisance Learning and Inference for Causal Mediation Analysis
著者: Shi Bo, Jiaming Hu
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13219
要約:
Causal mediation analysis decomposes a treatment effect into indirect pathways through mediators and direct pathways through other processes. Tabular foundation models perform classification and regression through in-context learning, providing estimates of conditional means and propensity scores used in causal mediation analysis. Constructing confidence intervals from these estimates requires accounting for uncertainty introduced by fitting and combining several nuisance functions. We introduce MediPFN, an amortized inference framework that adapts a pretrained tabular foundation model to nuisance prediction and combines its predictions through a cross-fitted influence-function estimator. A separate head learns quantiles of the estimator's standardized error from simulated tables with known indirect effects. Supervised fine-tuning, symmetrization, and independent calibration then produce a confidence interval. We establish coverage bounds for the resulting intervals under standard assumptions. Across experiments, MediPFN attains coverage closer to the nominal level than several comparators. It also reduces the computational cost of uncertainty quantification, running 59.1 times faster than bootstrap in matched comparisons.
#3 Continuous Horizon Forecasting
著者: Malcolm Wolff, Elliott Zackrone, Souhaib Ben Taieb, Michael W. Mahoney, Kin G. Olivares
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13228
要約:
Multi-horizon forecasting has been dominated by discrete forecast strategies that treat model outputs as fixed-vectors or recurrently create them. In this paper, we introduce the Continuous Horizon (CH) strategy that generalizes upon recursive, direct, and multi-step strategies by redefining forecasts as a continuous operator and the forecast horizon as a query variable rather than an index. We present the theoretical foundations for the CH strategy, a Universal Operator Approximation Theorem, and a continuous-horizon bias-variance decomposition that formally proves the estimation variance improvements of CH over the discrete strategies. We validate the CH strategy empirically through a simulation study, and on 16 large-scale datasets from the M-series benchmark, showing how CH achieves competitive RMSE accuracy, while uniquely enabling parallel inference, targeted horizon querying, and interpolation and extrapolation across unseen horizons.

This paper proposes a novel general Gaussian process framework for change point detection in time series. A change is defined as an alteration of the hyperparameters of the Gaussian process prior. We formulate the problem in the context of statistical hypothesis testing and demonstrate its performance over both offline and online multiple change points detection. The approach provides theoretical guaranties of its performance. #6 Differentially Private Statistical Inference for Rankings from Pairwise Comparisons

公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13485
要約:
Reliable inference for rankings of large language models based on human pairwise comparisons must preserve the privacy of individual preference records. To meet this need, we develop methods for score and rank inference under the Bradley-Terry model with edge differential privacy. For an objective-perturbed estimator, a new leave-one-out analysis establishes finite-sample bounds on Euclidean and entrywise estimation errors. For sufficiently dense comparison graphs, the error bounds extend to smaller privacy budgets, including regimes in which its privacy term dominates its statistical term. A uniform asymptotic expansion yields asymptotically valid confidence intervals for individual scores and pairwise score differences. We evaluate finite-sample performance through simulations and apply the proposed methods to rank large language models for creative writing.
#7 Simultaneous Near-Optimal Region Identification in Continuous Simulation Optimization: Minimax Complexity and Adaptive Sampling
著者: Yilin Zheng, Haowei Wang, Wenjia Wang, Szu Hui Ng
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13489
要約:
Simulation optimization typically returns a single estimated optimal point. When decisions also depend on criteria evaluated independently of simulation, such as resource cost, the decision maker instead needs the set of designs whose expected performance lies within a tolerance of the optimum, often choosing the tolerance only after seeing the tradeoffs. We study how to identify these near-optimal regions on a continuous design space, simultaneously for all tolerances up to a prescribed maximum $T$, with region estimation error at most $\eta$. We show that region identification is not a by-product of optimization. Specifically, when $\alpha>\nu$, any procedure, even one given the optimal design in advance, needs at least $\Omega\bigl(\eta^{-(2+d/\nu)}\min\{1,KT^{d/\alpha}\}\bigr)$ simulation evaluations, where $d$ is the dimension, $\nu$ the smoothness, and $(K,\alpha)$ the covering parameters that describe the geometry of the near-optimal designs. We propose SASORI, which returns an estimated optimal point and the regions for all tolerances from the same evaluations, matching this lower bound up to logarithmic factors. SASORI also achieves the optimal tradeoff between region estimation error and cumulative regret, and reduces to a minimax-optimal simulation optimization procedure at zero tolerance. Numerical experiments illustrate the effectiveness of our proposed method.
#8 Outliers and the Hard-Margin Linear SVM in High-Dimension, Low-Sample-Size Settings
著者: Yugo Nakayama
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13869
要約:
We study how outliers affect the hard-margin linear support vector machine when the dimension grows while the sample sizes remain fixed. For one-sided contamination, the centered Gram matrix converges to a constant inlier block plus an outlier-diagonal component, and the limiting dual problem has a closed-form solution. All outliers act through one effective distance. This representation yields explicit thresholds for loss of inlier support, margin shrinkage. intercept displacement, and asymptotic misclassification. We then treat contamination in both classes. The limiting intercept is the multiplier of the class-balance constraint, and the two classes pull it in opposite directions. Consequently, at most one class can collapse or be asymptotically misclassified, whereas symmetric contamination leaves the limiting discriminant function unchanged even though the margin shrinks. Seven numerical experiments verify the dual allocation, phase transitions, and discriminant limits.
#9 On the Spectral Estimation of VARMA Processes

要約:
We consider the problem of estimating the power spectral density of a Vector-valued Autoregressive Moving-Average (VARMA) process. A well-established approach to accomplishing this task consists of formulating a moment problem that selects the spectral density maximizing an entropy-like index while matching certain moments of the underlying process. However, existing methods assume that the moving-average (MA) component is either fixed or scalar, thereby reducing the class of ARMA models over which the optimal spectral density is sought. #10 Compositional random forest
著者: Khaled Masoumifard, Stephan van der Westhuizen, Sugnet Gardner-Lubbe
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14275
要約:
Compositional data have been analysed in the literature from three main points of view: directly on the simplex, in log-ratio coordinates, and as directional data on the unit sphere. In this paper, we construct a random forest framework for compositional responses that accommodates all three. Random forests are ensembles of decision trees, and the split criterion determines how each tree grows. We use the log-likelihood gain as the split criterion and consider distributions defined on the different spaces in which compositions can be represented. The Dirichlet likelihood has been studied in the literature, but its covariance is fixed by its mean up to a single scalar, and its maximum likelihood estimator has no closed form. To obtain more flexible criteria, we use two well-known transformations. First, the square-root transformation maps compositions onto the unit sphere, where existing directional distributions require numerical estimation. We introduce a tangent-normal (TN) distribution, which shares its parameters with the Kent distribution, and construct a split criterion whose gain is computed from additive sufficient statistics: the shape parameters are maximised out in closed form, up to an exponentially small truncation constant, so that no parameter is estimated iteratively. Second, the log-ratio transformation maps compositions to Euclidean space, where the multivariate logistic-normal distribution has a flexible covariance and a closed-form maximum log-likelihood, also based on additive sufficient statistics. Both criteria respond to a shift of the mean, measured relative to the covariance, and to a change in the covariance itself. In simulations, the most accurate forest depended on the covariance structure of the composition. The TN forest was the most accurate in two of the three designs.
#11 A Theory of Cross-Validation Variance
著者: Nathaniel S. O'Connell, Jaime L. Speiser
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14463
要約:
Cross-validation estimates predictive performance by repeatedly splitting the data into training and validation sets, fitting the model on the first and evaluating it on the second. Every observation influences the estimate in two roles, through its own evaluation when it is held out and through the models it helps fit when it trains. The random splits determine its weight in each role. From this representation, we prove an exact finite-sample identity for the variance of the cross-validated estimate. For a given splitting scheme with a common training set size, the variance is a function of these weights, with constants of the model, the metric, and the population. It covers metrics averaged over observations, such as mean squared error, and over case--control pairs, such as the AUC. The identity shows which schemes to use. The variance decomposes into sampling variance, which depends only on the training size, and variance due to the random fold splitting, which arises when the weights change from one draw of the splits to the next. K-fold cross-validation, whose weights are equal across observations by design, is therefore variance optimal at first order; Monte Carlo and bootstrap cross-validation are not. The first-order variance of K-fold does not depend on K, and under reasonable conditions on the model leave-one-out has the smallest variance of any K-fold scheme. For the AUC the same holds for leave-pair-out, which evaluates every case--control pair. Finally, we show the cross-validated estimate is asymptotically normal with the identity's full variance. Together, these results provide a general theoretical foundation for inference on cross-validated performance and establish principles for selecting variance-optimal cross-validation designs.

要約:
Flow-based and diffusion-based models have become the principal means of learning and sampling high-dimensional probability distributions, from images and video to proteins, molecules and physical fields, and increasingly language. #14 Predictive Surprise as Self-Grounding Concept Bottleneck for Interpretable Time Series
著者: Sachith Abeywickrama, Emadeldeen Eldele, Min Wu, Xiaoli Li, Chau Yuen
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12955
要約:
Time-series classifiers deployed in risk-sensitive domains must be (1) accurate, (2) interpretable, and (3) able to abstain when evidence is weak. Existing methods do not deliver all 3 at once, and the obstacle is structural, rather than logistical. Meaningful temporal patterns cannot be defined without knowing segment boundaries, yet meaningful boundaries cannot be placed without knowing which patterns to expect. Existing approaches break this circularity by using fixed windows or expert-annotated vocabularies, thereby sacrificing at least one of the 3 properties. We introduce ConceptTime, which closes the loop through a single self-supervised signal. A frozen probabilistic forecaster predicts a distribution over the next observation at every timestep of the sequence. We call the mismatch between its prediction and the realized signal the predictive surprise. This signal both locates segment boundaries and characterizes each resulting segment through the forecaster. Segments are thus grounded by how they behaved relative to expectation, not by what they look like. These summaries cluster into a vocabulary of dynamical regimes that we call concepts. A lightweight head classifies from the concept sequence, and the distance to the nearest concept yields reliability by design. Across 17 public benchmarks, ConceptTime is competitive with state-of-the-art black-box accuracy, beats every interpretable baseline, and retains near-98% accuracy on Epilepsy with 1% of labels. It further remains competitive with softmax, prototype-distance, and SHAP/TimeX/TimeX++ on OOD detection and deletion faithfulness, without a single annotation, language model call, or domain expert. Code is available at: https://github.com/Sachithx/ConceptTime.git.

要約:
GARCH-LSTM hybrid models have become increasingly popular in the volatility forecasting literature. To date, these models have almost exclusively focused on point estimation and accuracy measures such as RMSE and MAPE, while failing to assess the uncertainty of these point estimates. #16 Settling the Burn-in Gap: Sharp Risk Bounds for Tail-Averaged SGD in Linear Regression

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13383
要約:
We study the excess risk of constant-stepsize online stochastic gradient descent (SGD) with tail-averaging for high-dimensional linear regression. Building on the instance-dependent spectral framework of Zou et al. (2021), we establish matching initialization-explicit upper and lower bounds on the total excess risk of tail-averaged SGD in terms of the population covariance spectrum $\mathbf{H}$ and initialization $\mathbf{w}_0-\mathbf{w}^*$ whenever a trajectory spectral feedback condition holds. Existing analyses exhibit a fundamental gap in the effective variance: the upper bound of Zou et al. (2021) retains an undamped contribution from the initial error in the head eigenspace $\mathbf{I}_{0:k^\dagger}$, whereas their lower bound omits the variance induced by the head initialization altogether. We prove that our spectral feedback condition is satisfied in two concrete constant-stepsize regimes: (i) for arbitrary covariance spectra $\mathbf{H}$ and any finite horizon, under an explicit horizon-dependent stepsize chosen prior to execution; and (ii) for polynomially decaying spectra $\lambda_i\asymp i^{-a}$ ($a>1$), under a single horizon-independent positive stepsize. Finally, we complement these positive results with explicit one- and two-dimensional separation constructions proving an exponential-versus-polynomial gap in the lower bound of Zou et al. (2021) and showing why fourth-moment assumptions alone cannot determine the asymptotic exponential decay rate without additional structural conditions.

公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13545
要約:
BF16 is widely used to pretrain large language models because it has the same dynamic range as FP32 and converges similarly to FP32 training. However, in reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards, where learning rates are typically low, we find that standard mixed-precision BF16 training converges more slowly than FP16 training at the same learning rate. We attribute this gap to weight sticking: when FP32 shadow weights are quantized to BF16 using round-to-nearest, small updates often leave the BF16 weights used for rollout generation and training unchanged. We instead apply stochastic rounding when quantizing the FP32 shadow weights to BF16. This approach matches FP16's convergence speed across different models and datasets while reducing the KL divergence relative to BF16 training with a larger learning rate. Unlike FP16, it keeps BF16's wider dynamic range and needs no loss scaling.
