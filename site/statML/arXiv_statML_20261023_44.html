#1 Why Constrained Updates Still Forget: A Functional-Path Analysis of Continual SFT
著者: Qingsen Ma, Dianyun Wang, Jiahong Liu, Irwin King
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30191
要約:
Supervised fine-tuning adapts language models to specialized tasks but can disrupt previously acquired behavior. Existing approaches constrain parameters, gradients, or replay objectives, yet the connection between the protected object, the intervening trajectory, and retained outputs remains unclear. We develop a functional-path account that decomposes old-loss changes along realized updates into the controlled direction, gradient staleness, probe mismatch, and finite-step effects. The account yields a conditional refresh budget and identifies the restoring direction supplied by informative replay. Matched QLoRA interventions separate constraint granularity from placement before or after Adam: placement strongly affects retention across the studied code-label audits, whereas the granularity interaction depends on the endpoint. Checkpointed generation separates endpoint recovery from higher sampled-path accuracy, and matched replay schedules reveal loss exposure concealed by final scores. A sequential-task comparison extends this distinction to backward transfer: subspace regularization and projection improve average retention while trading off different old tasks; supervised replay protects both more consistently in this setting. Additional response interfaces distinguish content gains from exact-response retention. These findings connect update-level protection to task-wise behavior and motivate mitigation through explicit functional anchors, trajectory evaluation, and joint old/new auditing.
#2 Differentially Private Synthetic Data via Generalized Moment Matching
privacysynthetic data
著者: Rundong Ding, Yiyun He, Yizhe Zhu
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30196
要約:
We study $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-differentially private synthetic data for arbitrary databases of $n$ records in $[-1,1]^d$ under a specified integral probability metric (IPM). We introduce generalized moment matching, which estimates moments privately, fits a probability measure, and samples synthetic records. A general error theorem applies when moment error controls the target IPM. This framework extends Chebyshev moment matching for $1$-Wasserstein distance and smooth queries. We apply the framework to maximum mean discrepancy with Matern and Laplace kernels and a two-layer ReLU neural tangent kernel.We also obtain guarantees for sliced Wasserstein-$1$ and the IPM induced by norm-bounded ReLU networks. With sufficiently many synthetic records,these rates are minimax optimal up to logarithmic factors for fixed dimension.
#3 High-Value Training Data from Noisy, Diverse Sources
著者: Hedi Driss, Evan Frick, Anastasios N. Angelopoulos, Cho-Jui Hsieh, Yejin Huh, Tijana Zrnic, Andrew Ilyas
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30421
要約:
Training state-of-the-art models increasingly requires learning from large, heterogeneous datasets whose examples vary in quality, domain relevance, and source of supervision. Dataweighting---assigning different weights to training examples---offers a general way to control how such data influences training, but learning effective weights at scale remains challenging. We develop a scalable approach to learning dataweights: given a small, high-quality target dataset, we use metagradients through the training process to efficiently optimize training-example weights directly for performance on the target domain. To scale beyond a fixed dataset and model, we learn parametric weighting rules that generalize to new and streaming examples and transfer from smaller models to substantially larger ones. We study reward modeling as a prototypical application, where preference data may be noisy, adversarial, synthetic, or mismatched with the target domain. Learned dataweights improve held-out performance, automatically adapt reward models to specialized domains, and allow mixtures of human and synthetic supervision to outperform either source alone.
#4 Flow-based Spectral Kernel Learning for Nonstationary Attention
著者: Zi Yang, Ying Li, Alejandro Lancho, Dar\'{i}o C. Larese, Pablo M. Olmos, Michael Minyi Zhang
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30465
要約:
Kernelized attention provides a useful perspective for understanding self-attention as a similarity-based aggregation mechanism, but existing approaches often rely on fixed or stationary kernels, limiting their ability to adapt the similarity structure to complex token interactions. In this paper, we propose flow-based spectral kernel attention (FSKA), a framework for learning expressive nonstationary attention kernels. FSKA formulates kernelized attention through spectral density learning and parameterizes the resulting bivariate spectral density with normalizing flows. This design enables flexible and regularizable kernel learning while remaining compatible with scalable linear-attention computation. We further show that expressive kernel learning enables a simplified attention design with removed query--key projections and fixed orthogonal value projections, resulting in a more parameter-efficient architecture. Controlled experiments on classification, ablation, and scalability profiling show that FSKA achieves competitive or improved performance over existing attention mechanisms, while reducing projection-related trainable parameters by approximately $40\%$--$47\%$ compared with most baselines and maintaining favorable linear-attention memory scaling. Code Available: https://github.com/ziyang18/FSKA.
#5 xVGAE: A Hierarchical Variational Graph Autoencoder for Exchangeable Graphs
著者: Daniele Micheletti, Federica Zoe Ricci, Erik B. Sudderth
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30815
要約:
Graphs play a crucial role in many applications, ranging from drug discovery to social network modeling and astronomy. Generative models for graphs have substantially advanced in recent years, but we identify relatively simple scenarios where state-of-the-art models struggle to converge within a standard compute budget, or exhibit prohibitively slow generation times. We propose a generative architecture that achieves quality competitive with state-of-the-art models and is orders of magnitude faster at generation. Our approach is inspired by the Aldous-Hoover theorem, a classic representation theorem that characterizes any probability distribution of graphs that is invariant to permutations of node indices or exchangeable. This theorem establishes that three ingredients suffice: a graph-level latent variable, a set of exchangeable node-specific latent variables, and a so-called graphon function that maps graph- and node-level variables to the probability of an edge between any node pair. Given a training set of graphs, our exchangeable Variational Graph AutoEncoder (xVGAE) learns an approximation to their underlying graphons, as well as graph- and node-level latent variables. We show that our xVGAE encodes latent representations that correlate with graph- and node-level properties, and can (quickly, in one shot) generate new graphs that closely match key training set statistics.
#6 Continuous-depth Deep Gaussian Processes
著者: Ying Li, Sanyou Wu, Zi Yang, Qiaochu Xu, Yuhao Liu, Zhidi Lin, Michael Minyi Zhang, Petar Djuric, Alexandre H. Thiery
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30890
要約:
Deep Gaussian processes (DGPs) are appealing Bayesian regression models, but their standard discrete-depth parameterization often becomes harder to optimize as depth grows. We argue that depth is better treated as a latent continuous evolution than as a long discrete stack. We therefore propose a continuous-depth formulation in which the latent representation follows a stochastic differential equation with a depth-indexed drift family equipped with a GP-based prior. This reframes the core design choice as the prior placed on that family, rather than adopting a purely neural continuous-depth parameterization as in neural ODE or neural SDE models. We study two instantiations of this idea: CDGP, a direct GP prior over the state--depth drift, and FlowDGP, a flow-evolved prior that captures richer depth dependence at lower training cost. To motivate the redesign, we analyze how the single-sample DGP objective can become increasingly sensitive under repeated layer composition, while residual DGP is a useful first repair that still remains a finite discrete construction. We also give pathwise sensitivity analysis for the continuous-depth formulation. Experiments on synthetic, benchmark, and large-scale regression tasks show that residual DGP improves over plain DGP, while CDGP and FlowDGP provide a stronger overall story across the regimes we study.
#7 Random Projections for Spectral Algorithms: Optimal Rates in Sobolev Norms
著者: Caixing Wang, Yue Wang
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30948
要約:
Random projections (RP) offer an effective method for reducing computational and storage overhead while preserving the geometric structure of data. However, existing studies have primarily focused on the optimal generalization performance of specific kernel-regularized algorithms with RP in the well-specified setting and under restrictive conditions. In this paper, we provide a comprehensive and improved analysis of the generalization performance of RP-based spectral algorithms under general conditions \change{without sacrificing statistical efficiency}. By leveraging the embedding property of the reproducing kernel Hilbert space (RKHS) and a refined analysis of the operator similarity, we establish optimal learning rates in Sobolev norms that match the minimax lower bounds up to logarithmic factors. For two common implementations of RP, randomized sketches and Nystr\"{o}m sub-sampling (uniform or leverage-based), we show that the projection dimension required for optimality is proportional to the average or maximal effective dimension, yielding a significant reduction in computational cost while maintaining the statistical efficiency. Our results do not rely on the uniform boundedness assumption on the regression function and are valid across a wide range of source conditions, i.e., $s> \alpha_0-1/\beta$, where $s,\beta$, and $\alpha_0$ denote the smoothness index, capacity index, and the embedding index, respectively. In the benign case when $\alpha_0=1/\beta$, the optimality holds for all $s\in (0,2\tau]$ with $\tau$ and $\gamma$ denoting the qualification index and norm index. Experimental results demonstrate both the theoretical validity and the practical effectiveness of the proposed method.
#8 Prediction Uncertainty Controlled Computer Model Calibration
著者: Ryan Heejoon Byun, Dayeon Yoon, Won Chang
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31163
要約:
Computer-model calibration is usually undertaken in service of prediction, yet neither Bayesian Kennedy-O'Hagan (KOH) calibration nor amortized simulation-based inference is built to control the predictive error of the calibrated simulator. When parameters are weakly identified or the data-model discrepancy is misspecified, the simulator-output bands induced by a nominal posterior level can be far from nominal. We propose a two-layer framework that targets predictive miscoverage directly. First, a conditional flow-matching model learns an amortized posterior for the calibration parameters, trained on simulator outputs perturbed with Gaussian-process discrepancies of varying structure. Second, conformal risk control arranges posterior draws for the target observation into nested candidate sets and selects the smallest one whose fixed-rank pointwise bands attain a prescribed time-averaged miscoverage on a synthetic calibration sample. Under exchangeability of calibration and target observations, the bands control expected miscoverage in finite samples. In a synthetic benchmark, the proposed bands are roughly 20% the width of KOH-MCMC bands with miscoverage near the 10% target. In calibrating an age-structured SEIR model to the 2010 Malawi measles outbreak, they are again sharper and near-nominal, and the weeks they fail to cover coincide with the asymmetric epidemic decline, exposing structural inadequacy of the mechanistic model.
#9 Deep Learning for Random Objects in Metric Spaces: Beyond Manifold Assumptions
著者: Marcos Matabuena, Xiaokai Luo, Oscar Hernan Madrid Padilla
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31328
要約:
Deep learning (DL) methods have substantially improved predictive performance across a wide range of downstream prediction tasks, enabling new discoveries in multiple domains. However, principled DL methods for non-Euclidean random objects remain relatively scarce, despite the growing importance of modern biomedical outcomes taking the form of probability distributions, random graphs, functions, and other random objects in metric spaces. Motivated by a new generation of digital and clinical biomarkers, we propose a deep learning framework for regression with random objects in metric spaces. Our approach operates directly on the metric structure of the response space, avoiding restrictive manifold or Euclidean embedding assumptions imposed by many existing methods. At the same time, it retains modern statistical guarantees under the stated statistical assumptions. We establish statistical consistency and convergence rates for the proposed estimator, and characterize conditions under which it can mitigate the curse of dimensionality for hierarchically compositional regression functions. Extensive simulation studies demonstrate competitive predictive performance, with gains over several competing approaches in multiple Euclidean and non-Euclidean configurations. Finally, we demonstrate the practical utility of the proposed framework in two digital health case studies with distribution-valued outcomes in Wasserstein spaces, using high-resolution accelerometry and continuous glucose monitoring data. Embedding-based deep-learning methods may be less suitable when no informative low-dimensional representation exists or when the learned embedding does not adequately preserve the intrinsic geometry of the response space.
#10 Metric Conformal Prediction via the Conditional Expected Local Radius
著者: Rui Qiu, Fang Yao, Zhou Yu
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31336
要約:
We introduce the conditional expected local radius (CELR) and develop a new conformal prediction method for non-Euclidean responses in general metric spaces. CELR measures local conditional probability concentration, allowing prediction sets to accommodate heterogeneity and multimodality without requiring densities or a vector space structure. Its conditional distribution yields a nonconformity score that is comparable across covariate values and avoids costly transport comparisons between distance profiles. We further adapt a metric distributional random forest that handles multivariate covariates and nonlinear interactions, while a single forest fit supplies all score components, reducing computation by eliminating repeated local fitting. We establish uniform consistency of the estimators and asymptotic conditional validity under suitable regularity conditions, together with finite-sample marginal coverage under exchangeability. Numerical studies demonstrate effective uncertainty quantification across different metric spaces and substantial computational savings over the conditional profile score method.
#11 Dimension-free regimes for unadjusted Langevin in ridge-separable models: A subspace robust Wasserstein analysis
著者: Yuansi Chen, Francesco Pedrotti, Peter A. Whalley
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31431
要約:
The Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm (ULA) is widely used for high-dimensional Bayesian inference. Classical analyses in Wasserstein-2 distance predict an asymptotic bias and a mixing time that grow with dimension, but this metric is stronger than necessary for many statistical tasks. We instead analyze the bias in subspace robust Wasserstein distance, which controls the mean and arbitrary low-dimensional marginals and is therefore well suited to observables typically estimated in practice. For ridge-separable potentials formed with i.i.d. data, we prove high-probability bounds showing a bell-shaped mixing profile as the number of data points $N$ varies relative to the dimension $d$. In the overparameterized regime, the effective dimension is the rank $N$ of the data-dependent perturbation, leading to complexity depending on $N$ rather than $d$. In the large-sample regime, Gaussian-perturbation estimates yield dimension-free complexity once $N$ is sufficiently large. In contrast, near the proportional regime $\asymp d$, we prove that dimension-free guarantees are impossible in general.
#12 Nonparametric autoregressive estimation of probability distributions with parallel gradient boosting
著者: R\'emy Chapelle (CB, EVDG, CESP)
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31524
要約:
Nonparametric estimation of joint probability distributions is a core problem in statistics, which is notoriously challenging with high-dimensional data. Such challenges also arise with conditional distribution estimation and have led to flexible algorithms that aim to tackle the ''curse of dimensionality'' by exploiting predictive structures of lower dimension than the ambient space. In this paper, we study the use of such conditional distribution estimators to estimate joint distributions by factorizing the latter in an ''autoregressive'' way. We show that for loss functions satisfying a subadditivity property, bounds on the convergence rate of an autoregressive estimator can be derived from bounds on the convergence rate of the conditional distribution estimators. These bounds relate the difficulty of estimating the joint distribution to that of estimating the most challenging factor in the autoregressive factorization. When the conditional estimators adapt to sparsity in their conditioning sets, we characterize this difficulty through variable elimination in an undirected dependence graph. This allows bounding the rates of convergence by quantities related to the sparsity of the graph, and provides an additional motivation for the autoregressive approach. We then evaluate the practical performance of an autoregressive estimator based on parallel gradient boosting, a flexible nonparametric method for estimating conditional distributions. The estimator demonstrates competitive estimation performance on various simulated and real datasets, even without optimizing the factorization order. Together, these results support nonparametric autoregressive estimation as a practical approach to flexible joint distribution estimation.
#13 Reliable AI-Assisted Decisions via Selective Auditing
著者: Isaac Gibbs, Elise L. Han, Ryan J. Tibshirani, Tijana Zrnic
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31662
要約:
We consider online monitoring of an AI system that produces a sequence of decisions. At each round, the system outputs a proposed decision together with a confidence score estimating the trustworthiness of the decision. The decision and confidence score need not be produced by the same model: for example, one agent may propose a decision, while a separate verifier agent evaluates it and outputs a correctness or safety score. Based on this information, we must decide whether to accept the AI decision automatically or consult an expert, such as a human auditor, who reviews the proposal and either accepts or corrects it. We formulate this task as an online constrained optimization problem in which the goal is to minimize the frequency of expert auditing while guaranteeing that the fraction of AI errors that go unaudited stays below a user-defined threshold. We derive a family of algorithms with provable error-control guarantees with no assumptions, including a method that is optimally efficient: it asymptotically attains the best achievable auditing rate for both adversarial and i.i.d. data streams. Experiments on screening harmful requests to large language models, qualitative text annotation, and data labeling show that our methods achieve valid error control in practice while substantially reducing the need for human auditing compared to baselines.

#15 When Reconstruction Is Not Enough: Doubly Robust Counterfactual and Interventional Mediation Analysis with Learned Multimodal Representations

著者: Cong Cao
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30220
要約:
Learned representations of high-dimensional multimodal health records are increasingly used to adjust for confounding, yet they are usually trained to reconstruct the data rather than preserve the information that causal identification requires. We study counterfactual outcomes under alternative treatments, including phenotype-specific effects and patient-level counterfactual risk profiles of the kind digital twins provide, and interventional direct and indirect effects under exposure-induced intermediate confounding. We give conditions under which a representation preserves identification and show that interventional mediation is more demanding than counterfactual comparison. We prove that no covariate-only training objective, whether reconstruction, contrastive, or physics-informed, can certify that a representation is sufficient, and that, in a linear structural model, a representation can lose arbitrarily little reconstruction accuracy yet induce arbitrarily large bias. We decompose the error of cross-fitted doubly robust estimators into a first-order representation bias, which neither double robustness nor cross-fitting removes, and a second-order remainder of nuisance-estimation errors, so coverage worsens as the sample size grows when the representation is insufficient. We propose a workflow combining modality-specific variational autoencoders trained outside the estimation sample, cross-fitted doubly robust estimators, locked phenotypes, select-then-confirm pathway analysis, portable representations, and diagnostics for representation insufficiency, exposure leakage, and sensitivity. In simulations, discarding a rare, moderately strong confounder gave indirect-effect intervals with 2% coverage at n = 3,000; reconstruction loss missed the problem, and a simple predictive diagnostic flagged it in every replicate.
#16 Lifetime-valid decisions by continually evolving AI analysts
著者: Xiaojing Sun, Yuhan Zeng, Xiao Wang
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30562
要約:
AI agents increasingly act as analysts whose decision can often be cast as hypothesis tests. Classical tests control the Type I error rate, the probability of rejecting a true null hypothesis, for fixed procedures. However, AI analysts introduce internal randomness and can evolve continuously over time. As AI analysts evolve, the risk of approving versions with Type I error rates above the target can accumulate, and adaptive use of evaluation feedback can lead to overfitting. We introduce continual validation, which evaluates each proposed AI analyst version before deployment using simulated data under the null hypothesis, with scenarios that can be randomized and expanded over time. We prove that, regardless of how updates are chosen or how many are proposed, the lifetime probability of ever deploying a version whose Type I error rate exceeds the allowed level remains bounded. This guarantees that every subsequent decision controls the Type I error rate at the target level across these scenarios. At the same time, updates with sufficiently low Type I error rates pass validation with high probability, allowing the analyst to continuously improve its performance without sacrificing rigor. In two examples involving evolving AI analysts for bioequivalence studies and product A/B tests, Type I error rates reached 0.098 and 0.183 without continual validation, compared with at most 0.045 and 0.044 with it, while retaining 82% and 96% of the power from optimally selected valid updates.

Deep neural networks often struggle to provide reliable confidence estimates, making post-hoc calibration essential for reliable decision-making. Existing post-hoc methods largely rely on score-based transformations, which improve marginal calibration but often fail to capture spatially varying miscalibration in local regions of the learned feature space. To address this gap, we propose Local Adaptive Probability Estimation (LAPE), a post-hoc residual calibration framework. #18 Width and Depth Limits Commute During Training
著者: Ruijia Zhang, Soufiane Hayou
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30729
要約:
Recent work established that the infinite-width and infinite-depth limits of residual networks commute at initialization when residual branches are scaled by $1/\sqrt{depth}$. In particular, the network output converges to the same limit whether width and depth tend to infinity sequentially or jointly. We extend this commutativity result to training in linear residual networks. Under the same $1/\sqrt{depth}$ residual-branch scaling (Depth-$\mu$P) and the $\mu$P width parameterization, we prove that, for any fixed number of gradient-descent steps with gradient clipping, the network output converges in $L^2$ along every joint width--depth growth path as both width and depth tend to infinity. Consequently, the infinite-width and infinite-depth limits commute throughout any finite training horizon. Without gradient clipping, the corresponding $L^2$ convergence does not hold in general, but we establish the weaker convergence in probability.

To develop a theoretical understanding of chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning, a notable recent study by Huang et al. #44 WhatIf-Bench: Benchmarking Intervention-Aware Time Series Forecasting
著者: Arjun Ashok, Mihir Parmar, Palash Goyal, Bhavana Dalvi Mishra, Chun-Liang Li, Irina Rish, Alexandre Drouin, Jinsung Yoon, Tomas Pfister
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26582
要約:
Recent time series foundation models (TSFMs) can consume covariates in-context, extending zero-shot forecasting to problems driven by external factors. This enables scenario analysis: forecasting how a target responds to events that set covariates to chosen future values. Whether they can do this reliably is an open question. We introduce WhatIf-Bench, a benchmark measuring how well forecasters use covariates to forecast a target's response to future events. WhatIf-Bench is semi-synthetic: time series come from mechanistic simulators of data centers, power grids, road traffic, and water networks, with controlled covariate-target relationships grounded in each system. Each task presents a history of events intervening on covariates, then asks to forecast a future event that either replays seen values (Replay) or sets them to unseen ones (Novel). Varying the number of covariates, historical events, and event length yields 37,665 tasks. Evaluating TSFMs, frontier LLMs, and simple regression baselines, we find the LLMs reach the highest skill (improvement over a naive baseline), ahead of the best TSFMs. Every model forecasts well on Replay futures (48 to 85% skill) but drops to -7 to 50% on Novel futures, with the regression baselines falling the most. Skill on Novel futures also falls as events set more covariates jointly. More historical events raise skill on Novel futures across the board, but large differences remain. WhatIf-Bench reveals generalization to Novel futures as the core challenge, and offers a testbed toward arbitrary scenario analysis.
