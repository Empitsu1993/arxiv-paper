#1 Autoregressive Physics-Informed Neural Operator Surrogates and Joint 4-D Seismic-Production Bayesian History Matching
著者: Clement Etienam, David Blom, Hari Pattathil, Tsubasa Onishi, Harpreet Sethi, Lalson Vincent, Suraj Pawar, Kishan Ramesh Kumar, Pandu Devarakota, Faruk O. Alpak
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31677
要約:
We present a surrogate-accelerated Bayesian history-matching framework, demonstrated on a realistic synthetic model ($N=100{,}188$ cells). The forward map $\mathcal{G}$ is replaced by a Fourier neural operator $\mathcal{G}_\theta$ trained in a closed loop, $\hat{x}_{n+1}=\mathcal{G}_\theta(u,\hat{x}_n)$, with the discrete black-oil residual $R(x_{n+1},x_n)$ as a physics-informed neural operator (PINO) penalty, truncated backpropagation and noise injection. We assimilate production data and 4-D seismic using adaptive regularised ensemble Kalman inversion (aREKI), and synthesise $\Delta V_p$ inside the rollout via a closed-form Gassmann transform of the surrogate, under a two-stage schedule (aREKI on production, then an ensemble smoother with multiple data assimilation, ES-MDA, on both). To temper the ill-posedness, we invert $(\log K,\phi)$ not in $\mathbb{R}^{2N}$, where $N_e\ll 2N$ leaves a rank-deficient covariance and a null space the data never inform, but on a chart $u=\Psi(\tilde m)$, $\tilde m\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$, $d\ll 2N$, given by a Parseval-exact truncated cosine transform or a variational auto-encoder decoder. The ensemble then spans its own search space; the directions the data cannot constrain are cut to those the chart can express, and every member decodes to a geologically plausible field. For the rollout we prove an exposure bound $W_1\le\sum_j L^{\,n-1-j}\mathbb{E}e_j$, a physics-penalty contraction $\|D_x\mathcal{G}_\theta\|\le\alpha<1$ under a small residual defect, and an $\mathcal{O}(\rho^K)$ truncation bias. A Loewner-order inequality $\mathcal{I}_p\preceq\mathcal{I}_p+\mathcal{I}_s$ explains why joint assimilation is no less informative. On held-out data, $\mathcal{G}_\theta$ attains $R^2>0.999$ on pressure and saturation and rolls out in $0.579$ s, a $\sim2500\times$ speed-up, bringing an $N_e=190$-member, $N_a=10$-iteration joint inversion within one GPU-hour.
#2 Early learning in large residual networks
著者: Vladimir Narovlansky, Boris Hanin
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31783
要約:
We study the early training dynamics of nonlinear residual networks with blocks of zero hidden layers as a model of more general residual networks. We analyze networks in a sequential limit where first width and depth diverge, then the number of training data points diverges, and finally the input dimension diverges. This is a more refined setup compared to the typical mean-field theory analysis in which depth and width diverge but the number of training datapoints and input dimension are fixed. Our main technical result is a diagrammatic computation of both the network output and the hidden representations after the few steps of gradient descent in terms of the statistics of inner products of inputs from the training set. One of our principle conceptual findings is a negative result: in the limit of large input dimension the network output can learn only a linear function of its inputs early in training since higher learned monomials are more and more suppressed in the input dimension and vanish in the large dimension limit. This suggests that mean-field limits of ResNets are surprisingly rigid in the regime of high input dimension and may not be sufficient toy models for understanding feature learning. Finally, we investigate how to ensure hyperparameter transfer for the learning rate as a function of dataset size and input dimension. We test our predictions numerically and find excellent agreement with our theory.
#3 Combinatorial Characterizations of the Learnability of Conditional Distributions
著者: Mihir Dhanakshirur, Ambuj Tewari
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31890
要約:
We study the PAC-learnability of function classes that map inputs to probability distributions over a finite space. The learner observes i.i.d. input-label pairs, with each label sampled from the conditional distribution of an unknown target hypothesis in the class. We measure learning through excess risk under a bounded proper loss that satisfies two mild assumptions. We introduce the \textbf{fat$_\gamma$Natarajan dimension}, which generalizes both fat$_\gamma$ and Natarajan dimension, and prove that its finiteness at every positive scale is necessary and sufficient for learnability. We also introduce a \textbf{fat$_\gamma$Graph} dimension extending the classical \gra dimension and establish inequalities comparing the two dimensions at different scales, leading to an equivalent characterization of learnability. Finally, we show that a distribution-valued function class is learnable if and only if each of its coordinate classes is learnable in the probabilistic concept model.
#4 Conditional Flow Models for Offline Maximum Entropy Reinforcement Learning
著者: Hao Cheng, Yuling Jiao, Lican Kang, Jerry Zhijian Yang, Chen Zhong
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32125
要約:
Offline maximum entropy reinforcement learning seeks to estimate the optimal soft action-value function \(Q^*\) from a fixed dataset without further interaction with the environment. Although \(Q^*\) is characterized by the soft optimal Bellman equation, the corresponding Bellman operator cannot be evaluated directly because both the reward distribution and the transition kernel are unknown. Consequently, estimating \(Q^*\) requires the joint treatment of environment modeling and value function learning. We propose a framework that addresses these tasks separately. Conditional diffusion models based on probability flow ODEs estimate the conditional reward distribution and transition kernel. The resulting distributional estimators define a data driven approximation of the soft optimal Bellman operator, whose empirical Bellman residual is minimized over a class of deep neural networks to estimate \(Q^*\). We establish nonasymptotic guarantees for the proposed method. For operator learning, we derive total variation error bounds for the conditional flow estimators that account explicitly for score estimation, numerical discretization, and terminal smoothing. For soft value learning, we first use the soft Bellman equation to show that $Q^*$ inherits H\"older regularity from the conditional reward and transition densities. For any H\"older index $\beta$, the value learning stage achieves an excess Bellman residual risk bound of order $\widetilde{\mathcal O}\big(n^{-\frac{2\beta}{d+2\beta}}\big),$ where $d$ is the total dimension of the state and action spaces. In contrast to many existing theoretical analyses of deep reinforcement learning, our results do not require Bellman completeness. Numerical experiments support the theoretical findings and demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
#5 Implicit Neural Representations for Variational Problems on Graphons
著者: Taeyoung Kim, Jineon Baek, Joonkyung Lee, Hongseok Yang
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32147
要約:
We propose a neural exploratory framework for variational problems over graphons, i.e., symmetric measurable functions $[0,1]^2 \to [0,1]$, that arise as limits of dense graph sequences. Such problems are central to two parts of the mathematical literature: extremal graph theory, which studies graph parameter optimisation under given restrictions, often for those graphs with a large number of vertices, and the large deviation theory of dense random graphs, which characterises the structure of rare events through constrained graphon optimisation. We represent graphons by implicit neural networks and optimise graphon objectives by gradient descent. Our design combines three ingredients: a multi-scale sinusoidal residual architecture biased toward sharp, step-like graphons; an embedded solver that enforces a single empirical density constraint and is differentiated by implicit differentiation; and symmetry-aware Monte Carlo estimators. On generalised Tur\'an problems that previously required substantial human effort, our framework rediscovers known optimal graphons without human intervention. Applied to open instances, it produces candidate optima, including a previously unreported family of extremal structures. Applied to the variational problems from the large deviation theory of Erd\H{o}s--R\'enyi random graphs, the framework produces new candidate optimal graphons across both upper- and lower-tail regimes for multiple pattern graphs; for the case of the triangle graph and the upper-tail regime, these candidates improve upon the best-known reference construction of Lubetzky and Zhao. The structures found by our framework have also led to new theorems on the optimal graphons for both families of problems.
#6 Federated Nonparametric Regression with Heterogeneous Clients
著者: Xiaoran Jiang, Anand N. Vidyashankar
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32178
要約:
Whether a federated predictor benefits an individual client depends on both population heterogeneity and estimation uncertainty. We study this question for Nadaraya--Watson regression in a triangular array with potentially different fitting and evaluation distributions. Each client transmits local kernel summaries, allowing the server to construct a covariate-density-weighted federated estimator without centralizing individual observations. A client combines its fitted local predictor with the federated predictor through a prescribed weight, and gain is the paired reduction in squared prediction loss on an independent audit observation. Exact identities characterize both the gain of the realized fitted pair and its repeated-fitting mean. We also establish pointwise inference for the federated estimator. A two-stage analysis of client gains separates fitting variability from audit variability, retains the covariance created when a client's observations enter both its local estimator and the common server estimator, and yields fixed-block simultaneous inference across clients. Robustness is examined under two perturbation models. A Kullback--Leibler neighborhood provides a variance-sensitive lower certificate in a regular positive-gain-variance regime, while an $L_2$ neighborhood yields an exact least-favorable regression direction and robust value. Exact-model, vanishing-variance, and exact-zero-direction boundaries are kept separate because their scalar inputs are nonregular. The framework thus distinguishes decisions about a realized fitted pair from claims about the learning procedure under repeated fitting.
#7 Empirical concentration for chains with infinite memory
著者: Pierre Alquier, Paul Doukhan
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32265
要約:
Concentration inequalities for time series usually involve unknown quantities measuring dependence. This prevents their direct use as confidence bounds, or as criteria for learning algorithms. Recent works proved empirical concentration inequalities for stationary Markov chains, where the unknown quantities are estimated. We extend this approach beyond the Markov case, by considering stationary chains with infinite memory. We first establish a bounded-differences inequality on bounded Polish metric spaces under a summable Wasserstein contraction condition. We then estimate its dependence coefficients from a single trajectory, using either transition counts when the state space is finite, or derivatives of kernel conditional-density estimates for a continuous state space. We give general error bounds and illustrate their dependence on the memory tail. Finally, a fixed multiplicative safety factor turns these estimators into empirical concentration bounds. While some regularity assumptions are required to make the bounds empirical, our procedure remains fully adaptive in the sense that it does not require the knowledge of the constants involved in these assumptions. The empirical confidence bounds derived from our results are asymptotically as tight as the theoretical ones.
#8 Exact Global Minimax Risk near Nonidentification
著者: Kaiyue Yang, Yunan Wu
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32546
要約:
We study squared-error estimation when a nuisance parameter is unidentified on a submodel but affects a target that vanishes there. For models with higher-order profile expansions, we give sufficient conditions under which the largest critical-path minimax risk is the exact asymptotic minimax risk over the full parameter space. An efficient observable feature, negligible calibration error and stable recovery yield one estimator attaining the leading constant uniformly, with the profile coordinates unknown. The estimator uses calibration estimates, a local feature mean and an empirical recovery feature. The constant is expressed through a bounded-normal decision problem. We verify the conditions in two models. In centered Gaussian logistic errors-in-variables regression with one surrogate and two unknown variances, we determine the fifth-order global coefficient risk and its exact constant. A sparse extension separates variance-decomposition uncertainty from direction estimation and specifies when the scalar constant is preserved. In a degree-balanced replicated-network model, we determine the sixth-order global edge-contrast risk when block proportions are unknown. Triangles identify a parameter combination; a bowtie supplies the additional information needed for the contrast.
#9 Batched NN 4D-Var: a performant hybrid model framework for the Integrated Forecasting System
著者: Alban Farchi, Marcin Chrust, Massimo Bonavita, Marc Bocquet, Wei Pan, Patrick Laloyaux

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32582
要約:
Hybrid forecasting systems combining machine learning and numerical weather prediction offer a promising route toward improved forecast skill while preserving physical consistency. In this study, we investigate online learning of state-dependent model-error corrections within the Integrated Forecasting System using 4D-Var. Building on previous work, we represent model error using a neural network embedded within the forecast model and trained from observations through the data assimilation cycle. First, we introduce a targeted variant of neural-network 4D-Var in which only the neural-network parameters are estimated, while the atmospheric state is provided by a precomputed analysis. We then propose a batched extension that processes assimilation windows in random order and averages parameter increments over multiple windows, drawing inspiration from mini-batch stochastic optimization. Experiments are conducted with the Integrated Forecasting System of the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts at an intermediate resolution. Sequential online learning substantially improves forecasts within the training period, with error reductions exceeding 10%in some cases. In contrast, the proposed batched formulation produces hybrid models that consistently improve forecast skill in out-of-sample experiments. Larger batch sizes accelerate convergence and improve robustness by reducing the noise of parameter updates. Diagnostics further show that the online-trained corrections preserve realistic forecast variability and do not improve skill through artificial smoothing. These results demonstrate that the formulation of the online learning problem is critical for generalisation and establish batched online training as a promising framework for hybrid machine-learning/data-assimilation systems.
#10 Acquisition for Spherical-Linear Bayesian Optimization on the Cube
著者: Nathan Wycoff

要約:
Recent work demonstrated that executing Bayesian Optimization (BO) using simply a linear surrogate with a clever transformation is enough to approximate the performance of the standard Gaussian-Process (GP) based version on high-dimensional test functions. When the decision region is a sphere, this strategy admits rapid exact acquisition. However, it is the unit cube we encounter most often in the BO literature, and in this setting a standard approach, namely a multi-start quasi-Newton method, is employed for solving the acquisition subproblem. In this article, we will discuss a series of results, both positive and negative, regarding the complexity of global resolution of various acquisition subproblems over the unit cube. #11 Score-Preserving Continual Lasso under Task Shift
著者: Jingyi Guo, Runze Li, Lingzhou Xue
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32708
要約:
Continual learning (CL) aims to learn from sequential tasks while mitigating catastrophic forgetting when historical data are no longer accessible. We study continual learning for high-dimensional sparse linear regression, where $\ell_1$ regularization creates a difficulty absent from continual ridge regression: that recursively reusing an $\ell_1$-penalized estimator can propagate earlier shrinkage and accumulate regularization bias across tasks. We propose score-preserving continual Lasso (SP-CL), which separates sparse estimation from state transmission by preserving the historical squared-loss score. Under squared loss, its cumulative estimator exactly recovers a full-history Lasso minimizer. When task coefficients coincide, it inherits the full-history sparse rate. For related task-specific coefficients, SP-CL uses a regularized current-task correction that leaves the transmitted state unchanged and attains the offline source-assisted rate under an oracle related-task condition. We also derive Overall/BWT/FWT decompositions that distinguish historical information loss, task conflict, and current-task specialization, and validate the resulting regimes in simulation.
#12 Sparse Bayesian Neural Networks via Sticky Piecewise Deterministic Markov Processes
著者: August Arnstad, Geir Olve Storvik, Filippo Pagani, Leiv R{\o}nneberg
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32753
要約:
There is growing evidence that the performance of a neural network depends largely on a small subset of its weights, which are typically identified by training a dense network and pruning it afterwards. Within a Bayesian framework, pruning can be directly incorporated through a spike-and-slab prior, providing a proper treatment of the uncertainties involved, both with respect to pruning and to parameter uncertainty. In Bayesian neural networks (BNNs), sampling by MCMC is notoriously hard. We demonstrate that modern Monte Carlo methods based on piecewise deterministic Markov processes (PDMPs) can be used to sample posteriors of BNNs asymptotically exact whenever numerically constructed rate bounds hold. Spike-and-slab priors can be incorporated through sticky versions of the PDMPs, setting weights exactly to zero during sampling. We examine the predictive performance and convergence of the samplers, and show that sparse BNNs sampled this way can be more robust to input noise than dense ones.
#13 Singular Geometry of Low-Rank Adaptation
著者: Kamillo Ferry, Anthea Monod, Keisuke Yano
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32854
要約:
Low-rank adaptation (LoRA) is now a standard approach to parameter-efficient fine-tuning of large neural networks. Its low-rank factorization is inherently non-identifiable, making LoRA a singular statistical model whose learning complexity cannot be described by parameter dimension alone. We study this complexity using the real log canonical threshold (RLCT) from singular learning theory and how it depends on both the low-rank parameterization and the pretrained network. Under a local linearization, LoRA induces a low-rank trace regression model whose Kullback--Leibler geometry is controlled by a forward-backward covariance, constructed from activations and backpropagated sensitivities at the adapted layer. This covariance determines which low-rank perturbations are statistically observable. When it is nondegenerate along the relevant low-rank directions, we recover the classical learning complexity of reduced-rank regression; when its kernel meets these directions, additional statistically indistinguishable parameters change the singular geometry. We characterize this geometry locally through observable tangent directions and a residual singularity, and globally through affine linear sections of determinantal varieties. The global learning complexity need not be determined at a ground-truth parameter, as singularities on another rank stratum can dominate. We also introduce a curvature-corrected multi-temperature estimator of the RLCT. Experiments on multilayer perceptron and transformer architectures reveal strongly layer-dependent forward-backward geometry and show that ambient covariance degeneracy does not imply degeneracy along the low-rank directions relevant to adaptation.

Variables can predict an outcome jointly while each is individually independent of it, causing structure learners to discard informative links. Chain-of-thought (CoT) monitoring aims to detect potentially harmful behaviors in large language models (LLMs) from their CoTs. The CODIACS Vanguard randomized trial compared a depression care intervention with usual care for patients with persistent depressive symptoms following acute coronary syndrome. We assess whether its findings can be extended to broader patient populations represented by two cohorts constructed from the All of Us electronic health records. This extension is challenging when treatment effects vary across covariates whose distributions differ between trial and target populations. We develop Bayesian Propensity Score Predictive Inference (PSPI), an interaction-based outcome modeling framework that combines Bayesian additive regression trees for potentially high-dimensional covariates with natural cubic splines of the sampling propensity score. In the application, PSPI estimates larger intervention benefits in both target populations than the unadjusted trial estimate.
#17 Compatible Likelihoods for Flow Matching on Manifolds

要約:
On the simplest non-Euclidean manifold, the 1D flat torus, we exhibit a distribution where a recently proposed extension of Riemannian flow matching is globally optimized by a velocity field that pushes samples away from the data distribution. We trace this failure mode to a property we call compatibility; a likelihood-based flow matching objective is compatible if its population minimizer recovers the marginal velocity field induced by the data and coupling. We give a general condition for compatibility, apply it to existing methods, and bound the worst-case error in terms of a residual component vanishing under compatibility. Next, we note that existing compatible objectives put density on velocities that are infeasible on compact manifolds. This manifests as a reweighting of the flow matching loss which, on constant-curvature manifolds, treats errors in flow speed and direction differently. Empirically, our method recovers the correct velocity field on the test case, outperforms prior methods on high-dimensional product tori, and obtains substantial NLL improvements over prior work on two of four standard geospatial benchmarks on the sphere at moderate runtime overhead. On a large dataset of high-dimensional human poses, our method outperforms prior work according to sample-based metrics.
