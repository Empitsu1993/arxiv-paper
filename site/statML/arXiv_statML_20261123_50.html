#1 SDG: State-Dependent Gating at Chunk Boundaries for Linear Attention
著者: Renjia Deng, Yuxi Liu, Yutong He, Xue Wang, Kun Yuan
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26637
要約:
Recent gated linear attention models use state-independent gates, computed from token representations without directly reading the recurrent memory. This design supports efficient chunkwise computation: sequences are divided into chunks of consecutive tokens, with parallel computation within each chunk. However, the same token representation produces the same gate, even when the layer stores different information. We introduce State-Dependent Gating (SDG), which computes element-wise gates from the change in memory over a chunk and applies them at the chunk boundary. This provides state feedback for forgetting while preserving parallel computation within each chunk. We develop fused kernels for Gated DeltaNet, Kimi Delta Attention, Gated DeltaNet-2, and Mamba-3. Our theoretical analysis quantifies the potential reconstruction benefits of state-dependent, element-wise gating under a signal--noise model. We also show that boundary decay is an element-wise ridge proximal step when the gate is held fixed. Empirical evaluation of 1.3B linear attention and hybrid models trained on 100B tokens shows improvements across language modeling, commonsense reasoning, synthetic and real-world retrieval, and long-context understanding. These results demonstrate that SDG is an effective approach to enhancing existing linear attention models.
#2 Spectral Estimation with Deformed Decompression
著者: Siavash Ameli, Chris van der Heide, Liam Hodgkinson, Michael W. Mahoney
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26945
要約:
Sample covariance matrices are fundamental objects in machine learning and statistics, with valuable information encoded into their eigenvalues. When treating modern problems, the scale of these matrices can become prohibitive for tractable computation. This creates a critical need for "spectral decompression": a method for inferring the eigenspectrum of a very large-scale model using only information from its smaller realizations. Existing methods fall into two main categories: those relying on spectral inversion; and those that rely on Free Decompression techniques. On the one hand, spectral inversion is a fundamentally ill-posed problem that is notoriously unstable, often failing to yield reliable results in finite-size settings. On the other hand, while Free Decompression was recently proposed to address this scaling, its reliance on the Nica-Speicher framework restricts its use to unitarily invariant ensembles, a symmetry condition that subsampled covariance matrices fail to satisfy. In this work, we introduce Deformed Decompression (DD), a novel framework for the stable, forward-extrapolation of spectral densities. By leveraging properties of the companion matrix, DD recasts the spectral decompression problem as a directed evolution of the Stieltjes transform of the companion matrix. This approach bypasses the instabilities of traditional inversion and provides a robust methodology for scaling covariance matrices and even their generalized variants (including Hessian matrices) that lack unitary invariance. We demonstrate the utility of our framework through experiments on a variety of random matrix examples, showing that DD successfully captures the complex spectral signatures of large-scale systems.
#3 Semiparametric Efficiency for Entropic Optimal Transport under Structured Distribution Shift
著者: Yiming Ma, Weiwei Zhuang, Hang Liu
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27261
要約:
Can structural information improve estimation of entropic optimal transport (EOT), and when is the gain zero? We study a fixed-dimensional exponential density ratio model (DRM) with a nonparametric baseline, compact support, and fixed positive regularization. At positive limiting sampling fractions, an exact projection identity quantifies the variance gain from the restriction. The pooled empirical-likelihood estimator attains the restricted bound and supports asymptotically valid confidence intervals when the bound is positive. Even under correct specification, the gain can be zero and can shrink as the tilt space expands. The same projection residual determines direction-specific first-order bias under local misspecification. When both samples grow and the target fraction vanishes, a zero first-order tilt derivative can leave source fluctuations and quadratic tilt error as the leading terms. Their balance gives a phase transition in the limiting distribution. A continuous example gives different convergence rates for two correctly specified bases at the same distribution pair. Controlled experiments assess the fixed-proportion theory. Natural-data comparisons distinguish variance reductions from improvements in reference RMSE driven by smaller reference bias.
#4 Can pretrained Transformers deliver semiparametrically efficient inference?
著者: Han Yan, Jin Zhu, Chengchun Shi, Tengyao Wang
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27666
要約:
Semiparametric models provide a broad framework for statistical inference, yet efficient estimation typically relies on influence function correction tailored to each estimand and on nuisance estimation repeated for every sample. We show that a single pretrained Transformer can deliver efficient estimation and valid inference for a family of estimands in one forward pass, without deriving efficient influence functions or estimating nuisance functions. Pretrained on simulated data with the true parameter as the only label, the Transformer learns the posterior mean and covariance of the queried estimand and hence returns an estimate and a Wald-type confidence region. Theoretically, we prove that, for locally flat pretraining priors of slowly growing complexity, the posterior mean is efficient for every queried estimand. The Transformer inherits this efficiency with asymptotically exact coverage, in probability over evaluation populations and queries, even under distribution shift. We also reveal that the guarantee places much higher demands on pretraining when the data-generating distribution is fixed, as in classical frequentist inference. Experiments on causal inference, partial linear regression, and survival analysis show that the pretrained model generally achieves the lowest estimation error among competing methods, with nearly nominal coverage, at a computational cost of only about 0.02 seconds per dataset.
#5 Budgeted Subset Selection with a Transferable Learned Value Model
著者: Jade Lejeune Herman, Johan A. K. Suykens, Peter Goos
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27751
要約:
Subset selection is a fundamental task in machine learning, statistics and combinatorial optimisation, with applications in active learning, band selection, input selection and experimental design. The utility of a subset depends on information revealed only after the selection, such as labels or responses, and on interactions among selected candidates. Existing approaches combine value estimation and redundancy control in application-specific ways, which limits their use in other domains. This paper introduces Subset Acquisition with Learned Value Estimates and Search (SALVES), a framework that decomposes subset selection into two components. First, a value model is trained by supervised learning on tasks where the hidden information is known. On a new task, it predicts the utility each candidate would add to the current selection. Second, a selection step chooses the subset from these predictions, typically by simulated annealing with a diversity term penalising redundancy. The value model transfers to new classes, pretrained networks, datasets and sensors, and from synthetic to real data. On the four applications, SALVES matches or outperforms application-specific baselines on average. It requires substantially fewer bands for equal accuracy and remains reliable in input selection where individual baselines fail.
#6 Communication-Efficient Federated Learning of Latent Patient Representations from Multi-Institutional EHRs
著者: Zhiyu Yan, Shiao Liu, Rahul Mahajan, Anand Viswanathan, Christopher D. Anderson, Rui Duan
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27900
要約:
Electronic health records (EHRs) contain rich health information for uncovering latent patient representations and clinically meaningful subgroups. Learning from EHR data across institutions is valuable because representations learned at a single site may reflect local data patterns and lack generalizability, yet pooling patient-level records across institutions is often impractical because of privacy constraints and communication costs. We propose a communication-efficient, non-iterative federated spectral method for learning shared latent representation components from multi-institutional EHRs, in which each institution shares aggregate marginal frequencies and a low-rank spectral summary while keeping patient-level records local. A central server aggregates these summaries with the pooled marginal frequencies to recover both the shared low-rank structure and the canonical row-normalization direction required for interpretable representation recovery. We prove row-wise recovery guarantees and error bounds, with a leading federated variance term matching a corresponding lower bound under explicit regularity conditions. Simulations show that our method closely matches pooled analysis in estimating latent representation components, improves as more sites contribute summaries, and outperforms single-site and existing federated benchmarks across a range of data settings. Applied to real-world multi-facility EHR data for stroke patients, the method learns interpretable patient representations that highlight clinically distinct subgroups and could help inform subgroup-specific care.
#7 Optimal Linear Regression Without a Variance
著者: Artin Tajdini, Jasper C. H. Lee, Jonathan Scarlett, Kevin Jamieson
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27983
要約:
We study high-probability linear regression in the infinite variance regime, wherecthe regression noise has only a finite conditional $\alpha$-moment $\nu$ for some unknown $\alpha\in(1,2)$, and the covariates may only have finite moments up to a given order (e.g., 4th moment). We give a black-box reduction that converts finite-variance regression procedures into weak-moment procedures. The reduction is based on empirical residual pruning and is agnostic to the moment exponent, the noise scale, the covariance, and the design moment. Under a finite fourth-moment condition on the covariates, any finite-variance subroutine attaining the sub-Gaussian rate yields the following when the number of samples $n$ is above a suitably defined threshold: Without knowing $\alpha$ or $\nu$, we have with probability at least $1-\delta$ that $ \|\widehat\theta-\theta^*\|_\Sigma \lesssim_\alpha \nu^{1/\alpha} \big(\frac{d+\log(1/\delta)}{n}\big)^{\frac{\alpha-1}{\alpha}}, $ where $d$ is the dimension, $\Sigma$ is the covariate covariance matrix, and $\|v\|_\Sigma:=(v^\top\Sigma v)^{1/2}$. We further prove a matching minimax lower bound that holds under the same conditions. The reduction is computationally efficient whenever the finite-variance subroutine is. We also obtain a distribution-free prediction guarantee under a bounded raw $\alpha$-moment assumption on the response, and give an analogous $\alpha$-agnostic reduction for mean estimation.
#8 Language Diffusion Models are Associative Memories Capable of Retrieving Unseen Data
diffusion
著者: Bao Pham, Mohammed J. Zaki, Luca Ambrogioni, Dmitry Krotov, Matteo Negri
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26841
要約:
When do language diffusion models memorize their training data, and how to quantitatively assess their true generative regime? We address these questions by showing that Uniform-based Discrete Diffusion Models (UDDMs) fundamentally behave as Associative Memories (AMs) $\textit{with emergent creative capabilities}$. The core idea of an AM is to reliably recover stored data points as $\textit{memories}$ by establishing distinct basins of attraction around them. Historically, models like Hopfield networks use an explicit energy function to guarantee these stable attractors. We broaden this perspective by leveraging the observation that energy is not strictly necessary, as basins of attraction can also be formed via conditional likelihood maximization. By evaluating token recovery of $\textit{training}$ and $\textit{test}$ examples, we identify in UDDMs a sharp memorization-to-generalization transition governed by the size of the training dataset: as it increases, basins around training examples shrink and basins around unseen test examples expand, until both later converge to the same level. Crucially, we can detect this transition using only the conditional entropy of predicted token sequences: memorization is characterized by vanishing conditional entropy, while in the generalization regime the conditional entropy of most tokens remains finite. Thus, conditional entropy offers a practical probe for the memorization-to-generalization transition in deployed models.
#9 On the loss landscape of the linear neural network: beyond critical points
著者: Antoine Lejay (PASTA, IECL), Lionel Lenotre (Universit{\'e} de Haute-Alsace), Sa{\"i}d Toubra (IECL)
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26671
要約:
While the critical points of the loss landscape in linear neural networks have been extensively studied since the seminal work of Baldi \& Hornik in the late 1980s, a description of this landscape at an intermediate or global scale is still lacking. Such a characterization is crucial, particularly as a substantial body of literature indicates that gradient descent circumvents critical points. The present article introduces an intermediate structure, termed the critical thalweg, which encompasses all critical points and exhibits key geometric properties. Beyond providing a geometric proof of Baldi \& Hornik's results using simpler assumptions than the existing literature, this framework offers a global view of the loss landscape that can be easily visualized in a two-dimensional plane. Empirical observations suggest that gradient descent is attracted toward the critical thalweg, along which the loss continues to decrease to its global minimum.
#10 Learning under Bounded Manipulation for Quadratic Incentive Design
著者: Arda G\"u\c{c}l\"u, Subhonmesh Bose, Tamer Ba\c{s}ar
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26736
要約:
We study a repeated quadratic incentive design game in which a leader learns a follower's private type from noisy observations of the follower's actions and uses her estimates in an affine incentive rule. The follower knows the leader's learning policy, which incorporates past observations to produce estimates of the follower's type, and can deviate from his myopic best response to influence the leader's future estimates. We ask how the follower's ability to manipulate these observations affects the leader's performance. Under a manipulation budget $C_T$ constraining the expected cumulative squared deviation of the follower's actions from his myopic best response, we establish a minimax regret rate of order $\max\{\log T, \min\{C_T,T\}\}$ for approximately rational followers. Our information-theoretic lower bound applies to a general class of causal estimation and manipulation policies for the leader and the follower. We provide a simple learning policy that achieves the lower bound. Finally, we show that under a fixed learning rule, increasing the manipulation budget of the follower can counterintuitively reduce the leader's regret.
#11 Memorization and Generalization in Generative Models: A Perspective From Energy-based Modeling and Associative Memory
privacy
著者: Bao Pham
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26797
要約:
Generative models now write working code, produce realistic images, and hold long conversations, yet the mechanisms behind their intelligence still evade our understanding. This thesis explores that puzzle through Associative Memory (AM), in which patterns are stored as energy minima and retrieved through attractor dynamics. Beyond their capacity, AMs form new minima known as spurious states, long regarded as a failure of memory. We argue that this failure is where creativity begins, and that memorization and generalization can coexist as outcomes of one retrieval process. First, we show that diffusion models behave as AMs. With little data, a diffusion model forms a distinct basin of attraction around each training sample. As the data grows, these basins shrink, and spurious states emerge as attractors that blend stored samples yet match none of them. They are the first signs of generative capability. We characterize this transition by measuring the volume, energy, and curvature of these basins across datasets and architectures, including Stable Diffusion. We then turn to language and move beyond the energy-based view of AM. We show that uniform-state discrete diffusion models are AMs capable of retrieving unseen data, with basins of attraction that arise from conditional likelihood maximization alone. As the training set grows, basins around training examples contract while basins around unseen examples expand, until corrupted test sentences are recovered as reliably as training ones. Conditional entropy exposes this coexistence within a single sequence and cheaply diagnoses where a model lies along the transition. Together, these results suggest that AM is a general principle defined by basins of attraction, and that memorization and generalization can coexist within one model. AM thus offers a framework for asking when a generative model recalls and when it creates.

Knowledge distillation (KD) trains a student to match a teacher's output distribution. However, full-vocabulary KD requires costly output-head backpropagation. To reduce this cost, we introduce Distillation with Errors (DWE). Our key insight is that minibatch training already approximates the training gradient, so per-position backpropagation does not need to be exact. DWE therefore exploits the zero-sum structure of logit gradients to construct sparse paired gradient estimates whose expectation matches the full-vocabulary gradient. Our code is available at https://github.com/dwe-lab/DWE.
#15 Verifying and Strengthening Neural Tangent Kernel Theory
著者: Hao Zhu
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27487
要約:
Writing Neural tangent kernel (NTK) theory explains why very wide neural networks trained by gradient methods converge in the lazy-training regime. Its standard proof combines matrix concentration, random Gram matrices, continuous-time dynamics, spectral perturbation, and a bootstrap argument, but has lacked machine-checked verification. We present a Lean\~4 formalisation for two-layer networks, proving an end-to-end theorem: for a smooth bounded activation (instantiated with $\sigma=\tanh$), frozen biases, paired $\pm 1$ initialisation, and explicit width conditions, gradient flow exists uniquely with probability at least $1-\delta$, and $$ \|r(t)\|^2 \leq \|y\|^2 e^{-\lambda_0 t/2}. $$ The sole data assumption is population NTK non-degeneracy, $\lambda_0>0$. Initial coercivity, kernel drift, the movement bootstrap, the residual ODE, and flow existence and uniqueness are formally proved. The formalisation also sharpens the standard analysis in two ways. First, replacing the conventional matrix-Bernstein variance proxy with the classical row-covariance proxy yields estimated concentration-width certificates for $\tanh$ that are $36$--$53\times$ smaller at $(m,d)=(16,8)$ and $94$--$121\times$ smaller at $(32,16)$. Lean certifies the inequality; the ratios are numerical. Second, the network Hessian has operator norm $\Theta(\sqrt{p})$ on the sup-norm parameter ball (a paper calculation), preventing the expected $O(1/\sqrt{p})$ linearisation bound via sup-norm Taylor estimates. An $L^2$ parameter geometry resolves this issue. Required widths remain polynomial but far beyond practical scales, limiting applicability.

#17 Efficient inference for fairness-constrained sequential decision policies
著者: Yi-Hui Zhou

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27714
要約:
Adaptive decision systems in education and health choose interventions sequentially and are often required not to leave a protected group too far behind. We study the fairness-constrained optimal value: the largest population value attainable by a policy in a smooth parametric class subject to constraints that are smooth functions of group specific counterfactual functionals, such as value gaps, floors, max-min welfare and budgets. For multi-stage, non-Markov data under sequential ignorability, a sensitivity analysis of the Karush-Kuhn-Tucker system shows that this value is pathwise differentiable, with efficient influence function equal to the Lagrangian combination of the influence functions of the group values. #18 Solving the pre-image problem in kernel PCA: The reproducing property is all you need
著者: Joel-Pascal Ntwali N'konzi, Stefan Klus
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27718
要約:
Kernel PCA extends PCA to nonlinear dimensionality reduction by implicitly mapping data into a reproducing kernel Hilbert space (RKHS) via the kernel trick, with applications ranging from image denoising to out-of-distribution detection. While its projection map is well understood, appropriately defining the associated reconstruction map is a longstanding challenge since projecting back from the RKHS to the input space is non-trivial. This is known as the pre-image problem. Existing approaches have tackled this problem by either relying on nonlinear optimization, which is prone to numerical instabilities and sensitive to initialization, or by solving a separate supervised learning problem, incurring additional computational overhead. Furthermore, although the projection map of kernel PCA reduces to that of standard PCA when a linear kernel is used, most of these approaches are not guaranteed to recover PCA's reconstruction map in this case, a consistency property that one would naturally expect to hold. In this paper, we leverage the reproducing property of the RKHS to derive a reconstruction map for kernel PCA that is provably consistent. Our derivations rely on the mild assumption that the RKHS either contains linear functions or the kernel is universal, which holds for standard kernels. We further extend the method to the random Fourier features (RFFs) approximation of kernel PCA by introducing a corresponding notion of RFF reproducing property. Numerical experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that the proposed method performs comparably with state-of-the-art methods across kernel choices, while remaining more computationally efficient and maintaining consistent performance in settings where these baseline methods drastically fail.
