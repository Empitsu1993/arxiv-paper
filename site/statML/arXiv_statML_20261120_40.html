#1 DL101 The Representation Capability of Neural Network Models
著者: Fernando Berzal
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24980
要約:
Universal approximation theorems (Cybenko, Funahashi, Hornik & Leshno) provide the mathematical justification for why neural networks are powerful enough to model complex, nonlinear phenomena. Shallow networks can approximate any continuous function on compact sets. Leshno's theorem clarified that non-polynomial activations are necessary for universality, which explains why ReLU, logistic, hyperbolic tangent, and other common activations work (but pure polynomials do not). However, approximation theorems provide no efficiency, training, nor generalization guarantees. They do not indicate how many neurons are needed for a given accuracy, whether gradient descent will find the right parameters, or whether networks trained on finite data will generalize well. Barron provided quantitative error bounds for particular function classes (approximation rates for functions with bounded Fourier norm), whereas Mont\'ufar was the first to show that expressivity grows exponentially with depth (in terms of the number of linear regions). Deep networks are strictly more expressive than shallow ones with the same number of parameters. Depth is fundamentally more powerful than width, providing a rigorous mathematical explanation for the use of deep learning models.
#2 Risk-optimal active learning with informative abstention
著者: Fariborz Setoudehtazangi, Geoffrey J. McLachlan
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24986
要約:
We study label acquisition when an oracle may abstain and its response probability shares parameters with the classifier. A query can then provide information through both a returned label and the response outcome. Under a correctly specified parametric model, we derive the classifier information after adjustment for response-model nuisance parameters. Combining this information with the local curvature of multiclass zero-one risk gives an acquisition criterion for minimizing the leading asymptotic excess risk. We characterize the optimal allocation under a fixed query budget and show how query allocation affects response identifiability. The oracle criterion reduces to response-discounted acquisition when response probability does not depend on the classifier parameters. Simulations examine the resulting query allocations and isolate the contribution of the response-outcome term through an ablation. A Consumer Complaints replay retains observed human abstentions and reveals benchmark labels upon response. Response-aware acquisition has lower mean test error than response-discounted acquisition at the two largest budgets, with paired differences significant after Holm correction. Entropy sampling performs better at the smallest budget. The gains depend on the response mechanism and query budget.
#3 Variance-Adaptive Upper Confidence Bounds for Multi-Armed Bandits with Kaplan--Meier Survival Estimation
著者: Yangfan Cui, Andrew Papanicolaou
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24988
要約:
We study the stochastic multi-armed bandit problem in which the reward associated with each arm is the survival probability at a pre-specified time horizon, and the observed outcome is a right-censored time-to-event variable. Standard bandit algorithms face delayed and partial observations in this setting. Prior adaptations for the Kaplan--Meier (KM) estimator rely on Hoeffding-type bounds that ignore the inherent heteroscedasticity of continuous survival processes. To bridge this gap, we propose KM-UCB, a variance-adaptive policy that unites continuous-time survival analysis with discrete sequential decision-making. By leveraging martingale theory and continuous influence functions, we derive a fixed-cohort empirical Bernstein bound parameterized by Greenwood's variance formula. For realtime allocation, we retain the Greenwood index and use a survival bound from completed records to review the recommended arm. Under catch-up reporting, the policy has logarithmic expected regret with an arm-specific variance term in the exploration bound. We evaluate the original policy in a dual-layer delayed simulation and a retrospective replay of breast cancer trial data, and evaluate the correction in a separate paired simulation.
#4 Empirical Variational Bayes Factorization for Longitudinal Matrix Data
著者: Jiakun Li, Eric F. Lock
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25065
要約:
Collecting high-dimensional data over time for multiple individuals is increasingly common in biomedical research, particularly in studies of disease progression or molecular time-course experiments. Often data are missing or the timepoints are irregularly spaced across individuals. There is a lack of flexible methodology to explore latent patterns of change over time and account for missing data. We propose an empirical variational Bayes (EVB) approach to factorize high-dimensional longitudinal data, in which the underlying factors have temporal correlation over time. The EVB approach is computationally efficient and accommodates flexible covariance structures over time with automatic determination of all hyper-parameters (including the underlying rank, i.e., number of factors). Simulations demonstrate that the approach captures the underlying signal more precisely than other strategies, and performs well with respect to missing data imputation under different scenarios. An application to a gene time-course data for multiple sclerosis treatment provides a factorization with interpretable components over time and improved imputation accuracy for missing data.
#5 Statistical, Machine Learning and Deep Learning Methods for Missing Covariates: An Empirical Evaluation with Applications to Alzheimer's Disease Research
著者: Yiqing Pan, Sharon X. Xie, Shujie Ma, Angela L. Jefferson, Panpan Zhang
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25219
要約:
Missing data are common in biomedical research, particularly when biomarkers are costly or require invasive procedures to obtain, such as cerebrospinal fluid measures in Alzheimer's disease. Although a wide range of missing data methods exists, from traditional statistical approaches to modern machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) algorithms, their comparative performance under different missingness mechanisms remains insufficiently understood. In this study, we provide an empirical evaluation of eight methods for handling missing covariates, including complete-case analysis, multiple imputation, ML-based techniques, and DL frameworks such as multilayer perceptrons, variational autoencoders, and generative adversarial networks. We summarize their conceptual foundations, practical implementation considerations, strengths, and limitations. Through extensive simulations, we assess estimation bias, efficiency, standard error accuracy, confidence interval coverage, and computational burden across different missingness mechanisms and proportions. An application to the Vanderbilt Memory and Aging Project further illustrates the implications of different methods for regression analysis in a real-world biomedical setting. Finally, we provide practical recommendations for method selection and highlight remaining challenges that warrant future investigation.
#6 A Model of Diverse Sampling from Language Models
著者: Manuel Prada-Corral, Yahya Emara, Timothy J. O'Donnell, Ryan Cotterell, Tim Vieira
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25003
要約:
Language models often produce high-quality individual samples but poor sets, with repeated samples clustering around the same semantic modes. We formalize diverse generation as sampling a size-$K$ subset of complete strings from a determinantal point process (DPP), where the likelihood encodes item quality, and the embedding geometry encodes repulsion between similar outputs. Exact inference over all strings is intractable, so we propose PoolDPP: draw a finite candidate pool from a tractable proposal, importance-weight the candidates toward the target quality, and run a $K$-DPP sampler on the induced pool kernel; a greedy MAP variant provides deterministic selection. We prove a finite-sample bound with an explicit constant: the KL divergence between the sampler's output and the target $K$-DPP decays as O(1/N) in the pool size. Empirically, PoolDPP extends the quality--diversity frontier on open-ended generation and the precision--recall frontier on ambiguous text-to-SQL (Ambrosia), and on mathematical reasoning (GSM8K), it raises diversity at about the same pass@$K$.
#7 Near-Quadratic Sample Compression for All VC Classes
著者: Yang Cai, Vineet Gupta, Yanchen Jiang, Christopher Liaw, Aranyak Mehta, Shay Moran, Grigoris Velegkas, Di Wang, Manfred K. Warmuth
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25011
要約:
The sample compression conjecture is a longstanding open problem in learning theory, originating in the work of Littlestone and Warmuth in the 1980s. At its heart is a connection between simplification and generalization: if a predictor consistent with a training sample is defined using only a small part of that sample, then it is likely to generalize. Sample compression schemes formalize this idea, and many natural learning algorithms can be analyzed this way. The sample compression conjecture of Floyd and Warmuth (1995) asserts that every binary concept class of VC dimension $d$ admits a sample compression scheme of size $O(d)$; a weaker conjecture asks only for size polynomial in $d$. Both have remained open: the first bound depending only on $d$, due to Moran and Yehudayoff (2016), is exponential in $d$. We prove the weaker conjecture by constructing a sample compression scheme of size $O(d^2 \log^2 d)$.
#8 HapBO: Harnessed $\alpha$-$\pi$ Bayesian Optimization via Expert Natural Language Priors
著者: Jean-S\'ebastien Giroux, Rose Guay Hottin, Marco Bonizzato
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25100
要約:
Bayesian Optimization (BO) can be substantially more sample-efficient when informed by prior knowledge about the problem. In practice, however, such knowledge is often available only in natural language, e.g., expert intuitions or documentation, rather than as the explicit probability distribution landscape that prior-guided BO methods may require. Leveraging it effectively demands both an expressive mapping from language to priors and robustness to information that may be misleading. We propose HapBO (Harnessed $\alpha$-$\pi$ Bayesian Optimization), which elicits beliefs once from a human expert, a large language model (LLM), or both, and deterministically compiles a structured specification into a reusable belief function ($\pi: \mathcal{X} \to[0,1]$), with a rich representation not restricted to a Gaussian family. Building on $\alpha$-$\pi$BO, HapBO applies this belief function through a bounded positive multiplier on any nonnegative acquisition, preserving positive acquisition support and requiring no LLM calls during optimization. We prove that, for the Expected Improvement acquisition function (EI), HapBO preserves the standard simple-regret rate up to a bounded factor, even under adversarial beliefs. We evaluate HapBO on 40 HPOBench tasks, a synthetic suite in 6-15 dimensions and two real-world applications: cortical neurostimulation and constrained solar design. HapBO achieves the lowest aggregate HPOBench regret and strong real-world task performance, with zero in-loop token cost. In real-world applications it improves the optimization outcome even at budgets of 12-20 queries, and at a small fraction of the wall-clock and token costs of other LLM-based methods.
#9 The low-degree sample complexity of clustering Gaussian mixtures with more groups than dimensions
著者: Alexandra Carpentier, Nicolas Verzelen
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25163
要約:
We study the computational limits of clustering $n$ observations drawn from a mixture of $K$ isotropic Gaussian distributions in $\mathbb R^d$, when the number $K$ of groups is much larger than the dimension $d$. In this regime, distance-based and spectral procedures require a squared separation $\Delta^2$ of order $\sqrt d$. In contrast, tensor-based procedures cluster at a polylogarithmic separation but need a sample size that is a large polynomial of $dK$, and the low-degree lower bounds known for random centers only show that $n$ must exceed $dK^2$. Focusing on normally distributed centers, we characterize the minimal separation for clustering with low-degree polynomials in the regime $K>d$, through a low-degree lower bound and a matching upper bound, up to subpolynomial factors. In particular, for $K=d^{p/2}$ with $p> 2$ a real number, we show that a sample size of the order of $K^3 d^{(\lceil p\rceil-p)/2}$ is required to recover the clustering with subpolynomial separation, and that it suffices when $p$ is not an integer.
#10 Variance Reduction for Matrix Computations with Applications to Gaussian Processes
著者: Anant Mathur, Sarat Moka, Zdravko Botev
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.14565
要約:
In addition to recent developments in computing speed and memory, methodological advances have contributed to significant gains in the performance of stochastic simulation. In this paper we focus on variance reduction for matrix computations via matrix factorization. We provide insights into existing variance reduction methods for estimating the entries of large matrices. Popular methods do not exploit the reduction in variance that is possible when the matrix is factorized. We show how computing the square root factorization of the matrix can achieve in some important cases arbitrarily better stochastic performance. In addition, we detail a factorized estimator for the trace of a product of matrices and numerically demonstrate that the estimator can be up to 1,000 times more efficient on certain problems of estimating the log-likelihood of a Gaussian process. Additionally, we provide a new estimator of the log-determinant of a symmetric positive-definite matrix where the log-determinant is treated as a normalizing constant of a probability density.
#11 Survival of the fittest Cox model: Pivotal variable selection for time-to-event data
著者: Maxime van Cutsem, Sylvain Sardy
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19374
要約:
We revisit Cox's proportional hazards model to improve variable selection in survival analysis. A square-root transformation of the partial likelihood renders the selection of the regularization parameter asymptotically pivotal: the baseline hazard and the censoring rate cancel exactly, and the way censoring is spread over time only enters through a bounded correction of order $1/\log n$. Using martingale residuals, we compute this penalty level by Monte Carlo without reordering the design matrix. The resulting criterion borrows from information criteria such as BIC and from penalized regression methods such as the lasso, taking the best of both. On simulated and real data, our method substantially improves upon state-of-the-art approaches used daily.
#12 Sparse corruption in low-rank matrix inference: the PCA benchmark
著者: Urte Adomaityte, Gabriele Sicuro, Pierpaolo Vivo
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11927
要約:
Principal Component Analysis (PCA) is a standard tool for extracting a low-rank signal from noisy observations. It is known that when applying PCA to a rank-one signal corrupted by a dense, homogeneous noise, in the large matrix size limit, the BBP transition occurs, where the emergence of an outlying eigenvalue and the alignment of the corresponding eigenvector occur at the same critical signal strength. Here we study the case of sparse noise corruption. The noise matrix is modelled as the adjacency matrix of a weighted undirected graph with finite average connectivity. Using the replica method, we analytically compute the typical top eigenvalue, the top eigenvector component density, and the overlap with the signal, through recursive distributional equations solved by population dynamics. We identify two signal-strength transitions as functions of graph connectivity: $\theta_{\rm crit}$, marking signal recovery by the top eigenvector and generalising the BBP transition, and $\theta_{\rm b}$, where the signal-related eigenvalue detaches from the bulk. For noise with nonzero mean, these transitions need not coincide because of a structural sparse-graph outlier, leading to a discontinuous transition in the overlap with the top eigenvector. For a Random Regular degree distribution, we obtain exact closed-form expressions for both thresholds, the signal eigenvalue and the squared overlap, and show that the same formulas also predict the overlap of the signal with the second eigenvector in the intermediate regime $\theta_{\rm b}<\theta\leq \theta_{\rm crit}$. For a Poissonian degree distribution, an approximate closure of the recursions yields fast and accurate predictions in the recovery phase that agree with exact results from population dynamics. We also recover dense-noise results in the large-connectivity limit, and validate the theory by numerical diagonalisation of large matrices.
#13 Anytime-Valid Conformal Risk Control
著者: Bror Hultberg, Dave Zachariah, Ant\^onio H. Ribeiro
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04364
要約:
Prediction sets provide a means of quantifying the uncertainty in predictive tasks. Using held out calibration data, conformal prediction and risk control can produce prediction sets that exhibit statistically valid error control in a computationally efficient manner. However, in the standard formulations, the error is only controlled on average over many possible calibration datasets of fixed size. In this paper, we extend the control to remain valid with high probability over a cumulatively growing calibration dataset at any time point. We derive such guarantees using quantile-based arguments and illustrate the applicability of the proposed framework to settings involving distribution shift. We further show that our guarantees are asymptotically tight, in the sense that the risks converge to the target level from below, and that the rate of our correction cannot be improved. Finally, we demonstrate the practical performance of our methods through both simulations and real-world numerical examples.
#14 Wedge Sampling: Efficient Tensor Completion with Nearly-Linear Sample Complexity
著者: Hengrui Luo, Anna Ma, Ludovic Stephan, Yizhe Zhu
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05869
要約:
We introduce wedge sampling, a non-adaptive sampling scheme for recovering an order-$k$ low-rank tensor of side length $n$ from structured observations of its entries. The scheme samples pairs of entries in the same column of a tensor unfolding, forming length-two paths (wedges) in the associated bipartite sampling graph. The products of the paired entries supply the row correlations needed for spectral initialization in regimes where uniform sampling yields too few wedges. For fixed rank, incoherence, tensor order, and condition number, wedge sampling enables polynomial-time recovery with nearly linear sample complexity in $n$. The spectral initializer can be combined with existing refinement procedures using only an additional $\tilde O(n)$ uniformly sampled entries. Spectral refinement achieves weak recovery to any fixed relative Frobenius error. For symmetric third-order tensors with incoherent canonical polyadic factors, gradient descent achieves exact recovery in the noiseless setting, with error decreasing geometrically. The improvement over the $\tilde O(n^{k/2})$ sample complexity typical of efficient methods under uniform entry sampling shows how the conjectured computational barrier depends on the observation model. A key ingredient for the gradient descent analysis is a concentration bound for sparse random tensors in an incoherent spectral norm. We also give error bounds for both procedures under additive Gaussian noise and explicit signal-to-noise conditions. Beyond tensor completion, the same sampling scheme improves the sample complexity of one-sided matrix completion, where the goal is to recover the left singular subspace of a matrix with many more columns than rows.
#15 Continuous-time learning of probability distributions: a case study in a digital trial of young children with type 1 diabetes
著者: Antonio \'Alvarez-L\'opez, Marcos Matabuena
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24427
要約:
Understanding how biomarker distributions evolve over time is a central challenge in digital health and chronic disease monitoring. For example, in diabetes, changes in the distribution of glucose measurements can reveal patterns of disease progression and treatment response that conventional summary measures miss. Motivated by a 13-week clinical trial comparing the closed-loop insulin delivery system t:slim X2 with standard therapy in children with type 1 diabetes, we propose a probabilistic framework to model the continuous-time evolution of time-indexed distributions using continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) data collected every five minutes. We represent the glucose distribution as a Gaussian mixture, with time-varying mixture weights governed by a neural ODE. We estimate the model parameters using a distribution-matching criterion based on the maximum mean discrepancy. The resulting framework is interpretable, computationally efficient, and sensitive to subtle temporal distributional changes. In the CGM trial data analyzed here, the method detects treatment-related improvements in glucose dynamics that are difficult to capture with traditional analytical approaches.
#16 Learning Curves and Prediction Persistence to Under-Regularization of Spectral Algorithms in Large Dimensions
著者: Weihao Lu, Qian Lin, Yingcun Xia, Dongming Huang
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23212
要約:
We study how prediction risk changes as regularization is reduced for spectral algorithms when the sample size $n$ and dimension $d$ satisfy $n\asymp d^\gamma$ for fixed $\gamma>0$. For inner-product kernels on the sphere, under a source condition with smoothness $s\geq0$ and a signal nondegeneracy condition, we characterize the conditional excess risk up to constant factors at each fixed admissible scale $\lambda\asymp d^{-u}$, $u>0$, and at interpolation. The resulting risk orders describe the effects of source smoothness, filter qualification, and transitions between spectral blocks. We also compare the kernel risk with a population sequence risk for the same spectral filter, using a deterministic regularization scale bounded below by order $n^{-1}$. Finally, for noninteger $\gamma$, we extend the risk characterization for kernel ridge regression to kernels on general domains under spectral scaling, hyper-contractivity, and tail diagonal concentration conditions, together with the source and signal conditions.
#17 Gradient Regularized Newton Boosting Trees with Global Convergence
著者: Nikita Zozoulenko, Daniel Falkowski, Thomas Cass, Lukas Gonon
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00581
要約:
Gradient Boosting Decision Trees (GBDTs) dominate tabular machine learning, with modern implementations like XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost being based on Newton boosting: a second-order descent step in the space of decision trees. Despite its empirical success, the global convergence of Newton boosting is poorly understood compared to first-order boosting. In this paper, we introduce Restricted Newton Descent, a framework for convex optimization with Newton's method on Hilbert spaces with inexact iterates, based on the concepts of cosine angle and weak gradient edge. Within this framework, we recover Newton boosting with GBDTs and classical finite-dimensional theory as special cases. We first prove that vanilla Newton boosting achieves a linear rate of convergence for smooth, strongly convex losses that satisfy a Hessian-dominance condition. To handle general convex losses with Lipschitz Hessians, we extend a recent gradient regularized Newton scheme to the restricted weak learner setting, establishing the first global $\mathcal{O}(\frac{1}{k^2})$ convergence rate for second-order GBDTs. This scheme minimally modifies the classical algorithm by introducing an adaptive $\ell_2$-regularization term proportional to the square root of the gradient norm at each iteration. In numerical experiments, we show that this scheme converges while vanilla Newton boosting may diverge.
#18 Survey-aware Machine Learning: A Guideline for Valid Population Health Inference based on Scoping Review

Machine Learning (ML) models trained on complex health surveys such as the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) often ignore primary sampling units, stratification variables, and sampling weights. This practice violates the independence assumptions of standard evaluation methods. As a result, estimates become biased, uncertainty is underestimated, and fairness assessments fail to reflect population-level disparities. We propose Survey-aware Machine Learning (SaML), a nine-step guideline that incorporates survey design metadata across the ML lifecycle. SaML provides a checklist for valid population inference from survey data.
#19 Adaptive Inference for Resource-Constrained Dynamic Pricing

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03736
要約:
We study dynamic pricing over a finite selling horizon when limited resource capacity determines revenue and the observations available for inference at a prespecified price. Resource depletion can remove the target neighborhood from the feasible price set, changing the experiment generated by the pricing policy. We develop inference-aware re-solving controllers that check target-band feasibility before current covariates arrive and log the pricing mixture. Target-reserved and smooth controllers take population mean-pair geometry as a predeployment input; learned barycentric re-solving instead estimates stationary mean-consumption vectors of predeclared component kernels. Boundary results show when physical support is lost and why a $1/t$ target branch yields only $O_p(1)$ information if it is the sole target-local source. The policy reports an interval when its prespecified support and information conditions hold and otherwise abstains.
#20 Agnostic Smoothed Online Regression with Adversarial Responses
著者: Xuanyu Chen, Yue Yu
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32478
要約:
We study smoothed online prediction with bounded adversarial responses. This widely studied framework bridges i.i.d. sampling and adversarial covariate selection through a smoothness parameter $\textsf{C}_{\textsf{cov}}$, which bounds conditional covariate densities relative to a fixed, unknown base measure. We propose Hedge-Cover, an information-theoretic algorithm that achieves sublinear regret $\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{\text{Pdim}(\mathcal{F}) \textsf{C}_{\textsf{cov}} T})$ for function classes with bounded pseudo-dimension. The algorithm aggregates a carefully constructed family of experts using Hedge, with a prior that links regret to the number of disagreements between a consistent selector and a target function. We bound this number by exploiting covariate smoothness. This answers an open problem posed in \cite{blanchard2025agnostic} on the minimax optimal adaptive regret of the smoothed online regression problem. We establish a matching lower bound for the class of linear predictors. The main intricacy of the lower bound lies in explicitly constructing a challenging sequential covariate distribution supported on mutually orthogonal hyperplanes. This construction may be of independent technical interest. Finally, we revisit the well-specified setting and quantify the effect of response noise. For conditionally $\nu^2$-subGaussian responses, we extend the existing lower bound under realizable responses by showing that the minimax expected regret is $\Omega((1\vee \nu)\sqrt{(\textsf{C}_{\textsf{cov}}-1)dT})$ for a function class of VC dimension $d$. A corresponding upper bound for ERM matches this dependence on $\nu$.
#21 Prediction with Foundation Models under Deployment Budgets
著者: Zhaohui Xu, Xiaofei Xu, Doudou Zhou
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12466
要約:
Foundation models can supply predictions for a downstream task that draw on knowledge from extensive pretraining, but they differ in task-specific accuracy and query cost. We develop a framework that improves prediction for the task under a budget on the average query cost per input, using training data to learn which models to query and how to combine their outputs with features available without querying. The first method queries the same models for every input; the second lets them vary across inputs and uses Q-learning to decide which model to query next and when to stop. For the first method, we show that several individually inaccurate outputs can jointly outperform the best single one.
