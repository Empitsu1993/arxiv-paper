#1 Physical Layer Anomaly Detection via Adaptive Gaussian Mixture Model
著者: Mamady Delamou, Luan Chen, Arsenia Chorti
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18946
要約:
Physical-layer spoofing attacks pose a critical threat to wireless localization systems by manipulating channel state information (CSI). Detecting such attacks becomes particularly challenging when the attacker and the legitimate node are spatially proximate, as their CSI characteristics become increasingly similar. To address this challenge in indoor scenarios, we propose an adaptive Gaussian mixture model (GMM)-based detection framework that learns legitimate CSI patterns and detects spatial inconsistency through statistical anomaly detection. Using a massive multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) experimental dataset with 64 uniform linear array (ULA) antenna and 5 mm spatial resolution, we evaluated our detector against CSI spoofing attacks with spatial displacements ranging from 50 mm to 450 mm. The adaptive GMM achieves 95-98% detection rate for displacements above 150 mm while maintaining 1% false alarm rate. We benchmark our approach against conventional GMM, local outlier factor (LOF), one-class support vector machine (SVM) and k-nearest neighbors (k-NN) classifiers, demonstrating overall superior detection performance of our proposed classifier.
#2 Limits of Statistical Assurance for Embodied and Agentic AI under Adaptive Adversaries

著者: Inderjeet Singh
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18948
要約:
Finite safety assessments cannot exclude an adversary that preserves unattacked behavior until certification and activates afterward. We characterize this limitation for embodied and tool-use AI. An adversary that matches the complete observation law until observable, irrevocable certification and retains intervention access afterward attains $v\mathbb{E}[(n-S_0)_+]$ expected post-certification events over $n$ trials, where $v$ is the active event probability and $S_0$ the unattacked certification time. The result covers adaptive and privately randomized evaluators. Switching at certification is exactly optimal for a fixed likelihood-ratio test under memoryless, event-increasing interventions without a binding intervention budget. A positive clean-certification requirement forces linear worst-case expected excess when intervention raises event rates over an authorized deployment horizon, even if certification is deferred to the deadline. In simulated RoCo collaboration, switching yields commanded-separation violations in 34 of 36 post-certification episodes. Preregistered randomized AgentDojo trials estimate 1.98 additional security violations per four-task continuation (95% interval [1.67, 2.29]). An adversarial language-model controller infers hidden certification from noisy evaluation receipts and times intervention accordingly. With permanent revocation, a mixture likelihood-ratio monitor bounds cumulative excess above a prespecified reference rate at square-root order, up to logarithmic factors, on every sample path. A single fixed alternative can permit linear expected excess under stationary attacks. Against a controller observing the monitor's rule and statistic, live mixture monitoring reduces violations from 12.00 to 3.81 per 24 assigned task opportunities. Revocation also reduces tasks executed.
#3 CyberArena: Benchmarking AI Agents in Live Symmetric Attack-and-Defense Combat

公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19005
要約:
AI agents are demonstrating increasingly strong offensive and defensive cybersecurity capabilities in the real world. Current benchmarks mostly evaluate agents in isolation against static targets. Real cyber operations, however, are dynamic and coupled: security postures constantly change as attackers and defenders adapt to each other in real time. Static benchmarks quickly saturate and miss an agent's ability to attack and defend simultaneously under live pressure. To address this gap, we present CyberArena, a dynamic, symmetric adversarial benchmark built around multi-round attack-and-defense combat. In isolated sandboxes, two agents compete under identical rules, acting concurrently as both attackers and defenders over continuously changing services. This converts static targets into evolving, semi-open challenges, directly testing an agent's speed and precision in discovering, exploiting, and patching vulnerabilities. To sustain the benchmark's longevity, an automated agentic pipeline continuously generates, reviews, and validates new environments. Furthermore, because pairwise contests are notoriously hard to quantify, we introduce a dynamical model that maps match dynamics into unified, multidimensional scores across offensive, defensive, and domain-specific capabilities. Evaluating 12 frontier LLM agents across 40 challenges in 466 matches, CyberArena separates model capabilities where single-agent baselines hit a ceiling. The trajectories read like a fight rather than an exam: defenders patch one hole and miss the next and certify repairs on stale evidence, while capable agents think a move ahead, planting backdoors for later rounds and, in one case, writing their own packet sniffer to watch the opponent. Beyond a benchmark, CyberArena serves as an adaptable, scalable experimental platform for future research on cybersecurity agents.
#4 Combining Post-Quantum Cryptography and Quantum Key Distribution: Taxonomy, Constructions, Proofs, Standards, Deployments and Open Problems
著者: Mona Alotaibi, Noora Fetais, Saif Al-Kuwari
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19039
要約:
Post-quantum cryptography (PQC) and quantum key distribution (QKD) address the quantum threat from opposite directions: PQC replaces vulnerable public-key algorithms with schemes believed to resist quantum cryptanalysis, while QKD derives keys whose secrecy rests on physical assumptions rather than computational ones. Because each approach has a distinct failure mode, a growing body of work combines them so that a session key remains secure if either component holds. We first establish the deployed baseline, hybrid classical-plus-PQC key exchange, against which QKD-augmented designs must be judged. We then propose a taxonomy of hybrid PQC-QKD designs along eight dimensions (integration layer, combiner construction, authentication scope and source, QKD topology, trusted entities, key-delivery interface, classical component, and evidence type), and use it to compare published protocols, including Muckle and its successors, MACsec and TLS integrations, and IKEv2 mechanisms standardised in RFCs 8784, 9370, and 9867. We examine key-combiner constructions in light of the KEM-combiner literature and of NIST, ETSI, and ITU-T guidance, and map the security arguments for hybrid PQC-QKD protocols onto a common grid of security model, assumptions, permitted compromise, established guarantee, and limitations. We synthesise deployment evidence from metropolitan and national QKD networks, distinguishing the few testbeds that have run PQC in the key path from pure-QKD deployments, and summarise the standards and policy landscape, including the positions of national security agencies that recommend against relying on QKD alone. We close with open problems: authentication of the QKD classical channel, shared-infrastructure threat models, device certification versus formal proofs, formal verification of hybrid handshakes, and the lack of peer-reviewed deployment economics.

要約:
Agent skills are Markdown documents that a tool-using agent reads as instructions and then acts on with the privileges of the session that loaded them. Installing one grants authority (the standing to direct the agent, not new privilege), and the size of that grant is what we measure. We present a two-phase attack-surface profiling methodology that combines whole-bundle static analysis and dependency provenance checking with instrumented sandbox execution and separate model-based assessment of the resulting traces. We apply the methodology to 6,309 listings from two marketplaces, selecting listings for execution without conditioning that decision on a prior threat or risk signal. Declaring is also not declaring accurately: 59% of declaring listings understate what their bundle can do. ClawHub declares roughly twice as often as skillsmp, yet under-declares on 88% of its declarations against 31%. Triage at the marketplace boundary therefore cannot rest on what a listing declares. It must rest on what the bundle can do. These results position capability-oriented attack-surface profiling as a necessary complement to maliciousness detection.
#6 TrackShield: An Efficient Runtime Defense Against Availability Attacks on High-Performance Real-Time Multi-Object Tracking

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19278
要約:
High-performance camera-based perception pipelines must sustain per-frame real-time budgets on resource-constrained embedded GPU platforms. Availability attacks such as SlowTrack inflate the persistent state of multi-object trackers: data-association workload accumulates across frames, so per-frame latency increases and drives the pipeline past its deadline. Detector- and NMS-layer defenses do not directly control this persistent tracker-side resource. We present TrackShield, a lightweight runtime resource-management layer that protects the tracker at the point where attack-induced workload accumulates. TrackShield combines low-overhead anomaly monitoring with staged birth capping, pruning of young or weakly matched tracks, and per-stage association-input admission control. As an availability defense, TrackShield focuses on keeping the tracker responsive under attack instead of recovering the full tracking accuracy degraded by adversarial detections.

要約:
Privacy-preserving machine learning has become critical for deploying artificial intelligence in sensitive domains such as healthcare, finance, and infrastructure monitoring. Homomorphic encryption (HE) enables secure inference on encrypted data. Despite its advantages, HE is not compatible with nonlinear activation functions found in machine learning models. Existing methods mitigate this problem by manually designing polynomial approximations for these activation functions. However, these functions do not account for the differences in dataset characteristics or model architecture. Furthermore, these functions are also limited to the accuracies of convolutional neural network (CNN) models. In this work, we propose an autonomous framework that selects polynomial activation functions tailored to each dataset and model. In addition, latency analysis reveals that the proposed CLS token based inference pipeline significantly reduces computational overhead by eliminating depth-dependent operations, shifting the primary cost to encryption and decryption. These findings demonstrate that the proposed method not only adapts polynomial activations across architectures, but also enables efficientencrypted inference through both activation design and pipeline optimization.

#10 What You Sign Is Not What You Run: The Verifiability Gap in Quantized LLM Supply Chains
著者: Wei-Liang Liu, Cheng-Yen Tsai
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19498
要約:
Model signing authenticates the full-precision weights W an LLM is released with, yet users run a quantized artifact Q whose producing transformation is not uniquely determined under current disclosure. We separate the non-environment inputs from the execution environment E and measure a quantity that, to our knowledge, has not been measured before: the Independent Verifiability Rate (IVR), the fraction of a popularity-sampled GGUF ecosystem that a third party can re-derive bit-for-bit. Across the 200 most-downloaded Hugging Face GGUF repositories (198 in scope), no artifact or model card records the build environment, so under a recorded-inputs protocol the IVR is 0/198. Yet the artifacts are not irreproducible. On four in-sample repositories that disclose their inputs, the card's information alone reproduces no Q4_K_M file, but two coordinates the card omits - the build's floating-point-contraction setting and, for hybrid models, the per-tensor quantization recipe - re-derive 7/8 target-quant files bit-for-bit on x86 (5/8 on arm64); the fp16 re-derives from the provider's checkpoint, but the calibration imatrix must be shipped and hash-bound, since recomputing it did not match. An older release additionally pins the instruction set and did not re-derive. Reproducing the divergence reported with llama.cpp PR #25353, we show that E is a necessary coordinate and that one compiler setting yields a unique output across the tested platforms at negligible quality cost (+0.06% perplexity). As a security characterization, benign floating-point non-determinism separates from uniformly placed nibble-flip tampering above a detection floor that grows with the environments a verifier must allow, but a targeted embedding edit evades the statistic, so the proposed protocol rests on exact re-derivation; adversarial-fp16 attacks lie outside it. We release the audit data and a re-runnable harness.

Autonomous drones are increasingly deployed in safety-critical tasks, yet their autopilot software can still contain subtle logic flaws that lead to unsafe behaviors. With recent advances in LLMs, we observe that, when equipped with essential domain knowledge and carefully crafted context, LLMs demonstrate strong capabilities in understanding the high-level semantics of autopilot systems, making them suitable for logic flaw discovery. 要約:

Prior work (TabFIDS) showed that FedAvg combined with bootstrapping and temporal averaging improves transferability for cross-device NIDS.
