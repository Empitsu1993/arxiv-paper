#1 Ties at the Top: A Point-in-Time Comparison of CVSS and EPSS at a Fixed Budget
著者: Gianluca Martinuz, Paolo Ceravolo
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24983
要約:
Organizations must decide which vulnerabilities warrant further analysis and protective action when the volume of published vulnerabilities exceeds their analytical capacity. We present a methodology for comparing vulnerability-prioritization strategies under an explicit resource constraint, represented by a fixed number of CVEs selected from a common eligible population. We apply this methodology to the Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS) and the Exploit Prediction Scoring System (EPSS). For CVEs published in the National Vulnerability Database (NVD) from 17 March to 31 December 2025 and from 1 January to 31 March 2026 (Q1), we reconstruct each CVE's scores and status 30 days after NVD publication, using archived source data. Each cohort has one ranking per strategy at a 5% budget. Positives are CVEs added to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog within 180 days after their decision day. The catalog is maintained by the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA). EPSS captures 16 of 40 positives in 2025, versus 5 for CVSS; alternative selections among tied CVEs cannot raise CVSS capture above 9. In Q1, CVSS captures 4 of 11 and EPSS 3, but tie-breaking can reverse this ordering. The score alone leaves most of each CVSS list undecided: the tie-breaking rule allocates 1,678 of 1,919 CVSS places in 2025 and 589 of 758 in Q1 among CVEs tied at score 9.8. Most chosen CVSS scores come from non-NVD providers; disabling those scores reduces CVSS capture from 5 to 1 in 2025 and from 4 to 0 in Q1. A fixed-budget comparison should therefore report its decision cutoff, eligible population, score provenance, and tie-breaking rule.
#2 Signed, Valid, and Unauthorized: Authority Closure at the Trust Boundary Between Agent Commerce Protocols and Crypto Wallets
agent
著者: Shengwei You, Aditya Joshi, Andrey Kuehlkamp, Jarek Nabrzyski
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24989
要約:
Agent commerce protocols let software agents pay with stablecoins. A buyer approves a payee, asset, spending cap and time window, but the wallet signs an object whose effects depend on the contracts that consume it. Matching the displayed fields or simulating one payment can miss transfers that the signed object still permits. We define authority closure as the set of transfer sequences that holding the signed object can enable. A wallet object respects the mandate when every such sequence meets the buyer's constraints. We characterize when wallet objects can satisfy this property and implement Closure, a wallet-side checker covering 13 families of signed objects. In an evaluation of 2,130 generated objects and 62,979 execution programs, 265 objects with an observed violation passed both a field-and-expiry check and an address allow-list. Under the test model, Closure rejected all 1,680 objects with an observed violation and accepted the remaining 450. In two local checkouts using the x402 reference client, approved payments settled and Closure refused requests outside the mandate before signing. The median decision time was at most 12 microseconds per family.
#3 Attacks and Defenses in Decentralized Multi-Agent Systems with Hop-by-Hop Task Decomposition and Delegation
agent
著者: Matthew D. Laws, Maria Lazou, Alina Oprea, Cristina Nita-Rotaru
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25056
要約:
Decentralized multi-agent LLM systems enable independently administered agents, each with its own operator, model, memory, and configuration, to collaborate on complex tasks without centralized coordination. Agents autonomously decompose tasks, execute subtasks and delegate remaining work to peers, enabling scalability and eliminating single points of failure. However, decentralization also introduces significant security challenges, as no single entity has complete visibility into task execution or the authority to enforce correct behavior across agents. In this paper, we systematize threats from malicious agents in decentralized multi-agent systems and propose mitigation techniques. We first design and evaluate attacks against integrity, availability, and confidentiality, demonstrating that a single malicious agent can cause up to 100% task failure, inflate token consumption and runtime by up to 7.7x and 3.2x, respectively, and exfiltrate up to 94% of relevant prior tasks from benign agents. We then present a cryptographic protocol that produces an irrefutable verified execution trace of every action taken in the system and develop an auditor that analyzes these traces to detect and attribute malicious behavior. Evaluations on HiddenBench using gpt-6.1-sol and Qwen3.8-27B-FP8 show that our protocol incurs negligible latency overhead, while the auditor detects and correctly attributes 98% of attacks, including those that comply with the protocol.
#4 PriTabICL: Differentially Private Tabular In-Context Learning with Task-Aligned Cluster-Wise Synthesis
privacy
著者: Syed Ahzaz Shah, Evan Bramblett, H\'eber H. Arcolezi, Chen Gong, Qianqian Tong, Xiaochen Li
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25073
要約:
Tabular in-context learning (TabICL) enables large language models (LLMs) to predict target records using a handful of labeled demonstrations in their prompts. However, these demonstrations often contain sensitive personal information. Differential privacy (DP) offers principled protection, but noise-based methods introduce substantial noise, distorting the feature-label relationships essential for accurate prediction, especially as data dimensionality grows. In this work, we study DP data synthesis for generating privacy-preserving TabICL demonstrations. We identify two limitations of directly applying general-purpose DP synthesis to entire datasets: modeling a global distribution can obscure local structure and minority patterns in heterogeneous data, while measuring broad attribute dependencies under a limited privacy budget can reduce the accuracy available for relationships most relevant to prediction. More broadly, global statistical fidelity may fail to preserve heterogeneous structures needed by downstream predictive and generative models. To address these challenges, we propose PriTabICL, a general framework compatible with existing DP synthesis algorithms. It partitions records into DP clusters and synthesizes each independently, using a fixed feature-target workload to prioritize prediction-relevant statistical relationships. At inference, each query is routed to a DP cluster, and demonstrations are sampled from its synthetic dataset without additional privacy loss for the original records. Experiments on four real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of our methods across heterogeneous datasets. In particular, one of our instantiations, PriTabICL-PrivSyn, improves over global PrivSyn in 8 out of 12 settings and outperforms existing private TabICL approaches in nearly all evaluated settings. Open-source code is available.

#6 Mutable State in Enrich-by-Need Security Protocol Analysis
著者: Joshua D Guttman, Paul D Rowe, Eben Blaisdell
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25142
要約:
Security protocol analysis has emphasized message-passing, as this is how different principals interact in authentication, key exchange, and similar functionalities. However, in many protocols a principal also maintains mutable state that limits what actions its local sessions may take; this cross-session state is altered by other local sessions. In this paper we show how to represent this cross-session mutable state in the strand space approach to symbolic protocol analysis, and how to implement the resulting ideas in the Cryptographic Protocol Shapes Analyzer CPSA. The key theory is expressed in two axioms characterizing state, plus protocol-specific rules. It leads to tight, specific characterizations of protocol behavior in relevant cases, and aids in developing correct protocols relying on cross-session mutable state.

Modern vehicles collect extensive data about drivers, passengers, and vehicle use, with privacy and consent decisions increasingly mediated through infotainment software. Although dark patterns have been widely studied in web, mobile, and e-commerce interfaces, little is known about how they appear in automotive infotainment systems or how users perceive them. We study this problem through Android Automotive OS (AAOS), conducting a three-phase empirical analysis. First, 26 HCI and privacy annotators audit four OEM AAOS emulators (Honda, Volvo, GM, and Polestar), documenting 17 dark-pattern types across 65 observations. #8 Reject and Report: Forgery-Evident Undetectable Watermarks for Generative Image Models
intellectual property
著者: Yihan Wang, Haochen Sun, Xi He, Yaoliang Yu, N. Asokan
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25226
要約:
Provenance schemes such as those based on content watermarks let images from a generative model be traced back to it. A practical watermarking scheme must be accurate at a low false-positive rate (FPR), robust under content-preserving processing, and undetectable. In addition, it must resist forgery; otherwise, even from a single watermarked image and a surrogate model, an adversary can imprint the watermark onto an arbitrary image or regenerate new content that carries it, falsely attributing harmful content to a model or making authentic photographs appear AI-generated. We argue that a watermarking scheme should not only reject forgeries but also report them: a forgery rejected as unwatermarked leaves no trace of the attempt. State-of-the-art forgery-resilient undetectable watermarking schemes, such as SemBind, fall short on both counts. External anomaly detectors that do report forgeries either miss most of them or falsely flag genuine images after common processing. We propose Tripwire-WM, the first forgery-evident framework for undetectable watermarks. It commits the semantics of the generated image to the magnitudes of the initial latent noise, which the sign-based watermarks leave unused, through a distribution-preserving embedding; a forged image carries a valid watermark but with a commitment that does not match its content, so the detector both rejects it and reports it as forged. Built on the pseudorandom code (PRC) watermark and Gaussian Shading++, Tripwire-WM provably preserves their undetectability. On MS-COCO, with the owner's own model as the surrogate, it accepts no imprinting forgery, compared with up to 55.0% for SemBind, and reports all of them as forged; it also accepts at most 1.8% of reprompting forgeries, against up to 46.2% for SemBind. Meanwhile, it accepts at least 99% of genuine watermarked images at 10^{-6} FPR and matches the robustness of the base schemes.

A compromised cryptocurrency withdrawal backend can request signatures on unauthorized payments. Previously signed transactions may remain spendable after pause or become spendable again after a reorganization. Release-Bound Withdrawal Authorization (RBWA) orders permits against pause in a replicated journal and restores exposure when its closing evidence disappears from Bitcoin's active chain. An optional route delays large payouts and permits independent cold recovery. Direct payments confirm in one block on regtest, and staged payments confirm in three or seven at delays of two or six. #10 TRACE-5G: Trust-Aware Runtime Attestation and Continuous Enforcement for 5G User Equipment
著者: Al Nahian Bin Emran, Rajendra Paudyal, Rajendra Upadhyay, Arupjyoti Bhuyan, Duminda Wijesekera
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25272
要約:
The standard 5G Authentication and Key Agreement protocol (5G-AKA) authenticates subscriber credentials but does not verify the integrity of user equipment (UE). Existing literature includes admission-time device-certificate authentication and Trusted Platform Module (TPM)-based measured-boot attestation, which can additionally verify that the UE presents enrolled device credentials and boots into an approved state. However, these checks do not reveal whether harmful unauthorized software is executed after admission. We present TRACE-5G, which continuously verifies the UE's runtime software state and uses the resulting trust state to control network access. TRACE-5G retains the pre-admission certificate and boot checks, and in addition places the admitted UE in restricted connectivity while runtime integrity is verified. Linux Integrity Measurement Architecture (IMA) records runtime software measurements, TPM PCR (Platform Configuration Register) cryptographically accumulates them, and Keylime, a remote-attestation framework, repeatedly evaluates TPM and IMA evidence against enrolled references. A Trusted result grants full access on the existing PDU session, but an Untrusted result withholds or removes full access and causes network deregistration. We implement TRACE-5G on an srsRAN/Open5GS testbed with a hardware TPM2.0 UE and core-side firewall enforcement. Across ten trusted trials, Level 2 access was established in 9--12s. Across ten untrusted trials, promotion was consistently withheld; in a representative trace, restrictive policy was applied within 5ms of the Untrusted decision and UE-context release completed within 1.670s. TRACE-5G extends UE trust beyond admission without any modification of the standardized 5G protocols.
#11 Verified Netlist-to-RTL Recovery with LLM Agents
agent
著者: Ahmet Efe, Recep Bugra Uludag, Ulya R. Karpuzcu
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25324
要約:
Gate-level netlists circulate among IP integrators and foundries and can be extracted from chips. Reverse engineering them into readable register-transfer-level (RTL) code would help an adversary understand and modify a design, but remains difficult because word-level operations are scattered across many gates. Large language models (LLMs) can write and optimize RTL, but often fail to recover it from a whole netlist. We propose LOCA (LOgic Cone Attack), an LLM-based attack that divides an anonymized netlist into logic cones, has parallel agents rewrite them, and assembles compact RTL. Each cone contains the complete next-state logic of a group of related registers, giving an agent a small, meaningful task. Of five partitioning methods tested, only grouped cones lead to recovery. LOCA keeps only RTL proven equivalent to the netlist and measures its quality by compression. Function-preserving obfuscation does not stop LOCA on the tested designs, whereas unknown keys or hidden cell functions prevent it from proving recovery of the original design. Across 21 model-design pairs, LOCA recovers 17, versus 6 with whole-netlist prompting. Per-run recovery across models rises from 5% to 74%, at a median cost below one dollar with a low-cost model.
#12 SplitReAlign: Partition-Aware Representation Realignment for Split Federated Unlearning

公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500

要約:
Split federated learning (SFL) distributes neural networks across clients and a server, coupling class-specific knowledge across their representations. This coupling complicates split federated unlearning (SFU), where class-wise forgetting must remove target knowledge while preserving retained utility under heterogeneous representation spaces and asymmetric update constraints. We propose SplitReAlign, a partition-aware representation realignment framework for class-wise SFU. It constructs endpoint-specific discriminative subspaces coordinated through a shared semantic anchor, selectively realigning forgotten representations while penalizing changes outside the active subspaces. To accommodate asymmetric update constraints, SplitReAlign leverages gradients through frozen servers for Client-only updates and fixed client-feature statistics for Server-only guidance, while Joint updates optimize both partitions. Experiments on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 under heterogeneous client distributions demonstrate that SplitReAlign achieves the lowest average gap to retraining among all compared methods across six settings. Notably, Server-only achieves the closest agreement with retraining on both datasets, highlighting the effectiveness of partition-aware guidance under restricted updates.
#13 Braid: Sybil-Resistant Decentralized Identity with Trustless Key Recovery and Non-Transferable Anonymous Credentials
著者: Rui Song, Tianyu Zheng, Shang Gao, Guyue Li, Xue Liu, Bin Xiao
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.14679
要約:
Decentralized identity frameworks grant users full sovereignty over their digital assets in the Web3 ecosystem. However, allowing arbitrary creation of identifiers makes the system susceptible to Sybil attacks and puts assets at risk when keys are lost or compromised. Moreover, the lack of identification prevents anonymous credential schemes from deterring malicious transfers. Existing solutions link identifiers to entities through trusted intermediaries, which are not always accessible and require costly offline interactions. We introduce Braid, a decentralized identity system with Sybil resistance, trustless key recovery, and non-transferable anonymous credentials. Braid creates blockchain-based bindings between identifiers and gradually combines identifiers belonging to the same holder into a unified associated identifier. As all identifiers within an association are presumed to belong to one individual, any fraudulent activity can be detected. The association grows larger as interactions increase, substantially reducing the likelihood of successful Sybil attacks. This mechanism allows holders to recover identifiers with lost keys or partial key compromise by proving knowledge of specific association structures. Additionally, Braid prevents unauthorized transfers through blockchain-based identifier-key bindings and proofs of ownership for credentials. Compared with representative prior approaches that provide only campaign-scoped Sybil protection or stateless anonymous-credential presentation, Braid carries association state across applications, enabling progressive Sybil resistance and trustless recovery while preserving zero-knowledge credential presentation. The evaluation shows that Braid bridges privacy-preserving credentials and accountable identity, achieving identifier association and credential presentation times of 2.41s and 3.31s on consumer-grade devices.

著者: Lucien Martial, Alexis Rosio, Eleni Diamanti, Adrien Cassagne, Baptiste Gouraud
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02323
要約:
Continuous-variable quantum key distribution (CV-QKD) requires highly efficient reconciliation techniques to operate at low signal-to-noise ratios and long distances. Multidimensional reconciliation addresses this challenge by transforming the physical Gaussian quantum channel into a virtual binary-input additive white Gaussian noise (BIAWGN) channel, enabling the use of modern errorcorrecting codes. In this work, we review the principles of multidimensional reconciliation, with a particular focus on high-dimensional constructions beyond the algebraic dimensions 1, 2, 4, 8. We describe the construction of the virtual channel, discuss practical coding schemes for reverse reconciliation, and analyse their integration with linear error-correcting codes. We also present an opensource simulation framework, HDirac, implementing multidimensional reconciliation for arbitrary dimensions, and use it to evaluate state-of-the-art LDPC codes. The results highlight key trade-offs between dimension, reconciliation efficiency, and frame error rate, providing practical guidance for CV-QKD system design.
#39 Fool's Gold: Defensive Deception Against Safety-Removal Attacks on Open-Weight Models
著者: Mark Russinovich
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17202
要約:
Safety alignment in open-weight language models is trivially removable: abliteration projects a refusal-mediating direction out of the weights in minutes, and no release-time defense we are aware of prevents it durably. What cannot be prevented can be deceived. Our defense, decoy hardening ("Fool's Gold"), concedes the refusal strip and poisons its payoff: once refusal is stripped, most answers to hazardous operational requests are confident, fluent decoys whose critical elements are falsified. Decoys are trained inside a differentiable simulation of the attack, expressing only in the attacked state; a refusal pin and benign leash hold clean-state behavior to the original. We instantiate it on seven models from five families (9B-122B, dense and mixture-of-experts). On the six models passing our pre-registered efficacy gate, 0.51-0.90 of attacked-state responses to held-out prompts are decoys, +0.27-0.84 attributable to the defense; all six stay within registered benign-behavior and capability budgets; the seventh (smaller) fails the gate (boundary case). Rates replicate on a frozen test split or untouched strata. The claim is epistemic: without independent ground truth, no observation surface we tested separates falsified answers from correct ones - on external red-team benchmarks' CBRNE-adjacent slice, the defended 122B is fatally wrong on 0.82-0.86 of matched-quality answers vs at most 0.10 undefended. Repeated sampling does not restore trust: element-wise consensus at K=64 reconstructs a fully usable procedure on 0.083-0.625 of prompts where the instrument validates, vs 0.58-0.96 undefended, with no label-free way to tell the regimes apart; on the weakest such model the claim is per-draw only. We evaluate chemical and biological hazards; the defense does not address in-context jailbreaks and protects only the initially released defended weights.
