#1 Waldschmidt constants and invariant geometry for exceptional rank-four complex reflection groups
著者: Krishna Hanumanthu, Jack Huizenga, Jake Kettinger, Lukasz Merta, Tomasz Szemberg, Justyna Szpond
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13108
要約:
We compute the Waldschmidt constants of the point configurations dual to the reflection arrangements of the exceptional complex reflection groups $G_{29}$, $G_{31}$ and $G_{32}$. They equal $3$ for the forty points of $G_{29}$, $36/11$ for the sixty-point Klein configuration, and $10/3$ for the forty-point Penrose configuration. Together with the known cases $W(F_4)$ and $W(H_4)$, this completes the computation for all exceptional irreducible complex reflection groups of rank four. An invariant dodecic and its sixfold orbit product give extremal surfaces for the first two configurations, and we identify the six dodecics as pullbacks of the coordinate hyperplane sections of the Segre cubic under the Igusa-Segre duality. The matching lower bounds follow from restriction to quadrics and distinguished planes, including a weighted B\'ezout estimate arising from two related Hesse arrangements. As a consequence, we determine the asymptotic resurgences of the three ideals.
#2 Reciprocal mirror map derivatives, euler-twisted Gromov-Witten invariants, and instanton counts via quantum Serre duality
著者: Irit Huq-Kuruvilla, Michael T. Schultz
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13126
要約:
We study mirror map derivatives as a means of relating the genus zero Gromov-Witten theories of an anticanonical Calabi-Yau hypersurface in a smooth, almost minimal Fano variety and the local Calabi-Yau formed from the total space of the Fano's canonical bundle with the euler-twisted theory of the ambient Fano. A precise relationship between reciprocal mirror map derivatives is obtained from the non-equivariant limit of quantum Serre duality for the euler-twisted theory of the ambient Fano as formulated by Iritani, Mann, & Mignon. This allows us to express the generating series of 3-point euler-twisted invariants as the derivative of the local mirror map with respect to the closed one. In low dimensions, this series is shown to be a modular form of Eisenstein type which has arisen before in this area. We prove integrality of the twisted invariants from this identification. The regularized small quantum $D$-module of the Fano is reducible, and we show it is equivalent to an inhomogeneous Picard-Fuchs equation governed by this generating series. The reciprocal mirror map derivative is then shown to be a generating function of local 3-point invariants, which recovers some known results for local Calabi-Yau threefolds and proves a conjecture of the second named author and A. Malmendier in previous work on local fourfolds. Explicit calculations for $\mathbb{P}^2,\mathbb{P}^3$ and $\mathbb{P}^4$ illustrate these relationships, which includes the vanishing of positive-degree ordinary genus-zero invariants for anticanonical elliptic curves and K3 surfaces, the recovery of the quintic threefold's instanton numbers, and finally some formulas for genus-zero invariants of local projective spaces.
#3 The Cayley-Bedregal reduction for Milnor and Hirzebruch-Milnor classes
著者: R. Callejas-Bedregal, F. Caraballo, M. F. Z. Morgado, J. Seade
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13267
要約:
The Cayley--Bedregal reduction replaces a complete intersection $\{f_1=\dots=f_r=0\}$ by the hypersurface $\sum_i\varphi_if_i=0$ in the projectivized dual bundle. For an isolated complete intersection singularity of corank $c$, we express the Milnor class of this hypersurface on its singular fibre $\mathbb P^{c-1}$ in terms of the Milnor numbers of generic systems obtained by taking linear combinations of the defining equations. Its degree is the original Milnor number, and the higher components recover the Milnor numbers of the systems with fewer equations. These numbers are invariants of the analytic germ. Our second main result concerns the Hodge-theoretic refinement. For a complete intersection defined by a regular section of a rank-$r$ algebraic vector bundle, the Hirzebruch--Milnor class is determined by that of the Cayley--Bedregal hypersurface: the push-forward of the normalized Hirzebruch--Milnor class of the hypersurface, in the sense of Cappell, Maxim, Sch\"urmann and Shaneson, is $(-y)^{r-1}$ times that of the complete intersection, with no positivity assumption on the bundle. The factor $(-y)^{r-1}$ comes from the motivic decomposition of the projective bundle, and at $y=-1$ the identity recovers Aluffi's comparison of Milnor classes. When the tautological bundle is very ample, the hypersurface formula of Maxim, Saito and Sch\"urmann then gives the Hirzebruch--Milnor class of the complete intersection through the vanishing cycles of a single function, avoiding their induction through intermediate complete intersections and their restriction to powers of one line bundle. In integral homology we prove the analytic form of Aluffi's identity, and obtain formulas for the Milnor class in terms of local Milnor fibres and for the Chern--Schwartz--MacPherson class of any projective scheme.
#4 On Poincar\'e-Type Inequalities for Flags of Distributions
著者: Antonio M. Ferreira, Miguel Rodr\'iguez Pe\~na, Fernando Louren\c{c}o
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13349
要約:
In this paper, we establish enumerative formulas and derive geometric inequalities for flags of holomorphic distributions on complex manifolds. First, given a codimension-one holomorphic distribution $\mathcal{G}$ on a complex manifold $M$, we establish an upper bound for the number of independent holomorphic $p$-distributions tangent to $\mathcal{G}$ with a prescribed associated twisting line bundle $\mathcal{L}$. As an application, we prove Poincar\'e-type degree inequalities for flags on certain classes of manifolds. Finally, we investigate the Mumford--Takemoto stability of the tangent sheaves of rank-two distributions. In particular, we establish several stability results for such distributions on projective spaces, smooth weighted complete intersection threefolds, and, in particular, on smooth hypersurfaces $V\subset\mathbb{P}^4$.

#7 Gromov-Witten Nonvanishing and Symplectic Deformations of Toric Varieties
著者: Nathan Tausik
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13422
要約:
Let $A$ be nonzero in the cohomology ring of a smooth projective toric variety $X$. In this note, we observe the existence of a nonzero Gromov-Witten invariant of the form $\langle A, A, \ldots\rangle_{0, n,\beta}^X$ as a consequence of Iritani's work on the big quantum cohomology ring of $X$. This implies Koll\'ar's conjecture on the symplectic deformation invariance of rational connectedness in the case where $X$ is toric. 要約:
We give new necessary conditions on the Weil polynomial of a K3 surface over a finite field using techniques from the theory of sphere packing. By comparing the Artin-Tate formula over a finite extension, we derive a special values formula at roots of unity that involves the discriminant of the Frobenius coinvariant lattice. #9 Essential $p$-dimension of simple algebras and noncrossed products of prime exponent
著者: Sanghoon Baek
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13747
要約:
Let $p$ be a prime and $F$ a field of characteristic different from $p$. For integers $n\ge2$ and $1\le m\le n$, we complete the determination of the essential $p$-dimension of the class of central simple algebras of degree $p^n$ and exponent dividing $p^m$. In particular, we have $\operatorname{ed}_p(\operatorname{PGL}(p^n))=p^{2n-2}+1$, extending a result of Merkurjev for $n=2$. Using the comparison theorem of Chernousov and Merkurjev, we also determine the essential $p$-dimension of every split simple group of type $A$. To obtain the lower bounds, we apply a theorem of Ofek to block centralizers. As an application, we prove the existence of division algebras of exponent $p$ over field extensions of $F$ which remain noncrossed products after any field extension of degree prime to $p$, and answer in the negative a problem in the survey by Auel, Brussel, Garibaldi, and Vishne. The algebras can be chosen to have degree $p^n$ for every $n\ge3$ if $p$ is odd and every $n\ge4$ if $p=2$. For $p=2$ and every $n\ge3$, we also determine the essential $2$-dimensions of the classes of central simple algebras of degree $2^n$ with orthogonal involution and with symplectic involution.

#12 Loops and mirrors
著者: Nero Budur
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14110
要約:
For a smooth affine complex variety X we construct a Q-algebra LH(X), loop cohomology, in terms of the formal-loop space of X. We conjecture that it is isomorphic to the symplectic cohomology of X. The degree-zero loop cohomology subalgebra is finitely generated for polarized log Calabi-Yau X, constructing an intrinsic mirror affine coordinate ring. Its polyvector algebra is identified with the full loop cohomology algebra for a positive log Calabi-Yau pair with maximal boundary and smooth loop mirror, proving a form of mirror symmetry. Assuming in addition that X is a surface, we prove that the full loop and symplectic cohomology algebras agree.

#14 Virtual Two-Step Nilpotency of the Fundamental Groups of Special Quasi-Projective Varieties
著者: Tianshu Jiang, Jiayu Li
公開日: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14283
要約:
We prove that the fundamental group of every smooth connected complex quasi-projective variety that is special in the sense of Campana is virtually nilpotent of class at most two. This establishes the two-step nilpotency conjecture of Cao--Deng--Hacon--P\u{a}un, a quasi-projective analogue of Campana's almost abelian conjecture for special compact K\"ahler manifolds. Examples of Cadorel--Deng--Yamanoi show that the step bound is sharp. On a suitable finite etale cover, we construct a canonical second Albanese map to a torus torsor over an abelian variety. We study compact symplectic cuts of hypertoric varieties and characterise their smoothness. When the defining arrangement has a non-empty bounded base region, the fibre-rotation action has a proper moment map and gives compact K\"ahler orbifold cuts at regular levels.
