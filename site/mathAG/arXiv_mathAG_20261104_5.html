#1 $P=W$ via $\mathcal{H}_2$
著者: Tamas Hausel, Anton Mellit, Alexandre Minets, Olivier Schiffmann
公開日: Wed, 04 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.05429
要約:
Let $\mathcal{H}_2$ be the Lie algebra of polynomial Hamiltonian vector fields on the symplectic plane. Let $X$ be the moduli space of stable Higgs bundles of fixed relatively prime rank and degree, or more generally the moduli space of stable parabolic Higgs bundles of arbitrary rank and degree for a generic stability condition. Let $H^*(X)$ be the cohomology with rational coefficients. Using the operations of cup-product by tautological classes and Hecke correspondences we construct an action of $\mathcal{H}_2$ on $H^*(X)[x,y]$, where $x$ and $y$ are formal variables. We show that the perverse filtration on $H^*(X)$ coincides with the filtration canonically associated to $\mathfrak{sl}_2\subset \mathcal{H}_2$ and deduce the $P=W$ conjecture of de Cataldo-Hausel-Migliorini.
#2 Fundamental groups, coregularity, and low dimensional klt Calabi-Yau pairs
著者: Lukas Braun, Fernando Figueroa
公開日: Wed, 04 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01315
要約:
In this article, we study how the absolute coregularity of a projective log pair reflects on its fundamental group. More precisely, we conjecture that for a projective klt log pair $(X,D)$ of absolute coregularity $c$ (and arbitrary dimension) the fundamental group $\pi_1^{\rm reg}(X,D)$ admits a normal abelian subgroup of finite index and rank at most $2c$. We prove this conjecture in the cases $0 \leq c \leq 3$, building on the almost abelianity of the fundamental groups of klt Calabi-Yau pairs of dimension $\leq 3$. In the cases $c \in \{0,1,2\}$ and fixed dimension, we can furthermore bound the index of a solvable normal subgroup. In dimension three, we are able to prove almost abelianity of the fundamental group of the regular locus for projective klt Calabi-Yau pairs.
#3 Conic Multiview Varieties
著者: Felix Rydell, Isak Sundelius
公開日: Wed, 04 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03063
要約:
We present an algebraic study of projections of conics in space onto multiple images of pinhole cameras. We study the Zariski closures of the projection images, which we call conic multiview varieties. Extending previous work for point and line multiview varieties, we use back-projected cones to describe these varieties. For two views, we determine the defining ideals of conic multiview varieties and describe their singular loci. For any number of views, we characterize when the simplest possible set-theoretic description is achieved based on the geometry of the camera centers. Finally, we investigate the complexity of the associated triangulation problem and conjecture the Euclidean distance degree for the conic multiview variety for two cameras.
#4 Anticanonical divisor with good asymptotic base loci
著者: Sung Rak Choi, Sungwook Jang, Dae-Won Lee
公開日: Wed, 04 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04628
要約:
We characterize varieties of Fano type in terms of potentially log canonical pairs and birational Zariski decompositions. We also prove the existence of anticanonical minimal models of a potentially lc pair $(X,\Delta)$ under the assumptions that no potentially log canonical centers are contained in the augmented base locus of $-(K_X+\Delta)$ and that $-(K_X+\Delta)$ is a big $\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier divisor admitting a birational Zariski decomposition with NQC positive part. This can be considered as an anticanonical analogue of the results by Birkar--Hu.
#5 Dwork Motives, Monodromy and Potential Automorphy
著者: Lambert A'Campo
公開日: Wed, 04 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16481
要約:
In this paper we study certain families of motives, which arise as direct summands of the cohomology of the Dwork family. We computationally find examples of interesting families with the following three properties. Firstly, their geometric monodromy group is Zariski dense in $\operatorname{SL}_n$. Secondly, they realise many different unipotent operators as the monodromy operator at $t = \infty$. Thirdly, all their Hodge numbers are $\leq 1$. This has consequences for Galois representations. Namely, if a nilpotent operator $N$ appears as the monodromy at $t = \infty$ in one of our families, we can construct potentially automorphic representations with $\ell$-adic monodromy given by $N$ at a fixed prime $p$. As another application, we obtain a new proof of some cases of the recent local-global compatibility theorem of Matsumoto.
