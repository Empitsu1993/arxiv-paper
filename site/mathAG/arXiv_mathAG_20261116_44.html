#1 Sasakian Hodge Numbers of Calabi-Yau Links
著者: Edward Hirst, Henrique N. S\'a Earp, Tom\'as S. R. Silva
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17419
要約:
The link of an isolated weighted homogeneous hypersurface singularity whose degree equals the sum of its weights is a Sasakian manifold that admits a contact Calabi-Yau structure, and its Sasakian Hodge numbers are dimensions of graded pieces of the Milnor algebra of the defining polynomial. In earlier work these numbers were computed with Gr\"obner bases for the $7$-dimensional links of $7549$ of the $7555$ weighted Calabi-Yau threefold links, and two conjectures were raised from the data. The first asserts that polynomials with the same weights have Milnor algebras whose relevant graded pieces have equal dimensions; the second asserts that the Sasakian Hodge number $h^{2,1}_S$ of the link is bounded above by the Hodge number $h^{2,1}$ of the base Calabi-Yau threefold. Both conjectures are proved here. The first holds for every polynomial with an isolated singularity, because its partial derivatives form a regular sequence; an independent derivation of the Sasakian Hodge numbers by sheaf cohomology on the leaf-space orbifold is also given. For the second, the stringy Hodge numbers of the Calabi-Yau orbifold are shown to bound the middle Sasakian Hodge numbers in every dimension $n\ge2$, and for $n\ge3$ the gap in $h^{n-1,1}$ is shown to be a sum of geometric genera of loci of orbifold points; for threefolds the bound is attained exactly when all curves of orbifold points are rational. The resulting closed formulae give the Sasakian Hodge numbers of the six $7$-dimensional links left open in the earlier work and complete datasets for all $95$ five-dimensional K3 links and all $1\,100\,055$ nine-dimensional Calabi-Yau fourfold links; for the K3 links they recover the previously known second Betti numbers.
#2 A new tautological relation on $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{2,14}$ in cohomology
著者: Samir Canning, Bochao Kong, Rahul Pandharipande, Dan Petersen, Aaron Pixton, Johannes Schmitt
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17536
要約:
We construct a relation $\mathsf{Q}$ in the cohomological tautological ring $\mathsf{RH}^*(\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{2,14})$ which lies outside the space of $3$-spin relations. $\mathsf{Q}$ is a simple linear combination of pure boundary strata classes of codimension 9 supported on the divisor $\Delta_0 \subset \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{2,14}$ of irreducible nodal curves, and transforms according to the irreducible representation of $\Sigma_{14}$ associated to the partition $(2^7)$. The result shows that the set of $3$-spin relations cannot be complete in cohomology. Motivic conjectures relating algebraic cycles to special values of $L$-functions suggest that $\mathsf{Q}$ is nonzero in Chow. More precisely, we prove that the Chow nonvanishing of $\mathsf{Q}$ follows from a conjecture of Harder concerning the failure of the Manin--Drinfeld principle in genus 2. We prove that $\mathsf{Q}$ vanishes in cohomology by pairing with generators of the tautological ring. To prove $\mathsf{Q}$ is not in the set of $3$-spin relations requires an argument involving the $\Sigma_{14}$-representation-theoretic properties of the $3$-spin relations and the cohomology of local systems on $\mathcal{A}_2$ and $\mathcal{M}_2$. Parallel constructions for other moduli spaces $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}$ are discussed in the paper. There are many open questions.
#3 Vector bundles on spin K\"ahler surfaces and the multi-spinor Seiberg-Witten equations
著者: Minh Lam Nguyen, Ollie Thakar
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17647
要約:
We compute new lower bounds on the second Chern class of a vector bundle $\mathscr{E}$ on a spin K\"ahler surface $X$ that satisfies $c_1(\mathscr{E}) = 0$ and $H^2(\text{End}_0\mathscr{E}) = 0.$ When $X$ is a regular elliptic surface and $\mathscr{E}$ has rank 2, our bound is sharp. Our technique is to use Furuta's finite-dimensional approximation on the multi-spinor Seiberg-Witten equations, together with their algebro-geometric interpretation by the second named author. We also use these techniques to prove an existence theorem for singular Fueter sections and multi-spinor Seiberg-Witten solutions that holds for every Riemannian metric on a large class of 4-manifolds.
#4 Global generation of coinvariant vector bundles on $\overline{\mathrm{M}}_{0,n}$
著者: Avik Chakravarty, Jianqi Liu
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17734
要約:
Using fusion closed-ness and strong generation properties, we identify collections of modules over vertex operator algebras (VOAs) whose induced vector bundles of coinvariants on the moduli space $\overline{\mathrm M}_{0,n}$ of stable $n$-pointed curves of genus zero are globally generated. These results specialize to \cite{Fak12} for affine VOAs at positive integer levels, and of \cite{DG23} for strongly rational VOAs that are generated in degree 1. To illustrate the results, and to show that fusion-closedness is essential, we give numerous examples, including Lattice VOAs, Simple Current Extensions of Affine VOAs, and various tensor products.
#5 Exceptional sequences of line bundles on $\mathrm{Fl}(1,\ell;\ell+1)$
著者: Likai Yang
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17782
要約:
Let $X_\ell=\mathrm{Fl}(1,\ell;\ell+1)\subset\mathbb{P}^\ell\times(\mathbb{P}^\ell)^\vee$ be the incidence variety of points and hyperplanes in $\mathbb{P}^\ell$. We prove that for every $\ell\ge 3$, every exceptional sequence of line bundles on $X_\ell$ of maximal length $\ell(\ell+1)=\operatorname{rk} K_0(X_\ell)$ is full. Together with the case $\ell=2$, due to Altmann, Hochenegger and Witt, this shows that for all $\ell\ge2$ an exceptional sequence of line bundles on $X_\ell$ is full if and only if it has maximal length. The proof translates exceptionality into a combinatorial condition on lattice points of $\operatorname{Pic}(X_\ell)\cong\mathbb{Z}^2$ and reduces fullness to sequences containing a non-orthogonal descent. A capacity count for admissibly ordered subsets rules out all but one family of descents; for that family we classify the possible configurations and verify generation using Euler sequences and Koszul complexes.

#7 A Helly theorem for the Bruhat-Tits building
著者: Rudrendra Kashyap, Kiumars Kaveh, Christopher Manon, Boris Tsvelikhovskiy
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17844
要約:
Let $K$ be a discretely valued field with valuation ring $\mathcal{O}$. We show that a finite family of $\mathcal{O}$-lattices in $K^m$ is adapted to a common basis if any subfamily of at most $m+1$ $\mathcal{O}$-lattices is. In other words, in the Bruhat-Tits building of $\operatorname{GL}(m)$, a finite family of simplices lies in a common apartment if and only if any subfamily of at most $m+1$ simplices does. The proof uses a version of Radon's theorem for $\mathcal{O}$-lattices. We also give an alternative proof using truncated modules.

#9 Fano-Enriques threefolds of Fano index $>1$
著者: Kuzma Khrabrov
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17893
要約:
We study $\mathbb{Q}$-Fano threefolds of Fano index greater than $1$ whose Weil divisor class group has nontrivial torsion. We give a complete list of their possible numerical types: there are $176$ of them, which refine to $182$ possible graded Hilbert series. At least $40$ numerical types are realised: $27$ by quotients of weighted complete intersections by cyclic groups, twelve by quotients of threefolds of codimension $4$ or $5$ and one by a quotient of the del Pezzo threefold of degree $5$. Seventy-two numerical types are shown not to occur. In particular the torsion subgroup need not have prime order, and the Weil and $\mathbb{Q}$-Fano indices can be $(1,2)$, $(1,3)$ or $(1,4)$.
#10 Chow Stability of Polarized K3 Surfaces with ADE singularities
著者: Zhiyuan Li
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17901
要約:
The asymptotic Chow stability of smooth polarized K3 surfaces is known through the existence of Ricci-flat metrics and Donaldson's theorem. We give a purely algebraic proof and extend the result to polarized K3 surfaces with at worst ADE singularities. The proof gives a uniform effective bound for the exponent, depending only on the degree of the polarization. Following the approach of Mumford and Morrison, we establish quantitative estimates for linear systems and their contributions to Chow weights, without restrictions on the Picard number.

#12 Frobenius and perfection in framed motives
著者: Grigory Garkusha
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18212
要約:
We develop explicit Frobenius and perfection arguments for framed motives over imperfect fields. In positive characteristic, power framing homotopies and local framed transfers make relative Frobenius invertible after inverting the characteristic. Descent through successive Frobenius twists then gives a proof of strict homotopy invariance for homotopy invariant quasi-stable additive framed presheaves and identifies the canonical comparison with the perfect closure as a stable Nisnevich local equivalence of framed motives. We extend the comparison to E-framed motives for every motivic Thom spectrum E. It also yields the cancellation theorem and the cone theorem. For commutative symmetric Thom ring spectra, we identify triangulated categories of effective E-framed motives and effective E-modules and prove perfection invariance of these triangulated categories. All comparisons are made after inverting the exponential characteristic.
#13 Motivic Invariants of Automorphisms and the Plane Cremona Group

公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18222
要約:
Recent work of Campbell and Zakharevich studies the $K$-theory spectrum of varieties. In previous work, the second author constructs a derived version of the $\mathbb{A}^1$-Euler characteristic, giving a map out of this spectrum. We analyse the morphism that the derived $\mathbb{A}^1$-Euler characteristic induces out of $K_1(\mathrm{Var}_k)$. We obtain an explicit description of this map, which encodes information about automorphisms of varieties. #16 Non-abelian Hodge correspondence for semistable parabolic connections on curves in positive characteristic
著者: Shiyu Shen
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18317
要約:
Let $C$ be a smooth projective curve over an algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic $p>0$. We establish a non-abelian Hodge correspondence between semistable parabolic connections on $C$ and semistable parabolic Higgs bundles on its Frobenius twist $C'$, allowing arbitrary prescribed residue eigenvalues, parabolic weights and filtration types. The relationship between the parabolic weights and residue eigenvalues on the two sides is encoded in a table analogous to Simpson's table. We obtain this result by extending the notion of "very good regular connections" to incorporate a residue-dependent degree condition, and by developing a Cartier descent formalism for parabolic connections. Working with filtered sheaves, we obtain a particularly clean description of this Cartier descent formalism that potentially extends to higher dimensional varieties.

#18 The $\operatorname{Proj}$ construction for pro-graded Huber pairs
著者: Dimitri Dine
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18374
要約:
Inspired by the theory of pro-graded adic rings developed recently by Ishizuka and Yoshikawa, we introduce and study the notions of a pro-graded Huber ring and a pro-graded Huber pair \begin{equation*}((\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{A}_{\textrm{gr}}), \mathcal{A}^{+}),\end{equation*}which capture the intuition of a complete Huber ring (or Huber pair) equipped with a dense graded subring. Using these notions, we define and study an analog \begin{equation*}\operatorname{Proj}(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{A}_{\textrm{gr}}, \mathcal{A}^{+})\end{equation*}of the $\operatorname{Proj}$ construction from algebraic geometry in the realm of adic spaces, without restricting our attention to adic spaces satisfying any Noetherian or finite-type assumptions. We consider gradings both by $\mathbb{Z}$ and by $\mathbb{Z}[1/p]$ for a fixed prime number $p$, so our $\operatorname{Proj}$ construction recovers both classical examples such as usual adic projective space $\mathbb{P}_{K}^{n,\textrm{ad}}$ and infinite-type, perfectoid examples such as perfectoid projective space $\mathbb{P}_{K}^{n,\textrm{perfd}}$. Using GAGA, we prove that the adic analytification of any projective variety $X$ over a nonarchimedean field $K$ can be described as the adic $\operatorname{Proj}$ of a suitable pro-graded affinoid algebra $\mathcal{A}$. Moreover, we prove that in this case $\dim X=\dim \mathcal{A}-1$. We also relate our adic $\operatorname{Proj}$ construction to formal models.
#19 A quotient formula for the Behrend function
著者: Fatemeh Rezaee
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18432
要約:
The Behrend function is essential in enumerative geometry. However, computing it, even in zero dimension, has been a highly subtle and mysterious matter since its introduction in the 2000s. We present a quotient formula for the Behrend function of any closed $r$-dimensional affine scheme $X^r \subset \mathbb{A}^N$, evaluated at a singular point, where $0\leq r \leq N-1$. #20 On the pure part of the cohomology of character varieties
著者: Olivier Schiffmann, Eric Vasserot
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18560
要約:
We prove the purity conjecture of Hausel and Rodriguez-Villegas relating the graded dimension of the pure part of the cohomology of the twisted character variety $M^B(r,d)$ of $GL(r)$ of genus $g$ with the cohomology of a suitable quiver variety associated to the quiver $S_g$ with one vertex and $g$ loops. In particular, this provides an explicit formula for the pure part of the mixed Hodge polynomial of $M^B(r,d)$ in terms of the Kac polynomial $A_{S_g,r}(q)$. Our approach is based on the $P=W$ theorem to transfer the purity conjecture into a statement about the perverse filtration on the cohomology of th stack of Higgs bundles on a genus $g$ curve. We use the theory of BPS Lie algebras, their $\chi$-independence property (proven by Kinjo-Koseki), the recent proof of the stack version of $P=C$ due to Picovschi and Davison's analytic neighborhood theorem to reduce the conjecture to a comparison problem between (the 'pure' part) of the BPS Lie algebra asociated to the stack of Higgs bundles on a genus $g$ curve and the BPS Lie algebra of the stack of representations of the preprojective algebra of the quiver $S_g$. This last step is taken care of by a quiver analog of Markman's tautological generation theorem for the BPS Lie algebra of arbitrary quivers (this last result is also proven by Davison and Hennecart).
#21 Equivariant Quantisation of Hypertoric Varieties via Symplectic Cuts
著者: Benjamin C. W. Brown
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14090
要約:
We study the equivariant geometric quantisation of a non-compact hypertoric orbifold of complex dimension $2n$ by compact symplectic cuts. When the defining arrangement has a non-empty bounded base region and the fixed lift is integral, we prove that, at every positive regular integral level $d$, the space of sections on the cut is the sum of the fibre-rotation weight spaces of weight at most $d$ in the original quantisation. The higher cohomology of the cut pre-quantum line orbibundle vanishes, so its equivariant index is the character of its actual section space. Using the moment polyptychs introduced in a companion paper (arXiv:2610.37724), we give an orbifold Kawasaki-Riemann-Roch localisation formula for this character. We also compute it as a weighted lattice-point sum: a residual weight $x$ occurs at level $d$ if and only if the arrangement slack function satisfies $\mathcal{S}(x) \leq d$, with multiplicity $\binom{d - \mathcal{S}(x) + n}{n}$. The examples compare these calculations in rank one, including a cut with an orbifold point, and in rank two, for smooth cuts that are not toric.
#22 Wasserstein geometry of analytic cycle spaces
著者: Fabio Cavalletti, Lorenzo Cecchi, Antonio Lerario
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17496
要約:
We use optimal transport to define an intrinsic distance on connected components of the Barlet space of analytic cycles on a compact K\"ahler manifold, by associating to each cycle its normalized volume measure. We prove that any two cycles in the same connected component can be joined by a deformation of finite Wasserstein length, and that the resulting metric is complete and geodesic. #23 Semistable 1-Motives: Monodromy, Explicit Periods, and Local Duality
著者: Mohammadreza Mohajer
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17685
要約:
Let $K/\mathbf Q_p$ be unramified, with $p>2$. For a $1$-motive with constant lattice, split torus, and good abelian quotient, we compute the semistable extension complex from toric valuations and formal-group logarithms. A canonical monodromy boundary exists for every semistable representation; it detects splitting over crystalline periods and the increase in monodromy rank. Nonsplit elliptic examples, Raynaud uniformization, and a height-one positive-period bound explain the geometric range. #26 The Chabauty--Kim dimension inequality via noncommutative Iwasawa theory
著者: Sa'ar Zehavi
公開日: Mon, 16 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18218
要約:
We establish Kim's conjectural dimension inequality for smooth projective geometrically connected curves $C/\mathbb{Q}$ of genus at least two with a rational basepoint. For every odd prime $p$ of good reduction, we prove that $$\mathrm{dim} H_f^1(G_{\mathbb{Q},\Sigma},U_n^\mathrm{\'et}) < \mathrm{dim}(U_n^{\mathrm{dR}}/F^0)$$ for all sufficiently large $n$, where $U_n^\mathrm{\'et}$ and $U_n^\mathrm{dR}$ are the $n$'th lower central series truncations of the $\mathbb{Q}_p$-pro-unipotent geometric \'etale, and de Rham fundamental groups of $C$, and $\Sigma$ is any finite set of places containing $p$, $\infty$, and the primes of bad reduction. No restriction is imposed on the Jacobian's geometric endomorphism ring or its Tate-module Galois image. Consequently, the Chabauty--Kim loci are finite at all sufficiently large levels, recovering the Mordell theorem over $\mathbb{Q}$ through the Chabauty--Kim method.
