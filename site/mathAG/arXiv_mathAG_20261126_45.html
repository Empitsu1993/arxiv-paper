#1 Kov\'acs Vanishing for Excellent Log Canonical Threefolds
著者: Rahul Ajit, Shikha Bhutani
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33321
要約:
We prove a vanishing theorem of S. Kov\'acs for excellent log canonical threefolds whose closed points have perfect residue fields of sufficiently large characteristic. This vanishing theorem roughly says that LC threefold pairs are not far from being rational pairs (in the sense of Koll\'ar \& Kov\'acs and Schwede \& Takagi) if one includes a correction term. We apply this vanishing to prove a depth relation of Chou and resolution-invariance of cohomologies of ``essential divisors" for isolated log canonical threefolds in large characteristic, extending works of Ishii and Fujino. Using examples constructed by Bernasconi and Cascini-Tanaka, we show that these results fail in low characteristic. Finally, we show Kov\'acs and Alexeev-Hacon's result (in char 0) that ``Irrational centers are LC centers" fails in every positive characteristic.
#2 Gerbe-corrected stringy E-functions
著者: Michael Groechenig, Evan Sundbo
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33418
要約:
We define an invariant of $\mathbb{Q}$-Gorenstein complex varieties with at worst log-terminal singularities equipped with a rational Brauer class $\alpha$ that generalizes existing versions of the $\alpha$-corrected stringy $E$-polynomial, which are defined only for quotients of quasi-projective varieties by finite groups. Using recent results from $p$-adic integration, this new invariant can be shown to behave well for degenerate dual abelian fibrations and to agree with the intersection or BPS $E$-polynomial for certain moduli problems.
#3 Primitive Collections for Klyachko Varieties
著者: Nicole Lemire, Jason Palombaro
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33491
要約:
Klyachko introduced a family of $S_m\times S_n$-invariant toric Gorenstein Fano varieties, $X_{mn}$, with connections to representation theory, algebraic groups, and graph theory. He focused on the case in which $m$ and $n$ are coprime, for which $X_{mn}$ are smooth, and hence Fano. Klyachko and Voskresenskii later proved that all forms of $X_{mn}$ are stably rational when $m, n$ are coprime, and that all forms of $X_{mn}$ are rational when $n$ is congruent to 1 modulo $m$. In the same paper, they classified centrally symmetric toric Fano varieties as products of projective lines and generalized del Pezzo varieties, $X_{2,2k+1}$. For this reason, the special case of generalized del Pezzo varieties are frequently studied. For example, Ballard, Duncan, and McFadden proved that the bounded derived category of generalized del Pezzo varieties has a full exceptional collection of line bundles. An understanding of the maximal cones and primitive collections of the fan associated to $X_{2,2k+1}$ is crucial in their construction. In this paper, we focus on the Klyachko varieties $X_{mn}$ in the case in which $n$ is congruent to 1 modulo $m$. We determine a description of the maximal cones and primitive collections for the associated fan. We also determine a description of their cohomology ring and some stability results on the $S_m\times S_n$-orbits of maximal cones and primitive collections for $X_{mn}$.
#4 Simple ordinary geometrically split abelian varieties over finite fields
著者: S. A. Novoselov
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33638
要約:
In this paper we provide a partial classification (necessary conditions) for all possible simple ordinary geometrically split abelian varieties over a finite field $\mathbb{F}_q$. Let $A$ be such an abelian variety, i.e. $A$ is simple over $\mathbb{F}_q$ and splits as $A \sim B^s$ over $\mathbb{F}_{q^d}$ for some simple abelian variety $B$. Assume that $d$ is a minimal extension degree over which $A$ splits and $B$ has a minimal field of definition (up to isogeny) $\mathbb{F}_{q^e}\subseteq \mathbb{F}_{q^d}$. For dimension $2$, Howe and Zhu showed that the only possible degrees $d$ are $2$, $3$, $4$, and $6$. Moreover, for each such degree $d$, they provided explicit formulae expressing the isogeny invariant, namely the characteristic polynomial of the Frobenius endomorphism $\chi_{A,q}$, in terms of the coeffients of $\chi_{B,q}$. We extend this result to ordinary abelian varieties of higher dimension. For dimensions $3$--$10$, we provide complete lists of such varieties as components of the Weil restriction $W_{\mathbb{F}_{q^d}/\mathbb{F}_q}(B)$ together with all possible triples $(d, e, s)$ and restrictions on characteristic polynomial $\chi_{A,q}$ of the form $\chi_{A, q} \mid \chi_{V, q}$. Here, $V$ is a component of Weil restriction $W_{\mathbb{F}_{q^d}/\mathbb{F}_q}(B)$ containing $A$ as an isogeny factor and having explicit expression of the characteristic polynomial $\chi_{V,q}$ in terms of coefficients of $\chi_{B,q^e}$. For higher dimensions, we propose an algorithm for enumeration of all possible cases for a given dimension of $A$. We also provide an implementation of this algorithm.

Fake quadrics are minimal complex surfaces of general type with $b_1=0$ and $e=4$. The paper gives a construction of a fake quadric $S$ that is a $32$-fold branched cover of $\mathbf{CP}^1\times\mathbf{CP}^1$ along nine curves. #6 Semiorthogonal decompositions of limit categories for symmetric stacks
著者: Yukinobu Toda
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33771
要約:
In our earlier work with Tudor Padurariu, we introduced the notion of limit categories for derived stacks with self-dual cotangent complexes and used them to formulate the Dolbeault geometric Langlands conjecture. In this paper, we generalize the notion of limit categories to a broader class of derived stacks, which we call symmetric stacks. One of the motivations for this generalization is to formulate Dolbeault geometric Langlands-type correspondences for a broader class of moduli stacks. We establish several fundamental properties of limit categories for symmetric stacks, including compact generation, the existence of semiorthogonal decompositions, functoriality, and a Kunneth formula. In a companion paper, we apply the results of this paper to formulate a Dolbeault geometric Langlands-type conjecture for moduli stacks of sheaves on smooth surfaces with nef anticanonical divisors.
#7 The Dolbeault geometric Langlands-type conjecture for smooth surfaces
著者: Yukinobu Toda
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33778
要約:
Using a general theory of limit categories for symmetric stacks, we construct their semiorthogonal decompositions for moduli stacks of pure one-dimensional coherent sheaves on smooth surfaces with nef anti-canonical divisors. The components of these semiorthogonal decompositions are quasi-BPS categories associated with the corresponding local Calabi--Yau threefolds. By comparing these semiorthogonal decompositions with those of dg-categories of coherent sheaves on moduli stacks of semistable sheaves, we propose a Dolbeault geometric Langlands-type conjecture for surfaces, generalizing the Dolbeault geometric Langlands conjecture for $\GL_r$-Higgs bundles. Our conjecture includes a correspondence for Higgs bundles twisted by sufficiently ample line bundles on curves. We discuss examples and construct Hecke actions on limit categories and, under certain conditions, on quasi-BPS categories. For symplectic surfaces, these actions are expected to provide a categorical lift of the corresponding Hecke operators on BPS cohomology constructed by Davison--Hennecart--Kinjo--Schiffmann--Vasserot.
#8 Decomposition algorithms for rational curves on real classical groups
著者: Yifan Li, Zijia Li, Ke Ye
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33837
要約:
This paper is devoted to an algorithmic investigation of the decomposition problem for rational curves on real classical groups. The main contributions are: (1) We present two Maple-implemented algorithms for decomposing rational curves on real classical groups. The first algorithm decomposes a curve with multiple poles into a unique product of curves with single poles. For rational curves on (indefinite) orthogonal groups, the second algorithm further decomposes single-pole curves into products of low-degree curves. The former only involves linear equations, whereas the latter requires solving both linear and quadratic equations. (2) We compare the decomposition of the same curve with respect to different ambient groups. Through this comparison, we provide an interpretation of the "multiplication trick" for motion polynomials within our framework. Moreover, we demonstrate that our second algorithm aligns with this trick. (3) We prove that the solvability of linear equations in the second algorithm is preserved under the extension of the ambient group. For illustration, we provide comprehensive computational examples.

要約:
Tame character varieties are known to be irreducible when the parametrised local systems have monodromies lying in closures of generic conjugacy classes; we will establish their irreducibility without the genericity assumption. 公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34041
要約:
We prove an exact rational-curve formula for the nef-curve cone of every complex projective dlt pair with effective real boundary, answering the related question proposed by Cascini. Every $(K_X+\Delta)$-negative extremal ray is generated by a movable rational curve whose normalization is free in the smooth locus. #12 The asymptotics of maximal rank of three-way tensors
著者: Jaros{\l}aw Buczy{\'n}ski, Mateusz Micha{\l}ek
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34052
要約:
We prove that the maximal rank of an $n\times n\times n$ tensor is $n^2/3+O(n^{3/2})$, over the real numbers and over every algebraically closed field. More generally, in format $\lfloor an\rfloor\times\lfloor bn\rfloor\times\lfloor cn\rfloor$, with $a,b,c>0$ fixed, the leading term is $\frac{abc}{a+b+c}n^2$. The argument starts with a criterion for rank with respect to an arbitrary projective variety: a decomposition of zero with surjective differential, from a smooth parameter space, bounds the rank of every point. For Segre varieties, we construct such decompositions using a basis of units in a split algebra. We also give an optimized finite bound and a closed estimate with explicit constants for the maximal rank.
#13 Reflection subgroups and the Lookup Conjecture
著者: Christian Gaetz, Yibo Gao
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33572
要約:
The Lookup Conjecture of Boe-Graham proposes a Bruhat graph criterion for rational smoothness of Schubert varieties in the flag variety $\mathcal{G}/\mathcal{B}$ of a complex reductive group. Its extension to Kac-Moody groups of affine type $\widetilde{A}_n$ was considered by Graham-Li. We prove the original conjecture as well as its extension to $\widetilde{A}_n$. Our method could be applied more generally to any Kac-Moody group after checking the rank-three reflection subgroups of its Weyl group. We also prove a parabolic version of the Lookup Conjecture for the type $A$ affine Grassmannian, where a stronger Bruhat graph degree monotonicity property in fact holds, and discuss the implication via the geometric Satake correspondence for weight multiplicities.

#15 Frobenius lifts on invariant subrings, with applications to rational double points and perfectoid towers
著者: Kazuki Hayashi
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33994
要約:
We study Frobenius lifts on invariant subrings and give applications to rational double points and perfectoid towers. We realize rational double points of ADE over $W(k)$ as invariant subrings $A^G$, where $A$ is a ramified two-dimensional complete regular local ring and $G$ is a finite linearly reductive subgroup scheme of $\mathrm{SL}_{2,W(k)}$. We also prove a general theorem on the existence of Frobenius lifts on invariant subrings: if a flat affine group scheme $G$ over $W(k)$ admits a functorial Reynolds operator and a relative Frobenius lift, then any formal power series ring equipped with a linear action of $G$ admits a Frobenius lift that restricts to a Frobenius lift on the invariant subring. Building on these results, we construct perfectoid towers arising from rational double points in mixed characteristic. We identify their tilts with the perfect towers arising from rational double points in positive characteristic of the same ADE type.
#16 Splitting of abelian varieties in motivic stable homotopy category
著者: Haoyang Liu
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05674
要約:
In this paper, we discuss the motivic stable homotopy type of abelian varieties. For an abelian variety over a perfect field $k$, a top-dimensional cell always splits off in the motivic stable homotopy category $\text{SH}(k)$. Let $k=\mathbb{R}$. If $X$ is a real abelian variety of positive dimension $g$, there is, for $\mathbb{Z}[1/(2g)!]\subset\Lambda\subset\mathbb{Q}$, a concrete splitting in $\text{SH}(\mathbb{R})_\Lambda$ determined by the Deninger--Murre decomposition of the motive of $X$ and the real points $X(\mathbb{R})$. #17 On the splitting of surfaces in motivic stable homotopy category
著者: Haoyang Liu
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11922
要約:
Over a perfect field of characteristic zero, we study when the top cell splits off from a pointed smooth projective surface in motivic stable homotopy, with K3 surfaces as the main example. Adams--Riemann--Roch and the motivic Adams relations give a uniform top-codimension annihilator. This yields an integral extreme-cell splitting in particular for complex projective K3 surfaces. Chow--Witt correspondences also yield noncanonical three-term decompositions of Milnor--Witt motives with extreme Tate summands and a nilpotence criterion for lifting finite systems of Chow projectors. In positive characteristic, the annihilator statement holds after inverting the characteristic.

We study algebraic cycles on quartic double fivefolds, which are Fano fivefolds whose middle cohomology is numerically similar to that of a Calabi-Yau threefold, continuing the work of Skauli on lines, $1$-cycles and the coniveau filtration on double covers of projective space. #22 On Casagrande-Druel Fano varieties with Lefschetz defect 2
著者: Pier Roberto Pastorino
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20732
要約:
The larger the Lefschetz defect delta(X) of a smooth complex Fano variety X, the more information we can deduce about the geometry of X. The structure of varieties with delta(X) greater than 2 is known. In this paper, we study the case delta(X)=2. In particular, we focus on Fano varieties with delta(X)=2 arising from the so called Casagrande-Druel construction, which we refer to as Construction A. We show that among the 19 families of Fano 3-folds with delta(X)=2 classified by Mori and Mukai, 15 arise from such construction. Moreover, we construct all Fano 4-folds with Picard number greater than 3 and delta(X)=2 admitting such a structure, obtaining 145 distinct families in total. This completes the classification of all Casagrande-Druel Fano 4-folds with delta(X)=2. To broaden the scope, we also study a generalized version of Construction A, which we call Construction B, and we show that 18 out of the 19 families of Fano 3-folds with delta(X)=2 arise from it.
#23 Conics and rational points of bounded height on cubic surfaces
著者: Chunhui Liu
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10895
要約:
We construct auxiliary intersections covering points of bounded height on geometrically integral non-ruled cubic surfaces over a fixed number field, and prove uniform bounds for the contribution of their conic components to rational and integral point counts. In the affine case, when the section at infinity is geometrically integral, the conic contribution admits a bound with a height exponent strictly below one. Under these hypotheses and within the same covering, obtaining a uniform bound for all integral points with any exponent greater than one reduces to controlling components of degree at least three. The proof combines a form of the global determinant method that retains the dependence on the height of Cayley form with uniform estimates for the number of conics of bounded height. The Deligne pairing and the geometry of residual conic pencils yield cubic image curves in fixed coefficient spaces. Uniform point estimates on these curves control the distribution of conic heights, with constants independent of the surface and the pencil.

In this paper, we develop a general birational program toward the classification of toric 2-Fano manifolds through the investigation of the minimal projective bundle dimension \(m(X) \in \{1,\dots,\dim(X)\}\) introduced in our previous work. #25 A Peterson program for general Schubert varieties and mirror symmetry

公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30242
要約:
We initiate a `Peterson program' that seeks to extend Dale Peterson's theory for the quantum cohomology of flag varieties to arbitrary Schubert varieties, and that incorporates a mirror-symmetric approach. In this first paper we introduce a generalisation of the (localised) Peterson variety associated to an arbitrary Schubert variety $\check X_{w,P}$ inside a partial flag variety $\check G/\check P$. This generalisation is possibly a non-reduced affine scheme that lies in the Langlands dual full flag variety $G/B$. These conjectures are verified for all smooth Schubert divisors in the complete type $A$ flag variety, and we show that our superpotential agrees with that introduced for Grassmannian Schubert varieties by Rietsch and Williams, after a suitable isomorphism of the domains.

要約:
We investigate the structure of \'etale fundamental groups of arithmetic schemes over rings of integers of number fields. We show that the image of the geometric \'etale fundamental group is FAb, while the kernel of the map to the fundamental group of the base need not be FAb or finitely generated. #27 The geometric exceptional set for Fano threefolds
著者: Runxuan Gao
公開日: Thu, 26 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23024
要約:
We study the geometric exceptional set in Manin's conjecture for smooth Fano threefolds. We prove that every adjoint rigid surface with $a$-invariant one on a non-product smooth Fano threefold is normal. This yields a description of the geometric exceptional set in terms of the higher-$a$ locus, anticanonical conics, and del Pezzo fibrations. Zariski density arises only from the latter two contributions. For del Pezzo fibrations, we construct a finite collection of parameter covers which control the associated breaking maps. We classify their low-genus components for general smooth Fano threefolds, yielding examples with potentially Zariski dense geometric exceptional sets in $22$ of the $105$ deformation families. Extending the construction of Batyrev and Tschinkel, we give examples of Fano fourfolds with quartic del Pezzo fibrations and an explicit Fano threefold with two del Pezzo fibrations of degree six. All these varieties have Zariski dense geometric exceptional sets. For the threefold, the original form of Manin's conjecture fails on every nonempty open subset for every anticanonical height.
