#1 Automorphisms and Hodge isometries of surfaces with $K^2=p_g=1$
著者: Jorge Duque Franco, Alvaro Liendo, Pedro Montero
公開日: Tue, 27 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33364
要約:
Let $S$ be a minimal complex surface of general type with $K_S^2=p_g(S)=1$. By a theorem of Catanese, the canonical model of $S$ is a complete intersection of two sextics in $\mathbf{P}(1,2,2,3,3)$ whose equations admit a normal form, and the global Torelli theorem fails for these surfaces. We describe $\operatorname{Aut}(S)$ in terms of the coefficients of the normal form, prove that $|\operatorname{Aut}(S)|\leq 72$, and classify the surfaces attaining the bound: two surfaces with singular canonical model and a one-parameter family $X_r$ of smooth surfaces. For the surfaces $X_r$ we compute a basis of the primitive cohomology consisting of eigenvectors of a subgroup $\mathbf{Z}/6\mathbf{Z} \times \mathbf{Z}/6\mathbf{Z}$ of $\operatorname{Aut}(X_r)$, given by residues of explicit polynomials, together with the intersection form in this basis. We then show that the natural map from $\operatorname{Aut}(X_r)$ to the group of Hodge isometries of the primitive lattice modulo $\pm1$ is injective but has image of index at least $331\,776$, so that the Torelli principle for automorphisms fails for these surfaces.

#5 Uniform asymptotics and the Weil--Petersson constant
著者: Kefeng Liu, Hao Xu
公開日: Tue, 27 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33752
要約:
In their celebrated work on the large-genus asymptotic expansion of Weil--Petersson volumes, Mirzakhani and Zograf left open the question of whether the Weil--Petersson constant satisfies $C_{\mathrm{WP}}=\pi^{-1/2}$. We resolve the conjecture affirmatively. Our strategy is to consider a family of spectral curves interpolating between $y=z-4z^3$ and $y=\sin(2\pi z)/(2\pi)$. For each fixed number $n\geq2$ of marked points, we normalize the cusp coefficients using the action integral and the derivative at its first positive zero. We prove that the logarithmic derivatives of these normalized coefficients with respect to the family parameter are $O_n(g^{-1})$, uniformly along the family. The proof combines the descendant recursion, positivity, the dilaton equation, KdV, and coefficient asymptotics for the Painlev\'e I equation.

#8 The generalized Dixmier conjecture and the two-dimensional Jacobian conjecture
著者: Alexey Kanel-Belov, Timofey Fedorov
公開日: Tue, 27 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33919
要約:
We prove that the two-dimensional Jacobian conjecture $\mathrm{JC}_2$ implies the generalized Dixmier conjecture $\mathrm{DC}'_1$ for the first Weyl algebra $A_1$, which states, in one of several equivalent formulations, that every endomorphism of the two-by-two matrix ring $\operatorname{Mat}_2(A_1)$ is invertible. For the converse, an endomorphism of $\mathbb A^2_{\mathbb C}$ with Jacobian one gives rise to a family of holonomic $A_2$-modules over finite fields. One needs to lift this family to an $A_2$-module in characteristic zero with the prescribed arithmetic support. The existence of such a lift is a special case of Kontsevich's conjecture on holonomic $\mathcal D$-modules with prescribed arithmetic support. We prove that in this special situation such a lift is unique whenever it exists. If these lifts exists for every endomorphism with Jacobian one, then $\mathrm{DC}'_1$ implies $\mathrm{JC}_2$. For every $n\ge 3$, we construct a Lagrangian subvariety of $T^*\mathbb{A}^n$ that provides a counterexample to Kontsevich's conjecture. The counterexamples arise from the graph of a noninvertible symplectic endomorphism of $\mathbb A^4$ constructed by Long. We also present a different strategy for lifting $\mathcal D$-modules associated with a Jacobian-one endomorphism of $\mathbb A^2_{\mathbb C}$ to characteristic zero via ultraproducts and show that the existence of a characteristic-zero lift in this setting is equivalent to the uniform boundedness of the Berest--Wilson invariants of these $A_2$-modules, regarded as left $A_1$-modules. Finally, we study two other conjectures of Kontsevich on arithmetic support, giving a counterexample to one and partial results on the other.
#9 Delta Characters and Ranks of Minimal Subisocrystals of Abelian Schemes
著者: Rajat Kumar Mishra, Arnab Saha
公開日: Tue, 27 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33948
要約:
Using the framework of delta characters on arithmetic jet spaces, we construct a canonical sequence of filtered isocrystals associated to an abelian scheme $A$ of relative dimension $g$ over a $p$-adically complete discrete valuation ring. These isocrystals map injectively via a canonical morphism into the first (rational) crystalline cohomology group $\mathbf{H}^1_{\mathrm{cris}}(A)$ of the abelian scheme $A$, directly generalizing a prior construction due to Borger and Saha. Pandit showed that the canonical morphism is a map of isocrystals. This then implies that the image of this canonical embedding coincides with the minimal subisocrystal $F_{\mathrm{cris}}(\Omega_A)$ containing the space of invariant differential forms $H^0(A,\Omega_A)$. Berthelot and Ogus showed that the dimension of $F_{\mathrm{cris}}(\Omega_A)$ is $g$ if and only if $A$ has an endomorphism that lifts the absolute Frobenius on the special fiber of $A$. As a primary application, for a general abelian scheme $A$, we show that the minimal subisocrystal $F_{\mathrm{cris}}(\Omega_A)$ can be viewed as a module obtained from primitive delta characters of $A$ via appropriate twists by the canonical Frobenius morphism $\phi$ induced from the theory of arithmetic jet spaces. As a consequence, we show that the rank of $F_{\mathrm{cris}}(\Omega_A)$ depends on numerical invariants arising from the theory of delta characters of $A$. More precisely, the dimension of $F_{\mathrm{cris}}(\Omega_A)$ is given by the sum of the exact orders of the primitive delta characters associated to the abelian scheme $A$.
#10 Hodge--Deligne polynomials and $S_n$-representations on the cohomology of $\overline{\mathcal M}_{2,n}$
著者: Young-Hoon Kiem
公開日: Tue, 27 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33971
要約:
We compute the Hodge-Deligne polynomials of the moduli spaces $\overline{\mathcal M}_{2,n}$ of stable $n$-pointed curves of genus two, together with the action of the symmetric group $S_n$ on their cohomology, for all $n$. Using Hassett's moduli spaces of weighted pointed curves, we first show that the generating function of the $S_n$-equivariant Hodge-Deligne polynomials of $\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,n}$ is obtained from that of the moduli spaces of minimally weighted pointed curves by plethysm with the explicit symmetric function $Z=h_1-\sum_{k\ge3}(x+x^2+\dots+x^{k-2})h_k$; numerically, this is an explicit change of variables. The latter spaces map to $\overline{\mathcal M}_g$, and over each boundary stratum they are built from punctured universal curves and Losev-Manin spaces. Combining the stratification by stable graphs with Burnside's formula and twisted characteristics, we obtain in genus two a closed formula whose only non-elementary input is the cohomology of symplectic local systems on $\mathcal M_{1,1}$ and $\mathscr A_2$, known by work of Eichler-Shimura and Petersen. This determines the $S_n$-representations on all $H^{p,q}(\overline{\mathcal M}_{2,n})$ for $n\le 19$ and on all even degree $H^{p,q}(\overline{\mathcal M}_{2,n})$ for every $n$. We carry out the computation in all degrees for $n\le 18$, where it agrees with an independent computation by point counts, and in even degrees for $n\le 30$. As an application, we find that the even degree cohomology of $\overline{\mathcal M}_{2,n}$ is of Hodge-Tate type if and only if $n\le 20$: the space $H^{23,1}(\overline{\mathcal M}_{2,21})$ is the multiplicity free $S_{21}$-representation with Frobenius characteristic $s_1\cdot h_2[e_{10}]$, of dimension $1939938$, so that $H^{24}(\overline{\mathcal M}_{2,21})$ is not spanned by algebraic cycle classes.
#11 Fine points of the moduli space of cubic rational maps
著者: Tanush Shaska
公開日: Tue, 27 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33990
要約:
A rational point of the moduli space $\mathcal{M}_3$ of cubic rational maps is fine if its conjugacy class contains a map defined over $\mathbb{Q}$, and coarse otherwise. In the invariants $\xi_0,\dots,\xi_5$ that embed $\mathcal{M}_3$ in $\mathbb{P}(2,2,3,3,4,6)$, we prove that a point with trivial automorphism group is fine if and only if the quaternion algebra $(-m_{12},-2\xi_0)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ splits, where $m_{12}=\frac{1}{3}\xi_0^2\xi_1+\xi_0\xi_4-\xi_3^2$; we give the analogous criterion on the locus $\mathcal{L}_2$ of maps with an involution and show that all other strata are fine. The proof is constructive and yields an explicit descent. We prove that the obstruction class is unramified along every divisor of the compactification, that a prime $p\ge 5$ obstructs only if the point reduces modulo $p$ into the closure of one of the two codimension-two loci $\mathcal{L}_1$, $\mathcal{L}_2$ of maps with an involution, and that $\mathcal{M}_3$ has transverse $A_1$-singularities along both, above which the class ramifies; local densities predict that $\mathcal{L}_2$ is met modulo $p$ with probability $\asymp 1/p$, so that it carries the obstruction. A census of all 214,260,288 rational points of weighted moduli height at most 7 agrees with this mechanism and supports the conjecture that the proportion of fine points tends to zero like $(\log B)^{-1/2}$.

#13 Recovering the Topology of a Real Plane Algebraic Curve by Tropical Homotopy
著者: Weixun Deng
公開日: Tue, 27 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34392
要約:
We present a tropical homotopy method for computing the numbers of ovals and unbounded components of a real plane algebraic curve, together with the pairing of its ends at infinity. Under explicit support and nondegeneracy assumptions, the homotopy starts from a tropical patchwork and encounters finitely many events. At Morse event points, the curve changes by local surgery. To determine the global attachments, we follow descending separatrices of a single gradient-like field to a fixed collection of base curves. The order of their landing points defines a finite combinatorial state, updated by local rules that also handle events at infinity and simultaneous event points. This recovers component data without tracing the intervening curves. For the dequantizing lifting, given by the negative logarithms of coefficient magnitudes, we prove an explicit event window when the lifting is generic. For fixed support and coefficient signs, with independent, identically distributed log-normal magnitudes, the expected number of events is bounded by a constant divided by the standard deviation of the logarithms. When all event points are Morse, correctness is conditional on complete, correctly classified event data, a sufficiently small starting parameter, and correctly labelled and ordered landing points. A numerical implementation illustrates its behaviour on sparse curves of high degree and on coefficients spanning many scales; these conditions are not certified.

#15 Spectra, Growth, and Localization in Half-Bounded Tensor Triangular Geometry
著者: Liangyu Zhu
公開日: Tue, 27 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34469
要約:
Over a noetherian scheme, classical cohomological support determines thick tensor ideals of perfect complexes. In half-bounded derived categories, complexes with the same support can generate different radical thick tensor ideals. We study how these distinctions affect the Balmer spectrum and localization. For a commutative noetherian ring $R$, we prove that $\operatorname{Spc}(\mathbf D^-_{\mathrm{fg}}(R))$ is noetherian if and only if $R$ is artinian. For a nonempty noetherian scheme $X$, the corresponding condition is $\dim X=0$. Using the Sanders--Zhang classification over a discrete valuation ring, we compare objects whose annihilator exponents grow at different rates in different degrees. We lift the relevant generation relations before changing coefficients and obtain spectral retractions. These comparisons persist after thick quotients and algebraic localization under explicit uniform annihilation and nonvanishing conditions. A second argument uses nonnilpotent classes of positive degree and passes from cyclic subgroups to finite groups. Applications include relative results for schemes and bounded-below spectra with degreewise finitely generated homotopy. We also describe the cohomological information shared by the resulting ideals. The localization comparisons for bounded-below complexes of finitely generated modules and for bounded-below derived objects with finitely generated cohomology are established separately.
