#1 Reversible Higgs Bundles
著者: Krishnendu Gongopadhyay, Tathagata Nayak
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19037
要約:
We study reversible Higgs bundles on a compact Riemann surface $\Sigma$ of genus $g\geq 2$, that is, $G$-Higgs bundles $(E,\Phi)$ with $(E,\Phi)\cong(E,-\Phi)$. For a complex reductive group $G$ realized as a self-adjoint subgroup of $GL(n,\mathbb{C})$, we show that if $(E,\Phi)$ corresponds to a representation $\rho$ of $\pi_1(\Sigma)$, then $(E,-\Phi)$ corresponds to $(\overline{\rho}^{\,t})^{-1}$. As a consequence, the moduli space of reversible Higgs bundles is the fixed locus of an involution of the character variety and carries the structure of a real affine algebraic set. For a connected semisimple group and every topological type, we prove that the reversible locus in the smooth moduli space is a $(B,A,A)$-brane. We then describe reversibility through spectral data: in even rank, for a smooth spectral curve, reversible Higgs bundles correspond to line bundles invariant under the involution $\eta\mapsto-\eta$; in odd rank, under natural genericity assumptions for which the spectral curve is nodal, we give a gluing criterion together with an explicit polystability condition. Finally, for $SL(2n,\mathbb{C})$ we construct a relative Fourier--Mukai transform over the regular locus of the Hitchin base and prove that it maps the structure sheaf of the reversible locus to an object whose support is exactly the regular degree-zero $PSp(2n,\mathbb{C})$-locus in the Langlands dual $PGL(2n,\mathbb{C})$-moduli space. This support is of type $(B,B,B)$.

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19161
要約:
The automorphism groups of smooth cubic fourfolds over the complex numbers, together with actions on defining equations, are classified in previous works. In this paper, we complete the calculation of group actions on the primitive integral cohomology lattice for all of the $156$ families in the classification. We distinguish similar families, determine the remaining action-maximal cases, and use restriction to complete the list. We also determine the generic algebraic lattices for each family. #3 On the Arnold corank, the Zariski multiplicity, and the tangent Milnor fiber problems
著者: Jos\'e Edson Sampaio
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19357
要約:
In this article, we approach the Arnold corank problem posed by Arnold himself in 1975, which asks whether the corank of a holomorphic function is a topological invariant. Here, the corank of a holomorphic function is the corank of its Hessian matrix at 0, which is one of the most basic and fundamental invariants of a holomorphic function. Here, we prove the topological invariance of the corank mod 2. Consequently, we obtain another proof of the Arnold corank problem in dimension 3, which was recently proved by Fernandes, Jelonek, and the author of this article. We also prove that the corank is constant in a continuous family of holomorphic functions with isolated singularities and constant Milnor number. We also approach a conjecture recently posed by Budur, Fern\'andez de Bobadilla, L\^e, and Nguyen (2022), which states that if two germs of holomorphic functions are right topologically equivalent, then the Milnor fibers of their initial forms are homotopy equivalent. We prove that a (much) weaker version of this conjecture, which states that if two holomorphic functions are right topologically equivalent, then the Milnor fibers of their initial forms have the same Euler characteristic, is equivalent to the Zariski multiplicity conjecture for right topological equivalence. Some other results are also presented here. For instance, the multiplicity $> 2$ of generalized semi-Bireskorn-Pham hypersurfaces is an invariant for the right topological equivalence.

#6 Algebraically Integrable Smooth Convex Bodies
著者: Junjie Shan
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19660
要約:
We prove that a convex body with $C^\infty$ boundary in an odd dimension $n\ge3$ is algebraically integrable if and only if it is an ellipsoid. This answers Arnold's problem for smooth convex bodies. Together with Vassiliev's even-dimensional nonexistence theorem, it gives the classification in every dimension $n\ge2$. #7 Maximum Likelihood Degrees via One-Edge Chordal Completion
著者: Nilava Metya
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19669
要約:
The maximum likelihood degree of a Gaussian graphical model measures the algebraic complexity of its likelihood equations. This paper studies maximum likelihood degrees of Gaussian graphical models through chordal completion. When a graph becomes chordal after one missing edge is added, its maximum likelihood degree is determined by the maximal cliques and clique-tree separators containing that edge. This recovers Uhler's formula for complete bipartite graphs with one part of size two, and extends it to deleted-spine clique books, whose degree is unchanged when a page is thickened to a clique. For several fill edges, the same method gives a product formula when no clique or separator bag contains two fill edges. We also determine four classes of vertices at which clique blow-up preserves the degree. Two follow from known results, and two are established here.

要約:
Let $k_0$ be a field of characteristic $0$, and let $C/k_0$ be a smooth geometrically integral curve of positive genus with $C(k_0)=\varnothing$. We prove that the regular inverse Galois problem is solvable over every field existentially closed in the class of $k_0$-fields avoiding $C$, including the curve-excluding fields of Johnson and the author. #11 On the Topology of Fibers of Rational Functions
著者: Kiyoshi Takeuchi
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20025
要約:
In 1988 Broughton proved that if a complex polynomial function on ${\mathbb C}^n$ is tame at infinity its fibers are homotopy equivalent to bouquets of some spheres. Since then several basic properties of polynomial functions were studied by many mathematicians in various situations. In this paper, we show some evidence for the possibility of extending Broughton's result to rational functions satisfying some tameness condition at infinity and the one at their points of indeterminacy. Specifically, assuming some conditions on a rational function on ${\mathbb C}^n$ we prove that the cohomology groups of a generic fiber of it are isomorphic to those of a bouquet of $S^1$ and some spheres. In the course of the proof, the theory of the nearby and vanishing cycle sheaves for meromorphic functions will play an essential role.

要約:
To any compact holomorphically symplectic manifold and a nef BBF-isotropic class we associate a quasi-projective variety called the Markman--Markushevich space which parametrises the Lagrangian fibrations with this isotropic class modulo Tate--Shafarevich twists. We give a systematic interpretation of known results about Tate--Shafarevich twists of Lagrangian fibrations using this space, employ it to obtain a conjectural result for the last deformation type for which such a result is not yet proposed, and make some first steps towards its proof. #17 On the Structure of Radical Towers and their Parametrizations
著者: Javier Sendra-Arranz, Juan Rafael Sendra, Juana Sendra
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20404
要約:
Radical parametrizations describe algebraic varieties by means of nested radicals of polynomials, and their theory relies on three varieties associated with a parametrization, namely, the incidence, tower and radical varieties. In this paper we revisit this framework from a computational point of view and show that it is governed by the radical tower rather than by the particular parametrization. We prove that the tower variety depends only on the tower and that the incidence variety is always irreducible, so that no prime decomposition is needed. Moreover, we show how to replace a tower by an isomorphic one whose tower variety is defined by the equations of the tower, so that it can be computed without elimination. We introduce universal radical parametrizations and we use them to settle, under a natural hypothesis on the coefficients, an open case in the transformation of differential equations with radical coefficients into equations with rational coefficients. We show that radical subtowers form a lattice, which yields minimal radical parametrizations, and we give algorithms to compute them. We also prove that dominance between towers induces a unique dominant rational map between their tower varieties, through which unirationality is transferred. Finally, we study towers over subfields of $\mathbb{C}$ and, in the real case, relate the real points of the tower variety to the real domains of the associated parametrizations.

#22 Arithmetic Visibility in Polygons: Curved Sight in the Art Gallery
著者: Malik Barrett
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19506
要約:
In the classical art gallery problem, a guard sees a target when the segment joining them lies entirely within the polygon. We explore what changes when sight may bend along regular arcs on absolutely irreducible curves, but only those defined by primitive integer polynomials of total degree at most \(d\) and coefficient height at most \(H\). We call such curves admissible sight carriers. Arcs can dramatically improve sight. In a one-sided comb, no straight-line guard sees selected targets in two different teeth, yet a single central guard sees a chosen target in every tooth along parabolic carriers. Sight can also fail in two different ways. Geometrically, the polygon may block every sight arc of the allowed degree between two points. For instance, in an alternating comb, the number of guards needed for a row of targets spanning the comb grows linearly with the number of sides at each fixed degree, even for carriers with arbitrary real coefficients. Arithmetically, no admissible carrier may pass through the points at all. Even in the unit square, at fixed \(d\) and \(H\), the proportion of rational targets that must guard themselves tends to one as the height bound grows. An algebraic target may lie on no rational curve of degree at most \(d\), however large \(H\) is. Both failures can be overcome for finite target sets by allowing the degree and coefficient height to grow. We show that every finite set of algebraic targets in a rational polygon becomes visible from any rational interior guard, with explicit bounds. We also construct, for every \(d\), arbitrarily large Galois orbits in the unit square, all of projective height \(4^{d+1}\), whose points must each guard themselves at degree \(d\) for every \(H\). Yet at degree \(d+1\) and coefficient height one, a single guard sees the whole orbit along \(Z(Y-X^{d+1})\).

#24 The parity module of a matroid

公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20234
要約:
We introduce the parity module of a matroid with coefficients in a field $\mathbb{k}$. When $\mathbb{k}$ has characteristic zero, we show that the parity module of a matroid is isomorphic to its intersection cohomology module, yielding a new intrinsic characterization of the latter. #25 GCD and ABC inequalities for abelian varieties over function fields
著者: Guoquan Gao
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20379
要約:
We prove two height inequalities for rational points on abelian varieties over function fields of characteristic 0. The first is the GCD inequality, which shows that the height with respect to a closed subscheme of an abelian variety of codimension at least 2 is much smaller than the usual height function. This can be viewed as a compact and geometric analogue of a theorem of Levin. The second is a special case of the (geometric) abc conjecture proposed by Vojta, which compares the truncated counting function and the usual counting function. This is a compact analogue of a theorem of Guo, Nguyen, Sun and Wang, and also resolves a conjecture of Buium, which was only known for traceless abelian varieties or constant abelian varieties previously. The proofs of these inequalities follow a common three-step pattern: from microscopic local estimates to local estimates, and then to global estimates. Building on the canonical Xie-Yuan heights, we control discrete counting functions by continuous integrals of Betti forms. The main new tools are uniform Nevanlinna-type estimates for families of linear entire curves on abelian varieties; combined with covering arguments based on Besicovitch theory and averaging, these handle overlaps and gaps precisely.
#26 Topological recursion for spin enumeration of hypermaps

公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20464
要約:
We establish topological recursion for the spin enumeration of hypermaps, or, in the dual language, spin dessins d'enfants. This problem has remarkable features distinguishing it from other applications of topological recursion to enumerative combinatorics. First, the spectral curve is elliptic rather than rational, as in most cases studied before. Second, it contains degenerate ramification points, and one has to apply a version of degenerate topological recursion. Finally, the meromorphic correlator differentials have "moving" poles on the antidiagonals between pairs of distinct arguments, and one has to adjust the loop equations to this kind of degeneracy as well. The computations involve concepts of different kinds, such as BKP integrability, Gaussian integrals, the x-y swap, and symplectic duality.

著者: Ryu Tomonaga
公開日: Thu, 15 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26199
要約:
For $d\geq1$, a tilting bundle on a regular proper $d$-dimensional variety over a field is called $d$-tilting if its endomorphism algebra has global dimension $d$, the smallest possible value. We prove that, over any field, the existence of a $d$-tilting bundle implies that the anticanonical bundle is semiample and big, and hence the variety is weak Fano. Moreover, the endomorphism algebra is $d$-representation tame. Conversely, every weak del Pezzo surface over an algebraically closed field of arbitrary characteristic admits a $2$-tilting bundle.
