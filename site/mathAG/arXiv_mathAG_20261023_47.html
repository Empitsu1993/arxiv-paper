#1 Denominators of Hodge integrals and Minkowski numbers
著者: Daniel Holmes
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30274
要約:
We prove that the least common denominator of all genus $g$ Hodge integrals is the Minkowski number $T_{2g}$ or, equivalently, the Bhargava factorial $(2g+1)!_{\mathbb{P}}$ associated to the set of primes $\mathbb{P}\subset\mathbb{N}$. As a corollary, we prove a conjecture of Liu-Xu on the multiplicity of $3$ in the least common denominators of certain descendant integrals, and generalise their result on a conjecture of Itzykson-Zuber to Hodge integrals with $\lambda$ classes. Namely, the order of the automorphism group of any pointed stable curve of genus $g'\le g$ divides the least common denominator of all genus $g$ Hodge integrals. We also comment on the use of generative AI via IMProofBench, which played a fruitful part in the development of this (entirely human-written) paper.
#2 Relative Hitchin fibrations and endoscopy for Unitary Friedberg--Jacquet varieties
著者: Thomas Hameister, Spencer Leslie
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30366
要約:
We prove a relative fundamental lemma for the unitary symmetric variety $GL_n(k')\backslash U_{2n}$, proving a conjecture of Leslie, Xiao, and Zhang. Our proof reduces to the Lie algebra and argues over function fields in positive characteristic, using methods from Ngo-Laumon and Ngo. In particular, we reinterpret orbital integrals in terms of the cohomology of relative affine Springer fibers and globalize this using unitary versions of Hitchin moduli spaces for symmetric pairs via a product formula. As the main global input, we prove a matching of perverse sheaves between relative Hitchin moduli spaces and their corresponding endoscopic variants.

要約:
We extend the dual complex of a simple normal crossings divisor to a homotopy type invariant $\mathcal{D}(X)$ of arbitrary complex varieties, and we prove that it is contractible when $X$ is connected and of klt type. #5 Obstructions to deforming space curves lying on a complete intersection of quadrics
著者: Hirokazu Nasu
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30962
要約:
Let $S=S_{2,\ldots,2}\subset\mathbb P^{n+2}$ be a smooth complete intersection of $n$ quadrics containing a line $E$, where $n\geq 3$, and set $D=(n-1)\mathbf h+E$, where $\mathbf h=[\mathcal O_S(1)]$. We study the deformations of smooth curves $C\in |mD|$ and their obstructions. The linear system $|D|$ defines a birational morphism $f:S\to\overline S$ that contracts $E$ to a point and realizes an unprojection in the sense of Papadakis--Reid. When $n=3$, the surface $S=S_{2,2,2}\subset\mathbb P^5$ is a $K3$ surface and $E$ is a $(-2)$-curve. We prove that the complementary open surface $S\setminus E$ admits first-order infinitesimal deformations in $\mathbb P^5\setminus E$ that are obstructed at the second order. Using these obstructions, we show that, for every sufficiently large $m$ with $\operatorname{char} k\nmid m$, there exists a generically non-reduced irreducible component $W_m$ of the Hilbert scheme $\operatorname{Hilb}^{sc}\mathbb P^5$ of smooth connected curves in $\mathbb P^5$. Its general member $C$ lies on a complete intersection $S_{2,2,2}$ of three quadrics, satisfies $C\sim mD$, and has $h^0(N_{C/\mathbb P^5})-\dim_{[C]}\operatorname{Hilb}^{sc}\mathbb P^5=3$. In contrast, when $n>3$, we prove that, for every sufficiently large $m$ with $\operatorname{char} k\nmid m$, the corresponding family of curves in $|mD|$ gives rise to a generically smooth irreducible component of $\operatorname{Hilb}^{sc}\mathbb P^{n+2}$.
#6 Uniformity of the minimal exponent on the GIT boundary of projective hypersurfaces
著者: Yasutaka Shibata
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30985
要約:
Let $X$ be a non-stable (in the sense of GIT) hypersurface of degree $d \ge 3$ in the projective space $\mathbb{P}^{n}$. S. G. Park proved that the global minimal exponent of $X$ satisfies the inequality $\widetilde{\alpha}(X) \le \frac{n+1}{d}$. In this paper, we establish the uniform sharpness of this inequality: we prove that $\widetilde{\alpha}(X) = \frac{n+1}{d}$ holds for a general member $X$ of every irreducible component of the strictly semistable locus. Moreover, a general point of each irreducible component of the GIT boundary $\partial M^{\mathrm{GIT}}$ admits a strictly semistable zero-weight representative with this minimal exponent. By the lower semicontinuity of the minimal exponent on the affine cone and a theorem of Musta\c{t}\u{a}--Popa for general linear combinations of monomials, the problem is reduced to computing the log canonical thresholds, at the cone vertex, of the monomial ideals associated with the maximal strictly semistable supports with respect to a maximal torus and their weight-zero parts. Since the barycenter of the degree-$d$ simplex lies in the convex hull of each of these supports, Howald's formula forces these thresholds to be uniformly equal to $\frac{n+1}{d}$.
#7 Third-Order Weierstrass Points on Plane Quartics
著者: Ewelina Nawara
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30992
要約:
For a smooth plane quartic $C$, the $3$-Weierstrass points are the $300$ points (counted with weights) at which some plane cubic has contact order at least ten with $C$. Revisiting the work of Kamel and Elshareef, we write the third Veronese Wronskian as an $\text{SL}_3$-covariant form $\Omega_3$ of degree $75$, given by a determinant of iterated Hamiltonian derivatives, and explain the special $3$-Weierstrass points through contact cubics and homologies preserving $C$. We determine $\Omega_3$ for the Fermat, Komiya--Kuribayashi, Klein and Picard quartics; for the Klein quartic $\Omega_3\equiv\Phi_6^2\,\Phi_{21}\,(3\Phi_{14}^3+21962\,\Phi_6^7)$ modulo the curve, up to a non-zero scalar. Finally, the dectactic points of the Fermat quartic are not $4$-Weierstrass points, which disproves a conjecture of Kamel and Elshareef. All computations are supported by \texttt{SINGULAR} scripts.

#15 The Penrose Correspondence over a Non-Archimedean Field
著者: Alberto Verjovsky
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31379
要約:
Let $K$ be a non-Archimedean local field of characteristic different from $2$ and let $V$ be a four-dimensional symplectic $K$-vector space. We study the Penrose incidence correspondence $\mathbb{P}(V) \leftarrow \mathrm{Flag}(1,2;V) \rightarrow \mathrm{Gr}(2,V)$ after Berkovich analytification. The Klein quadric $\mathrm{Gr}(2,V)$ decomposes into the open orbit $\mathrm{Spin}(5)/\mathrm{Spin}(4)$ and the Lagrangian quadric, which parametrize respectively the projective lines transverse and tangent to the standard contact distribution on $\mathbb{P}(V)$. We construct explicit free discrete subgroups $\Gamma\subset\mathrm{PSp}(V)$ and analytic domains, cut out by Drinfeld upper half-spaces, in all three members of the incidence diagram. Both incidence projections are surjective on these domains, the three $\Gamma$-actions are free and properly discontinuous, and the Penrose double correspondence descends to the analytic quotients. The quotient of the twistor domain is a contact analytic threefold, and the Grassmannian domain is contained in the analytification of the $\mathrm{Spin}(5)/\mathrm{Spin}(4)$ open orbit.
#16 An asymptotic $\Gamma$-BMT inequality
著者: Nick Rekuski
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31399
要約:
We refine a bound of Cheng-Feyzbakhsh to prove an asymptotic $\Gamma$-Bayer-Macr\`i-Toda ($\Gamma$-BMT) inequality on every smooth polarized complex threefold. The proof relies on a reduction to small tilt stability parameters. Using this inequality we verify Feyzbakhsh-Thomas's condition $(wBG)$ whenever $K_{X}$ is numerically proportional to the polarization. For polarized Calabi-Yau threefolds, this yields a reduction of positive rank generalized Donaldson-Thomas (DT) invariants to those counting sheaves pure in codimension one. We further express these invariants in terms of rank one generalized DT invariants. Thus, the rank reductions of Feyzbakhsh-Thomas hold without assuming the BMT conjecture.

#19 The generic splitting type of the logarithmic bundle of an arrangement of at most sixteen lines is combinatorial
著者: Cristian Anghel, Rares Stan
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31556
要約:
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an arrangement of $d$ lines in $\mathbb{P}^2_\mathbb{C}$ and let $\mathcal{T}\langle\mathcal{A}\rangle$ be its bundle of logarithmic vector fields. On a general line, $\mathcal{T}\langle\mathcal{A}\rangle$ splits as $\mathcal{O}(-k)\oplus\mathcal{O}(k+1-d)$ with $k=\min\{\mathrm{mdr}(\mathcal{A}),\lfloor\frac{d-1}{2}\rfloor\}$, where $\mathrm{mdr}$ is the minimal degree of a Jacobian syzygy. We prove that $k$ is determined by the intersection lattice whenever $d\le16$, answering in this range a question of Cook, Harbourne, Migliore and Nagel. Since freeness can be read off from $k$ and $c_2(\mathcal{T}\langle\mathcal{A}\rangle)$, this strengthens Terao's freeness conjecture in this range; in particular we obtain a proof of Terao's conjecture for $14$ lines that uses it only for $13$ lines. For any number $d$ of lines, it follows that $\mathrm{mdr}$ is determined by the lattice whenever $\mathrm{mdr}\le6$ and $2\mathrm{mdr}\le d-3$, and that Terao's conjecture holds for every free arrangement whose smaller exponent is at most $7$. The proof reduces the question to critical arrangements: $2s+3$ lines, each containing at most $s+1$ intersection points, with $\mathrm{mdr}=s$. For these, the inflection polynomial of the minimal derivation factors as $Q_\mathcal{A} R$ with $R\in H^0(\mathcal{I}_Z^2(s-3))$, where $Z$ is the zero scheme of the derivation, of length $\nu=c_2(\mathcal{T}\langle\mathcal{A}\rangle)-s(s+2)$. For $s\le5$ this forces $\nu\le1$, and Chern class arguments then reduce everything to Terao's conjecture for $13$ lines. For $s=6$ we show $\nu\le3$, using a Riccati equation along multiple components of $R$, Ziegler's multirestriction, and local computations verified by computer algebra; an exhaustive enumeration of the 26,265,318 candidate lattices with $\nu\in\{2,3\}$ that satisfy Bojanowski's inequality completes the proof.
#20 Leafrians, Poisson structures, and Frobenius splittings on moduli spaces
著者: Jun Peng
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31619
要約:
The Poisson $T$-Pfaffians of Bott--Samelson varieties, $G/U$, and $G/B$ produce Frobenius splittings, but only by a local, coordinate-bound argument. We isolate the local mechanism: a weighted trace pairing on the deformation space of a fat point, whose top coefficient factors over the blocks. At the members of a presentation, polyvector fields on the base $X$ transfer to the moduli space $M$. Applied to a Poisson bivector, this produces a Poisson bivector, extending Bottacin's structure from surfaces to arbitrary dimension. Applied to a top degree polyvector $s$ and wedged with a transverse Euler frame, it produces an anticanonical section on $M$, which we call the \emph{leafrian}. When $X$ is a Poisson surface, we prove that the leafrian splits the moduli of stable sheaves, of principal $G$-bundles, and of instantons if and only if $s$ Frobenius-splits the base. The mechanism itself is dimension-free. The construction also recovers and generalizes the splittings of the Lie-theoretic examples: the $r$-matrix need not satisfy the classical Yang--Baxter equation.

#29 Triple Quantum Schubert Calculus
著者: Matthew J. Samuel
公開日: Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31533
要約:
We prove a triple quantum Pieri formula: the product of a quantum double Schubert polynomial $\widetilde{\mathfrak{S}}_u(x;y)$ by a quantum factorial elementary polynomial $\widetilde{E}_{p,k}(x;z)$ in a second set of variables $z$ is a sum over chains in the quantum Bruhat graph, indexed by a quantum analogue of Sottile's relation, whose coefficients are monomials in the quantum parameters times polynomials in the differences $y_i-z_j$ with nonnegative integer coefficients, and which is Graham-positive at $z=y$.
