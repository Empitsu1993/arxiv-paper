#1 Tate and Artin--Tate Motivic Tensor-Triangular Geometry
著者: Jean Paul Schemeil, Fraser Sparks
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40806
要約:
We compute the Balmer spectra of geometric Tate and Artin--Tate motives with $\mathbb{F}_p$-coefficients over any field whose characteristic differs from $p$. For Tate motives, we find the bigraded comparison map to be a homeomorphism to the homogeneous spectrum of the motivic cohomology of the base field. For Artin--Tate motives, we describe the spectrum as a gluing of the spectrum of Artin motives computed by Balmer--Gallauer with the punctured Tate spectrum. We determine all specialisations between these pieces and the resulting topology. Under the hypothesis that the spectrum of rational Tate motives is a singleton, we also compute the integral Tate spectrum after inverting the exponential characteristic of the base field. As a consequence of our results, we identify the punctured Tate spectrum as an open subspace of the spectrum of the category of geometric Voevodsky motives, and draw some conclusions concerning the latter. Finally, we include an appendix where we define generalised periodicity in the context of tensor-triangular geometry, and explore its applications in motivic settings.
#2 Integral Hodge structures and atoms of fourfolds
著者: J\'er\'emy Gu\'er\'e
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40811
要約:
Following the recent work by OpenAI on the irrationality of very general cubic fourfolds in Hassett divisors, we prove some compatibility of Iritani's blow-up formula with the classical blow-up decomposition of integral Hodge structures. As a consequence, we express OpenAI's birational invariant in terms of the theory of atoms developped by Katzarkov--Kontsevich--Pantev--Yu. We also show that, for every smooth complex rational cubic fourfold, the primitive cohomology is isometric as an integral Hodge structure to the twisted middle cohomology of a K3 surface and we prove irrationality of the very general Verra fourfold.
#3 Truncated Grassmannians: polytopes, representations, and Cauchy formulas
著者: Evgeny Feigin, Igor Makhlin
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40891
要約:
The goal of this paper is to develop algebraic and combinatorial techniques for studying the geometric properties of truncated Grassmannians, a class of algebraic varieties obtained via certain projections of classical Grassmannians within their Pl\"ucker embeddings. We introduce a class of cyclic representations of maximal parabolic subalgebras of the general linear Lie algebra, which are proved to serve as duals of the homogeneous components of coordinate rings of truncated Grassmannians. We prove that the characters of these representations are computed via truncated Cauchy formulas and establish a connection with coordinate rings of complete collineations. We also construct monomial bases of these representations in terms of certain generalized chain polytopes and use the RSK correspondence to establish connections with Cauchy-type formulas and with Gelfand--Tsetlin patterns.

#5 Hyperelliptic curves on $(1,d)$ abelian surfaces for $d\leq 15$
著者: Pawe{\l} Bor\'owka, Paolo Grossi, Anatoli Shatsila
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40989
要約:
Bryan, Oberdieck, Pandharipande and Yin predicted the number of hyperelliptic curves on general abelian surfaces under a transversality condition. Some of the numbers have already been confirmed. In this paper we construct singular hyperelliptic curves on $(1,d)$ polarised abelian surfaces for $d\leq 15$ confirming a few predicted numbers, namely of (geometric) genus $g=d$ and of geometric genus $k+3$ on $(1,2k)$ and $(1,2k+1)$ polarised surfaces. #6 Rational curves of submaximal quantum index
著者: Tangi Pasquer
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41031
要約:
On a complex toric surface, oriented real rational curves tangent to the toric boundary at real points are distinguished by their value of quantum index. Using variants of usual Welschinger multiplicities, G. Mikhalkin defined enumerative invariants $W_{\tilde{\Delta}}^{\kappa}$ that are provided by a signed count of oriented real rational curves with given quantum index $\kappa$ and fixed tangency points on the boundary of the positive quadrant (the tangency orders being encoded by $\tilde{\Delta}$). We prove that, for most toric surfaces and tangency orders, the invariant $W_{\tilde{\Delta}}^{\kappa}$ is zero in the case of the highest nonmaximal value of $\kappa$. This is done by enumerating tropical curves with Block--G\"ottsche multiplicities. Additionally, we prove that, in many situations, the lower bound, given by the absolute value $|W_{\tilde{\Delta}}^{\kappa}|$ of $W_{\tilde{\Delta}}^{\kappa}$, for the number of oriented real rational curves is not sharp close to the tropical limit in the case of the highest nonmaximal value of $\kappa$.

#8 Odd-Degree Rigidity and a Numerical Characterization of Complete Flag Varieties
著者: Kiwamu Watanabe
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41237
要約:
Let $F=G/B$ be the complete flag variety of a connected semisimple complex linear algebraic group. We prove that if a smooth Fano variety $X$ has the same dimension and Picard number as $F$, then the existence of a continuous map $X\to F$ of odd topological degree implies $X$ is isomorphic to $F$. In particular, a smooth Fano variety homotopy equivalent to $F$ is isomorphic to $F$. The algebraic input is a numerical characterization of complete flags: a positive-dimensional smooth Fano variety $X$ is a complete flag variety if and only if its pseudoindex $\iota_X$ is at least two and ${\rm ker}({\rm Sym}^2N^1(X)_{\mathbb R}\to N^2(X)_{\mathbb R})$ contains a positive-definite quadratic form. The pseudoindex condition can equivalently be replaced by the divisibility of $-K_X$ by two in ${\rm Pic}(X)$. The key observation is that a single definite quadratic relation excludes surfaces in every fiber of any elementary contraction; standard contraction theorems then yield smooth ${\mathbb P}^1$-fibrations and the characterization of complete flags applies. For the odd-degree theorem, Poincar\'e duality and a mod-two Wu-class argument show that the anticanonical class is divisible by two; comparison of the integral coroot lattices then identifies the flag. Most proofs were developed through extensive discussions with OpenAI's ChatGPT (GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-6), based on mathematical directions supplied by the author and arguments suggested by ChatGPT; see the AI Disclosure.

公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41287
要約:
We show that if the projectivised stratum $\mathbb{P}{H}_g(\mu)$ associated to a partition $\mu=(m_1,\ldots,m_n)$ of $2g-2$ contains a complete curve, then \[ \kappa_\mu:=\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{m_i(m_i+2)}{m_i+1}>12. \] The weak inequality follows from semipositivity applied to a quotient of the Hodge bundle, while equality is excluded using the second Fujita decomposition and the geometry of fibred surfaces with maximal unitary rank. #10 Disconnectedness of stability manifolds and their geometric loci, and separation of braid orbits of semiorthogonal decompositions
著者: Genki Ouchi, Tomoki Yoshida
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41344
要約:
This paper proves that the Bridgeland stability manifold $\mathrm{Stab}(X)$ has infinitely many connected components for general quintic threefolds, other general complete intersection Calabi--Yau threefolds, certain odd-dimensional Calabi--Yau varieties, and certain rational weak Fano varieties. Moreover, we show that the geometric locus $\mathrm{Stab}^{\mathrm{geo}}(X)$ meets distinct connected components of $\mathrm{Stab}(X)$ for general quintic threefolds and their products with varieties with finite Albanese morphisms, which gives examples in any dimension at least three. For the weak Fano examples, we also construct semiorthogonal decompositions in infinitely many braid group orbits. The proof is an extension of Nordskova's argument to families, using semilinear autoequivalences and absolute Galois actions on geometric generic fibers to permute mutually orthogonal spherical objects. Then, we specialize the separation to closed fibers. In our examples, the spherical objects are constructed from rigid rational curves or spinor bundles on quadrics.

#16 A Tropical Viewpoint on the Nagata--Biran--Szemberg Conjecture
著者: Juliette Bruce
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41931
要約:
We give a tropical perspective on the recent arguments announced by OpenAI for Nagata's conjecture and the Nagata--Biran--Szemberg conjecture. By degenerating to both the normal cone and a semistable model whose dual graph is a cycle, we replace sections on a surface by Laurent polynomials in two variables. In place of theta functions, tropical methods bound the exponents of these Laurent polynomials, and a lift of the tropical Abel--Jacobi class of the normal bundle controls the shape of the resulting exponent diagram. #17 The metric Zariski multiplicity conjecture in dimension 4
著者: Jos\'e Edson Sampaio
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41954
要約:
Recently, OpenAI announced a counterexample to the Zariski multiplicity conjecture in dimension 4. Here, we prove that the metric Zariski multiplicity conjecture is true in dimension 4. More precisely, let $f,g\colon(\mathbb{C}^n,0)\to(\mathbb{C},0)$ be germs of reduced holomorphic functions, and let $\varphi\colon(\mathbb{C}^n,0)\to(\mathbb{C}^n,0)$ be a bi-Lipschitz homeomorphism such that $\varphi(V(f))=V(g)$. We prove that if every irreducible component of the tangent cone of $V(f)$ at the origin has a singular locus of dimension at most two, then $V(f)$ and $V(g)$ have the same multiplicity at the origin. In particular, the multiplicity of complex analytic hypersurface singularities in $\mathbb{C}^n$, $n\leq 4$, is an embedded bi-Lipschitz invariant; the cases $n=2$ and $n=3$ were known. This extends a previous result of the author for tangent cones whose components have singular loci of dimension at most one. We also prove that two irreducible homogeneous hypersurfaces whose singular loci have dimension at most two, and which are mapped onto each other by a homeomorphism of $\mathbb{C}^n$ fixing the origin, have the same degree.

We introduce Grassmannians of absolute Banach--Colmez spaces and prove the $k$-Grassmannian is perfectoid if and only if the space has slope $< 1/k$. #21 Weighted flip graphs from cyclic quantum mutations: pentagon inequalities and exponential separations
著者: Xiaomin Tang
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40661
要約:
Positive cyclic quantum mutations at a third root of unity induce an explicit weight on polygon flip graphs. We study the resulting discrepancy between minimum flip count and minimum accumulated normalization loss. Every pentagon three-to-two replacement strictly decreases weight, and in connected types $A_1$, $A_2$, and $A_3$ every minimum-weight path is a flip geodesic. In type $A_4$, however, a six-flip path has smaller weight than the unique five-flip geodesic. A compatible positive projective gluing construction amplifies this example to an exponential success-probability separation in every connected type $A_n$, $n\ge4$. We also give a quantitative perturbation bound preserving the separation. The low-rank minimality statement has an exhaustive finite certificate; the pentagon inequality and the all-rank construction are analytic. In the associated sequential postselection model, differences of path weights determine success-probability ratios independently of the input state. The comparison holds for separately normalized exact mutation steps.
#22 On Boundary State Screening via Solitons
著者: Vivek Chakrabhavi, Babak Haghighat, Jonathan J. Heckman
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40777
要約:
We present a coarse classification scheme for the boundary data of massive QFTs based on transitions of boundary states via soliton / kink profiles. Starting from the lattice of boundary states, we introduce the boundary screening group $\mathbb{B}$: the equivalence classes of boundary states which cannot be connected by such transitions. In string theory backgrounds which geometrically engineer an interacting QFT in the target space, $\mathbb{B}$ computes the worldsheet analog of the target space defect group $\mathbb{D}$. When $\mathbb{B}$ is non-zero, it provides a diagnostic for having a target space with non-normalizable modes. For interfaces between QFTs, similar considerations imply the existence of a non-trivial conserved charge associated with soliton absorption / transmission, which means that the interface cannot fully dissolve / disappear. Similar considerations hold for junctions of QFTs, which in the special case of string worldsheet CFTs translate to junctions of string theories. We illustrate these general considerations with a number of non-geometric 2D $\mathcal{N} = (2,2)$ Landau-Ginzburg models, as well as their natural lifts to 4D $\mathcal{N} = 1$ Wess-Zumino models.

#28 Baker--Akhiezer sections from Higgs bundles and Landau levels in hyperbolic band theory
著者: Duong Dinh, Steven Rayan
公開日: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42134
要約:
As observed by Kienzle and the second author, rank-two $\mathcal{O}(3)$-twisted Higgs bundles on the projective line encode all hyperbolic crystals of genus two: the spectral curves are the unit cells, and the Hitchin fibers are the abelian Brillouin zones. In a uniform magnetic field of two flux quanta, the lowest Landau level is one-dimensional at every nonzero momentum, and, as found by Iengo and Li, its dimension jumps to two at the zero momentum. We identify the states of the lowest Landau level with the Baker--Akhiezer sections of the Higgs bundles, and their zeros, the vortices, with the Baker--Akhiezer divisors.
