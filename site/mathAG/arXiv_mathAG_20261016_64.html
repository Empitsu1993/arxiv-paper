#1 Nilpotent Deformation Theory via Ultrasolid Algebra
著者: Sof\'ia Marlasca Aparicio
公開日: Fri, 16 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20801
要約:
Deformation theory studies how varieties and other algebro-geometric objects vary in families. A central part of the subject is formal deformation theory, where one deforms over an Artinian base; such deformation problems are governed by Lie algebraic models. We pose the question of deforming varieties over nilpotent but not necessarily Artinian bases. These turn out to be classified by the same Lie algebraic models plus some topological structure. More precisely, we will consider partition Lie algebroids in the category of ultrasolid modules, a variation of the solid modules of Clausen and Scholze that give a well-behaved category akin to topological modules. To approach this result, we decompose deformation problems into n-nilpotent layers. Each of these layers is individually easier to understand, and is classified by simpler variants of partition Lie algebras.

#5 Positivity of Wahl defects and representations from index-one covers
著者: Heesang Park, Dongsoo Shin
公開日: Fri, 16 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21092
要約:
Let \((X,E) \to (V,0)\) be the minimal good resolution of a normal complex surface singularity, with \(E\) reduced. The Wahl defect is \(\Delta(V,0) :=h^1(\mathcal{O}_X)-h^1(S_X)+h^1(\bigwedge^2 S_X)\), where \(S_X=\Theta_X(-\log E)\). For non-Gorenstein \(\mathbb{Q}\)-Gorenstein singularities \((V,0)\), we prove that \(\Delta(V,0)\geq0\), with equality precisely in the quasi-homogeneous case. This proves Wahl's Main Conjecture in this class and his \(\mathbb{Q}\)-Gorenstein and Rational Conjectures. The proof expresses \(\Delta\) as the multiplicity of the canonical character in a representation constructed from differential forms and residues. We also prove \(\Delta+4p_g\geq0\) for arbitrary normal surface singularities, with equality precisely for rational quasi-homogeneous singularities. For smoothings of rational surface singularities, we give a Riemann--Roch formula for the lengths of the cokernels of the restriction maps to the central fiber for all reflexive canonical powers, in terms of the Milnor number and periodic local Riemann--Roch corrections. This yields numerical and representation-theoretic criteria for \(\mathbb{Q}\)-Gorenstein smoothings, defined as quotients of equivariant smoothings of the index-one cover with trivial group action on the base. If a rational singularity admits a \(\mathbb{Q}\)-Gorenstein smoothing, such smoothings are precisely those with minimal Milnor number, or equivalently those whose smoothing components have minimal dimension. Combining these results, we prove that every rational homology disk smoothing is \(\mathbb{Q}\)-Gorenstein, has a rational weighted homogeneous central fiber, and occurs over a one-dimensional smoothing component. Together with the known weighted homogeneous classification, this completes the analytic classification of normal surface singularities admitting rational homology disk smoothings.
#6 Nonlinear neighborhoods of smooth curves and arbitrarily negative Wahl defects
著者: Heesang Park, Dongsoo Shin
公開日: Fri, 16 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21093
要約:
We develop a theory of nonlinear neighborhoods of smooth projective curves of genus at least two, constructed by gluing products of open subsets of a curve with a disc using nonlinear changes of the disc coordinate. Their zero sections have normal bundles of negative degree and contract to normal surface singularities. For the minimal good resolution \((X,E) \to (V,0)\) of a normal complex surface singularity other than a rational double point, the Wahl defect is defined by \(\Delta(V,0) := h^1(\mathcal{O}_X)-h^1(S_X)+h^1(\bigwedge^2S_X)\), where \(E\) is reduced and \(S_X = \Theta_X(-\log E)\). We give a finite formula for this defect in terms of extension obstructions when \(E\) is a single smooth curve. For our constructed neighborhoods, the formula involves extensions of functions alone, includes obstructions at every order, and gives a sharp lower bound depending only on the genus. We apply the theory to hyperelliptic curves and smooth plane curves. Both families consist of singularities that are neither \(\mathbb{Q}\)-Gorenstein nor quasi-homogeneous. The hyperelliptic examples realize every nonpositive integer as a Wahl defect and attain the genus bound in every genus at least two. They disprove Wahl's Main Conjecture, which asserts nonnegativity for non-Gorenstein singularities, with equality precisely in the quasi-homogeneous case.
#7 A refinement of the strange duality conjecture on K3 surfaces
著者: Shizhuo Zhang, Yu Zhao
公開日: Fri, 16 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21272
要約:
Let $v,w$ be primitive Mukai vectors of positive rank on a polarized K3 surface with $\chi(v\otimes w)=0$. Marian and Oprea conjectured that the strange duality map $H^0(M(v),\Theta_w)^\vee\to H^0(M(w),\Theta_v)$ is either zero or an isomorphism. We give three families of counterexamples to this conjecture. In each of them the map is nonzero but fails to be an isomorphism: in the first (a quartic surface) it is not surjective; in the second (Picard rank one, including very general polarized K3 surfaces) the source has strictly larger dimension than the target; and in the third (an elliptic surface) source and target both have dimension six, yet the map has rank five. The three examples share a common mechanism: one of the moduli spaces carries an effective divisor of square $-2$ on which a theta line bundle has negative degree, so that this theta line bundle is not nef and has nonvanishing higher cohomology, and the theta sections produced by the strange duality map vanish to excess order along this divisor. We therefore propose a refined conjecture, in which both theta line bundles are assumed to be big and nef, for instance ample.
#8 Fundamental Groups and Coregularity: Klt Pairs with Trivial and Nef Log-Anticanonical Class
著者: Lukas Braun, Fernando Figueroa
公開日: Fri, 16 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21358
要約:
In this article, we study how the absolute coregularity of a projective log pair reflects on its fundamental group. More precisely, we show that for a projective klt log pair $(X,D)$ of absolute coregularity $c$ and arbitrary dimension the fundamental group $\pi_1^{\rm reg}(X,D)$ admits a normal abelian subgroup of finite index and rank at most $2c$. We reduce this statement to klt Calabi-Yau pairs with standard coefficients of dimension $c$. In the cases $0\le c\le2$ and fixed dimension, we can furthermore bound the index of a solvable normal subgroup. Combining the reduction with the recent regular-locus theorem of Fu-Guo-Song-Wang, we prove the coregularity bound in every dimension. In particular, for a projective klt pair $(X,D)$ with $-(K_X+D)$ nef, the orbifold fundamental group of the smooth locus is virtually abelian of rank at most $2\, {\rm dim}X$.

#10 Complete homogeneous varieties over a field
著者: Michel Brion, Matilde Maccan, Srinivasan Srimathy
公開日: Fri, 16 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21497
要約:
The main objects of this paper are complete homogeneous varieties under the action of a semisimple group $G$ over field $k$ of characteristic $p>0$. When the isotropy groups of the geometric points are smooth parabolics, these are classically known as twisted flag varieties and are well studied in the literature. If the smoothness assumption is removed, we get a larger class of varieties which we call as \emph{twisted parabolic varieties}. The main goal of this paper is to study the structure, classification and properties of these varieties. We first understand their structure over a separable closure of $k$ by classifying non-smooth parabolics containing a Borel; this builds on the recently completed classification of such parabolics over an algebraically closed field. Then, via Galois descent we get a combinatorial criterion for such a variety to descend over $k$. We compute the Picard group of twisted parabolic varieties by embedding them into products of twisted flag varieties, study their local rigidity properties and show that they are rational if they have a $k$-rational point for $p \geq 3$. When $k$ is perfect, we show many interesting properties such as existence of a universal purely inseparable cover, transitivity of $k$-rational points under $G(k)$ as well as the existence of a smooth parabolic in the stabilizer of every such point. However, these properties fail if $k$ is imperfect and we demonstrate this with illuminating examples.
