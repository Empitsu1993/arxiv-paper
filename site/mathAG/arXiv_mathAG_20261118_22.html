#1 Quantum $K$-theoretic Whitney relation for Lagrangian Grassmannian
著者: Takafumi Kouno
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21654
要約:
We give a quantum $K$-theoretic analogue of the Whitney relation for the torus-equivariant $K$-ring of the Lagrangian Grassmannian. As a consequence, we give a presentation of the torus-equivariant quantum $K$-ring of the Lagrangian Grassmannian. The strategy for obtaining such relations is to use the quantum $K$-theoretic Whitney relation for the full-flag manifold in type $C$ established by the author and Kato's pushforward map from the quantum $K$-ring of the full-flag manifold to that of the partial flag manifold. Then the Nakayama-type argument established by Gu et al., together with the classical Whitney presentation for the torus-equivariant $K$-ring of the Lagrangian Grassmannian, gives a proof of such a presentation.

#3 Topology of some smooth real Fano threefolds arising from blow-ups along rational curves
著者: Guillaume Kineider
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21866
要約:
We describe the topological blow-up of a fibre of a Seifert fibration. We then apply this result to determine the diffeomorphism types of the real loci of smooth real Fano threefolds obtained by blowing up either $\mathbb{P}^3_{\mathbb{R}}$ or a smooth real quadric threefold along a smooth real rational curve. As an application, we also determine the topology of the real loci of the two real forms of the quintic del Pezzo threefold $V_5$, completing the topological classification of smooth real rational del Pezzo threefolds.
#4 The Nygaard graded piece, the Frobenius--Witt cotangent complex and regularity criteria
著者: Ryo Ishizuka, Kanau Shimada
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22214
要約:
For a $p$-torsionfree $p$-quasisyntomic ring, we construct a fiber sequence relating the $p$-th Nygaard graded piece of absolute prismatic cohomology to the Frobenius--Witt cotangent complex. The latter is an arithmetic analogue of the cotangent complex in which the differential of $p$ need not vanish. We also clarify the relationship between the regularity criterion proved by Mao using the Frobenius--Witt cotangent complex and the regularity criterion established by Bhatt and Mathew using the Nygaard graded piece. This relationship was anticipated by Bhatt and Mathew.
#5 The injectivity of the Kudla--Millson lift on unitary Shimura varieties
著者: Hanyu Yao

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21692
要約:
We prove the injectivity of the Kudla--Millson lift on unitary Shimura varieties over imaginary quadratic fields under some lattice hypotheses. We express integrals of the lift over special cycles as special values of Dirichlet series and deduce injectivity by separating the Fourier coefficients of the cusp forms. We also establish a converse theorem for Borcherds products on $\mathrm{U}(n,1)$.
#6 Bounded functions on the character variety for quadratic extensions of $\mathbb{Q}_p$
著者: Laurent Berger, Johannes Sprang, Rustam Steingart
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21796
要約:
For any finite extension $L$ of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, Schneider--Teitelbaum defined and studied the character variety in $p$-adic Fourier theory. It is a rigid analytic curve over $L$ that parameterizes the set of locally $L$-analytic characters of $o_L$. It is conjectured that all bounded functions on the character variety of Schneider--Teitelbaum come from $p$-adic measures. We prove this conjecture for all quadratic extensions $L/\mathbb{Q}_p$.
#7 A local-local principle for quadratic forms over semiglobal fields
著者: Karim Johannes Becher, David Grimm, Parul Gupta
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21870
要約:
Given a quadratic form over a semiglobal field, the set of discrete valuations on the field for which the form is anisotropic over the completion is either empty, or it contains a valuation extending that of the base field with a transcendental residue field extension. In dimension at least $3$, this gives a refinement of the local-global principle for isotropy. In dimension $2$, the result yields a description of the set of elements that are locally squares everywhere.
#8 Hyperk\"ahler Periods in Families of Parabolic Higgs Bundle Moduli Spaces
著者: Panagiotis Dimakis, Laura Fredrickson, Arya Yae
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21990
要約:
We consider the period map on families of parabolic Higgs bundle moduli spaces with varying parabolic weights $\alpha$ and Higgs field eigenvalues $\sigma$. We assemble the moduli spaces into a smooth family $\pi:\mathscr M\to\Lambda$ where $\Lambda$ encodes $(\alpha,\sigma)$, and define parallel transport for homology along paths in an open dense subset $\Lambda_0\subset\Lambda$. By generalizing a classical theorem of Duistermaat and Heckman, we prove that the period map is affine linear in $(\alpha,\sigma)$, with slope governed by the curvature class of a principal torus bundle over $\mathscr M$. For the four-dimensional moduli spaces of types $E_6,E_7,E_8$, we compute these classes to get explicit formulas for the period maps.
#9 Picard Groups of Spectral Varieties and Moduli of Higgs Sheaves
著者: Xiaoyu Su, Bin Wang
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.10296
要約:
We study moduli spaces of Higgs sheaves valued in line bundles and the associated Hitchin maps on surfaces. We first work out Picard groups of generic (very general) spectral varieties which holds for dimension of at least 2, i.e., a Noether--Lefschetz type theorem for spectral varieties. We then apply these Picard-group calculations to obtain a necessary and sufficient condition for the non-emptiness of generic Hitchin fibers in the range where the spectral Noether--Lefschetz theorem applies. Then we move on to detect the geometry of the moduli spaces of Higgs sheaves as the second Chern class varies.
#10 Secant sheaves on abelian n-folds with real multiplication and Weil classes on abelian 2n-folds with complex multiplication
著者: Eyal Markman
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23079
要約:
Let K be a CM-field, i.e., a totally complex quadratic extension of a totally real field F. Let X be a g-dimensional abelian variety admitting an algebra embedding of F into the rational endomorphisms End_Q(X) of X. Let A be the product of X and Pic^0(X). We construct an embedding e of K into End_Q(A) associated to a choice of an F-bilinear polarization h on X and a purely imaginary element q in K. We get the [K:Q]-dimensional subspace HW(A,e) of Hodge Weil classes in the d-th cohomology of A, where d:=4g/[K:Q]. Let V be the first cohomology of A. The even cohomology S^+ of X is the half-spin representation of the group Spin(V) and so the projectivization P(S^+) contains the even spinorial variety. The latter is a component of the Grassmannian of maximal isotropic subspaces of V. We associate to (h,q) a rational 2^[F:Q]-dimensional subspace B of S^+ such that P(B) is secant to the spinorial variety. Associated to two coherent sheaves G and G' on X with Chern characters in B we obtain the object E in D^b(A) by applying Orlov's equivalence between D^b(XxX) and D^b(A) to the outer tensor product of G and G'. The flat deformations of a normalized Chern class k(E) of E remain of Hodge type under every deformation of (A,e) as an abelian variety (A',e') of Weil type. We provide a criterion for the tensor product of ch(G) and ch(G') to belong to the open subset in the tensor square of B for which the algebraicity of the flat deformation of k(E) implies the algebraicity of all classes in HW(A',e'). The algebraicity would thus follow if E is semiregular in the appropriate sense. Examples of such secant sheaves G and G' are provided for X the Jacobian with real multiplication by a real quadratic number field F of a genus 4 curve. The search for semiregular such E is left for future work.
#11 On the decomposition of the diagonal for n-fold hypersurfaces via cubic n-1-folds
著者: Elia Fiammengo, Morten L\"uders
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07111
要約:
We study the question of the existence of a decomposition of the diagonal for very general degree $d\geq 4$ and $(2,d\geq 3)$-complete intersection $n$-folds. Using cycle-theoretic techniques of Lange, Pavic and Schreieder we reduce the question to the existence of such a decomposition for $n-1$-dimensional cubic hypersurfaces. This extends results of Nicaise and Ottem from stable rationality to the existence of a decomposition of the diagonal. A key ingredient is the essential dimension of cubic hypersurfaces due to Voisin. As an application, we use a recent result of Engel, de Gaay Fortman and Schreieder on the decomposition of the diagonal for cubic threefolds to give a new proof of the retract irrationality of a very general complex quartic $4$-fold, originally due to Totaro, and of a very general complex $(2,3)$-complete intersection $4$-fold, originally due to Skauli. As another application we show that a very general quintic fourfold containing a three-dimensional linear subspace with multiplicity $2$, which is unirational due to results of Massarenti, does not admit a decomposition of the diagonal and is therefore not retract rational.

#14 Tropical Matsusaka and Matsusaka--Ran Criteria
著者: Zhijie Ji
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30148
要約:
The tropical Schottky problem asks which principally polarized tropical abelian varieties are isomorphic to tropical Jacobian varieties equipped with their canonical principal polarizations. We prove the tropical Matsusaka criterion and the tropical Matsusaka--Ran criterion, which are the main results of this paper, and show that their hypotheses are equivalent. Together with the tropical Poincar\'e formula proved by Gross--Shokrieh, these results give a geometric solution to the tropical Schottky problem. In particular, we give an affirmative answer to a question posed by Brannetti--Melo--Viviani. Several tools are developed for the proofs of the two criteria. In particular, we establish Hodge-type inequalities for ample tropical Cartier divisors on tropical abelian varieties and prove a splitting criterion for principally polarized tropical abelian varieties.
#15 The Constant in Thomae-Type Formulas for Eight Points on the Complex Projective Line
著者: Ryunosuke Nakano
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06439
要約:
We consider the family of cyclic fourfold covers $C(x)$ of the complex projective line branched at eight points $x=(x_1,\ldots,x_8)$. It is known that the period map identifies the configuration space $X(2,8)$ of the branch points with a Zariski open subset of a quotient of the five-dimensional complex ball, and that the inverse of the period map is expressed projectively by $105$ automorphic forms $f_J$, which are proportional to the signed branch-point polynomials $\hat x_J$ with a common scalar factor. We normalize an element $\omega$ of the one-dimensional eigenspace of the deck transformation group acting on the holomorphic differentials on $C(x)$. For the period $\eta$ of $\omega$, we determine the common scalar factor: it is the product of a constant expressed in terms of the gamma function and the square of a quadratic form in $\eta$.
#16 The Legendre potential and the gradient case of the differential Galois criterion for the Jacobian Conjecture
privacy
著者: El\.zbieta Adamus, Zbigniew Hajto
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36736
要約:
Let $h\in\C[X_1,\dots,X_n]$ have constant nonzero Hessian determinant, let $h^{*}$ be its formal Legendre transform and $g=\vv{X,\grad h}-h$. The Nambu derivations of $\grad h$ satisfy $\delta_i(g)=X_i$ and $\delta^\alpha(g)=(\partial^\alpha h^{*})\circ\grad h$. Consequently, in the gradient case the differential Galois criterion of \cite{ACH19}, consisting of $n$ membership conditions, reduces to a single membership condition. Equivalently, $\grad h$ is a polynomial automorphism if and only if $g\in\C[\partial_1h,\dots,\partial_nh]$. Moreover, $\grad h$ is a polynomial automorphism if and only if $\delta^\alpha(g)=0$ for $|\alpha|=(\deg h-1)^{n-1}+2$, and then the coefficients $\delta^\alpha(g)(x_0)/\alpha!$ of $h^{*}$ at $\grad h(x_0)$ determine the inverse. If the Hessian determinant of $h$ is merely a nonzero polynomial, then $g$ is still a primitive element of $\C(X)$ over $\C(\partial_1h,\dots,\partial_nh)$. For the Nambu-tower criterion, doubling a Keller map yields no independent conditions. After an affine change of coordinates putting $\grad h$ in the normal form $X+H$, the formal Legendre transform is the sum of the formal Pascal difference series of $g$, and the termination of this series implies polynomial invertibility. In at most three variables the normal form of every gradient Keller map has strongly nilpotent Jacobian part and is therefore Pascal finite, by a theorem of de Bondt, and an explicit gradient automorphism in four variables has a normal form which is not Pascal finite. The five-variable counterexample of Meng and Yang shows that the properties used here do not imply the Hessian conjecture. The results do not settle the open cases $\JC_2$ and $\HC_4$.
#17 An application of "Selmer group Chabauty" to arithmetic dynamics
著者: Michael Stoll
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.05893
要約:
We describe how one can use the "Selmer group Chabauty" method developed by the author to show that certain hyperelliptic curves of the form $$ C \colon y^2 = x^{2g+1} + h(x)^2 \,, $$ with $h \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ such that $\operatorname{deg} h \le g$ and $h(0)$ is odd, have only the "obvious" rational points $\infty$ (the unique point at infinity on the smooth projective model of the curve) and $(0, \pm h(0))$. Let $c \in \mathbb{Q}$ and write $f_c(x) = x^2 + c$. We denote the iterates of $f_c$ by $f_c^{\circ n}$; i.e., we set $f_c^{\circ 0}(x) = x$ and $f_c^{\circ(n+1)}(x) = f_c(f_c^{\circ n}(x))$. As an application of the method, we prove that if $f_c^{\circ 2}$ is irreducible, then $f_c^{\circ 6}$ is also irreducible. Assuming the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis (GRH), it also follows that $f_c^{\circ 10}$ is irreducible.
#18 Greatest Common Divisors and an Unlikely Intersection Type Conjecture
著者: Keping Huang, Aaron Levin
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.14432
要約:
We prove inequalities involving greatest common divisors of functions at integral points with respect to numerically parallel divisors, generalizing a result of Wang and Yasufuku (after work of Bugeaud-Corvaja-Zannier, Corvaja-Zannier, and the second author). In addition to proving results in a broader context than previously considered, we also study the exceptional set in this setting, for both the counting function and the proximity function. Based on our study of exceptional sets, we propose new conjectures connected to the topic of unlikely intersections. #19 Cohomological VC-density: Bounds and Applications
著者: Saugata Basu, Deepam Patel
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.09670
要約:
The concept of Vapnik-Chervonenkis (VC) density is pivotal across various mathematical fields, including discrete geometry, probability theory and model theory. In this paper, we introduce a topological generalization of VC-density. Let $Y$ be a topological space and $\mathcal{X}$ a family of closed subspaces of $Y$. For each $p \geq 0$, we define a number, $\mathrm{vcd}^{p}_{\mathcal{X}}$, which we refer to as the degree $p$ VC-density of the family $\mathcal{X}$. The classical notion of VC-density within this topological framework can be recovered by setting $p=0$. Our definition of degree $p$ VC-density extends to higher orders as well. For $p \geq 0$, $q \geq 1$, we define the degree $p$, order $q$ VC density $\mathrm{vcd}^{p,q}_{\mathcal{X}}$ of $\mathcal{X}$, which recovers Shelah's notion of higher order VC-density for $q$-dependent families when $p=0$. Our definition introduces a completely new notion when $p > 0$. We examine the properties of $\mathrm{vcd}_{\mathcal{X}}^p$ (as well as $\mathrm{vcd}^{p,q}_{\mathcal{X}}$) when the families $\mathcal{X}$ are definable in structures with some underlying topology (for instance, the Euclidean topology for o-minimal structures over $\mathbb{R}$, the analytic topology over $\mathbb{C}$, or the \'{e}tale site for schemes over arbitrary algebraically closed fields). Our main result establishes that in any model of these theories \[ \mathrm{vcd}_{\mathcal{X}}^p \leq (p+1) \dim X, \] and more generally for any $q \geq 1$ \[ \mathrm{vcd}^{p,q}_{\mathcal{X}} \leq (p+q) \dim X. \] We give examples to show that our bounds are optimal. We also present combinatorial applications of our higher-degree VC-density bounds, deriving higher degree topological analogs of well-known results such as the existence of $\varepsilon$-nets and the fractional Helly theorem.
#20 Non-commutative crepant resolutions of toric singularities with divisor class group of rank one
著者: Ryu Tomonaga
公開日: Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26252
要約:
We classify the basic reflexive modules giving toric non-commutative crepant resolutions (NCCRs) of Gorenstein toric singularities whose divisor class group has rank one. Their isomorphism classes are parametrized by nonempty proper upper sets in a partially ordered quotient of the class group. Each upper set determines a finite subset called its window, which specifies the divisorial summands. The number of summands in each such module equals the normalized volume of the associated lattice polytope, as conjectured by Van den Bergh. If two upper sets differ by one element, their modules are related by a finite sequence of Iyama--Wemyss mutations. Such sequences connect any two of the classified modules, so all toric NCCRs of a fixed singularity are derived equivalent. We construct higher-dimensional analogues of dimer dual quivers when the associated $d$-dimensional lattice polytope is non-pyramidal. The same window determines a cut of a periodic quiver on the $d$-torus. The cut gives an explicit presentation of the endomorphism algebra by a quiver with relations. For $d=2$, the resulting quiver is dual to a dimer model, a bipartite tiling of the two-torus.
