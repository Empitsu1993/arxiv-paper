#2 Convergence of the big J-function of a GKM manifold
著者: Todor Milanov
公開日: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23650
要約:
The genus-$0$ equivariant Gromov--Witten (GW) invariants of a GKM manifold can be computed recursively by a recursion discovered by Brown and studied further by Coates--Corti--Iritani--Tseng. In this paper, we prove a slightly modified version of this result by realizing the J-function as a solution to a fixed-point problem. As an application, we prove that both the equivariant and the non-equivariant quantum cohomologies of a GKM manifold are convergent. We also show that the equivariant central charges have no poles in the spectral parameter, compute their monodromy and their asymptotics at $z=0$, and formulate an equivariant mirror symmetry conjecture for GKM manifolds.
#3 Functorial height and Orlov's rationality conjecture
著者: Xun Lin, Shizhuo Zhang
公開日: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23707
要約:
An exceptional collection in the derived category of a smooth projective variety may fail to generate the whole category, and this failure cannot be detected by K-theory or by Hochschild homology. Kuznetsov's height detects it through Hochschild cohomology, that is, through natural transformations from the identity functor to its shifts. We replace the identity functor by an arbitrary endofunctor $F$ and define the height of a collection with coefficient $F$. It controls, in a range of degrees, the restriction of natural transformations $\mathrm{id}\to F$ to the orthogonal complement of the collection, and it is computed from the collection by a finite complex. For collections of line bundles on surfaces whose Hom divisors have nonnegative canonical degree, the tensor product with a skyscraper sheaf has height exactly two, and the projections of skyscraper sheaves form a two-dimensional family of point-like objects in the orthogonal complement. We apply these tools to Orlov's rationality conjecture. A smooth projective complex surface is rational if its derived category has a full exceptional collection in which all members except possibly one are line bundles, or one that consists of at most three completely orthogonal blocks of arbitrary objects; we also obtain rationality in several cases with four blocks or with a hereditary component. If $S\times\mathbb P^1$ has a full exceptional collection of the form $(M_i\boxtimes\mathcal O(a_i))$, then the $M_i$ with relative degrees of a fixed parity form a full exceptional collection on $S$; consequently $S$ is rational whenever $S\times\mathbb P^1$ has a full exceptional collection of line bundles. Finally, we show that the general case of the conjecture for surfaces is equivalent to the surjectivity and injectivity of two maps in Hochschild cohomology.
#4 Gopakumar-Vafa invariants and Mordell-Weil lattices
著者: Lutian Zhao
公開日: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23735
要約:
Let $X\to B$ be a Weierstrass fibration with integral fibers from a smooth Calabi--Yau threefold to a del Pezzo surface. For a smooth curve $D\subset B$ with smooth inverse image $Y$, we decompose the moduli fiber of stable sheaves with Euler characteristic one and projected cycle $D$. In fiber degree $k$, its components are indexed by all sections $C$ of $Y\to D$ with $\tfrac12\langle C,C\rangle\leq k$, where $\langle\, ,\,\rangle$ is the Mordell--Weil height pairing. #5 Heights and energies of polarized fibrations of varieties
著者: Shou-Wu Zhang
公開日: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23739
要約:
We study nef line bundles on polarized fibrations $(X/C,\bar{H})$, with a fixed nef metrized ample polarization satisfying $H^d=1$, where $d=\dim X$. We prove a quantitative arithmetic height inequality under relative metric positivity, allowing singular arithmetic models and relatively nef adelic bundles. The height deficit defines a local energy. For nef, relatively ample line bundles over finitely generated fields, this energy is bounded below for almost every complex or nonarchimedean local-field realization of fixed polarized global model data, outside one null set common to the entire relative metric class. The results include big non-semiample examples. For ample $L$ and smooth positively curved $\bar{H}$, the complex critical equation is the Calabi--Yau equation; at zero volume, projective bundles recover Donaldson's functional. We also study critical metrics and conjectural canonical heights defined by energy envelopes.

#11 A semialgebraic description of matrices of positive semidefinite rank at most 2
著者: Kaie Kubjas, Lilja Mets\"alampi, Milo Orlich
公開日: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24237
要約:
The main result of this paper is a semialgebraic description of the set of matrices of rank at most 3 and psd rank at most 2. Such a semialgebraic description allows one to check whether a matrix has rank at most 3 and psd rank at most 2. This description builds on the geometric characterization of matrices of rank 3 and psd rank 2 using the existence of an ellipse nested between two polygons. The main step towards deriving the semialgebraic description is the observation that we need to consider only finitely many ellipses that go through some of the vertices of the inner polygon and are tangent to some of the edges of the outer polygon. Although the description is not completely quantifier-free, for a general matrix, the quantified variables satisfy a system of polynomial equations that has at most four solutions corresponding to reduced conics.

#15 Positivity and uniform log-concavity for rank-two Chern coefficients
著者: Hang-Cheng Dong, Changle Zhao
公開日: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24563
要約:
We study the binomial-basis coefficients in the Schur expansion of the Chern classes of symmetric powers of a rank-two bundle. B\'erczi and Feh\'er conjectured that these coefficients are nonnegative and log-concave in the Newton index. An inverse-series formula yields an integral gamma-positive refinement with exact support and a positive expression for the derivatives of the shifted Chern quotient. We prove that generalized Bernoulli polynomials of real order at least their degree have simple real zeros. Applied to the shifted quotient, this gives strict ultra-log-concavity in the Schur index at every positive shift. For the original Newton coefficients, we establish strict log-concavity with an explicit multiplicative margin in two uniform regions. A Hahn projection gives a square-root band about the balanced Schur index; a complex saddle estimate extends this to a band of linear width. Uniform estimates for the cumulants of a normalized Schur functional give a terminal Newton region of linear width. Polynomial coefficient identities also establish every row with Schur index at most six or imbalance at most nine. The argument separates log-concavity in the Schur, derivative and Newton indices and identifies the factorial factors in the transfer between the latter two. The unrestricted Newton log-concavity conjecture remains open.
#16 Cox rings of Jacobian elliptic surfaces of Kodaira dimension one
著者: Sichen Li, Antonio Laface
公開日: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24570
要約:
We study Cox rings of relatively minimal semistable Jacobian elliptic surfaces over $\mathbb P^1$ with $\chi=\chi(\mathcal O_X)\ge3$ and finite Mordell--Weil group. When the Mori cone is generated by the zero section and the reducible-fiber components, we give a finite criterion for Cox-ring generation: a numerically determined set of nef classes must admit sections nonvanishing along the zero section. #26 Superspecial curves of genus two over prime fields: Frobenius types and rational (2,2)-kernels
著者: Hung Dang
公開日: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23694
要約:
Superspecial curves of genus two over a prime field $\mathbb{F}_p$, and the rational $(2,2)$-isogenies between their Jacobians, are the vertices and edges of the graph on which genus-two isogeny-based cryptography is built. Let $C$ be such a curve, with Jacobian $J$ and Frobenius endomorphism $\pi$. We show that $\pi^2 = p\,\alpha_*$ for a unique automorphism $\alpha$ of $C$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$ whose action on the Weierstrass points is the square of that of Frobenius. For $p \ge 7$ this gives the list of Frobenius types of the Weierstrass points: eight types, each with its Weil polynomial, a congruence condition on $p$, and its number 15, 3, 3, 7, 1, 1, 3 or 1 of $\mathbb{F}_p$-rational $(2,2)$-kernels; when all Weierstrass points are rational, the 2-Tate pairing on $J[2]$ is determined by $p \bmod 8$. We then prove closed formulas, in terms of class numbers of imaginary quadratic orders and of $\zeta_{\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt p)}(-1)$, for the mass of the curves of each type and for the mass of the rational kernels whose quotient is a product of elliptic curves. For $\pi^2 = \pm p$ the total masses are due to Ibukiyama and to Xue and Yu; our proofs use the 2-adic Tate modules, the symmetry of the Richelot correspondence and Eichler's mass formula, and, for the other curves, modules over orders of a quartic CM field. The kernels are counted through gluings of supersingular elliptic curves. As a consequence, the proportion of rational kernels with decomposed quotient is of order $p^{-1/2}(1+L(1,\chi))$, where $\chi$ is the quadratic character of $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-p})$. All formulas agree with an exact enumeration for the 37 primes $7 \le p \le 173$. Finally we describe the graph of $\mathbb{F}_p$-rational Richelot isogenies, which is far from connected in the range computed, and discuss what these results say about its use in isogeny-based cryptography.
#27 Optimal homological stability: bounding secondary terms of Cohen-Lenstra moments and $S_d$-fields over $\mathbb{F}_q[t]$

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23721
要約:
We prove an explicit stability line for the homology $H_i(\mathrm{Hur}_n^c,\mathbb{Q})$ of any Hurwitz space associated to a finite rack $c$ depending only on its homology up to some finite $n$. Moreover, as the range of $n$ increases, the slope of the resulting stability line converges to the optimal slope.
