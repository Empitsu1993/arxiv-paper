#1 Closedness of bounded strength and partition rank loci
著者: Benjamin Baily, Alessandro Danelon
公開日: Thu, 22 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28792
要約:
We show the existence of a quartic form of strength $3$ in the closure of the strength at most $2$ locus, answering a question of Ballico--Bik--Oneto--Ventura in the negative. In the analogous setting of partition rank as introduced by Naslund, we further show that the locus of tensors of partition rank at most $2$ is closed, but the locus of tensors of partition rank at most $3$ is not. Together with a suspension lemma, this answers for all $r$ whether the locus of forms/tensors of strength/partition rank at most $r$ is Zariski-closed.
#2 The Logarithmic A-Discriminant
著者: Madeleine Goertz, Bernd Sturmfels, Simon Telen
公開日: Thu, 22 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29204
要約:
We extend the GKZ theory of discriminants to the setting of Euler integrals where both coefficients and exponents are variables. The integrand is the likelihood function of a toric model in algebraic statistics, whose number of critical points is the volume of the Newton polytope. Our logarithmic A-discriminant vanishes when two critical points come together. This discriminant is a hypersurface, parametrized by the logarithmic Horn uniformization. Hurwitz forms of toric varieties yield a degree formula, and a description of leading coefficients. We emphasize applications and computations.

#6 Lax descent and foliated Verdier duality
著者: Tobias Dyckerhoff, Till Heine, Simon Schneider, Kostiantyn Tolmachov
公開日: Thu, 22 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29977
要約:
Higher categorical methods allow us to view recollements as lax descent data and build a corresponding descent theory governed by an $\infty$-bicategorical theory of limits. We demonstrate some concrete innovations brought along by these new techniques in the classical context of constructible and perverse sheaves on stratified spaces. Our first application uses partially (op)lax descent to recover the famous result of Beilinson telling us how to glue perverse sheaves in terms of vanishing and nearby cycles. The remainder of the article is concerened with establishing a uniform view on a series of classification results for perverse sheaves culminating in recent work of Kapranov-Schechtman. Again, this is obtained as an application of lax descent, and seems to provide a reasonable framework to formalize the idea of ``mixed sheaf-cosheaf objects'' promoted by Kapranov and Schechtman.

#8 The recent counterexamples to the Eisenbud-Schreyer-Weyman Ulrich problem (2003) and to Hochster's small Cohen-Macaulay conjecture (1973): a survey, with a generalization to arbitrary dimension
著者: Cristian Anghel
公開日: Thu, 22 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30027
要約:
In September 2026 four preprints and an AI-generated manuscript moved one numerical obstruction from projective geometry to local algebra. For a smooth complex projective surface $Y$ with an ample, globally generated divisor $H$ such that $H^2<\sigma(Y)$, the signature, one obtains in turn: no Ulrich bundles when $H$ is very ample (Anghel, arXiv:2609.02718; extended to every dimension by Lopez, arXiv:2609.06678), answering negatively the Eisenbud-Schreyer-Weyman question of 2003 for sheaves supported on the whole variety; no arithmetically Cohen-Macaulay bundles, i.e. section rings without graded maximal Cohen-Macaulay modules in characteristic zero (Anghel, arXiv:2609.15589); and complete local domains without small Cohen-Macaulay modules, counterexamples to Hochster's 1973 conjecture, if $K_Y\equiv4H$ (Chen, arXiv:2609.24142, proposed) or $\sigma>2H^2$ (OpenAI, released 6 October). We recall the definitions and main statements, and add two things. First, through a finite projection to $\mathbb P^2$ and an identity already present in the OpenAI manuscript, the three obstructions become one variance identity; for surfaces this explains the factor $2$ between the graded bound $\sigma\le H^2$ and the local $\sigma\le2H^2$. Second, we generalize the argument of the OpenAI manuscript to every dimension $m\ge2$, with complete proofs: if $X$ is a smooth complex projective variety of dimension $m$ and $H$ an ample, globally generated divisor, a small Cohen-Macaulay module over the completed section ring of $(X,H)$ forces $(K_X^2-2c_2(X))\cdot H^{m-2}\le(m+4)H^m$. Consequently the completed section rings of all Hesse pairs, including the ball quotient of the first preprint, have no small Cohen-Macaulay module, and products of Hesse surfaces with projective spaces give complete local domains with an isolated singularity, of every dimension $\ge3$, without small Cohen-Macaulay modules.
#9 On OpenAI's proof of the Nagata conjecture
著者: Jenia Tevelev
公開日: Thu, 22 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30050
要約:
These notes are an expanded account of my talk at UMass Amherst on OpenAI's remarkably simple proof of the Nagata conjecture. I hope they will be useful, especially to non-experts. The standard reduction to the normal cone and the collision of fat points into one monomial scheme reduce the problem to interpolation of monomial schemes on the normal ruled surface of a smooth plane cubic. The novelty is that the study of the asymptotics of theta functions as the cubic develops a node, together with interpolation duality, reduces the problem further to interpolation on a triangle of lattice points, which is straightforward. The same calculation determines the nef and pseudoeffective cones of a weighted blow-up of ${\Bbb P}^2$ at one point for very general local parameters when the ratio of weights is greater than nine.

#11 Pontryagin-Thom, $\pi_0$ of the motivic sphere, and principal quadratic divisors
著者: Kyle Ormsby
公開日: Thu, 22 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29065
要約:
Segal computed $\pi_0$ of the equivariant sphere spectrum as the Burnside ring by a Pontryagin-Thom construction, transversality, and marks. We carry out the same argument in the motivic setting over a field $k$: the Burnside ring is replaced by the Grothendieck ring $A^u(\Gamma_k)$ of finite \'etale $k$-schemes decorated with a unit, the marks by the Dress map $d(L,u)=\mathrm{tr}_{L/k}\langle u\rangle$ into $\mathrm{GW}(k)$, and a Pontryagin-Thom map $A^u(\Gamma_k)\to\pi_0(\mathbb{S}_k)(k)$ is shown to be surjective with kernel $\ker(d)$. This gives $\pi_0(\mathbb{S}_k)(k)\cong\mathrm{GW}(k)$ for infinite perfect fields of characteristic $\ne 2$ without Morel's computation. We then identify $\ker(d)$, by a reciprocity law on $\mathbb{P}^1$, as the group of principal quadratic divisors of rational functions, so that $\pi_0$ of the motivic sphere is the unit-decorated Burnside ring of $\Gamma_k$ modulo principal quadratic divisors. The transversality step is proved by an elementary "re-topping" argument.
#12 On the gradient conjecture at infinity: Finite spherical length for gradient trajectories of definable potentials
著者: Thang Do, Arnulf Jentzen, Benno Kuckuck
公開日: Thu, 22 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29211
要約:
We prove that for every open $ U \subseteq \mathbb R^n $ the spherical projection of every gradient trajectory $\theta(t)$, $t \geq 0$, of a definable $C^1$-potential $F \colon U \to \mathbb R$ has finite length and, in particular, converges (directional convergence). In other words, every gradient trajectory of a definable $C^1$-potential has finite total angular motion. Here, definable means definable in an arbitrary o-minimal structure expanding the real field. Notably, the gradient trajectory in this result is an arbitrary solution of the gradient flow differential equation (whose solutions may not be unique) with no further assumptions: The gradient trajectory may be unbounded and diverge to infinity or it may be convergent. The established theorem therefore provides affirmative answers to both the gradient conjecture at infinity for definable potentials and the definable Thom gradient conjecture. We apply our theorem to the training of deep neural networks (DNNs) to conclude that, in the case of definable and continuously differentiable activation and loss functions, the spherical projection of the DNN training flow must always converge (directional convergence).

#15 Topological degree of fewnomial maps
著者: Fr\'ed\'eric Bihan
公開日: Thu, 22 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29808
要約:
We study the topological degree on the positive orthant of a map $F:\mathbb{R}^n_{>0}\to\mathbb{R}^n$ whose components are real polynomials, possibly with real exponents, with the same support $\mathcal{A}$ of $N=n+k+1$ monomials. This degree is well defined under a half-space condition on the columns of the coefficient matrix indexed by the faces of the Newton polytope, which holds for generic exponents and coefficients. We give three formulas for it, all in terms of signs of maximal minors. The first one is a Viro formula: the degree is a signed count of the simplices of a regular triangulation of $\mathcal{A}$ which are positively decorated in the sense of Bihan, Santos and Spaenlehauer. The second one is a sum over the facets of the Newton polytope, and the third one a sum over the vertices of the polyhedron of the Gale dual system. As a consequence, the degree only depends on the oriented matroids of the exponent and coefficient matrices restricted to the vertices of the Newton polytope. We also obtain a Descartes rule of signs for the degree and, in the generic case, the upper bound $|\deg F|<\frac12\min(M(n,N),M(k,N))$, where $M(d,N)$ is the largest number of facets of a $d$-polytope with $N$ vertices. This bound is sharp when $n=2$ or $k=2$. Using the H-duality of Bihan, Dickenstein and Forsg{\aa}rd, we show that the set of possible degrees is symmetric in $n$ and $k$. Since a nonzero degree forces a positive zero, our formulas give sign conditions for the existence of positive solutions, as well as parity statements for their number.

#18 Nashtopes and their A-discriminants
著者: Elke Neuhaus, Irem Portakal, Luca Sodomaco, Katherine Tung
公開日: Thu, 22 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29889
要約:
An $n$-player game in normal form is said to be of format $\boldsymbol{d} = (d_1, \dots, d_n)$ if the $i$-th player has $d_i$ options for $1 \le i \le n$, and is said to be balanced if $d_n - 1 \le \sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(d_i - 1)$. The Nash equilibria of the game correspond to solutions of the system of indifference equations for the game, with payoff differences as coefficients and strategy probabilities as variables. As such, the mixed discriminant of this system gives some information about which games could have an unusual number of totally mixed Nash equilibria. This mixed discriminant can be regarded as an $\mathcal{A}$-discriminant for some matrix $\mathcal{A}$ in the sense of [Gelfand--Kapranov--Zelevinsky 1994], and the convex hull of the columns of $\mathcal{A}$ is a polytope, which we call the Nashtope. We study this $\mathcal{A}$-discriminant for each balanced game format with at least three players. This discriminant is a subset of the Nash discriminant variety introduced in [Abo--Portakal--Sodomaco 2026]. We compute the codimension and degree of this discriminant for all balanced game formats, including on-boundary formats, using the Euler obstruction formula in [Matsui--Takeuchi 2011]. Our $\mathcal{A}$-discriminant locus corresponds to a special multigraded Hurwitz locus studied in [Pratt--Sodomaco--Sturmfels 2026]. As a consequence of [Pokidkin 2026], we obtain the codimensions and degrees of the irreducible components of the Nash discriminant variety for each balanced game format with at least three players, fully resolving Problem 3.8 of [Abo--Portakal--Sodomaco 2026].
