#1 Arinkin's sheaf and Hilbert schemes
著者: David Zhiyuan Bai
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26699
要約:
Arinkin's sheaf gives a relative Fourier-Mukai autoequivalence of a general Beauville-Mukai system, extending the usual Poincar\'e bundle over the smooth locus. We provide an alternative construction of it as a deformation of the Fourier-Mukai autoequivalence on the Hilbert scheme of an elliptic K3 surface obtained by lifting the classical Poincar\'e kernel for an elliptic surface. As applications, we show that the $P=C$ property for a general Beauville-Mukai system is equivalent to that for the Hilbert scheme fibration, thus providing new proofs for both. We also construct an explicit deformation that connects Markman's construction of a projectively hyperholomorphic bundle on products of hyper-K\"ahler manifolds of K3${}^{[g]}$-type to that of Hartlieb and Shah. This answers a question of Hartlieb and Shah.
#2 Opposite filtrations on the parabolic compactified Jacobian
著者: Oscar Kivinen
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26789
要約:
We show that the Hikita filtration on the cohomology of the parabolic compactified Jacobian of the plane curve singularity $x^m=y^n$, $(m,n)=1$, is opposite to the perverse filtration in a precise sense. This implies the parabolic Hilb-vs-Quot conjecture from https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19633, and consequently the main conjecture from https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.2115 for these singularities. The new ingredient is an analysis of how the Bott-Mitchell filtration on the homology of the affine Grassmannian of $SL_n$ relates to both of the above filtrations.
#3 Tensor products of additive groups as reciprocity sheaves
著者: Toshiro Hiranouchi, Rin Sugiyama
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26947
要約:
We compute the Nisnevich reciprocity sheaves associated with tensor products of the cube-invariant objects for the additive and multiplicative groups, with at least one additive factor. Over a perfect field of characteristic different from two, these sheaves decompose into direct sums of absolute K$\"a$hler differential sheaves with binomial multiplicities. As an application, we compute the associated reciprocity sheaves of the Hochschild homology of the additive object.

#5 Period maps and the $C_{\rm st}$ conjecture
著者: Pierre Colmez, Wies{\l}awa Nizio{\l}, Xinyu Shao
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27054
要約:
We construct a family of elliptic curves over an open disc whose total space violates the $C_{\mathrm{st}}$ conjecture in degree $1$. Hence the conjecture, known for proper and for Stein varieties, as well as for products of such, does not extend to proper families over Stein bases. This is an instance of a general criterion: the $C_{\mathrm{st}}$ conjecture holds for the total space of a smooth proper family over a sufficiently small open disc if and only if its period map is constant, i.e., all Gauss--Manin Higgs fields vanish or, equivalently, the $p$-adic local systems $R^if_*\mathbf{Q}_p$ are geometrically constant. This criterion is special to curves: over a $2$-dimensional polydisc, we give a family of $p$-adic analogues of non-K\"ahler elliptic surfaces whose period maps and local systems are constant, but whose total space violates the conjecture.
#6 Demazure-Lusztig type operators and correspondences on Kato Catalan varieties
著者: Taiyo Kodama
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27081
要約:
We study the integral equivariant cohomology rings of Kato Catalan varieties $\mathscr{X}_\Psi$ associated with arbitrary root ideals $\Psi$ in type $A$. These varieties are smooth projective compactifications of the vector subbundles $T^*_\Psi\mathcal{B}$ of the cotangent bundle of the complete flag variety $\mathcal{B}$. Let $K$ denote the product of the general linear group (or its maximal torus) with the loop rotation torus $\mathbb{C}^\times$. We give an explicit presentation of $H^*_K(\mathscr{X}_\Psi;\mathbb{Z})$ with relations expressed in terms of divided difference and Demazure-Lusztig type operators. Furthermore, for every simple reflection preserving $\Psi\cup(-\Psi)$, we construct a geometric realization of the associated Demazure-Lusztig type operator on $H^*_K(\mathscr{X}_\Psi;\mathbb{Z})$ via correspondences. These correspondences yield a faithful graded action of the subalgebra of the degenerate affine Hecke algebra generated by these reflections and the polynomial subalgebra. For the maximal root ideal $\Psi=\Delta^+$, our construction recovers the cohomological counterpart of the $K$-theoretic Steinberg correspondences of Cautis and Kamnitzer.
#7 Combinatorial relative orientability for toric and tropical geometry
著者: Nathan Tiggemann
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27238
要約:
Many problems in enumerative geometry are solved by computing the degree of the top Chern class of a suitable vector bundle. Results from $\mathbb{A}^1$-homotopy theory allow to answer enumerative questions over arbitrary fields, replacing the top Chern class by the Euler class in Chow-Witt groups. For its degree to be well-defined, one needs the vector bundle to be relatively oriented. Another way to solve enumerative problems is to translate them to tropical geometry, where they become combinatorial problems. In this paper, we give a combinatorial description of relative orientability for vector bundles over smooth proper toric varieties. We show that it behaves well under tropicalization, establishing a tropical version of relative orientability, which will allow to the use of tropical geometry to answer enumerative questions over arbitrary fields. As a demonstration, we prove a quadratically enriched version of the Bernstein-Khovanskii-Kushnirenko theorem via tropical geometry. Lastly, we define the notion of $k$-phase structures on tropical hypersurfaces, generalizing the real case.
#8 Chern classes, Poincar\'e polynomials, and Bernstein-Sato polynomials of plane curves
著者: Piotr Pokora
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27297
要約:
We show that the Poincar\'e polynomial of a reduced plane curve $C$ is the Chern polynomial of the dual of the bundle of Jacobian syzygies of $C$. Using the Zeuthen-Segre formula, we prove that its value at $t=-1$ equals the Euler number of the complement of $C$ up to the correction term $\mu(C)-\tau(C)$, so the two numbers coincide if and only if all singularities of $C$ are quasi-homogeneous. As applications, we show that the defect in a combinatorial inequality for free $d$-arrangements is the square of the difference of the exponents, extend the addition technique to arbitrary reduced plane curves, and locate the roots of local Bernstein-Sato polynomials at ordinary singular points.

#10 Classification of strongly $F$-regular and $F$-pure surface singularities with imperfect residue fields

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27597
要約:
We study strongly $F$-regular and $F$-pure normal surface singularities whose residue fields are not necessarily perfect. We characterize strong $F$-regularity in terms of the weighted dual graph and geometric reducedness of the exceptional components of the minimal resolution, extending Hara's classification over algebraically closed residue fields. #11 Projectivized ASEP-Bethe system and counting solutions
著者: Shinsuke Iwao
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27706
要約:
This paper addresses the problem of counting solutions to the ASEP-Bethe ansatz equations. In an earlier preprint, Brattain, Do, and Saenz presented an algebro-geometric method for counting the number of such solutions. As their method relies on sophisticated machinery, it requires advanced knowledge of algebraic geometry and topology. In this paper, we provide an alternative and simpler method for counting solutions, based on a standard intersection theory on projective varieties. We take a conventional approach in algebraic geometry: ``Projectivize the problem and count degrees.'' We introduce a projectivized version of the ASEP-Bethe equations, whose solution set is realized as a subscheme of a certain projective variety. Varying the hopping-rate parameter $p$, we give a one-parameter family $S$ of the solution sets. We show that, for the ASEP with $n$ sites and $m$ particles, the fiber $S|_p$ with generic $p$ contains $\binom{n}{m}$ points with multiplicity, which equals the expected number of admissible Bethe roots to the ASEP-Bethe ansatz equation.

#13 Incidence Correspondences and Chow Motives in the Symplectic (Z/2Z)4-K3 Locus
著者: Sixuan Gu
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27800
要約:
We establish a Chow-motivic framework for projective K3 surfaces with a symplectic (Z/2Z)^4-action. For the universal Heisenberg-invariant quartic family, we show that the generically defined codimension-two cycles on the relative square are generated by three ambient classes and sixteen automorphism graphs, and that these nineteen classes are cohomologically independent. Combining this square-Franchetta property with a finite-cover trace construction and the Kuga-Satake correspondence, we prove that every smooth Heisenberg-invariant quartic K3 surface has a Chow motive of abelian type. We also consider the diagonal complete-intersection family of three quadrics and establish stability of the Heisenberg result under rational Hodge isogenies. For arbitrary projective K3 surfaces with a faithful symplectic (Z/2Z)^4-action, we obtain a motivic reduction to the minimal resolution of the fifteen-node quotient. Applying a rational Kuga-Satake criterion, we establish abelianity of Chow motives for explicit infinite arithmetic subfamilies of all three Garbagnati-Sarti lattice types, including their Picard-rank specializations. Finally, we prove a square-Franchetta theorem for the ten-dimensional family of double-cubic K3 surfaces.
#14 Kneser-Tits over fields of cohomological dimension $\le 2$ (with an appendix by Alexis Bouthier and Federico Scavia)
著者: Kestutis Cesnavicius
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27803
要約:
We settle the Kneser-Tits problem over every field $k$ of (separable) cohomological dimension $\le 2$, thus resolving a conjecture of Gille. More precisely, for such a $k$ and a semisimple, simply connected $k$-group $G$ whose indecomposable factors over $k$ are isotropic, we show that $G(k)$ is generated by "elementary matrices" (the $k$-points of the unipotent radicals of the parabolic $k$-subgroups of $G$), in other words, that $K_1^G(k) \cong 1$. In fact, we show this vanishing already when $k$ has separable $p$-dimension $\le 2$ in the sense of Gille with respect to a subset of the torsion primes $p$ of $G$, and we reinterpret the separable $p$-dimension itself via syntomic cohomology. To approach those $G$ that escape the net of previously known cases, for an inclusion of reductive $k$-groups $M \subset L$, we develop a new method based on the Rost invariant for transferring $R$-equivalence on $L/M$ to $R$-equivalence on $L$. To find homogeneous spaces to which our method fruitfully applies, we use the theories of prehomogeneous vector spaces and of special symplectic representations: concretely, for a $32$-dimensional half-spin representation $V$, we argue that the vectors in its unique open orbit $O \subset V$ admit unique up to interchange decompositions into sums of two pure spinors, and show that various double covers of $O$ built from such decompositions are rational varieties over any base field.
#15 Elementary length of polynomial automorphisms in dimension at least three
著者: El\.zbieta Adamus, Zbigniew Hajto
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27840
要約:
In the plane the number and the degrees of the de Jonqui\`eres letters in a reduced word of a polynomial automorphism are invariants. For $n\ge3$ we study how much of a decomposition of an automorphism $F$ of $K^n$ into elementary maps can be recovered from $F$. Let $\Gamma(K,n)$ be the group of tame automorphisms of $K^n$ with Jacobian determinant $1$, which is generated by the elementary maps, and let $\ell(F)$ be the least number of elementary factors of $F\in\Gamma(K,n)$. The decompositions of length $\#\operatorname{supp}(F)$, the number of coordinates moved by $F$, are exactly those obtained by successive extraction of left elementary factors, which are determined by $F^{-1}$. Decompositions of length $\#\operatorname{supp}(F)+1$ reduce to this case up to one unknown tail, and $\ell(F\times\mathrm{Id}_{K^r})=\ell(F)$ whenever $\ell(F)\le\#\operatorname{supp}(F)+2$. Vasyunin's quadratic automorphism of $K^5$, $\mathrm{char}\,K\ne2$, has exactly six minimal decompositions, of length $4$, which differ in the degrees of their factors. Its dynamical degree is $\sqrt2$, and in characteristic zero its iterates $F^{(m)}$, $m\ge2$, satisfy $5\le\ell(F^{(m)})\le4m$. In characteristic zero the automorphisms of $K^3$ in the normal form of He and Yan satisfy $\ell(F)=\#\operatorname{supp}(F)+1$, and their minimal decompositions form two classes up to natural moves. Together with the classification of Wang and Yan this shows that a map $X+H$ of $K^3$ with $H(0)=0$ and nilpotent $J_H$ is Pascal finite if and only if the components of $H$ are linearly dependent. The Anick automorphism $A$ of $K^4$ satisfies $\ell(A)\ge\#\operatorname{supp}(A)+4=6$ over every field over which $A\in\Gamma(K,4)$, $\ell(A)\le16$ if $\mathrm{char}\,K\ne2$, and $\ell(A\times\mathrm{Id}_K)\le6$ over every field.\ell(A\times\Id_K)\le6$ over every field.
#16 H\"older continuity of meromorphic functions and equisingularity
著者: Fran\c{c}ois Bernard, Antoni Rangachev
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27974
要約:
For a complex analytic variety and an exponent between zero and one, we study the sheaf of meromorphic functions that are locally H\"older continuous with that exponent for the outer metric. It sits between the structure sheaves of the seminormalization and of the Lipschitz saturation of Pham and Teissier. We prove that these sheaves are coherent and that only finitely many of them are distinct, the exponents at which they change being rational numbers governed by the Rees valuations of the ideal of a certain diagonal. Each exponent defines a H\"older saturation of the variety. We show that a variety whose H\"older saturation is smooth for some exponent larger than one third is itself smooth and that one third is sharp. For reduced curve germs we compute the H\"older saturations from the characteristic exponents of the branches and give explicit thresholds, in terms of classical invariants, at which they become the seminormalization, the Lipschitz saturation, or a curve of the same multiplicity. We prove that homeomorphic curves are bi-H\"older homeomorphic for some positive exponent, whereas bi-H\"older homeomorphisms with exponent close enough to one preserve the multiplicity. We then introduce the relative H\"older saturation of a family and prove that it specializes to the saturations of the fibers over a dense Zariski-open subset of the base. This leads to two notions of equisingularity for families of complex analytic varieties, H\"older equisingularity, defined by a bi-H\"older trivialization, and the stronger H\"older equisaturation, defined by the triviality of the relative saturation. For families of reduced curve germs we show that they interpolate between topological, Whitney and Lipschitz equisingularity, with explicit exponents depending only on the invariants of the special fiber.

#18 The weighted fundamental lemma for non-split groups
著者: Cass Halleck-Dub\'e
公開日: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26750
要約:
In this work, we prove the Weighted Fundamental Lemma for Lie algebras for general (quasi-split) unramified $G$. The strategy is to adapt the proof of Chaudouard--Laumon from the split case. In particular, we define the notion of a $\xi$-stable Higgs bundle for a quasi-split reductive group scheme $G$ on a curve, and prove that the stack of such form a proper, finite-type Deligne--Mumford stack over the Hitchin base when $G$ has anisotropic center. We then adapt the global cohomological theorem of Chaudouard--Laumon to the quasi-split setting, and perform the required Frobenius-point count to obtain an identity of adelic orbital integrals. A local-to-global argument deduces the WFL for Lie algebras. In the last chapter, we perform a parallel reduction to deduce Waldspurger's nonstandard WFL from a global version which follows from the same geometric theory. Our work implies the Twisted Weighted Fundamental Lemma in good characteristic, which completes Moeglin--Waldspurger's stabilization of the twisted Arthur--Selberg trace formula. We therefore render unconditional the endoscopic classification for classical groups and an array of downstream consequences in automorphic theory.

This article confirms predictions made by Hopkins' chromatic splitting conjecture in stable homotopy theory.
