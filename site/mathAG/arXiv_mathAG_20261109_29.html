#1 An Explicit Equation for the $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{C})$ Character Variety of $\mathbb{RP}^2 \# \mathbb{RP}^2 \# \mathbb{RP}^2$
著者: Yinxi Chen
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08513
要約:
Let $N$ denote the closed non-orientable surface of Euler characteristic $-1$, i.e. the connected sum of three real projective planes $N = \mathbb{RP}^2 \# \mathbb{RP}^2 \# \mathbb{RP}^2$, with fundamental group presented as $\langle A,B,C \mid A^2 B^2 C^2 = I \rangle$ via the standard symmetric cross-cap model. Building on the classical trace-coordinate methods of Fricke, Vogt, and Goldman for free groups, and on the qualitative study of representation varieties of non-orientable surfaces by Benyash-Krivets and Chernousov, we give an explicit affine equation for a dense open piece of the $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{C})$ GIT character variety $X(N)$. We prove that the trace coordinates $a = \mathrm{tr}(A)$, $b = \mathrm{tr}(B)$, $c = \mathrm{tr}(C)$, $p = \mathrm{tr}(AB)$ satisfy the polynomial relation $c^2 = abp - a^2 - b^2 + 4$, that this hypersurface is smooth and irreducible, and that on the open locus $c \neq 0$ it is isomorphic to the image of $X(N)$ under the trace map, with the remaining trace coordinates $\mathrm{tr}(BC)$, $\mathrm{tr}(CA)$, $\mathrm{tr}(ABC)$ recovered as regular functions on this locus. We further identify the boundary locus $c = 0$ with representations satisfying the matrix identity $A^2 B^2 = -I$, and describe its representation-theoretic structure, while leaving a complete global classification along this locus as an open problem. All key trace identities are proved from Cayley-Hamilton first principles and independently checked numerically against explicit matrices.
#2 Special line bundles on tetragonal curves
著者: Feiyang Lin
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08679
要約:
One can associate a pair of vector bundles $(E, F)$ on $\mathbb{P}^1$ to every tetragonal curve: $E$ is the Tschirnhausen bundle and $F$ records numerical data of the ideal for the curve under its relative canonical embedding. In this note, we show that a Hurwitz--Brill--Noether general tetragonal curve must have balanced $E$, $F$ bundles, and provide counterexamples for the converse when $g = 8$ or $g \geq 10$.
#3 Multi-monopoles and monodromy around the wobbly divisor
著者: Brad Wilson
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08760
要約:
The multi-monopole equations are a generalisation of the Seiberg--Witten equations where the spinor is coupled to an auxiliary vector bundle. The signed count of multi-monopoles on a 3-manifold can vary according to a new type of wall-crossing phenomenon. For products $S^1\times C$, where $C$ is a closed Riemann surface, we relate the wall and chamber structure to loops in the complement of the wobbly divisor of $C$. Such a loop induces a monodromy map on the multi-vortex moduli space, and the count of its fixed points is expected to agree with the count of multi-monopoles on $S^1\times C$ in the given chamber. By studying singularities of framed multi-vortex moduli spaces, we are able to understand these monodromy maps for various loops. Counting fixed points suggests that product 3-manifolds display incredibly rich wall-crossing behaviour.

#6 The splitting type of jet bundles on the projective line
著者: Annet Kyomuhangi, Emanuela Marangone, Claudiu Raicu, Ethan Reed
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08979
要約:
Given a vector bundle $\mathcal{E}$ on a smooth variety X, one can associate to it its jet bundles, or bundles of principal parts. These bundles encode the Taylor expansions of local sections of $\mathcal{E}$ and arise naturally in the study of differential operators. When $X=\mathbb{P}^1$ is the projective line, jet bundles decompose as direct sums of line bundles, making it natural to study their splitting types. The splitting type is well understood in characteristic zero, but exhibits subtler behavior in positive characteristic. Our goal is to give a complete answer in every positive characteristic, and in particular to provide a characterization of semistability for jet bundles on $\mathbb{P}^1$. We also determine their extremal summands and give applications to differential operators and formulas for cohomology of line bundles on infinitesimal thickenings of the diagonal.
#7 Cuspidal sections for varieties of arbitrary dimension
著者: Magnus Carlson, David Corwin
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09080
要約:
We define cuspidal sections for varieties of arbitrary dimension over fields of characteristic zero, extending the definition for curves by Grothendieck. Our notion of cuspidal sections is defined relative to a normal crossings compactification. We prove that the closure of the resulting set is independent of the choice of a compactification. For varieties iteratively constructed from fibrations, we compare our definition to an inductive notion of cuspidal sections, and discuss connections to the section conjecture of Grothendieck.
#8 Centres and semisimple deformations of contraction algebras
著者: Joseph Karmazyn, Shengyong Pan
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09220
要約:
Let $A_{\rm{con}}$ be the contraction algebra of a $3$-fold flop of an irreducible curve, with genus-zero Gopakumar-Vafa invariants $n_1, \dots, n_l$. We show that $\prod_j M_j(\mathbb{C})^{n_j}$ is the only semisimple algebra to which $A_{\rm{con}}$ deforms. Hence $A_{\rm{con}}$ encodes its Gopakumar-Vafa invariants intrinsically, answering a question of Wemyss. Hua and Toda showed that this deformation exists, and Jelisiejew and Smoktunowicz proved its uniqueness in types A and D. The key step in our proof is that the centre of $A_{\rm{con}}$ has dimension $n_1 + \dots + n_l$. To prove this, we lift the contraction algebra of the universal flop to the Cartan subalgebra, where it becomes a corner of a deformed preprojective algebra. We show that the universal contraction algebra and its zeroth Hochschild homology are free over a polynomial ring, with presentations whose determinants are products of roots. Pulling these presentations back along the arc that classifies the flop gives the count, and recovers Toda's formula for $\dim A_{\rm{con}}$. We also prove Hua and Toda's flatness conjecture, and determine which Gopakumar-Vafa invariants occur in lengths 3 to 6.
#9 The Woo-Yong conjecture
著者: Minghua Dou
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09237
要約:
We prove the Woo-Yong conjecture: the non-Gorenstein locus of a type A Schubert variety is the union of its generically non-Gorenstein maximal singular components. More strongly, at every scheme point, local Weil divisor classes are detected at the generic points of the maximal singular components containing that point. For arbitrary finite crystallographic type, the analogous localization map is injective on the torsion subgroup of the source local class group. This determines Cartier indices of locally $\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier divisors and gives the Woo-Yong criterion on the $\mathbb{Q}$-Gorenstein locus. A type D family shows the necessity of this hypothesis: the ambient variety is generically Gorenstein along its singular locus but not $\mathbb{Q}$-Gorenstein along a deeper nonempty locus. The type A proof and the counterexamples are geometric; the general torsion proof also uses bounded finite inputs with explicit certificates and reproducible reconstruction programs.
#10 Dominant root sequences of arbitrary degree
著者: Ling Chen, Chongrui Li, Minggang Wei
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09284
要約:
We show that for root-subgroup multiplication maps, dominance does not imply birationality. This gives a negative answer to a question of Fang, Fourier and Littelmann. More precisely, for every integer $r\geq 2$, we construct a sequence of positive roots in type $A_{3r-1}$; the associated multiplication map is dominant of generic degree $r$, hence not birational. In our construction, a key observation is that Lusztig's flag incidence projection for a triangle quiver is, up to birational equivalence and identity factors, simultaneously a root-subgroup multiplication map and a multiplicative partial Grothendieck--Springer map. Moreover, we extend these examples to types $B$, $C$ and $D$ in every generic degree $r\geq 2$, and to types $E_6$, $E_7$ and $E_8$ in generic degree $2$.
#11 One complex metric and geometric adelic nefness
著者: Kaicheng Dong
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09319
要約:
We prove that an arithmetic adelic line bundle on a smooth quasi-projective variety over a number field has nef geometric part if its prescribed continuous metric at one complex embedding has semipositive first Chern current. The proof constructs semiample approximations to every positive rational ample perturbation by boundary extension, multiplier ideals, and Nadel vanishing, followed by specialization and Galois descent. This gives geometric nefness of Gao--Zhang's canonical Gross--Schoen adelic line bundle.

#14 Simply connected quotients of fake quadrics
著者: Zolt\'an Szab\'o
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09603
要約:
Fake quadrics are minimal complex surfaces of general type with $b_1=0$ and $e=4$. The paper gives a short introduction to their arithmetic construction, aimed at low-dimensional topologists, and studies their holomorphic and antiholomorphic quotients and their fundamental groups. The running example in the paper comes from a quaternion algebra over a totally real cubic field. The lattice is generated by three explicit pairs of matrices. A holomorphic involution gives (after resolving the singularities) a simply connected minimal complex surface of general type with $p_g=0$ and $K^2=4$, a smooth $4$-manifold homeomorphic but non-diffeomorphic to $CP^2$ blown up $5$ times. An antiholomorphic involution gives a smooth four-manifold homeomorphic to $S^4$, with a knotted torus as branch locus.

#16 Exploring the Regular and Singular Loci of Group Representation Varieties
著者: S. Liriano
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09774
要約:
Let $B$ be the Borel Subgroup of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{C})$, and let $G_m=\langle x,y,z\mid x^2y^3=z^{2m+1}\rangle$, $m\geq2$. The $G_m$ are parafree of rank two and deviation one. We compute the integral homology of the regular and singular loci of $X_m=\mathrm{Hom}(G_m,B)$. Each $X_m$ is a four-dimensional irreducible affine variety with the homotopy type of a two-dimensional torus. Its singular locus is a disjoint union of $16m$ affine lines. The regular locus has Betti numbers $b_0=1$, $b_1=2$, $b_2=16m+1$, and $b_3=b_5=16m$, with all other Betti numbers zero. Thus, although each $X_m$ has the homotopy type of a torus and its singular locus is a disjoint union of contractible lines, removing the singular locus creates $16m$ additional independent homology classes in each of degrees $2$, $3$, and $5$, arising from the transverse link $S^2\times S^3$. We also establish that the regular loci are not affine.

#20 Log-concavity of polynomials arising from equivariant cohomology
著者: Yairon Cid-Ruiz, Yupeng Li, Jacob P. Matherne
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.17572
要約:
We study the equivariant cohomology classes of torus-equivariant subvarieties of the space of matrices. For a large class of torus actions, we prove that the polynomials representing these classes (up to suitably changing signs) are covolume polynomials in the sense of Aluffi. We study the cohomology rings of complex varieties in terms of Macaulay inverse systems over $\mathbb{Z}$. As applications, we show that under certain conditions, the Macaulay dual generator is a denormalized Lorentzian polynomial in the sense of Br\"and\'en and Huh, and we give a characteristic-free extension (over $\mathbb{Z}$) of the result of Khovanskii and Pukhlikov describing the cohomology ring of toric varieties in terms of volume polynomials.
#21 Certified Monodromy Groups for Families of Hypersurfaces
著者: Eric Pichon-Pharabod, Simon Telen
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06785
要約:
The monodromy group of a family of algebraic varieties is the group of isometries of the homology of a generic fibre, induced by parallel transport along a loop in the base space. We present a method to compute the monodromy group of families of generically smooth projective hypersurfaces using certified numerical algebraic geometry. This leads us to develop efficient algorithms for computing the discriminants of these families, which is of independent interest. Our flagship example is the case of families of symmetric cubic surfaces. The monodromy group is the Galois group of the 27 lines. We find that, when imposing symmetry, this Galois group is often a small subgroup of the Weyl group $W(E_6)$. We also include computational results for symmetric quartic surfaces.

#23 Geometric Detection and Finiteness of Log Crystalline Local Systems
著者: Zhenmou Liu, Jinbang Yang
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26318
要約:
We prove a geometric detection theorem for log crystalline local systems in the smooth proper setting with a normal crossings boundary. The associated special-fiber log $F$-isocrystal detects geometric absolute irreducibility and, modulo constant rank-one twists, determines the pullback to the geometric generic fiber. The main ingredient is a determinant argument combining a finite-order rank-one reduction, logarithmic period comparison, and the Pl\"ucker embedding. As an application, using Abe--Esnault's finiteness theorem and finiteness of stable lattices, we prove finiteness, up to geometrically constant rank-one twists, of geometrically absolutely irreducible log crystalline local systems of fixed rank. Coefficients may lie in any fixed finite extension of $\mathbb Q_p$, and no bound on the Hodge--Tate weights is imposed.

#27 Quasihomogeneous isolated singularities in terms of syzygies and foliations
著者: Hamid Hassanzadeh, Abbas Nasrollah Nejad, Aron Simis
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16933
要約:
One considers quasihomogeneous isolated singularities of hypersurfaces in arbitrary dimensions through the lenses of three apparently quite apart themes: syzygies, singularity invariants, and foliations. In the first of these, one adds to the well-known result of Saito a syzygy-theoretic characterization of a quasihomogeneous singularity affording an effective computational criterion. In the second theme, one explores the Milnor-Tjurina difference number from a commutative algebra viewpoint. Building on the Briancon-Skoda theorem and exponent, we extend previously known inequalities by Dimca and Greuel to arbitrary dimension and provide algebraic formulas involving the syzygy-theoretic part and reduction exponents. In the last theme, one recovers and brings to algebraic light a result of Camacho and Movasati by establishing a couple of characterizations of quasihomogeneous isolated singularities in terms of the generators of the module of invariant vector fields.

#29 Biholomorphic rigidity and symmetry breaking for domains with Bergman metric of constant positive holomorphic sectional curvature
著者: Gordon Heier, Ravi Mudaliar, Haotian Zuo
公開日: Mon, 09 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13831
要約:
Based on earlier work by Wiegerinck and Huang-Li, Hayashida-Kamimoto constructed unbounded non-smooth Reinhardt domains in \({\mathbb C}^n\), \(n\geq 3\), whose Bergman spaces are finite-dimensional and whose Bergman metrics have constant positive holomorphic sectional curvature \(2\). In this note, we prove a Sandwich Principle for the Hayashida-Kamimoto domains that allows us to construct, inside every prescribed sandwich, uncountably many pairwise biholomorphically inequivalent smooth non-Reinhardt domains whose Bergman metrics also have constant positive holomorphic sectional curvature \(2\). We also classify biholomorphisms between domains sandwiched by the domains of Hayashida-Kamimoto with the help of an affine rigidity theorem in the spirit of Cartan rigidity, which should be of independent interest. For explicit angular perturbation families, we determine the full automorphism group and obtain, in particular, an abundance of examples with trivial automorphism group.
