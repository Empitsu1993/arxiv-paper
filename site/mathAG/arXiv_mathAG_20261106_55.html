#1 Tangle Invariants Valued in Derived Categories of Sheaves and the Rouquier Complex
著者: Jian Qiu, Alex Takeda
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07046
要約:
We develop effective calculation tools for constructing categorified invariants of tangles and knots, following the work of Cautis and Kamnitzer. These invariants, originally constructed by means of Fourier-Mukai transform functors between derived categories of coherent sheaves on certain iterated flag varieties, can be given an alternative algebraic description by using tilting bundles. We use this method and provide several examples of its application, in the hope of showing its utility to explicit pen-and-paper computations. This tilting-bundle method also allows us to lift the construction from derived to homotopy categories of complexes of modules, reducing the calculation of tangle invariants to a type of categorified matrix algebra, where the matrices in question have maps between their elements and calculations get turbocharged with chain homotopy.

#3 Isospectral Deformations of Diagonal Matrices by Symmetric Matrices in Dimension Four
著者: Hari Asokan
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07150
要約:
For a diagonal matrix $D$ with distinct diagonal entries in an algebraically closed field $K$ we study the symmetric matrices $Q$ with entries in $K$ such that the affine line $D+tQ$ has the same eigenvalues in $K$ as $D$. We study the equality of characteristic polynomials, $\chi_{D+tQ}(x)=\chi_D(x) \text{ for all }\: t\in K$ and examine the resulting system of equations. For generic diagonal matrices of size at most $4$ we show that only the trivial symmetric matrix $Q=0$ satisfies this isospectral condition. We use this result to compute the degree of a field extension that appears in the invariant theory of quadratic forms under congruence action.

#7 Bernstein-Sato polynomials of generic Pfaffian ideals
著者: Yifan Chen Huaiqing Zuo
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07571
要約:
We determine all roots and their multiplicities in the Bernstein--Sato polynomial of every generic Pfaffian ideal. In the nonmaximal case, the roots lie in explicit rational windows indexed by the rank strata. Integral roots retain their label multiplicities, whereas every nonintegral root is simple, including values with several labels. The proof combines graph-module relations, skew Capelli operators, central characters, and a weighted Mellin integral. We deduce the strong motivic monodromy conjecture for Pfaffian ideals, with pole orders bounded by Bernstein--Sato root multiplicities. For Pfaffians of size $2r$, the largest possible root multiplicity as the matrix size varies is $\lfloor(\sqrt{8r+1}-1)/2\rfloor$. In the nonmaximal case we also prove that the nonunipotent part of the Verdier specialization has zero nilpotent monodromy. Keywords. Bernstein-Sato polynomial, Pfaffian ideal, root multiplicities, strong monodromy conjecture.
#8 Fermat quotients with CM Jacobians and trivial automorphism group
著者: Saeed Tafazolian
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08176
要約:
Let $F_n$ be the Fermat curve of degree $n$ over $\mathbb Q$, and let $D_n=F_n/S_3$ be its quotient by coordinate permutations. We prove that $D_n$ has trivial geometric automorphism group for every $n\ge 11$ with $\gcd(n,6)=1$. Its Jacobian has complex multiplication. For $n=11$, this Jacobian is absolutely simple of dimension five. We construct an isogeny over $\mathbb Q$ of degree $11^5$ from $\operatorname{Jac}(D_{11})$ to the Jacobian of $y^{11}=x(1-x)^2$. For primes $p\equiv 1\pmod 3$, we also consider the metacyclic quotients $C_p$ of $F_p$ over $\mathbb Q$. By a theorem of Carocca, Lange and Rodr\'iguez, their Jacobians are absolutely simple and have complex multiplication. We prove that $C_p$ has trivial geometric automorphism group if and only if $p\ge 19$. Write $p=r^2+rs+s^2$ with $r,s$ positive integers. The natural degree-three cover $Y_p\to C_p$ satisfies $\operatorname{gon}(Y_p)=r+s$, so $\operatorname{gon}(C_p)\ge \lceil(r+s)/3\rceil$. Since $g(C_p)=(p-1)/6$, both the genus and the gonality of $C_p$ tend to infinity with $p$.

要約:
Butler's conjectures predict that, on a general smooth curve $C$ of genus $g\ge3$, a general coherent system $[\mathcal{O}_C\otimes V\xrightarrow{\varphi}E]$ with semistable underlying bundle $E$ and $\dim V>\operatorname{rk}(E)$ has surjective evaluation map $\varphi$ and stable kernel. #12 Ramification of Weighted Central Paths and Super-linear Interior-Point Methods for Semi-definite Optimization
著者: Ali Mohammad-Nezhad, Pouya Sampourmahani, Tamas Terlaky
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07080
要約:
The local convergence of primal-dual interior-point methods for semi-definite optimization is closely tied to the regularity of the central path at its limiting point. Without strict complementarity, the central path can exhibit fractional power asymptotics and need not be differentiable in the ordinary barrier parameter. We show that this obstruction can be removed simultaneously for a family of weighted \AHO~central paths. More precisely, there exists a common ramification exponent $N$ such that all weighted paths in a fixed neighborhood of the identity become analytic at the limiting point after the reparametrization $\mu\mapsto\mu^N$. Using higher-order derivatives of these reparametrized paths, we construct a feasible $p$th-order predictor-corrector interior-point method. Under a uniform predictor regularity condition, and without assuming strict complementarity, we prove that the normalized complementarity gaps converge with $Q$-order at least $(p+1)/\rho$, where $\rho$ is the reparametrization exponent used by the method. We also establish super-linear convergence of the simultaneously vanishing primal-dual blocks, derive an explicit quantitative reduction bound throughout the predictor regularity region, and obtain a doubly logarithmic outer iteration bound once the iterates enter the local super-linear regime. Finally, we illustrate the predicted higher-order behavior on a collection of degenerate and non-degenerate semi-definite optimization problems.

要約:
We study the topology of the normalized space of real nonnegative forms of degree $2d$ in $n$ variables that are not sums of squares. Whenever it is nonempty, we prove that it is path-connected but not acyclic, and in particular not contractible. We give explicit bounds on the degrees in which its reduced homology can be nonzero. #15 On explicit units in a family of cubic fields and the arithmetic of curves arising from powers of these units
著者: Iwao Kimura, Hikaru Umemoto
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07333
要約:
For an integer $b\neq0,1$ let $K_b$ be the cubic field generated by the unique real root of $x^3-3bx-b^3$, let $\eta_0$ be its fundamental unit, and let $\eta_b$ be the explicit unit studied in the companion paper (arXiv:2609.37389). Write $\eta_b=\eta_0^{n(b)}$. Deciding whether $n(b)$ is divisible by $q$ amounts to finding the integral points of a plane curve $C_q$ of degree $2q$. We prove that for every integer $q\ge1$ the curve $C_q$ is irreducible with $g(C_q)=\binom{q-1}{2}$, the genus of a smooth plane curve of degree $q$ rather than of degree $2q$. The proof is not a genus computation but a structural one: $C_q$ is the inverse image, under the $q$-power map of a two-dimensional torus, of the curve traced by the minimal polynomial of $\eta_b$ as $b$ varies, and the formula falls out of two applications of Riemann-Hurwitz in the resulting tower. As a consequence the problem splits into exactly three regimes: $q=2$ rational, $q=3$ elliptic, and $q\ge4$ of general type. In particular only finitely many $b$ have $q\mid n(b)$ for each $q\ge4$. A lower bound for linear forms in two logarithms gives $n(b)<6082138$ for every $b$, so $n(b)\in\{1,2\}$ for all but finitely many $b$, though not effectively. We add two computations: $n(b)$ is determined outright for $|b|\le10^{30}$, and the rational points of $C_4$ are determined by quadratic Chabauty, giving $4\mid n(b)\iff b\in\{-75,-3,5,96\}$. We state precisely which parts of the latter rest on certified arithmetic and which do not. Finally, we show that $K_b\cong K_{b'}$ with $b\neq b'$ holds for $\{b,b'\}=\{-1,5\}$, and otherwise only if one of the two parameters, $c$ say, satisfies $|c|>10^{30}$ and $n(c)$ is divisible by a prime $q\ge5$. There are only finitely many such pairs.

#18 The residual map as a stratified fibration over the resonance arrangement
著者: Dawei Chen, Scott Mullane, Nick Salter, Guillaume Tahar
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07499
要約:
Given a stratum $\mathcal{H}(\mu)$ of meromorphic one-forms on a Riemann surface with prescribed orders of zeros and poles, the residual map assigns to each differential the collection of its residues at the poles. In this paper, we prove that the residual map is a locally trivial topological fibration over each open flat of the stratification induced by the resonance arrangement. To give an algebro-geometric proof of this result, we introduce a refined compactification of the projectivized stratum $\mathbb{P}\mathcal{H}(\mu)$, namely the moduli space of residue-aligned multi-scale differentials $\operatorname{RAMS}(\mu)$. We prove that the projectivized residual map extends to $\operatorname{RAMS}(\mu)$ as a stratified fibration over the projectivized resonance arrangement, thereby yielding a resolution of the projectivized residual map. This approach naturally extends to the analogue of the residual map for strata of $k$-differentials. Complementing the algebro-geometric approach, we also develop a flat-geometric point of view. In genus zero, each isoresidual fiber carries a canonical higher-dimensional analogue of a translation structure. These fibers admit a metric completion whose boundary has a modular interpretation in terms of the WYSIWYG compactification, for which we provide explicit local models. Isoresidual fibers admit a polyhedral cell decomposition whose geometry varies linearly with the underlying configuration of residues in period coordinates. Degenerations of these cells occur precisely when resonance conditions are satisfied, providing a geometric proof that the residual map is a stratified fibration. Finally, certain isoresidual fibers admit natural interpretations as Hurwitz spaces of branched covers, yielding unexpected homeomorphisms between moduli spaces of covers with prescribed ramification profiles.
#19 Arithmetic Kac Theory for Ramified Species
著者: Jiarui Fei
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07549
要約:
Let $K=k((t))$, where $k$ is algebraically closed of characteristic zero, and let $\Lambda$ be an acyclic ramified species over $K$. An indecomposable $\Lambda$-module can split after extending $K$. The number of its distinct geometric indecomposable summands is the degree of its residue endomorphism field $\operatorname{End}_\Lambda(M)/\operatorname{rad}\operatorname{End}_\Lambda(M)$. We use the positive roots and cyclic automorphism of the unfolded quiver to determine the number of isomorphism classes at every nonzero rank and residue degree $e\ge1$: it is zero, an explicitly computable finite number, or $|K|$. For every invariant imaginary root of the unfolding, including at nonprimitive ranks, we construct a smooth invariant family of absolutely indecomposable representations whose rational quotient by the change-of-bases group has dimension $1-q_d(\beta)$. We characterize ranks admitting absolutely Schur representations by the Schur roots of the unfolded quiver. For the associated species over finite fields, we realize the polynomials counting absolutely indecomposable representations as graded traces of the unfolding automorphism on preprojective BPS cohomology. Galois-orbit formulas and M\"obius inversion then recover the counts at each residue degree. Finally, we prove an all-rank symmetrizable analogue of Kac's constant-term conjecture: the constant term of the polynomial counting all indecomposables, rather than only absolutely indecomposable ones, equals the corresponding Kac--Moody root multiplicity. This constant is independent of the symmetrizer and modulation.

#22 Universal $x$-$y$ duality in equivariant topological recursion and quartic Kontsevich model

要約:
Topological recursion is a universal procedure that generates an infinite family of multi-differentials with different interpretations in various areas of mathematics. Recently, a new understanding of topological recursion has emerged from $x$-$y$ duality, which relates two such families through explicit formulae. We study $x$-$y$ duality, its parametrised version, and symplectic duality in the setting of equivariant topological recursion. #23 Uniform Three-Fold Product Expansion of Algebraic-Geometry Codes over a Fixed Field
著者: Cassie Ding, Min-Hsiu Hsieh
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08172
要約:
A collection of classical codes $C_1,\dots,C_m$ has product expansion if every low-weight word in the sum of their extensions to the product space can be written as a sum of words supported on few lines, each line being a codeword of the corresponding $C_i$. For $m=2$ product expansion is equivalent to robust testability of the tensor code, while for $m\ge 3$ it is strictly stronger; it is the hypothesis on the local codes behind recent constructions of good quantum LDPC codes, good quantum locally testable codes, and quantum codes with transversal non-Clifford gates. No explicit family over a fixed field was previously known to achieve constant rate, linear distance for both the code and its dual, and 3-fold product expansion at growing length. We prove that for the one-point evaluation codes $C_i$ of length $N_i=728\cdot27^{\,i-1}$ and pole order $\lfloor N_i/5\rfloor$ on the Garcia--Stichtenoth tower over $\mathbb{F}_{729}$, the triple $(C_i,C_i,C_i)$ has product expansion with a constant independent of $i$. The dual codes $C_i^\perp$ then form an explicit fixed-field family of rate tending to $109/130$ and relative distance at least $8/65$. The proof is geometric: we view the words of the sum code as arrays on the product of three copies of the $i$-th curve of the tower, where the parity checks are sections of a line bundle. Furthermore, we extend the Helffer-Nourrigat cone to a singular subalgebroid and show that it is an embedded Poisson $C^\infty$-subscheme of $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathcal{F}(\mathscr{G}))$.
#55 Multicomplexes in Action: Strong Formality of Compact Hyperk\"ahler Manifolds
著者: Antonio Pio Contr\`o
公開日: Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40639
要約:
We show that all compact hyperk\"ahler manifolds are bigraded strongly formal in the sense of Milivojevi\'c and Stelzig, proving that in the context of pluripotential homotopy theory they play the same role as compact K\"ahler manifolds in rational homotopy theory. Indeed, the main result arises from general algebraic considerations on categories of objects with more differentials, that of $n$-complexes and commutative $n$-differential graded algebras. For them we discuss a standard model category structure, introduce a generalization of the $\partial\bar{\partial}$-Lemma and establish an algebraic formality theorem.
