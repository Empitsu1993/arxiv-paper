#4 A vanishing-cycle obstruction to smoothing Calabi-Yau threefolds
著者: Bernd Johannes Wuebben
公開日: Wed, 28 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35911
要約:
We prove a homological obstruction to smoothing projective Calabi-Yau threefolds with nodes and anticanonical cones over smooth del Pezzo surfaces of degrees six and seven. On a crepant resolution that is small over the nodes and blows up the cone vertices, any smoothing forces a relation among link classes bounding in the local Milnor fibers. The relation is nonzero at every node and degree-seven point, and at each degree-six point smoothed through the one-dimensional component. The proof uses vanishing periods and applies to smoothing arcs of arbitrary contact order, extending the necessity direction of Friedman's criterion. We apply the obstruction to a Batyrev hypersurface whose thirty singularities are individually smoothable but which admits no smoothing, even formally. On its mirror partner, two nodes cannot be smoothed by any analytic deformation, independently of a locally nonsmoothable cone point.

#7 Smoothing Calabi-Yau threefolds with nodes and del Pezzo cone points

著者: Bernd Johannes Wuebben
公開日: Wed, 28 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35915
要約:
We give a necessary and sufficient homological criterion for smoothing projective Calabi-Yau threefolds with nodes and anticanonical cones over smooth del Pezzo surfaces of degrees five, six and seven, or over a smooth quadric surface. For some choice of local smoothing components, the criterion requires a relation on a crepant resolution among link classes whose associated local parameters avoid every discriminant. The complexified relation space describes first-order deformations modulo locally trivial ones. Necessity follows from local period and vanishing-cycle arguments; sufficiency applies Friedman's unobstructedness theorem to a crepant partial resolution. Whenever the criterion holds, the deformation space on the chosen components is smooth. A nonsmoothable example, whose deformation space is reduced with four smooth components, shows why nonvanishing at every singular point is insufficient. We also construct a smoothable Batyrev hypersurface admitting no smoothing induced by proper ambient families obtained from Minkowski decompositions at a single lattice degree.

要約:
We formulate a parametrized Jacobian Problem over $\mathbb{C}^{n}$ by incorporating independent scale parameters into the linear part of a polynomial map via polar decomposition. リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35920
要約:
We investigate how geometric priors shape neural approximations of the complex Monge--Amp`ere equation on the quintic Calabi--Yau threefold. This fixed-$\psi$ diagnostic compares four single-patch ans"atze: a coordinate SIREN potential model, a direct-metric Cholesky baseline, an algebraic section ansatz, and a section-plus-neural hybrid. #18 Quartic Keller Maps and Tameness in Three Variables
著者: Jie Wang, Dan Yan
公開日: Wed, 28 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36495
要約:
We study invertibility and tameness for polynomial maps of affine three-space of total degree at most four. We prove that every complex Keller map in this degree range is a polynomial automorphism and, independently, that every complex polynomial automorphism of total degree at most four is tame. The invertibility argument combines homogeneous factorization of the leading part, invariant rings of conic pencils, and residue obstructions on cubic fibers. The tameness argument uses a criterion based on a constant second directional derivative, low-degree coordinate classifications, and local tameness of plane polynomial automorphisms over a polynomial parameter ring. The invertibility conclusion extends to every field of characteristic zero.
#19 Cluster type smooth Fano threefolds
著者: Eduardo Alves da Silva, Fernando Figueroa, Jos\'e Ignacio Y\'a\~nez, Joaqu\'in Moraga
公開日: Wed, 28 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36656
要約:
We prove that most rational smooth Fano threefolds are compactifications of $\mathbb{G}_m^3$. More precisely, we study which smooth Fano threefolds are cluster type, i.e., for which smooth Fano threefolds $X$ one can find a boundary $B\in |-K_X|$ and an embedding $\mathbb{G}_m^3\hookrightarrow X\setminus B$. We prove that many important classes of smooth Fano threefolds are cluster type. For instance, a smooth Fano threefold which is the blow-up of either $\mathbb{P}^3$ or $\mathbb{Q}^3$ and is general in its deformation space is of cluster type. As a consequence, we show that among the $105$ families parametrizing all smooth Fano threefolds, the general element of precisely $87$ families is of cluster type. We prove that a general prime smooth Fano threefold of genus $7$ is not of cluster type. This is the only family whose general element is rational but not of cluster type. Finally, we prove that cluster type projective klt varieties are cylindrical and we give a criterion for such a cylinder to be anticanonically polar. Using this, we reduce the cylindricity problem for smooth Fano threefolds to the families $\mathcal{N}^{\mathrm{o}}\,1.3$, $\mathcal{N}^{\mathrm{o}}\,1.5$, and $\mathcal{N}^{\mathrm{o}}\,1.6$.
#20 The Legendre potential and the gradient case of the differential Galois criterion for the Jacobian Conjecture
privacy
著者: El\.zbieta Adamus, Zbigniew Hajto
公開日: Wed, 28 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36736
要約:
Let $h\in\C[X_1,\dots,X_n]$ have constant nonzero Hessian determinant, let $h^{*}$ be its formal Legendre transform and $g=\vv{X,\grad h}-h$. The Nambu derivations of $\grad h$ satisfy $\delta_i(g)=X_i$ and $\delta^\alpha(g)=(\partial^\alpha h^{*})\circ\grad h$. Consequently, in the gradient case the differential Galois criterion of \cite{ACH19}, consisting of $n$ membership conditions, reduces to a single membership condition. Equivalently, $\grad h$ is a polynomial automorphism if and only if $g\in\C[\partial_1h,\dots,\partial_nh]$. Moreover, $\grad h$ is a polynomial automorphism if and only if $\delta^\alpha(g)=0$ for $|\alpha|=(\deg h-1)^{n-1}+2$, and then the coefficients $\delta^\alpha(g)(x_0)/\alpha!$ of $h^{*}$ at $\grad h(x_0)$ determine the inverse. If the Hessian determinant of $h$ is merely a nonzero polynomial, then $g$ is still a primitive element of $\C(X)$ over $\C(\partial_1h,\dots,\partial_nh)$. For the Nambu-tower criterion, doubling a Keller map yields no independent conditions. After an affine change of coordinates putting $\grad h$ in the normal form $X+H$, the formal Legendre transform is the sum of the formal Pascal difference series of $g$, and the termination of this series implies polynomial invertibility. In at most three variables the normal form of every gradient Keller map has strongly nilpotent Jacobian part and is therefore Pascal finite, by a theorem of de Bondt, and an explicit gradient automorphism in four variables has a normal form which is not Pascal finite. The five-variable counterexample of Meng and Yang shows that the properties used here do not imply the Hessian conjecture. The results do not settle the open cases $\JC_2$ and $\HC_4$.
