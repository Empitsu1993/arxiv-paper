#1 On Fujita's Advanced Generalized Mukai Conjecture
著者: Duo Li
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05452
要約:
We prove Fujita's advanced generalized Mukai conjecture for smooth complex Fano varieties. Our starting point is the existence of independent two-point Gromov--Witten correspondences. We show that there are finitely many unsplit families of rational curves whose chains connect $X$. We use these families to bound the anticanonical degrees throughout the successive minimal horizontal construction, so every family in that construction is unsplit. Fujita's contraction theorem and inductive classification then give the required list of varieties.

#3 Equivariant elliptic cohomology via loop spaces
著者: Emile Bouaziz, Adeel A. Khan
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05707
要約:
We construct a cohomology theory $H_A(-)$ canonically attached to any 1-dimensional commutative group scheme A over a connective $E_\infty$-ring. The additive group in characteristic zero gives 2-periodic de Rham cohomology, the multiplicative group gives topological periodic cyclic homology, and an elliptic curve gives elliptic cohomology. The construction extends to the G-equivariant setting for an algebraic group G, yielding in the elliptic case a theory of equivariant elliptic cohomology on algebraic varieties with G-action that takes values in sheaves on the stack of G-bundles. Over the complex numbers, and for G connected reductive, this lifts Grojnowski's theory from the moduli space of G-bundles to the stack. The key player is the category of perfect complexes on A with 0-dimensional support, equipped with the convolution product induced by the group law of A. It plays the role of a categorified circle and is used to define a loop space $L_A^G(X)$, whose structure sheaf gives rise to $H_A^G(X)$ after periodization. Sheaves on $L_A^G(X)$ equivariant under loop rotation provide a category of coefficients for $H_A^G(-)$ in the same way that D-modules provide coefficients for de Rham cohomology. At the point X = *, the theory also gives rise to an $E_\infty$-ring spectrum (or sheaf thereof) $K_A$, which recovers complex K-theory for $A = \mathbf{G}_m$, and the sheaf $\mathcal{O}^{top}$ of topological modular forms for the universal elliptic curve.
#4 On rational curves relative to a divisor
著者: Jiun-Cheng Chen, Hsian-Hua Tseng
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05809
要約:
Let $X$ be a smooth complex projective variety and $D$ a reduced simple normal crossings divisor. We prove that, given a complete curve of negative $(K_X+D)$-degree, through its general points there is a rational curve whose normalization meets $D$ in at most one point. The proof uses ordinary weighted jets of a smooth family constructed from $(X,D)$, a positive parameter line bundle, and an intersection calculation on a ruled surface. We obtain an explicit degree bound depending on a projective embedding. We apply this construction to a cyclic cover compactification associated with a smooth Campana orbifold $(X,\sum_i(1-1/m_i)D_i)$. If its anticanonical divisor is big, we show that there is a covering family of free divisible Campana rational curves with fixed numerical class and contact data. In Picard number one case, the orbifold is Campana rationally connected.
#5 Categorical Torelli and Fourier-Mukai partners of hypersurfaces
著者: Xun Lin, Shizhuo Zhang
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05915
要約:
Let $X\subset\PP^{M-1}$ be a smooth hypersurface of degree $d<m><M$ with $d\nmid M$. The same algebraic reconstruction proves the classical generic Torelli theorem for these quartics, which completes generic Torelli for smooth hypersurfaces whose degree does not divide $M$, cubic surfaces excepted. When $d$ divides $M$, the Kuznetsov component is a Calabi--Yau category, as for cubic fourfolds, where categorical Torelli is known to fail. We show that, apart from cubic fourfolds, such a hypersurface has a number of Fourier--Mukai partners bounded by a constant depending only on $d$ and $M$, and that categorical Torelli holds in sufficiently large degree, in every dimension. The main tool is a deformation theorem: when geometric deformations surject onto the second Hochschild cohomology of the Kuznetsov components, an equivalence deforms together with the hypersurfaces, so statements proved for general hypersurfaces extend to all smooth ones.
#6 The integral Chow rings of $\mathcal A_3$ and $\mathcal M_3$
著者: Emma Bishop
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06014
要約:
Over $\mathbb C$, we compute the integral Chow ring of $\mathcal A_3$, the moduli stack of principally polarized abelian threefolds. Along the way, we compute the integral Chow ring of $\mathcal M_3$, the moduli stack of smooth genus-three curves. The ring $\CH(\mathcal A_3)$ is generated by the Chern classes of the Hodge bundle, along with three classes supported on the locus of decomposable abelian varieties, subject to 16 relations. The ring $\CH(\mathcal M_3)$ is generated by the Chern classes of the Hodge bundle, with five relations. In particular, we find that the top Chern class of the Hodge bundle, $\lambda_3$, has order 504 on $\mathcal A_3$ and 252 on $\mathcal M_3$. For $\mathcal A_3$, this removes the factor of 4 from the bound $252\mid\operatorname{ord}(\lambda_3)\mid 1008$ of Ekedahl and van der Geer.
#7 Capelli Operators and the Bernstein-Sato polynomials of Minors
著者: Yifan Chen, Huaiqing Zuo
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06030
要約:
For every ideal of minors of a generic complex matrix, we determine all roots and their multiplicities in the Bernstein-Sato polynomial. These roots lie in explicit rational intervals indexed by the rank strata. Every nonintegral root is simple, whereas the multiplicity of each integral root equals the number of labels producing that root. We deduce the strong motivic monodromy conjecture, including the inequality relating pole orders to root multiplicities; for minors of size $r$, the largest possible root multiplicity over all matrix dimensions is $\lfloor\sqrt{4r-1}\rfloor$. Keywords. Bernstein-Sato polynomial, determinantal ideal, root multiplicities, strong monodromy conjecture.

#13 The super Weil-Petersson asymptotic expansion and the coefficients
著者: Xuanyu Huang, Kefeng Liu, Hao Xu
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06621
要約:
We determine the universal constant in the large-genus asymptotic expansion of super Weil-Petersson volumes. The argument adopts the uniform-transport strategy for shifted theta intersection numbers. The uniform large-genus theorem for normalized BGW numbers of Guo-Norbury-Yang-Zagier determines the amplitude at a simpler endpoint. The result is $C=\pi^{-9/2}$. We give a proof of the Griguolo-Papalini-Russo-Seminara conjecture for fixed boundary lengths and fixed descendant degrees. The boundary formula is uniform when the sum of the lengths is $o(\sqrt g)$, and we exhibit an obstruction at the $\sqrt g$ scale. We also compute the first four inverse-genus coefficients by two exact recursive methods and derive general polynomial structure results. Finally, we show that, for each fixed genus, the super Weil-Petersson volume divided by $n!$ is a polynomial in the number $n$ of marked points.
#14 On the geometric degree of the prolongation varieties of the differential system $x_1'=x_2^2$, $x_2'=x_1^2$
privacy
著者: Mattia Puddu
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06638
要約:
A prolongation variety of a polynomial differential system $S$ is an algebraic variety defined by the equations of $S$ and their derivatives up to a given order. We compute the geometric degree of the prolongation varieties associated with the system $x_1'=x_2^2$, $x_2'=x_1^2$ in terms of the prolongation order, thereby proving a conjecture stated by Gleb Pogudin in Section 2, Question 6 of "Some Open Problems in Differential Algebra". Our approach is motivated from the beginning by doing computational experiments in OSCAR in order to find the number of solutions of a zero-dimensional system using algebraic Thomas decomposition. The result will follow from a coprimality condition on a family of polynomials with integer coefficients, which is verified by showing that their roots satisfy a strong interlacing property. Finally, we propose an extension of the conjecture for the systems $x_1'=x_2^n$, $x_2'=x_1^n$ with $n>2$.
#15 Hessenberg character
著者: Patrick Brosnan, Jaehyun Hong, Donggun Lee
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06852
要約:
Let $G$ be a connected reductive group with Borel subgroup $B$, flag variety $\mathcal B=G/B$, and Weyl group $W$. We define the \emph{Hessenberg functor} on the geometric Hecke category by microlocalizing along the diagonal in $\mathcal B\times\mathcal B$ and pushing forward along the Springer map. It induces the \emph{Hessenberg character} of the Iwahori--Hecke algebra, with values in graded $W$-representations. On Kazhdan--Lusztig basis elements indexed by smooth elements of $W$, this character recovers the cohomology of the associated regular semisimple Hessenberg varieties. We identify the Hessenberg functor with the microlocalization of Lusztig's character functor and compute the Hessenberg character using Lusztig's nonabelian Fourier pairing. More generally, for families of Hessenberg schemes defined by pure-dimensional $B$-stable closed cones in the tangent space $T_{eB}\mathcal B$, we show that monodromy on the intersection cohomology of regular semisimple fibers factors through $W$. When the cone is the tangent cone of a Schubert variety, we give a local condition at $eB$ under which the same character formula computes the graded $W$-character of this intersection cohomology. Combined with the methods of Precup and Sommers, our character formula can be used to compute the graded $W$-characters of all regular semisimple Hessenberg varieties in all Lie types of rank at most ten, including all exceptional types. These computations also motivate positivity and unimodality conjectures for Hessenberg varieties and Lusztig varieties in types $B$, $C$, and $D$, together with positivity conjectures for their partial flag versions.
#16 The pliability of del Pezzo surfaces
del Pezzo
著者: Elias Kurz, Egor Yasinsky
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06861
要約:
We study $G$-Mori fibre space models of $G$-del Pezzo surfaces, focusing on surfaces over non-closed fields. We construct explicit examples of birationally rigid sextic del Pezzo surfaces and give a negative answer to a generalization of Koll\'ar's question on birational rigidity and field extensions: a sextic del Pezzo surface of Picard rank $1$ can fail to be birationally rigid over its ground field but become birationally rigid after an algebraic field extension. We interpret this phenomenon in terms of the failure of injectivity of natural maps in nonabelian Galois cohomology induced by the inclusion of automorphism groups into birational automorphism groups. We also show that finite pliability is unbounded: for every integer $d\geqslant2$, we construct a sextic del Pezzo surface of index $3$ over a field of characteristic zero whose pliability is exactly $3^d+1$.
#17 Heavy-Light Wall Crossings and Motivic Asymptotics of Moduli Spaces of Curves. Part I: Stabilization Graphs and Euler Characteristics
著者: Giordano Cotti
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06862
要約:
In this paper, we develop an explicit calculation of ordinary topological Euler characteristics of the coarse moduli spaces underlying Hassett spaces in the minimal all-light chamber and the Deligne-Mumford compactification. For genus $g\ge2$, we organize the calculation through a decomposition indexed by stabilization graphs and their decorations. The Burnside-Lefschetz formula allows us to compute the resulting contributions, taking into account graph symmetries and their fixed loci. The calculation in genus two illustrates the method explicitly and, in the Deligne-Mumford case, recovers the generating function of G. Bini, G. Gaiffi, and M. Polito (arXiv:math/9806048), with the corrections of G. Bini and J. Harer (arXiv:math/0506083), providing a check on the construction. We also carry out the calculation in genus three in an appendix. Together with an elementary genus-one calculation based on collision partitions and rational necklaces, these formulas yield complete asymptotic expansions as the number of labeled markings tends to infinity. The passage from all-light to ordinary Euler characteristics is governed by the universal change of variables $\Phi(z)=(2-z)e^z-2$, which we derive by a direct wall-crossing argument. For arbitrary fixed genus $g\ge2$, the same framework identifies the leading contribution of rational trivalent graphs and gives the ordinary asymptotic scale $n!(e-2)^{-n}n^{(3g-5)/2}$. The leading constants are the symmetry weights of these graphs; we derive their generating function, recurrence, and large-genus behavior. We also determine the asymptotic ratio of all-light to ordinary Euler characteristics, answering a question of S. Kannan, S. Serpente, and C. H. Yun (arXiv:2207.02800).
#18 Representations of 3D Rotations: Mathematical Foundations and Comparative Analysis
著者: Aizierjiang Aiersilan, Haochen Liu, James Hahn
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08086
要約:
Rotation representations are foundational in fields such as computer graphics, robotics, and machine learning, where precise and efficient modeling of 3D orientations is critical. This paper comprehensively investigates diverse representations of the special orthogonal group $SO(3)$, such as Euler angles, axis-angle vectors, quaternions, rotation matrices, exponential maps, and emerging continuous and probabilistic methods, evaluating their mathematical formulations, continuity, susceptibility to gimbal lock, computational efficiency, storage requirements, interpolation properties, and composition operations, while integrating detailed algebraic insights with practical applications in fields like animation, pose estimation, inertial navigation, 3D shape registration, and neural networks. Empirical evidence highlights quaternions' dominance due to their compactness and computational efficiency, while alternatives like 6D continuous representations and matrix Fisher distributions provide enhanced continuity and uncertainty modeling. Future research could explore hybrid methods and thorough large-scale evaluations to help build a solid foundation for improving rotation representation techniques.
#19 Quantum error correcting codes from quiver moduli: Grassmannians, cyclic monodromy, and argyle collision divisors
著者: Steven Rayan, Evan Sundbo
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05526
要約:
Building on aspects of a recent diagrammatic field-theory programme for quantum error correction put forward by the first-named author (arXiv:2607.08911), we construct exact finite-dimensional codes from twisted cyclic and argyle quiver moduli on the projective line. A regular fibre of the node-labelled cyclic Hitchin map records the ways to distribute simple spectral zeroes among the arrows with prescribed degrees. The Hilbert space spanned by these allocations is a Young permutation module. Root monodromy acts on its Specht factors and leaves their multiplicity spaces noiseless. Resetting the Specht factor therefore recovers the encoded state for every channel in the monodromy noise model. We prove that the largest Kostka multiplicity is the optimal dimension of a subspace code correcting the full monodromy algebra, even among codes spanning several isotypic blocks. Quantizing an argyle master space recovers the cyclic sheet space as a weight sector of a tensor power, with the identification preserving monodromy. For the balanced three-cycle with $t$ zeroes per arrow, the multiplicity of a partition $(a,b,c)$ of $3t$ is $1+\min(a-b,b-c)$. The optimal logical dimension is thus $t+1$, attained uniquely by the block indexed by $(2t,t)$. At $t=1$ we give explicit codewords for a qubit on six sheets. The commuting right action generates its full logical algebra, yielding errors outside the monodromy model that leave the measured footprint statistics unchanged yet require different recoveries. We also compute collision-parity ranks, give a higher-rank Grassmannian example, and derive a conditional criterion for recovery across splitting types.

#28 Uniform localization and boundary corrections for two-node quiver Coulomb branches
著者: Xiaomin Tang
公開日: Thu, 05 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06509
要約:
We construct simple coherent classes and compute their normalized difference operators for the two-node quiver Coulomb branch with gauge group $GL_1\times GL_2$ and $m-1$ framing columns at the second node. The first magnetic charge is fixed at zero. For every $m\geq1$, every support $(n,-1)$ with $n\geq1$, and every integral electric twist, we prove that termwise saturation of the matter Koszul complex gives the simple intermediate extension. A graded normalization formula expresses its localization on every stratum as a finite binomial shuffle sum. The resulting operators satisfy bar symmetry, positive bubbling order, paired root-residue cancellation, and integral Laurent polynomial action. For $m=4$, we construct the derived boundary correction on $(2,-2)$, retaining the nonsemisimple odd-electric boundary object, and compute all electric twists.
