#1 The integral $K_0$-ring of the stack of smooth hyperelliptic curves
著者: Michael Ruofan Zeng
公開日: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27267
要約:
We compute the integral $K_0$-ring of the moduli stack $\mathscr H_g$ of smooth hyperelliptic curves of genus $g\ge2$ over a field of characteristic zero. We show that it is a free abelian group of rank $8g+2$ for even $g$ and $8g+4$ for odd $g$. We use the isotypic decomposition of the $K_0$ of a related $\mu_{g+1}$-gerbe to obtain presentations for both even and odd $g$'s from the standard quotient presentation of $\mathscr H_g$. Thus, our technique differs from the $CH^\bullet(\mathscr H_g)$ computations of Edidin--Fulghesu, Fulghesu--Viviani, Di Lorenzo, and the $K_0(\mathscr M_2)$ computation of Edidin--Hu. Using Pagani's description of the inertia stack of $\mathscr H_g$, we apply Riemann--Roch to interpret the rank of $K_0(\mathscr H_g)$ as the number of inertia components. Finally, we compare the $K_0$ and Chow rings of $\mathscr H_g$ through the gamma-filtration and give presentations to the associated graded rings $\operatorname{gr}_{\gamma}K_0(\mathscr H_g)\otimes\mathbb F_p$ for every prime $p$.
#2 Hodge Theory of Novikov Type Landau--Ginzburg Models
著者: Huijun Fan, Jieran Wang
公開日: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27517
要約:
We establish a strong Hodge decomposition theorem for Novikov type Landau--Ginzburg models, that is, triples $(M,g,\theta)$ consisting of a non-compact complete K\"ahler manifold $(M,g)$ and a non-trivial closed holomorphic one-form $\theta$ on $M$.Under the well-tameness condition and under the assumption that $M$ is Stein, we prove that the space of $L^{2}$-forms on $M$ admits an orthogonal decomposition into the space of harmonic forms of the twisted Laplacian $\Delta_{\theta}$ and the images of the twisted Dolbeault operators $\bar\partial+\theta\wedge$ and $\bar\partial^{\dagger}+\iota_{\bar\theta^{\sharp}}$; moreover, the harmonic space is concentrated in degree $n=\dim_{\C}M$ and is isomorphic to the Milnor rings of $\theta$. As applications, we endow the complement of a hyperplane arrangement with a complete cylindrical K\"ahler metric, and we show that the differential of a master function with nonresonant weights is well-tamed with respect to this metric. After passing to a log resolution in the non-normal-crossing case; we then apply the results to the Yang--Yang functional of the Gaudin model, whose critical points are the Bethe roots: the top-degree harmonic space is described by the corresponding Milnor rings, and the number of Bethe roots is given by the Varchenko--Orlik--Terao lattice formula.
#3 Secondary Chow Invariants beyond Lawson--Massey Products
著者: Wenchuan Hu
公開日: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27557
要約:
We construct secondary Chow invariants that cannot be recovered from Lawson--Massey realization. For every smooth complex projective curve $C$ of positive genus and every $n\ge2$, explicit cubical defining systems yield $n$ distinct Massey values in $\rm{CH}^{3}(C\times P^{2},1)$, with zero full indeterminacy, forming a cyclic group of order $n$. All corresponding triples have identical morphic classes and zero morphic, Lawson, and singular Massey brackets, despite nonzero target groups. The values are detected by the Weil pairing. We further construct secondary quotient classes of every finite order on smooth projective varieties with ample canonical bundle in every dimension at least two, and prove that generically finite pullbacks of coprime degree preserve their exact orders. These constructions obstruct integral and prime-local motivic formality, although the corresponding brackets become $\{0\}$ after reduction modulo every integer $m\ge2$. All specified values are torsion and vanish after rationalization.

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27625
要約:
The Terracini locus of a projective variety tells us the sets of points at which Terracini's lemma will fail to recover the dimension of the corresponding secant variety. We study the Terracini loci of tangential varieties of Veronese varieties, classifying several cases, including a complete classification of the Terracini loci of tangential varieties to rational normal curves. #6 Lattice Moduli and Residual Atoms
著者: Mauricio Corr\^ea
公開日: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27723
要約:
We study prescribed holomorphic lattices for regular-singular connections and residual factors of quantum $F$-bundles. For an arbitrary ordered monodromy spectrum, we classify the fully framed local groupoid: each residue fibre is a discrete affine space of dimension $r(r-1)$, with a unique normal form and trivial stabilisers. We then analyse elementary changes of lattice. A strictly positive integral residue difference obstructs the oriented wall; otherwise, in arbitrary rank, the extending normal-form arcs form an affine torsor of relative dimension $r(r-1)$, and specialisation to the shifted residue slice is polynomial and triangular by root height. Adjacent walls exhibit the composite-root correction dictated by the type-$A$ root-subgroup relations, while an example over the dual numbers detects a nilpotent saturation defect. For one-branch $F$-bundles, the scalar branch is recovered from the residual trace, and scalar residualisation yields a symmetric monoidal equivalence with zero-branch $F$-bundles. This construction yields residual atoms, functorial Jordan strata, and a residual chemical formula compatible with blow-ups and projective bundles. In the $A$-model, residualisation removes the unit direction; the rationally marked large-radius residual germ recovers the divisor cubic and distinguishes the marked residual germs of the Pfaffian and Grassmannian Calabi--Yau threefolds.

要約:
A SNC $(p,k)$-plane is a $(\mathbb Z/p)^k$-Galois cover $\pi\colon X \to \mathbb P^2$ with a simple normal crossing branch locus. In this setting, there exist several intermediate quotients $Y_1, Y_2,\dots, Y_{\phi_{p,k}}$ corresponding to subgroups of $(\mathbb Z/p)^k$ of index $p$. We focus on the case where each $Y_i$ is either a surface with geometric genus $0$ or $1$, with at least one with geometric genus $1$. #12 Real Nullstellensatz for nilpotent Lie algebras
著者: Philipp Schmitt, Matthias Sch\"otz
公開日: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28113
要約:
We prove a real Nullstellensatz for the universal enveloping algebra U(g) of a nilpotent finite-dimensional real Lie algebra g: An ideal of U(g) is real if and only if it is the intersection of kernels of irreducible integrable *-representations of U(g) by (in general unbounded) operators on a Hilbert space. For this, we build upon some classical results about the complexification of U(g) that lead to a complex Nullstellensatz (every semiprime ideal is the intersection of primitive ideals), but focus only on the ``real points'', i.e., the *-representations on Hilbert spaces. On the technical side, this requires to take into account an involution of U(g), namely, the antipode of its Hopf algebra structure, which goes into the definition of real ideals of U(g). Our result is the first noncommutative real Nullstellensatz for *-algebras with unbounded irreducible *-representations, demonstrating how methods of real algebraic geometry can lead to results about operator *-algebras also in situations where C*-algebraic methods fail.

#14 There are no Miyaoka--Kobayashi octics
著者: Alexandru Dimca, Piotr Pokora
公開日: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28135
要約:
Let $C$ be a reduced plane curve of degree $n$ with only ADE singularities. Using K\"ahler-Einstein metrics, Kobayashi proved an upper bound, in terms of $n$, for a sum of local invariants of the singular points of $C$. For even $n$ this bound had been obtained earlier by Hirzebruch and Ivinskis. Curves of even degree for which equality holds are called Miyaoka-Kobayashi curves, and the only known examples are three types of plane sextics. We show that there are no Miyaoka--Kobayashi curves of degree $8$, and additionally that a Miyaoka-Kobayashi curve of degree $10$ cannot be free. #15 The stacky approach to de Rham-Witt cohomology
著者: Yuanning Zhang
公開日: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28149
要約:
Let $k$ be a perfect field of characteristic $p>0$. Ekedahl proved an equivalence of triangulated categories between bounded coherent complexes over the Raynaud ring and complexes of $F$-gauges. We give a geometric proof, independent of his proof, that the de Rham-Witt realization is a symmetric monoidal equivalence of stable $\infty$-categories between perfect $F$-gauges and bounded coherent complexes over the Raynaud ring with Ekedahl's completed star product, as expected by Illusie. The geometry comes from topological restriction homology: $\mathrm{TR}^r(k;\mathbf Z_p)^{hS^1}$ is an iterated fibre product of copies of $\mathrm{TC}^-(k;\mathbf Z_p)$, and taking even stacks before and after the fibre product gives the genuine and the naive $r$-Nygaard stacks. Pushforward from the genuine to the naive $r$-Nygaard stack realizes Andriopoulos' $r$-Nygaard filtrations, which interpolate between the Nygaard filtration at $r=1$ and the Hodge-Witt filtration at $r=\infty$. On the genuine $r$-Nygaard stack, which is a chain of Nygaard charts, restriction and Frobenius come from the inclusions of sub-chains, Verschiebung from duality, and the differential as a Bockstein operator, and the relations of the star product become projection formulas. As applications, we deduce Ekedahl's K\"unneth formula and Poincar\'e duality for de Rham-Witt cohomology from their counterparts for $F$-gauge cohomology, and show that the special-value formulas of Illusie and of Mondal over finite fields are equivalent.

要約:
It is known that all group codes of dimension at most $3$ are permutation equivalent to abelian group codes.
