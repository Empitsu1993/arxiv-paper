#1 Nef Hattori classes and products of projective lines
著者: Daniel \'Alvarez, Andrew Clarke, Eduardo Gonz\'alez
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12683
要約:
Let \(X\) be a smooth connected complex projective variety with an effective algebraic \(\mathbb C^{\times}\)-action, a nonempty finite fixed locus whose weights are \(\pm1\). We show that if its Hattori line bundles are nef, then \(X\) is algebraically isomorphic to \((\mathbb{P}^1)^{\dim X}\), and these bundles become the hyperplane bundles of the factors. The product statement follows from the nef-complexity classification of Enwright and collaborators, and we describe the isomorphism through global sections. To test the required nefness we specialise the orbit-graph criterion of Romano and Wi\'sniewski. The Hirzebruch surface \(\mathbb F_2\) is an example why the topological hypotheses alone do not imply either the product conclusion or the Fano property.
#2 $G$-Equivariant Hilbert Schemes and Quadratic Forms
著者: Samuel Jay Bachhuber, Amin Gholampour
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12736
要約:
For a finite abelian group $G$ acting linearly on the plane let $\mathcal{X}$ be the associated quotient stack. We analyze a fine moduli space which parameterizes zero dimensional substacks of $\mathcal{X}$ whose corresponding subschemes of the plane have a given representation type, denoted by $\mathcal{X}^{[\rho]}$. By means of a Grothendieck-Riemann-Roch theorem of To\"en we give explicit representation theoretic formulas of $\dim(\mathcal{X}^{[\rho]})$ for all $\rho \in \operatorname{Rep}(G)$ which are similar to the McKay correspondence. We also give a classification of all such quotient stacks according to an associated quadratic form called the Euler form. For $[\mathbb{A}^2/\mu_n]$ given by the cyclic action $\zeta \cdot (x,y) = (\zeta x, \zeta^{-1} y)$, we give an interpretation of the corresponding Euler form in terms of border strips which may be removed by a diagram, an equational characterization of $n$-core partitions, and the number of significant arrows of a monomial ideal as defined in "Smooth and Irreducible Multigraded Hilbert Schemes" by D. Maclagan and G. Smith.
#3 Algebraic Vector Bundles on Cluster Varieties
著者: Haoyang Liu, Tianle Liu
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12748
要約:
We study algebraic vector bundles on cluster varieties. Over a perfect field of characteristic different from two, every algebraic vector bundle is trivial on a cluster variety with principal coefficients at an acyclic seed of irreducible finite Dynkin type. The proof is based on integral contractions of cellular $\mathbb A^1$-chain complexes and successive freezing. We also construct smooth cluster varieties admitting indecomposable rank-two vector bundles, whose nontriviality is detected by quadratic Euler classes. The cellular calculation also gives a criterion for stable freeness and computes integral real cohomology and the images of cycle class maps.
#4 Normal bundles of hyperelliptic curves and uniform propagation of semistability
著者: Xuyan Liu
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12854
要約:
Let $C$ be a smooth hyperelliptic curve of genus $g\ge2$ over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. We prove that the normal bundle of the embedding of $C$ in $\mathbb{P}^n$, $n\ge3$, by the complete linear series of a general line bundle of degree $n+g$ is semistable if and only if $n\ge3g+1$. By openness, general complete embeddings of general curves of genus $g$ also have semistable normal bundle when $n\ge3g+1$. We then show that a general nonspecial curve of genus two and degree $d$ in $\mathbb{P}^n$ has semistable normal bundle except when $(n,d)$ is $(3,5)$, $(4,6)$, $(5,7)$ or $(6,8)$. The degenerations used in genus two extend to every genus $g\ge3$: semistability in all nonspecial degrees and dimensions follows from the cases $0\le d-n-g\le4g-4$. Together with a separate estimate for large $n$, this reduces semistability in each fixed genus to finitely many cases. The proofs combine degenerations to reducible nodal curves, explicit bounds on the degrees of subsheaves, and, in genus two, finitely many exact computations, which are provided as ancillary files.
#5 A Comparison Theorem for $p$-adic GKZ Hypergeometric $\mathcal D$-Modules and Complexes

公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12918
要約:
We compare the $p$-adic GKZ hypergeometric complex with the hypergeometric arithmetic $\mathcal D$-module defined via six functors. Under the $p$-adic semi-nonresonance condition, the natural comparison morphism is a quasi-isomorphism. We prove concentration of the $p$-adic GKZ hypergeometric complex in degree $n$, establish overholonomicity under another non-Liouville hypothesis. #6 Covering plane curves over finite fields with lines
著者: Shamil Asgarli, Sarthak Dassarma, Dragos Ghioca, Simon MacLean, Evan Stokdyk
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13042
要約:
We prove that, for every fixed $d\geq 2$ and $\varepsilon>0$, the $\mathbb{F}_q$-points of a geometrically irreducible reflexive plane curve of degree $d$ over $\mathbb{F}_q$ can be covered by at most $\left(\frac{1}{d}+\varepsilon\right)q$ many $\mathbb{F}_q$-lines, provided that $q$ is sufficiently large. The same conclusion holds for $\mathbb{F}_q$-irreducible curves that are not geometrically irreducible, since they have only $O(d^2)$ $\mathbb{F}_q$-points. In particular, for sufficiently large $q$, the bound applies to every $\mathbb{F}_q$-irreducible plane curve of degree $d$ when $\operatorname{char}(\mathbb{F}_q)>d$. Our bound is asymptotically sharp. The proof combines Entin's Chebotarev density theorem for varieties over finite fields with the Pippenger--Spencer theorem on covers of nearly regular hypergraphs.

#8 Classification of can-Var diagrams of mixed Hodge structures and monodromic mixed Hodge modules on $\mathbb{C}$
著者: Takahiro Saito
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13192
要約:
By our previous work, the category of unipotent monodromic mixed Hodge modules on $\mathbb{C}$ is equivalent to the category of can-Var diagrams of mixed Hodge structures. In this paper, we show that the associated graded diagram with respect to the weight filtrations decomposes as a finite direct sum of four basic types tensored with pure Hodge structures, which we call a split graded model. We then show that every unipotent monodromic mixed Hodge module is represented by a split graded model together with a finite family of $\mathbb{C}$-linear maps. We also obtain an analogous description in the case where the monodromy has no eigenvalue $1$.
#9 Artin-Schreier curves of prescribed p-rank without extra automorphisms
著者: Saeed Tafazolian, Ahmad Kazemifard
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13279
要約:
Let p be an odd prime. We study the Artin-Schreier analogue of Poonen's problem of constructing curves without extra automorphisms. An Artin-Schreier curve carries an unavoidable deck group Cp, so the smallest possible full geometric automorphism group inside the Artin-Schreier locus is Cp. We determine the exact numerical range in which this minimum is attained, for every p-rank. More precisely, for integers r >= 0 and N >= 1, there exists an Artin-Schreier curve X/Fp with g(X) = (p - 1)N/2, gamma(X) = r(p - 1), Aut_Fp(X) is isomorphic to Cp if and only if either r = 0, p does not divide N + 1, N >= 3 and (p,N) not equal to (3, 3), or r >= 1, N >= 2r and (r,N) not equal to (1, 2). All models are defined over the prime field by explicit equations together with elementary choices of auxiliary polynomials, and every excluded admissible case is shown to carry an extra geometric automorphism. The main structural input is the theorem of Valentini and Madan on the uniqueness of the rational subfield of index p outside three exceptional families. We exclude these families by intrinsic arguments: the genus and p-rank in most cases, Nakajima's bound on p-subgroups of automorphism groups of ordinary curves for the ordinary family, and the Lehr-Matignon normal form for isomorphisms of one-point covers for the monomial family.
#10 Geometry of some determinantal arrangements
著者: Marek Janasz
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13291
要約:
Let $D_{m,n}\subset\C^{m\times n}$ be the union of the zero loci of the maximal minors of a generic $m\times n$ matrix, and let $U_{m,n}$ be its complement. We show that $U_{m,n}\cong\GL_m(\C)\times\Gr(m,n)^\circ$, where $\Gr(m,n)^\circ$ is the open stratum of the Grassmannian on which all Pl\"ucker coordinates are nonzero. For $m\in\{2,n-2\}$ this gives closed formulas for the topology of $U_{m,n}$ and recovers results of Yim. Our main result, which is computer-assisted, is that $D_{3,5}$ is \emph{not} a free divisor. This answers a question of Yim. We determine $\Der(-\log D_{3,5})$ explicitly. It has projective dimension one, and it fails to be free exactly on the matrices of rank at most two. There the failure comes from a non-free transversal arrangement: the ten planes spanned by pairs of five general vectors in $\C^3$. Computations in further cases support the conjecture that $D_{m,n}$ is free only for $m\le 2$ or $n=m+1$.
#11 Gamma conjecture II and explicit Stokes data for a quadric projective bundle
著者: Xiaobin Li
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13331
要約:
We study the connection problem for the quantum differential equation of the Fano fourfold $X=\PP_{Q^3}(\OO\oplus\OO(1))$. The problem is to identify its canonical asymptotic solutions with exceptional objects and compute the associated Stokes data. We use exact Mellin--Bessel integral representations and uniform vector asymptotics on sectors wider than $\pi$, followed by analytic continuation that preserves the Gamma framing. Cohomological chiral symmetry and mutations of exceptional collections put all eight sections at a common phase. This proves Gamma conjecture II for $X$ and determines a full exceptional collection and its Stokes and central connection matrices at the unit Novikov point and a specified admissible phase. The chiral symmetry also gives a flat symmetric bilinear pairing, an orthogonality identity for the Stokes matrix, and an integral unipotent square root of the monodromy matrix for a full turn. We obtain explicit formulas for the connection data at the opposite phase; on the Gamma lattice, the half-turn acts by tensoring with a square root of the anticanonical bundle. The same spinor integral transform constructs two canonical Gamma sections for $\PP_{Q^{2m+1}}(\OO\oplus\OO(1))$, $m\geq1$, at positive parameters with distinct spectrum. Their sectors have opening greater than $\pi$, and their leading constants are independent of the dimension.
#12 Quillen's local parameter conjecture in dimension four
著者: Yunpeng Li
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13469
要約:
Let $R$ be a Noetherian regular local ring of mixed characteristic and dimension four, and let $r$ be a member of a regular system of parameters. We prove that every finitely generated projective module over $R[1/r]$ is free. We also prove that $\mathrm{Sp}_4(R[1/r])$ acts transitively on unimodular columns of length four and that every determinant orientation of a rank-two projective module is induced by a basis. A finite-rank symplectic comparison relates the image of the completion obstruction to Milnor--Witt multiplication and yields the required symplectic frames.

#16 Weighted, GIT and N\'eron--Tate heights on genus-two moduli and Jacobians
著者: Sajad Salami
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13850
要約:
We compare three heights attached to a curve of genus two: the weighted height of its Igusa--Clebsch invariants, the GIT height of a binary sextic defining it, and the N\'eron--Tate height on its Jacobian. For a morphism from an abelian variety to a weighted projective space we define a weighted N\'eron--Tate height and show that the weighted height differs from it by a linear form and a bounded function; the linear form vanishes when inversion is induced by an automorphism of the weighted projective space. For binary sextics we prove the explicit inequality $h_{\inv}\le h_{\text{GIT}} + \frac{1}{2} \log 302$, with nonnegative local differences at the finite places that vanish at places of good reduction. We then construct a family of semistable binary sextics over $E\times E$, where $E:y^2=x^3-2$, whose relative Jacobian carries a section of infinite order, and we show that the Jacobian over the base point is absolutely simple. Along every curve in the parameter space through the base point, the N\'eron--Tate height of the section is a positive multiple of the weighted moduli height up to an error of square-root size, and up to a bounded error when the curve is rational. As applications, we obtain explicit families of genus-two Jacobians of positive rank over $\mathbf{Q}$, an infinite congruence lattice of fibres of positive height, and canonical-limit formulas along elliptic curves in $E\times E$.

#18 Finiteness of planar central configurations of five bodies for all positive masses
著者: Tejasvi Singh Tomar
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13100
要約:
We prove that for every choice of five positive masses the planar five-body problem has finitely many central configurations up to similarity. This settles Smale's sixth problem for five bodies in the plane. Albouy and Kaloshin proved it for masses outside a closed algebraic subset of codimension two. We follow their method. Infinitely many central configurations would give an algebraic curve of complex solutions. At the places at infinity of the curve the valuations of the mutual distances must balance. We determine the possible valuation vectors at all positive masses, together with the mass conditions under which each can occur. We treat limits in which four bodies form a cluster of zero potential energy by expanding the potential, which is constant along the curve, at a place. This excludes, at every positive mass, one of the two diagrams in which such clusters occur, and part of the other. The same expansion excludes one further diagram at every positive mass. What remains is an exact analysis, by computer algebra, of the sets of mass conditions that can hold together at positive masses. Exact rational certificates show that the balance fails under each of them, except on a few one-parameter families of masses, which we treat separately. On the rays through $(1,4,4,4,4)$ and $(1,1,4,4,4)$ the certificates do not apply. On the first ray they cannot apply: there the complex equations have a continuum of solutions, as Albouy and Kaloshin observed. On these rays we count the real central configurations: there are 306 and 294 up to orientation-preserving similarity. The certificates, the programs that check them and a proof of the theorem in the Lean proof assistant are available.
#19 Open WDVV equations for non-commutative families of bounding cochains
著者: Elad Kosloff
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13209
要約:
We derive the noncommutative open WDVV equations for the open Gromov-Witten superpotential introduced in joint work of the author and Solomon, extending the Solomon-Tukachinsky equations to noncommutative differential graded algebras. The resulting recursive formulas facilitate the computation of open Gromov-Witten invariants with multiple boundary point constraints for even-dimensional Lagrangians. We apply the open WDVV equations to compute all open Gromov-Witten invariants for the real loci of even-dimensional complex quadrics of dimension greater than two. Open Gromov-Witten invariants with three boundary point constraints determine the two extensions of the character from the subalgebra to big quantum cohomology.

#22 Travel time tomography for elliptically anisotropic elastic planar media
著者: Joonas Ilmavirta, Teemu Saksala, Hj{\o}rdis Schl\"uter
公開日: Mon, 12 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13747
要約:
We study the gauge of the travel time tomography problem for elliptically anisotropic planar media. Under this assumption the respective slowness surfaces consist of an inner ellipse and an outer circle. Hence, the singularities of seismic waves propagate on geodesics given by two Riemannian metrics of which the one related to shear waves is conformally Euclidean. We show that if both of these metrics are lens rigid then, up to boundary fixing diffeomorphisms, each elliptically anisotropic stiffness tensor is uniquely determined by the lens data whenever it has at least one isotropic point. If none of those points exists, then there are exactly two elliptically anisotropic stiffness tensors with the same lens data. Clearly, not all boundary fixing diffeomorphisms preserve the imposed structure of the slowness surface. We show that the set of well-behaved diffeomorphisms is never a subgroup of all boundary fixing diffeomorphisms. Our proofs are based on an interplay of algebra and geometry, and the key of the proofs is a characterization of the elliptic anisotropy via several polynomial conditions.
#23 The (right) homotopy between two A$_{\infty}$-morphisms

要約:

We note that the A$_{\infty}$-algebra $B\otimes R$, where $R:=\mathbb{K}[t,dt]$, is a model for the interval $[0,1]\times B$ in the sense of Fukaya-Oh-Ohta-Ono, and we give a short alternative proof, in the unfiltered case, of the existence of comparison morphisms between such models.
