#1 Monomial singularities of maps in low codimension and their deformations
著者: R. Gim\'enez Conejero, M. E. Rodrigues Hernandes
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25057
要約:
We study complex analytic map germs whose coordinate functions are monomials up to a change of coordinates. Motivated by recent normal form results of Rodrigues Hernandes and Ruas for $\mathcal{A}$-finite monomial maps in the range $p\ge 2n$, we address the remaining gap $p<2n$, solving an open problem posed in their work in the range $n<p><2n$. Our main results show that a monomial map germ $f:(\mathbb{C}^n,0)\to(\mathbb{C}^p,0)$ with $p < 2n$ and $(n,p) \neq (1,1)$ is $\mathcal{A}$-finite if and only if it is $\mathcal{A}$-equivalent to an $\mathcal{A}$-stable germ. For $n = p = 1$ we also obtain the family of simple singularities of type $A_k$. Finally, we construct new families of $\mathcal{A}$-finite (generally non-monomial) map germs starting from monomial ones.
#2 Globally $F$-regular varieties are rationally chain connected
著者: Eric Jovinelly, Brian Lehmann, Eric Riedl
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25068
要約:
We prove that projective globally $F$-regular varieties are rationally chain connected. We deduce this from a study of the positivity of their tangent bundles and their purely inseparable covers. Although some globally $F$-regular varieties admit non-vanishing pluridifferential forms, we show that each admits a unique purely inseparable crepant cover that does not have this pathology. These covers carry very free curves, which can be degenerated to chains of rational curves through general pairs of points.

#5 A Hopf algebra action on the cohomology of moduli of abelian varieties
著者: Urshita Pal, Sam Payne
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25187
要約:
We show that the rational compactly supported cohomology of the moduli space $\mathcal{A} = \bigsqcup_{g \geq 0} \mathcal{A}_g$ of principally polarized abelian varieties is free as a bigraded module over the Hopf algebra formed by its weight-zero subspace. The action arises from a symplectic analog of the Waldhausen $S$-construction. This structure yields new lower bounds for Betti numbers and the dimensions of the graded pieces of the weight filtration on the cohomology of $\mathcal{A}_g$. In particular, $\dim H_c^{2g + k}(\mathcal{A}_g)$ grows at least exponentially with $g$ for every nonnegative $k \neq 1, 3$. We also give conjectural descriptions of the full mixed Hodge structures in genera four and five, and of mixed Tate subquotients of $H_c^{2g}(\mathcal{A}_g)$ associated with shuffle algebras and multiple zeta values in every genus.
#6 Preservation of big diagonal for automorphisms of Hilbert schemes
著者: Supravat Sarkar
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25277
要約:
If $X$ is a smooth projective surface that is not K3 or abelian surface, we show that any automorphism of the Hilbert scheme of points $X^{[n]}$ preserves the big diagonal. This generalizes several previous works, and gives a complete description of automorphisms of $X^{[n]}$ when $X$ is not K3 or abelian surface. We also obtain a Torelli-type theorem: a smooth projective surface $X$ can be recovered from $X^{[n]}$ unless $X$ is a K3 surface. Further, for each integer $n\geq 2$, we construct automorphisms of $A^{[n]}$ not preserving the big diagonal for an abelian surface $A$.

公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500

要約:
We study quiver-invariant triality between 2d (0,2) supersymmetric gauge theories described by brane brick models associated with toric Calabi-Yau 4-folds. 著者: Ruocheng Yang
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25118
要約:
We prove that every invariant subvariety M in a genus g stratum H(k_1,...,k_n) of Abelian differentials satisfies rank(M) <= rel(M) + 1 + sum_i (k_i - 1). In particular, every invariant subvariety with rel zero in a principal stratum is a Teichm\"uller curve. If rank(M) < g, we also show that rank(M) + rel(M) <= g - 1 + n/2.
#9 The rational points of the non-split Cartan modular curve of level 121
著者: Manvir Jaswal
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25183
要約:
We prove that the modular curve $X_{\mathrm{ns}}^{+}(121)$ of the normalizer of a non-split Cartan subgroup of level 121, of genus 511, has exactly seven rational points, all of them CM. This is the level 121 case of a conjecture of Rouse, Sutherland and Zureick-Brown, and with their theorem it determines the 11-adic images of Galois of non-CM elliptic curves over $\mathbb{Q}$. The Jacobian has rank equal to the genus. At $p = 17$ and $43$ one Hecke block of the Mordell-Weil group has logarithm divisible by $p^2$ but $p$-adic height of valuation one, and a comparison of valuations in the quadratic Chabauty identity then shows that each CM point is the only rational point in its residue disc, with no computation of the local height at $p$, no basis of the Mordell-Weil group and no equations for the curve. The inputs come from expansions of differentials at CM points and from a formula we prove for intersection numbers of CM divisors of coprime fundamental discriminants under Hecke operators on $X_{\mathrm{ns}}^{+}(\ell^e)$ (Theorem 1.3).
#10 Rational Elliptic Period Relations Across Realizations: Complex Lattices, Local p-adic Periods, and Drinfeld Modules
著者: Mohammadreza Mohajer
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25399
要約:
We determine the algebraic relations among full de Rham comparison periods of elliptic curves over finite extensions of $\mathbb Q_p$. The connected local monodromy group gives the transcendence degree and an explicit affine linear relation ideal for the full matrix in Fontaine's field $B_{\mathrm{dR}}$. After normalizing the holomorphic row and reducing to $\mathbb C_p$, the transcendence degree drops by one and the normalized determinant supplies the additional relation. Every polynomial relation of degree at most $d$ is detected modulo $t^{d+1}$. For $d\ge1$ this bound is sharp for each elliptic curve. A rational quasi-period row gives a Galois-stable Tate line. Together with an algebraic holomorphic period ratio it forces potential splitting, which identifies the exact ordinary CM obstruction. We compute the full Tate matrix. For ordinary non-CM curves with $p\ge5$, the standard quasi-period row is rational precisely at a zero of Katz's $E_{2,p}$. Away from the zeros the two full ratios are algebraically independent. At the zeros, a nonzero quasi-period and the normalized holomorphic ratio have algebraically independent images in $\mathbb C_p$. The complex question that motivates these results, the analogue for Drinfeld modules, and a conditional arithmetic consequence for Katz values complete the comparison.
#11 Formality of finite abelian covers and Jordan blocks of the monodromy
著者: Alexander I. Suciu
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25421
要約:
Let $X$ be a connected finite-type CW-complex with a map to the circle that is surjective on fundamental groups. If $X$ is formal, the algebraic monodromy of the associated infinite cyclic cover has no Jordan block of size greater than one at the eigenvalue 1, over the rationals; if the $d$-fold cyclic cover of $X$ along the map is formal, the same holds at every $d$-th root of unity. We study how formality of finite abelian covers and Jordan blocks of the monodromy constrain each other. We call $X$ Alexander formal if there are no such blocks at any root of unity, rationally, nor at the eigenvalue 1 modulo any prime $p$. Each condition amounts to the vanishing of certain Massey products along the class of the map; together they say that the rational cochains of every finite cyclic cover along the map, and the mod-$p$ cochains of $X$, are formal as DG modules over the cohomology of the circle. For the complement of a central hyperplane arrangement, with its Milnor fibration, Alexander formality holds exactly when the numbers of hyperplanes in its irreducible factors have no common divisor greater than 1; so the complement of an irreducible arrangement of two or more hyperplanes is never Alexander formal, although it is formal. On the side of covers, we show that a CDGA graded by an abelian group, over a field of characteristic zero in which the characters of the group separate its points, is formal if and only if it is formal as a graded CDGA. Consequently, the strong formality of Kohno and Pajitnov implies formality of every finite abelian cover of $X$; the converse fails, through a Jordan block at an eigenvalue of infinite order, which no finite cover detects. Modulo $p$, eigenvalues of $p$-power order collide with 1, producing Jordan blocks that strong formality allows. The two conditions are incomparable; all four resulting classes contain arrangement complements.

#16 On the Coupled Cluster Doubles Truncation Variety of Four Electrons
著者: Fabian M. Faulstich, Vincenzo Galgano, Elke Neuhaus, Irem Portakal
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16580
要約:
We extend recent algebro-geometric results for coupled cluster theory of quantum many-body systems to the truncation varieties arising from the doubles approximation (CCD), focusing on the first genuinely nonlinear doubles regime of four electrons. Since this doubles truncation variety does not coincide with previously studied varieties, we initiate a systematic investigation of its basic algebro-geometric invariants. #18 The maximum Cox complexity of a Fano-type variety and the Mukai conjecture
著者: Heath Pearson
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25023
要約:
We define an intrinsic invariant $\gamma_{\mathrm{Cox}}(\cdot)$, the maximum Cox complexity, of Fano-type varieties $X$. We prove $\gamma_{\mathrm{Cox}}(X)\in\mathbb{Q}_{\geq 0}$ and $\gamma_{\mathrm{Cox}}(X)=0$ if and only if $X$ is toric. We then apply this invariant to the Mukai conjecture, which characterises powers of projective spaces among smooth Fano varieties in terms of $\dim(X)$, the Picard number $\rho(X)$, and the Fano index $i(X)$. All klt Fano varieties $X$ canonically embed into a projective toric variety $Z$ via their Cox ring. When $Z$ is smooth, we call $X$ ambiently smooth. We prove the Mukai conjecture for ambiently smooth Fano varieties by establishing the stronger inequality: $\dim(X)+\rho(X)-i(X)\rho(X)\geq\gamma_{\mathrm{Cox}}(X)\geq 0$. Notably, 45% of the deformation families of smooth Fano threefolds have an ambiently smooth member. For a Fano-type variety $X$, the invariant $\gamma_{\mathrm{Cox}}(X)$ is defined using coefficient sums of anticanonical divisors supported on the divisors corresponding to minimal homogeneous generators of the Cox ring. Our proof proceeds by reducing the Cox ring to positive characteristic, using strong F-regularity to obtain the coefficients of an anticanonical divisor, and the F-pure threshold to establish the nonnegativity of $\gamma_{\mathrm{Cox}}(X)$.

#20 Generalized Nikulin surfaces and irreducible symplectic fourfolds
著者: Chiara Camere, Alice Garbagnati, Grzegorz Kapustka, Micha{\l} Kapustka
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00130
要約:
A Nikulin surface is the minimal resolution of the quotient of a $K3$ surface $S$ by a symplectic involution $\iota_S$. Equivalently, it is the $2$-dimensional component of the fixed locus of the involution induced by $\iota_S$ on the Hilbert scheme $S^{[2]}$. We study $K3$ surfaces $F$ that are the $2$-dimensional component of the fixed locus of a symplectic involution $\iota$ on hyper-K\"ahler manifolds $X$ of $K3^{[2]}$-type; we call them generalized Nikulin surfaces. We show that a projective $K3$ surface is a generalized Nikulin surface if and only if its N\'eron-Severi lattice contains primitively the lattice $E_7(-2)$. Moreover, we show that the transcendental lattices $T_F$ and $T_{\widetilde{X/ \iota}}$, where $\widetilde{X/ \iota}$ is the terminalization of the quotient $X/\iota$, are Hodge isometric. Finally, we describe projective models of generalized Nikulin surfaces of small degrees.
#21 Real morsifications via the trace map
著者: Pablo Portilla Cuadrado
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07212
要約:
We prove that every reduced real plane curve singularity admits a real morsification. This settles a question of A'Campo and Gusein-Zade, later stated as conjectures by Leviant--Shustin and by Fomin--Pylyavskyy--Shustin--Thurston. In particular we overcome the main obstruction that remained posed by conjugate pairs of nonreal branches. Our new main ingredient is a construction that produces the divide from a nodal smoothing of two normalization disks. This is what we call the trace map. For real branches, it recovers Gusein-Zade's construction using Chebyshev polynomials. For pairs of complex conjugate branches with distinct tangents, the construction gives an explicit formula for the divide in terms of the Puiseux data. The general method consists in a delicate combination of the trace map with A'Campo's translations and contractions to produce divides and real morsifications for all reduced real plane curve singularities.

#28 Positivity and uniform log-concavity for rank-two Chern coefficients
著者: Hang-Cheng Dong, Changle Zhao
公開日: Fri, 20 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24563
要約:
We study the binomial-basis coefficients in the Schur expansion of the Chern classes of symmetric powers of a rank-two bundle. B\'erczi and Feh\'er conjectured that these coefficients are nonnegative and log-concave in the Newton index. An inverse-series formula yields an integral gamma-positive refinement with exact support and a positive expression for the derivatives of the shifted Chern quotient. We prove that generalized Bernoulli polynomials of real order at least their degree have simple real zeros. Applied to the shifted quotient, this gives strict ultra-log-concavity in the Schur index at every positive shift. For the original Newton coefficients, we establish strict log-concavity with an explicit multiplicative margin in two uniform regions. A Hahn projection gives a square-root band about the balanced Schur index; a complex saddle estimate extends this to a band of linear width. Uniform estimates for the cumulants of a normalized Schur functional give a terminal Newton region of linear width. Polynomial coefficient identities also establish every row with Schur index at most six or imbalance at most nine. The argument separates log-concavity in the Schur, derivative and Newton indices and identifies the factorial factors in the transfer between the latter two. The unrestricted Newton log-concavity conjecture remains open.

We propose quantum differential equations (QDEs) for periods of nonabelian mirror theories and develop an algorithm for constructing them directly from the mirrors. We verify the proposal by recovering known quantum differential equations of Grassmannians and Picard-Fuchs equations of Calabi-Yau complete intersections in Grassmannians. Our algorithm applies to a broad class of nonabelian GLSMs and yields new examples of quantum differential equations for several Fano varieties. The associated cohomology-valued generating functions can be extracted directly from these equations. Using them, we compute instanton numbers of both complete-intersection and non-complete-intersection Calabi-Yau manifolds, including the Pfaffian and Gulliksen--Neg{\aa}rd Calabi-Yau threefolds. The resulting instanton numbers agree with all previously known computations, while the generating functions obtained for the non-complete-intersection examples are new.
#30 Symbolic powers of the generic linkage of maximal minors

要約:
Let $I$ be the ideal generated by the maximal minors of a matrix of indeterminates over a field and let $J$ denote the generic link, i.e., the most general link, of $I$. The generators of the ideal $J$ are not known in general. We provide an explicit description of the lead terms of the generators of $J$ using Gr\"obner degeneration. Indeed, we construct a degeneration which preserves the entire graded Betti table of $J$ on passing to the initial ideal. We leverage this construction to establish the equality of the symbolic and ordinary powers of $J$. Our analysis of the initial ideal readily yields the Gorenstein property of the associated graded ring of $J$, and, in positive characteristic, the $F$-rationality of the Rees algebra of $J$. Using the technique of $F$-split filtrations, we further establish the $F$-regularity of these blowup algebras.

We use geometry to solve a problem in arithmetic relevant for physics. Namely, for any $m$ we find all integer solutions to the equations $\sum_{i=1}^m x_i^k = \sum_{i=1}^m y_i^k$, where $k \in \{1,2,3\}$; this seems to be new for $m \geq 5$. In physics, these are the anomaly cancellation conditions for models with $m$ fermions which can be naturally light and carry small electric charges (a.k.a. millicharged or minicharged particles). We consider the period map on families of parabolic Higgs bundle moduli spaces with varying parabolic weights $\alpha$ and Higgs field eigenvalues $\sigma$. We assemble the moduli spaces into a smooth family $\pi:\mathscr M\to\Lambda_0$ where $\Lambda_0$ encodes the admissible pairs $(\alpha,\sigma)$, and define parallel transport for homology along paths in $\Lambda_0$.
