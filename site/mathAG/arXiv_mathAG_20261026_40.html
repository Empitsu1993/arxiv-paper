#1 On the Hodge conjecture for hyper-K\"ahler varieties of generalized Kummer type of dimension divisible by four
著者: Salvatore Floccari, Lie Fu
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31740
要約:
We prove the Hodge conjecture for all hyper-K\"ahler varieties of generalized Kummer type of dimension $2n$ with $n+1$ a prime number, and all powers of these varieties. For any variety $X$ of generalized Kummer type of dimension divisible by 4, we show that the Lefschetz standard conjecture for $X$ implies the Hodge conjecture for $X$ and all its powers.
#2 Chern-Schwartz-MacPherson classes of symmetric powers and applications
著者: Barbara Fantechi, Lothar Goettsche
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31826
要約:
The Chern-Schwartz-MacPherson class $c_{SM}(X)$ is defined in the Chow group $A_*(X)$ of a scheme $X$, and generalizes $c(T_X)\cap [X]$ if $X$ is smooth. In this paper we independently compute $c_{SM}(S^nX)\in A_*(S^nX)$ modulo $n!$ torsion; we use this result to compute the pushforward to $S^nX$ of $c_{SM}(Quot^n_X(E))$ (and in particular $c_{SM}(Hilb^n X)$) when $X$ is smooth and $E$ is a vector bundle on $X$. Finally, when $X$ is smooth and complete of dimension $3$ we propose a refined version of the the MNOP conjecture that computes the image of the pushforward of $[Hilb^n X]^{vir}$ to $A_0(S^nX)$ and prove it modulo $n!$ torsion when $X$ is Calabi-Yau.
#3 Comparison of Logarithmic Hochschild Homologies
著者: Joseph Stahl
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31879
要約:
We construct a comparison morphism $\operatorname{HH}^{\mathrm{Rog}}\to\operatorname{HH}^{\mathrm{Ols}}$ between Rognes' logarithmic Hochschild homology and Olsson's logarithmic Hochschild homology which generalizes the comparison between Gabber's log cotangent complex $\mathbb{L}^{\mathrm{Gab}}$ and Olsson's log cotangent complex $\mathbb{L}^{\mathrm{Ols}}.$ In particular, Olsson's comparison is recovered as the first associated graded piece of our comparison morphism $\operatorname{HH}^{\mathrm{Rog}}\to\operatorname{HH}^{\mathrm{Ols}}.$ In the development of this comparison, we also prove a descent result for the cotangent complex generalizing a result of Bhatt, and construct a notion of HKR completed Hochschild homology for a general morphism of derived stacks which is functorial in the morphism and generalizes existing constructions of Hochschild homology when the base is affine.
#4 Kashiwara's Lemma for Potent D-modules
著者: Jacob Erlikhman, Kendric Schefers
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32003
要約:
On stacks there is a richer version of $\mathcal{D}$-modules---called $\textit{potent }\mathcal{D}\textit{-modules}$---which decompletes at equivariant parameters, in the same sense that genuine equivariant $K$-theory decompletes equivariant cohomology. In this paper, we prove a ``genuine'' version of Kashiwara's lemma for $\mathcal{D}$-modules. Namely, we show that for a broad class of geometric stacks $X$, if $Z \subset X$ is a closed substack, the category $\mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{pot}}(Z)$ of potent $\mathcal{D}$-modules on $Z$ is equivalent under pushforward to the category $\mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{pot}} _Z(X)$ of potent $\mathcal{D}$-modules on $X$ with support contained in $Z$.

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32036
要約:
Let $(X, \Omega)$ be an affine Calabi--Yau variety, by which we mean a klt affine variety $X$ with a non-vanishing algebraic volume form $\Omega$. In connection with complete Calabi--Yau metrics of Euclidean volume growth on $(X, \Omega)$, we study algebraic aspects of polarizations of $X$ given by a negative valuation. #7 Power Sum Varieties over Finite Fields
著者: Nardo Gim\'enez, Juan Francisco Gottig, Miguel Palacios, Mariana P\'erez, Melina Privitelli
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32056
要約:
We study a family of affine algebraic varieties over a finite field $\mathbb{F}_q$ defined by systems of power sum polynomials. Our main goal is to investigate their geometric and arithmetic properties, with particular emphasis on the structure and dimension of their singular loci. Using tools from the theory of symmetric polynomials and generalized Vandermonde determinants, we establish explicit conditions under which these varieties are complete intersections and prove that, in a broad range of parameters, their singular loci have codimension at least two. As a consequence, we derive normality and irreducibility results for both the affine varieties and their projective closures. Building on this geometric analysis, we obtain estimates for the number of $\mathbb{F}_q$-rational points and deduce effective existence results for solutions of systems of symmetric power sum equations over finite fields. Finally, we apply these results to the study of Waring's problem and to the analysis of the cost of computing a $\mathbb{F}_q$-rational point of such systems.

#9 Reflection groups on smooth projective threefolds

公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32450
要約:
Let $Y$ be a smooth projective threefold with $H^2(Y, \mathcal{O}_Y)=0$ and unobstructed deformations. We define reflection groups $W_Y^{sm}$ and $W_Y^{big}$ canonically associated to $Y$, acting on the nef, resp. movable cone of a very general deformation of $Y$. #10 Jacobian layers and higher Milnor sequences: rigidity, stabilization, and log-concavity
著者: Zhiwen Liu, Stephen S. -T. Yau, Mingyi Zhang
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32523
要約:
Let $f$ be an isolated hypersurface singularity and let $J(f)$ be its Jacobian ideal. We study the higher Milnor numbers $\mu_k(f)=\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathcal O_n/\mathfrak m^kJ(f).$ Our main result concerns an ordinary semi-homogeneous singularity $f=f_r+f_{r+1}+\cdots$, where the projective hypersurface defined by $f_r$ is smooth. We prove that $\mu_k(f)=\mu_k(f_r) \qquad\text{for every }k\geq0.$ This gives the equal-weight case of a conjecture of Shi, Yau, and Zuo. The main algebraic input is the common-order case of the Valabrega--Valla criterion. From it, we obtain the graded Jacobian module and the exact intersection formula. As a consequence, we obtain a closed formula for $\mu_k$ and determine the sharp stabilization index of $\mathfrak m^kJ(f)$. We also determine the optimal uniform range in which the Tjurina formula holds for all ordinary semi-homogeneous germs with fixed $n$ and $r$. We next consider the log-concavity property. For ordinary semi-homogeneous singularities, the higher Milnor sequence has an initial log-convex part and a log-concave tail. For an arbitrary isolated hypersurface singularity in $n\geq2$ variables, both the higher Milnor and higher Tjurina sequences are eventually strictly log-concave. In the plane curve case, we prove that the log-concavity defects change sign at most once, and we characterize the sequences which are log-concave at every positive index. Finally, we give examples showing that the higher Milnor sequence does not determine the contact-equivalence class.
#11 Deformations of cohomology classes and spectral sequences
著者: Borislav Mladenov
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32593
要約:
We study spectral sequences as an obstruction theory to deforming cohomology classes via the Rees construction. A filtered complex is interpreted as an equivariant family over the affine line, in which higher spectral sequence differentials arise as obstructions to lifting cohomology classes through infinitesimal neighborhoods of the special fibre. This leads to various geometric and module-theoretic degeneration criteria. We give various applications to $\mathrm{Ext}$ spectral sequences for complex Lagrangians and the formality results of Melani-Rubi\'o amongst others. For double complexes, we revisit the Khovanov-Qi decomposition on the Rees side and introduce a two-parameter Rees construction over the affine plane. The higher differentials of the two associated spectral sequences correspond to torsion modules supported on the thickened coordinate axes. We obtain degeneration criteria in terms of these modules and an intrinsic characterisation of the $\partial\bar\partial$-lemma.

#15 Rank 3 curve semistable Higgs bundles: the nilpotent case
著者: Ugo Bruzzo, Beatriz Gra\~na Otero
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32900
要約:
A Higgs bundle on a smooth complex projective variety is curve semistable if its pullback to every smooth projective curve is semistable. A 2008 conjecture asserts that this is equivalent to slope semistability for some polarization plus vanishing discriminant. One direction is known, the converse holds in rank two, and Huang recently gave a rank-four counterexample. We prove the conjecture for rank three nilpotent Higgs bundles in any dimension. We treat separately the two possible cases: if the Higgs field $\phi$ is nilpotent of order two, there is a divisorial component of a Higgs Grassmannian where nefness of the normalized tautological class forces the discriminant to vanish; otherwise, $\phi$ is regular nilpotent, and then the canonical kernel flag, commutativity and Bogomolov's lemma on line subbundles of the cotangent bundle complete the proof.
#16 The Verra involution on special cubic fourfolds
著者: Augustinas Jacovskis, Reinder Meinsma, Riccardo Moschetti
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32952
要約:
We present a detailed study of the Hodge theory, geometry and derived categories of Verra fourfolds, and their relationship with twisted K3 surfaces and cubic fourfolds. We classify Brauer classes of Verra type on K3 surfaces of degree 2, and use this to derive an explicit counting formula for Fourier--Mukai partners of a large class of Verra fourfolds. We develop the basic theory of cohomology of Verra fourfolds and apply it to prove a derived Torelli theorem for a large class of Verra fourfolds. As an application, we prove that L-equivalent Verra fourfolds in this class are Fourier--Mukai partners, and conjecture that this implication holds for all Verra fourfolds. We then give a construction of cubic fourfolds birational to Verra fourfolds. The construction yields examples of cubic and Verra fourfolds whose associated twisted K3 surfaces have non-trivial Brauer classes. Therefore, these fourfolds are expected to be irrational.

公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31964
要約:
In this paper, we study perfectoid ideals using the reduction modulo $p$ of perfectoid rings. We show that perfectoid ideals correspond to ideals in the reduction modulo $p$ on which Frobenius is surjective. #20 Singular Loci of SL2(C)-Representation Varieties of Torus Knot Groups
著者: S. Liriano
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32064
要約:
Let $T(q,p)=\langle x,y\mid x^p=y^q\rangle$, where $p,q>1$ are relatively prime, and let \[ R=\Hom(T(q,p),\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb C)). \] We compute the integral homology of the singular locus of $R$. We show that this locus has complex dimension $2$ and is a disjoint union of $\mu=(p-1)(q-1)$ smooth affine surfaces, each homotopy equivalent to the two-dimensional sphere. Consequently, \[ H_0(\Sing R;\Z)\cong H_2(\Sing R;\Z)\cong\Z^\mu, \] and all remaining homology groups vanish. Thus both nonzero Betti numbers equal the first Betti number of the Milnor fiber of the plane curve singularity $z^p+w^q=0$, namely its Milnor number $\mu$.
#21 Contributions to the Stable Derived Categories of Gorenstein Rings
著者: Vincent G\'elinas
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32074
要約:
The stable derived category $D_{sg}(R)$ of a Gorenstein ring $R$ is the Verdier quotient of the bounded derived category $D^b(\mathsf{mod}\, R)$ by the thick subcategory of perfect complexes, and was introduced by Buchweitz as a homological measure of the singularities of $R$. This thesis contributes to its study, centered around representation-theoretic and Koszul duality aspects. In Part I, we complete (over $\mathbb{C}$) the classification of homogeneous complete intersection isolated singularities $R$ for which $D^{\mathbb{Z}}_{sg}(R)$ (respectively, $D^b(\mathsf{coh}\, X)$ for $X = \mathsf{proj}\, R$) contains a tilting object. We then use results of Buchweitz-Iyama-Yamaura to describe the indecomposable objects in $D_{sg}^{\mathbb{Z}}(R_Y)$ for $R_Y$ the coordinate rings of $4$ points on $\mathbb{P}^1$ and $4$ points on $\mathbb{P}^2$ in general position, and classify their Betti tables. In Part II, for $R$ a Koszul Gorenstein algebra, we study a natural pair of subcategories whose intersection $\mathcal{H}^{\mathsf{lin}}(R) \subseteq D_{sg}^{\mathbb{Z}}(R)$ consists of modules with eventually linear projective resolutions. We prove that such a pair forms a bounded t-structure if and only if $R$ is absolutely Koszul in the sense of Herzog-Iyengar, in which case $D^b(\mathcal{H}^{\mathsf{lin}}(R)) \cong D_{sg}^{\mathbb{Z}}(R)$. We then relate the heart to modules over the Koszul dual $R^!$. This extends the BGG correspondence to any pair of Koszul dual algebras $(R, R^!)$ with $R$ absolutely Koszul Gorenstein, applying e.g. to elliptic normal curves and del Pezzo surfaces of degree $\geq 4$. Finally, we relate our results to conjectures of Bondal and Minamoto on the graded coherence of AS-regular algebras and higher preprojective algebras; we characterise when these conjectures hold in a restricted setting, and give counterexamples to both in all dimensions $\geq 4$.

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32384
要約:
We show that holomorphic maps from a neighborhood of a compact set in a Stein space to an algebraically elliptic smooth projective variety can be approximated by meromorphic maps, provided that the map extends continuously to the whole space and the compact set is convex with respect to a submonoid of the Picard group. #26 Structure and Exact Certification of Nonnegative Tensor Decompositions
著者: Haoming Wang, Ming Yuan
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32787
要約:
We study the structure and exact certification of nonnegative tensor decompositions supplied through rational factor matrices. Every failure of minimality or essential uniqueness contains an irreducible positive exchange between $p$ supplied terms and $q$ competing terms. A dimension count based on the Lovitz--Petrov splitting theorem bounds the effective order of such an exchange, the sum over the modes of the joint factor-span dimension minus one, by $p+q-2$, and this bound is attained at every supplied length $R\ge2$. After eliminating ambient-row variables by the Farkas lemma, the resulting semialgebraic systems have fewer than $2R^2$ real variables. For input bit length $L$, this gives a deterministic $G(R)L^{O(R^2)}$-time algorithm that computes the nonnegative rank and an exact minimum-length decomposition, decides minimality and essential uniqueness, and returns exact witnesses when either property fails. The exponent is independent of the tensor order and the mode dimensions. For Lebesgue moment tensors, positive quadrature determines the nonnegative rank and the uniqueness behavior, and some rational length-$R$ inputs force a common field of degree at least $\lfloor R/2\rfloor!$ for any exact minimum-length output. As an illustration, an implementation resolves all 114 instances left inconclusive by earlier positive-scattering criteria, of which 95 are minimal and essentially unique and 19 are minimal but nonunique.
#27 Atiyah duality for motivic spectra
著者: Toni Annala, Marc Hoyois, Ryomei Iwasa
公開日: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01561
要約:
