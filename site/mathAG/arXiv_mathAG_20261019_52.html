公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22573
要約:
This paper discusses the algebra of symmetric tensors for ruled surfaces defined by rank 2 stable vector bundles on curves of genus 2. The algebra is computed for special bundles $E$ when $S^2 E$ is destabilized by a line bundle and when $S^3 E$ is destabilized by a bundle of rank 2. A strategy for computation is discussed in regards to the other special bundles $E$. #2 Generic vanishing for Dolbeault cohomology of mixed Hodge modules on abelian varieties
著者: Ze Yun
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22678
要約:
We study generic vanishing for the Dolbeault cohomology of mixed Hodge modules, where the differentials in the Dolbeault complex are modified by a one-form. We prove that, on the Dolbeault moduli space of Higgs line bundles, the cohomology support loci of mixed Hodge modules agree with the images of the cohomology support loci of their underlying $\mathscr{D}$-modules on the de Rham moduli space under the map induced by the nonabelian Hodge correspondence. We further apply this result to study Fourier-Mukai transforms of mixed Hodge modules on abelian varieties. We show that the extended Fourier-Mukai transform for bounded complexes of mixed Hodge modules has cohomology sheaves that are flat over the twistor line of $\lambda$-connections. This also gives an extension of Fourier transform of mixed Hodge modules to a compactification of the de Rham moduli space.
#3 Vector bundles on singular surfaces and threefolds
著者: Jean Fasel, Maria Yakerson
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22679
要約:
We fully classify vector bundles of critical rank on normal affine surfaces and threefolds over algebraically closed fields via their Chern classes. We also prove existence of rank $2$ bundles on threefolds with prescribed first and second motivic Chern classes. The main input is the motivic filtration on algebraic $K$-theory: its third piece is generated by smooth closed points and is identified with motivic cohomology by the third Chern class.

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22730
要約:
Inspired by the recent preprint of Ellenberg, Libedinsky, Plaza, Simental, and Williamson, we study the geometry of Richardson varieties whose associated Bruhat intervals are boolean posets. We define magic words for a Bruhat interval and prove that for intervals with magic words the associated Richardson variety is a tower of $\mathbb{P}^1$-s. #6 $R$-trivial Galois cohomology classes and cohomological $p$-dimension
著者: Charles De Clercq
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22758
要約:
Given a prime $p$, a field $F$ of characteristic different from $p$ and a finite $p$-primary Galois module $V$, a Galois cohomology class in $H^n(F,V)$ is called $R$-trivial if it can be connected to $0$ by a class defined over an open subscheme of the projective line. All classes of $H^n(F,V)$ are $R$-trivial if $F$ has cohomological $p$-dimension at most $n$, by the Bloch-Kato conjecture. We show that this bound is sharp: working over fields of iterated Laurent series, we produce, for every $n\geq 1$, a field $F$ of cohomological $p$-dimension $n+1$ and a Galois module $V$ such that $H^n(F,V)$ contains classes which are not $R$-trivial. The module $V$ is a quotient of the permutation module on the affine hyperplanes of an elementary abelian $p$-group, arising as a quotient of the absolute Galois group of $F$. We also describe $R$-trivial classes as sums of corestrictions of cup products involving a Kummer class.
#7 Low-weight compactly supported cohomology of $\mathcal M_{g,n}^{ct}$ and the extended Torelli morphism
著者: Amy Bradford
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22809
要約:
We study the weight zero and weight two graded pieces of the compactly supported cohomology of the moduli space $\mathcal M_{g,n}^{ct}$ of stable $n$-pointed curves of genus $g$ of compact type and show that they are computed by bridgeless graph complexes. In weight two, we construct a natural embedding of shifted weight zero cohomology from lower genus. For unmarked curves, we construct canonical classes represented by pinched odd wheel graphs and prove that distinct wheel monomials give linearly independent classes in the dual of $W_0H_c^{2g}(\mathcal M_g^{ct};\mathbb Q)$. By relating the bridgeless graph complex to the $K$-theory of graphs and the Quillen spectral sequence, we show that these classes remain linearly independent after pushforward along the extended Torelli morphism $\mathcal M_g^{ct}\to\mathcal A_g$. Finally, we prove that the realizations of the motivic Lie algebra arising from $\mathcal M_g$ and $\mathcal A_g$ have the same image in the weight zero compactly supported cohomology of $\mathcal M_g^{ct}$.
#8 The geometric exceptional set for Fano threefolds
著者: Runxuan Gao
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23024
要約:
We study the geometric exceptional set in Manin's conjecture for smooth Fano threefolds. We prove that every adjoint rigid surface with $a$-invariant one on a non-product smooth Fano threefold is normal. This yields a description of the geometric exceptional set in terms of the higher-$a$ locus, anticanonical conics, and del Pezzo fibrations. Zariski density arise only from the latter two contributions. For del Pezzo fibrations, we construct a finite collection of parameter covers which controlling the associated breaking maps. We classify their low-genus components for general smooth Fano threefolds yielding examples with potentially Zariski dense geometric exceptional sets in $22$ of the $105$ deformation families. Extending Batyrev-Tschinkel's example, we construct Fano fourfolds with quartic del Pezzo fibrations and an explicit Fano threefold with two degree-six del Pezzo fibrations, all with Zariski dense geometric exceptional sets. For this threefold, the original form of Manin's conjecture fails on every non-empty open subset for every anticanonical height.
#9 Seven ordered points and the nonexistence of arithmetic ball quotient compactifications
著者: Ryunosuke Nakano (Hokkaido University), Hisato Matsukawa (Hokkaido University)
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23227
要約:
We prove that the ordered GIT quotient of seven points on $\mathbb P^1$ with equal weights is not isomorphic to the Baily--Borel compactification of any arithmetic complex ball quotient. Together with our companion paper and the known positive examples, this determines the equal weight case for every $n\ge6$: such a realization exists precisely for $n=6,8$. The lattice and the algebraic isomorphism are unrestricted, and torsion and divisorial ramification are allowed. The proof combines arithmetic reductions with identities involving Chern classes of fixed subvarieties and intersection inequalities for nef divisors on the GIT quotient. The final comparison includes all fixed components and requires no bound on the degree of an individual fixed curve.
#10 Weighted configurations and the nonexistence of arithmetic ball quotient compactifications
著者: Ryunosuke Nakano (Hokkaido University), Hisato Matsukawa (Hokkaido University)
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23228
要約:
We prove that, for every $n\ge9$, no ordered GIT quotient of $n$ points on $\mathbb P^1$ with positive rational weights smaller than one and of total weight two is isomorphic to the Baily--Borel compactification of an arithmetic complex ball quotient. The weights may lie on stability walls, and the lattice may have torsion and ramify along divisors. No compatibility with a prescribed period map is assumed. The proof compares homomorphisms from the tangent bundle to line bundles of negative degree with normal bundles of arithmetic fixed subvarieties. We describe these homomorphisms through coincident marked points and prove vanishing statements for their kernels. The comparison, together with integrality of Chern classes and the invariant theory of finite reflection groups, gives the nonexistence theorem without a Fano assumption on the GIT quotient.

#12 Brauer-Manin obstruction for symmetric powers
著者: Yang Cao, Lin Zhou
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23430
要約:
For a quasi-projective smooth geometrically integral variety $X$ over a number field, we prove that the Brauer-Manin obstruction for its symmetric powers coincides with the obstruction coming from $X$. As a consequence, Brauer-Manin obstruction for the symmetric powers cannot provide new cohomological obstruction to weak approximation of zero-cycles for $X$. 著者: Krishna Hanumanthu, A. J. Parameswaran
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23485
要約:
Let $X$ be a nonsingular complex projective surface and let $L$ be a very ample line bundle on $X$. We study the question: when does a general pencil in the linear system $|L|$ consist of only irreducible and reduced curves? We give a precise condition which guarantees an affirmative answer: in fact, this holds if and only if the genus of a general member of $|L|$ exceeds the irregularity of $X$. The proof is topological, using vanishing cycles and the theory of Lefschetz pencils. #17 Local topological zeta functions of generic symmetric and catalecticant determinantal varieties
著者: An Khuong Doan
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23689
要約:
We give explicit formulas for the local topological zeta functions at the origin of generic symmetric determinantal varieties and catalecticant determinantal varieties associated with generic Hankel matrices. In the symmetric determinant hypersurface case, the formula simplifies to a closed product expression and implies the Strong Monodromy Conjecture. For catalecticant determinantal varieties, we establish a simultaneous local reduction of the determinantal ideals and obtain a closed product formula for their local topological zeta functions. #18 Spectral networks, finite webs, and the $T_3$ cubic differential
privacy
著者: Omar Kidwai, Guillaume Tahar
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23752
要約:
We describe spectral networks for cubic differentials on $\mathbf{P}^1$ with exactly three poles all of order two, following observations of Hollands-Neitzke. To do so, we give a precise definition and establish some basic properties of a class of finite webs (corresponding to BPS states in physics) in the cubic differential setting, including their non-vanishing central charge. Turning to the example, we prove that at rational angles the network is totally degenerate; the number of walls and Stokes regions is explicitly computed and depends on an arithmetic criterion. Physically, this example corresponds to a fundamental building block for a large class of four-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=2$ QFTs with gauge group $\mathrm{SU}(3)$.

#20 Twisted $\mathbb{A}^1$-degrees of Wronski maps
著者: Thomas Brazelton, Jake Levinson
公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23816
要約:
The Wronski map is a central object of study in osculating Schubert calculus, and has been the focus of much work in real algebraic geometry over the last few decades due to its remarkable reality properties. In this work, we study it over arbitrary fields through the lens of quadratically-enriched enumerative geometry. A basic obstruction is that the Wronski map is not oriented in general, so existing approaches do not apply directly. To that end, we introduce twisted $\mathbb{A}^1$-degrees of finite flat lci maps of schemes, which generalize ordinary $\mathbb{A}^1$-degrees by allowing us to twist an orientation by the equation of a divisor, and $\mathbb{A}^1$-chambers, which generalize the notion of connected component of a real manifold. We then show that a certain weighted count of the fibers of the Wronski map depends only on the action of the absolute Galois group of the base field on the roots of the Wronski polynomial. This unifies Eremenko and Gabrielov's work on real degrees of Wronski maps, Levinson and Purbhoo's work on the character orientation of the real Wronski map, and Brazelton's work on enriched degrees of Wronski maps into one framework.

#23 Approximation and extension theorems for normal affine toric varieties

公開日: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 00:00:00 -0400
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22926
要約:
We study approximation and extension problems for holomorphic maps from Stein spaces to normal affine toric varieties, continuing the work of L\"ark\"ang and L\'arusson. Normality allows us to obtain twisted factorizations for arbitrary holomorphic maps from factorial complex spaces, including degenerate maps. This yields approximation and extension theorems under weaker cohomological assumptions.
