公開日: Wed, 25 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31485
要約:
I define a locus inside stacks of parabolic vector bundles on smooth projective curves which is important for the construction of twisted Hecke eigensheaves in the ramified geometric Langlands program for $\mathrm{GL}_n$. I give an explicit description of this locus in the case of rank $3$ parabolic vector bundles on $\mathbb{P}^1$ with parabolic structure at four points. #2 An Introduction to Semialgebraic Geometry
著者: Michel Coste
公開日: Wed, 25 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31550
要約:
The first chapter of these lecture notes explains algorithms for counting real roots and the Tarski-Seidenberg theorem. In the next two chapters we study semialgebraic subsets of $\mathbb R^n$ , which are defined by boolean combinations of polynomial equations and inequalities. The main tool for this study is the cylindrical algebraic decomposition, which is introduced in Chapter 2. The principal result of Chapter 3 is the triangulation theorem. This theorem shows that semialgebraic sets have a simple topology, which can be effectively computed from their definitions. Chapter 4 contains examples of finiteness results and uniform bounds for semialgebraic families. In particular, we give an explicit bound for the number of connected components of a real algebraic set as a function of the degree of the equations and the dimension of the ambient space.

#4 Heights and energies of polarized fibrations of varieties II. Fibrations over a polarized base

公開日: Wed, 25 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31691
要約:
We extend the height and energy inequalities for fibrations over curves to a fibration $f:X\to Y$ over a polarized variety $(Y,B)$. For an integrable relatively nef adelic line bundle $\bar L$, we prove a height inequality under the assumption that $L$ is nef on the inverse image of a geometric generic complete-intersection curve in $Y$. We deduce a lower bound for the local energy at almost every complex or nonarchimedean realization of a fixed polarized model when $L$ is $f$-ample. #5 Schubert positivity for filtered bundles
著者: Enhan Li
公開日: Wed, 25 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31862
要約:
We characterize formal filtered nefness of a formal quotient flag on a polarized Hodge--Lefschetz module by the formal nefness of its positive-rank total quotients. Using this criterion, we prove that quotient Schubert functors preserve formal nefness and that Schubert incidence modules inherit every commuting formally filtered nef flag. As geometric and numerical consequences, we recover the positivity criteria of Fulton--Lazarsfeld and Fulton and extend the Lorentzian polynomial theorem of Lu and Zheng to derived Schubert classes of filtered bundles. In combinatorics, our framework applies to tautological flags of flag matroids on ordinary and augmented matroid Chow rings as well as on the intersection cohomology of rational projective fans equipped with a common weight map. We deduce concordance-sensitive intersection inequalities, establish the Schubert positivity of the leading Riemann--Roch Chow class of a flag matroid, and show that the nonvanishing supports of derived Schubert numbers on an augmented Chow ring form the Higgs lifts of an intrinsically determined matroid quotient.

#13 Theta Functions and Intrinsic Mirrors of Laurent Phenomenon Algebras
著者: Jiarui Fei
公開日: Wed, 25 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32571
要約:
We construct bases of upper Laurent phenomenon algebras over a field by minimizing Newton polytopes in a single seed. For any translation-invariant total order on the exponents, each least exponent $a$ that occurs determines a smallest Newton polytope $Q_a$. Choose one element with Newton polytope $Q_a$ and least term $x^a$ for each such $a$. These elements form a basis. In every reachable seed chart, every Gauss valuation takes a nonzero basis expansion to the minimum of its values on the nonzero summands. Suppose the algebra has Laurent coefficients and smooth spectrum over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. After setting the curve parameters to one, we compute theta products on the intrinsic mirror from the exchange polynomials and give a finite presentation of the mirror algebra. A seed has a polynomial corner if each exchange polynomial can be multiplied by a unit of the coefficient ring to give a polynomial with constant term one. At such a corner, order the exponents by a weight with strictly positive, rationally independent coordinates. For every lattice exponent $a$ and integral weight $p$, the theta function indexed by $p$ has least $a$-weight $\min_{b\in Q_a}\langle b,p\rangle$ in the corresponding mirror chart. This reciprocity holds for cyclic exchanges and arbitrary pole orders. For fixed exchange supports and constant terms one, we determine when the mirror is smooth and the second mirror recovers the original algebra in the chosen torus coordinates. The coefficient tuples for which both conditions hold form a computable finite set. All their entries are positive integers.
#14 Uniform Algebraic Degeneracy of Entire Curves into Complex Projective Varieties
著者: Xianjing Dong, Yanda Lv
公開日: Wed, 25 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32597
要約:
This paper is devoted to the algebraic degeneracy of entire curves intersecting a simple-normal-crossing divisor on smooth projective varieties over C, within the logarithmic Green-Griffiths-Lang program. For negativelytwisted global logarithmic jet differentials, we introduce an intrinsic weighted conormal filtration along each positive-dimensional closed boundary stratum, with weights prescribed by the contact orders along boundary components. Applying the maximal Harder-Narasimhan slopes of logarithmic cotangent bundles and the normal degrees of the strata, we define an integer vanishing threshold which can improve the pole bound obtained from the slope-based ceiling. Under the stable-base-locus hypothesis for the logarithmic Semple tower, we then establish an improved second main theorem whose counting coefficients depend on the boundary stratum, actual contact orders and the ratio of source weight to twisting exponent. These coefficients are independent of the powers used to realize the stable base locus. We further derive a uniform algebraicdegeneracy criterion allowing positive corrected pole coefficients, formulated via a finite system of stratumwise inequalities together with the ampleness of a residual divisor class. This yields conditional orbifold Green-Griffiths-Lang type statements. Refinements arising from conormalbundle decompositions and sharp local pole estimates at surface boundary crossings are established. Our examples exhibit strict improvement over naive slope-based bounds and illustrate that full-weight poles can persist at boundary crossings, and furnish concrete instances satisfying the stablebaselocus hypothesis.

#20 Positive Geometry of Oriented Matroid Strata
著者: Arne Kuhrs, Artyom Lisitsyn
公開日: Wed, 25 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32956
要約:
We study the positive geometry of Grassmannians whose boundary is a union of Pl\"ucker divisors. For the full Pl\"ucker boundary the regions are closures of realization spaces of uniform oriented matroids. In rank three we show that the pair $(\mathrm{Gr}_{3,n},Z_{3,n})$ is a positive arrangement in the sense of Sturmfels and Telen for $n\le 9$ points and compute its combinatorial rank from point counts of the configuration space $\mathcal{X}_{3,n}$ over finite fields. For seven points the canonical forms of the regions span all logarithmic forms. For eight points they do not, because the M\"obius-Kantor configuration is not realizable over the reals. This determines for seven and eight points whether the integrands of Cachazo, Early and Zhang for the generalized biadjoint amplitudes of Cachazo, Early, Guevara and Mizera span the space of logarithmic forms. From ten points on, the genus is positive because a K3 surface appears in the boundary. For smaller boundaries and arbitrary rank we give a recursion that adds one point at a time and preserves the genus and the combinatorial rank. It recovers the positive Grassmannian and shows that realization spaces of stacked polytopes are regions of positive arrangements of combinatorial rank one.

#24 Flexible algebraic curves and fake vanishing cycles

リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31551
要約:
We prove that complex curves in sufficiently ample linear systems on simply connected projective surfaces are topologically (spin) flexible: any (spin) mapping class of the underlying real surface extends to a topological mapping class of the ambient 4-manifold. As a corollary, we prove that smooth curves in the complex projective plane are topologically (spin) flexible. #27 Linear rank-\(k\) nonincreasing and preserving maps
著者: Huajun Huang
公開日: Wed, 25 Nov 2026 00:00:00 -0500
リンク: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31807
要約:
We characterize the linear maps between rectangular matrix spaces that carry matrices of rank at most \(k\) to matrices of rank at most \(k\). Over every field, such a map has the form \(X\mapsto MXN\) or \(X\mapsto MX\T N\), or its entire image has upper rank at most \(k\). The proof uses polynomial functions on determinantal loci and extends Loewy's infinite-field theorem to all finite fields. Over fields with more than two elements, exact preservation of rank \(k\) implies rank-\(k\) nonincrease. Over \(\F_2\), for each \(k\ge2\), we construct a rank-\(k\) preserver that sends a rank-one matrix to a matrix of rank \(k+1\). For maps whose images have upper rank \(k\), three block rank equalities characterize exact preservation. If either source dimension exceeds five, every complex rank-four preserver is standard with full-rank factors or is a compression. A \(4\times5\) example attains the bound. The degree obstruction also shows that, over any algebraically closed field, every linear map \(\M_k\to\Alt_{k+1}\) sends some invertible matrix to a matrix of rank at most \(k-2\) when \(4\mid k\).
